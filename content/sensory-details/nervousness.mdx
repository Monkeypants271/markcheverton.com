**Physical (Touch and Internal Sensations)**

-   **Heart pounding** in the chest, often felt as a rapid or irregular heartbeat.
-   **Sweaty palms** or perspiration on the forehead and other parts of the body.
-   **Shaking hands** or trembling lips, a general unsteadiness in the limbs.
-   **Dry mouth** or a lump in the throat, making it hard to swallow.
-   **Stomach churning** or fluttering sensations, sometimes described as ‘butterflies’.
-   **Tense muscles**, particularly in the neck, shoulders, or jaw.
-   **Cold chills** or hot flashes, often accompanied by goosebumps.

**Sight**

-   **Wide eyes** or a fixed stare, often indicative of heightened alertness or fear.
-   **Fidgeting movements**, such as tapping feet, wringing hands, or shifting from side to side.
-   **Pale or flushed skin**, which can indicate a rush of adrenaline.

**Sound**

-   **Stammering or a shaky voice**, with frequent pauses and hesitations.
-   **Heightened sensitivity to sounds**, where even quiet noises seem loud or startling.
-   **Rapid, shallow breathing**, which can sometimes be audible.

**Smell**

-   An **increased sensitivity to smells**, particularly noticing odors that might otherwise be ignored.

**Taste**

-   A **metallic taste** in the mouth, often associated with anxiety and adrenaline.

**Internal Sensations**

1.  **Increased Heart Rate**: The heart beats faster than usual, which can be felt as a pounding or racing sensation in the chest.
2.  **Butterflies in the Stomach**: This is a common description for the fluttering sensation in the abdomen, often caused by increased adrenaline.
3.  **Dry Mouth**: The mouth may feel unusually dry, which is due to decreased saliva production when nervous.
4.  **Sweating**: Increased perspiration, especially on the palms, soles, and forehead.
5.  **Tightness in the Chest**: Feeling like the chest is constricted or heavy.
6.  **Shaky Hands or Trembling**: Minor shaking of the hands or other parts of the body.
7.  **Restlessness**: Difficulty sitting still, fidgeting, or a general feeling of being unsettled.
8.  **Breathlessness or Shortness of Breath**: Feeling short of breath or an inability to take deep, full breaths.
9.  **Dizziness or Light-headedness**: A spinning sensation or feeling faint, often due to changes in breathing patterns.
10.  **Headache**: Tension headaches or a feeling of pressure in the head.

**External Sensations**

1.  **Blushing**: Reddening of the face, neck, or chest, typically a visible sign of nervousness.
2.  **Sweating**: Noticeable perspiration that may soak through clothing.
3.  **Stuttering or Stumbling Over Words**: Difficulty speaking fluently, which can be evident in choppy or halted speech.
4.  **Avoiding Eye Contact**: Difficulty maintaining eye contact, often due to feeling overwhelmed or anxious.
5.  **Frequent Swallowing or Clearing the Throat**: These actions might increase as a response to the dry mouth and nervousness.
6.  **Tapping Feet or Drumming Fingers**: Physical manifestations of restlessness or impatience.
7.  **Posture Changes**: Slouching, closing off the body (e.g., crossed arms), or other protective postures.
8.  **Facial Expressions**: Grimacing, frowning, or displaying other expressions that reflect discomfort or worry.
9.  **Voice Changes**: Changes in tone, pitch, or volume, where the voice might become higher, softer, or shaky.
