**Sight**

-   Furrowed brows or frowning
-   Darting eyes, looking around restlessly
-   Pale, ashen, or flushed skin
-   Slight tremors in the hands or fingers

**Sound**

-   A high-pitched, nervous laugh
-   Stuttering or stammering speech
-   Heavy, sighing breaths
-   The sound of pacing feet or tapping fingers

**Touch**

-   Sweaty palms or cold, clammy skin
-   A tightness or knot in the stomach
-   Tense muscles, especially in the shoulders and neck
-   A twitching eyelid or jittery leg movements

**Smell**

-   The sharp scent of sweat under stress
-   The metallic taste of fear in the mouth (also gustatory)

**Taste**

-   Dry mouth or a bitter taste
-   Loss of appetite or a nauseous feeling

**Internal Sensations:**

1.  **Increased Heart Rate:** A noticeable acceleration of the heartbeat.
2.  **Stomach Discomfort:** Feelings of nausea, butterflies, or a knot in the stomach.
3.  **Tension:** Muscle tightness, especially in the neck, shoulders, and back.
4.  **Sweating:** Excessive perspiration, particularly in the palms and underarms.
5.  **Breathing Changes:** Rapid or shallow breathing, sometimes leading to hyperventilation.
6.  **Fatigue:** Feeling drained or exhausted without physical exertion.
7.  **Restlessness:** An inability to sit still or relax, accompanied by a sense of urgency.
8.  **Dry Mouth:** A parched feeling in the mouth and throat.
9.  **Dizziness:** Lightheadedness or a sensation of spinning.
10.  **Sleep Disturbances:** Difficulty falling asleep, staying asleep, or experiencing restful sleep.

**External Sensations:**

1.  **Fidgeting:** Constant movement of hands or feet, such as tapping, shaking, or shifting.
2.  **Facial Expressions:** Furrowing of the brow, squinting eyes, or pursed lips.
3.  **Body Language:** Closed posture, such as crossed arms or legs, and protective gestures like hugging oneself.
4.  **Speech Changes:** Speaking faster or slower than usual, with possible stammering or a quivering voice.
5.  **Pale Skin:** A noticeable paleness or ashen complexion.
6.  **Sweaty Palms:** Clammy hands and a general feeling of being sweaty.
7.  **Shaking:** Trembling hands or a noticeable shake in the voice or body.
8.  **Avoidance Behavior:** Steering clear of situations or people that might trigger further worry.
9.  **Eye Movements:** Darting eyes or a fixed, faraway stare.
10.  **Nail Biting:** Chewing or picking at nails or cuticles as a nervous habit.
