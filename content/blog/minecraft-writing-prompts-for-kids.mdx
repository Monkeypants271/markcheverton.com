One of the easiest ways to get a Minecraft-loving kid excited about writing is to let them write about Minecraft. They already know the world inside and out — the mobs, the biomes, the rules, the close calls — so the blank page stops feeling so scary. They're not inventing a world from scratch; they're telling a story in a place they already love.

Below are Minecraft-inspired writing prompts you can use at home or in the classroom. Pick one, set a timer for ten minutes, and just start. There are no wrong answers.

> These prompts are unofficial and Minecraft-inspired. Minecraft is a trademark of its owner. They are not official Minecraft products and are not endorsed by Mojang or Microsoft.

## Survival prompts

1. It's almost night, you're far from home, and you have one block of wood, half a loaf of bread, and no shelter. Write what happens next.
2. You log in one morning and discover that the sun never rises. The world is stuck in permanent night. What do you do?
3. You fall into a ravine you can't climb out of. Write the story of how you survive — and whether you ever get out.

## Character prompts

4. Write a day in the life of a single zombie. What does it want? What is it afraid of? Does it _know_ it's a zombie?
5. A villager decides they're tired of being a villager. Write their plan to do something completely different.
6. You meet another player who claims they've been stuck in the game for ten years. Write your conversation.

## Mystery and adventure prompts

7. You find a chest hidden deep underground with your own name carved into it. What's inside, and who put it there?
8. Strange new blocks start appearing overnight, blocks that don't exist in the game. Write what they do and where they came from.
9. A map leads you to the edge of the world. Write what you find when you finally get there.

## "What if" prompts

10. What if your pet — a dog, cat, or parrot — could talk for one day? Write everything they tell you.
11. What if you could craft one impossible item? Invent it, name it, and write the story of the first time you use it.
12. What if Herobrine knocked on your door and asked for help instead of trying to scare you? Write what he wants.

## Building and creativity prompts

13. Describe the greatest base you can imagine, then write the story of the day someone, or something, tries to break in.
14. You're given unlimited blocks but only 24 hours. Write what you build and why it matters so much to finish in time.
15. Write a legend that the villagers tell about a hero who built something no one thought was possible.
