If your reader loves Minecraft and you want to hand them a book they'll actually finish, the Gameknight999 series is one of the best places to start. But with two dozen Minecraft-inspired novels across several story arcs, parents and teachers often ask the same question: **what order should kids read them in?**

The short answer: **start with _Invasion of the Overworld_ and read in order from there.** Below is the complete reading order, plus a little about each arc.

> These are unofficial Minecraft-inspired novels. Minecraft is a trademark of its owner. These books are not official Minecraft products and are not endorsed by Mojang or Microsoft.

## Start here: Invasion of the Overworld

_Invasion of the Overworld_ is the first Gameknight999 adventure and the best entry point into the whole universe. A gamer named Gameknight999 is pulled inside his favorite game, where the monsters are real, the danger is real, and the only way home is to survive. Everything else builds on this book, so it's the right starting point for almost every reader.

## Do they have to be read in order?

Mostly, yes. Each three-book arc tells a complete story, but the arcs build on one another, so reading them in sequence gives the biggest payoff. If a reader is desperate to jump in somewhere new, the start of any arc — the first book in each group above — is the most forgiving place to do it.

## A great fit for reluctant readers

Kids who normally resist reading often fly through these books. The Minecraft setting removes the hardest part of starting a novel: they already know the world, the mobs, and the rules, so they can pour all their attention into the story. Short chapters and cliffhangers do the rest. For many families, a Gameknight999 book is the first novel a child finishes entirely on their own.
