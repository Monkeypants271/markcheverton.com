## The Mystery of Herobrine

### By: Serin

My name is Peenike and I have three friends Igav, Nohik and Czech.  We play Minecraft and mess with Joe most of the time.  This all starts when I picked up Joe’s bike.

**Chapter 1**

    Joe screamed, “Hey that’s my bike, get back here!”

But all I thought was should I respond or not and I decided to  respond with jumping off and running home but laughing.

Finally I’m back at home and ready to play Minecraft till I thought, “Hmm what’s this my sister downloaded? A mod probably, nothing huge?” But when got on I thought that my friends will want to see this.  Then I called them.

When they arrived I told them, “I found Herobrine skins were downloaded and my sister has no name tag .”

But then Czech said, “We should make sure, Igav joins the world.” So Igav joined the game.

“Make sure you don’t have enderpearls.” I joked.

“Nice joke Peenike.” Igav said while I was still laughing.

Czech yelled “snap out of it!”

Then my mom poked her head and all stared at her till she left “what was that about?” Igav questioned

“It was probably us yelling.” I answered.

“Oh I’m in.” Igav said jumping then we looked at each other and we saw our name tags were there looking back at the figure it was GONE! Suddenly Igav’s character died his stuff going into my inventory I opened it and asked “Igav is anything gone.”

“YEAH SOME RARE STUFF TO BE EXACT,Herobrine stole my twenty six obsidian and all my arrows,” Igav had responded quickly.

Then Joe snuck up behind us all so sudden and pushed us in the computer!

**Chapter 2**

    “It’s all your fault if you didn’t push us in my computer then we would’ve never gotten stuck here.” I said to nothing.

“Hey Peenike that’s air,”  Nohik said.

“Wait how did we even fall in?” Czech questioned.

“Well I kind of added something in my computer to go inside.” I had answered

“But why?” Nohik and Czech both asked “ Let’s just get find a way out of here and talk about this later.”  Then we noticed we didn’t know how to get out of Minecraft and Igav was gone!

“Hey get us out NOW!” Czech said angrily and randomly.

“Um I don’t know how.” I nervously answered.

“WHAT?!” he screeched.

“wait what I was dozing off?” “I said how do we get out? ”  Czech said calmly.

“Oh now I remember we have to kill a boss such as Herobrine or the wither.”

“What are we waiting for let’s go find Igav and then kill Herobrine.” Czech said in confidence.

Then as we began walking Joe slowly teleported into Minecraft.

**Chapter 3**

       Six hours later: “Who’s that?” Nohik questioned “What is it.” I responded then we heard Joe screaming . “Wait Joe?” We all questioned. As Czech suddenly pulled out a diamond sword.

“Wait you keep your stuff.” I said “Well that’s good.”

Czech shouted “HEY GUYS WE GOT A PROBLEM!” And then pointed to a mountain.

We all looked over at the mountain and it was covered in skeletons! “Wh … wh … WHAT!” Nohik said shocked.

Then Czech ran head first in the misshaped mountain of rock, dirt and arrows.

I looked at who was leading all the skeletons.  What saw was the figure in obsidian armor wielding one obsidian sword with one emerald sword.  Joe was running toward us but instead zoomed away from the arrows so, I did the same.

**Chapter 4**

       We stopped at a large village “Do think we lost (pant) Herobrine?” I slowly questioned

“Yeah (pant) I think so?” Nohik answered.  
“I think Czech has been captured?” Nohik said at random.

“Hey let’s see what we can find in this village you up for it Nohik.”

“Yeah I’m ready Peenike so let’s go.” Then we went searching for some good trades.

We found a blacksmith who wanted a zombie head but she might want more after that head so we ran to the other side of the village. “Hey she’s creepy Peenike we should stay away from her.” Nohik said suddenly

Then we looked over our shoulders and saw dancing giant ugly spiders.     “Eww what is that?” Nohik questioned grossed.

“Umm I think that’s a spider making fun of zombies?”

Then I pulled Czech’s sword and jumped at it but I missed it bit my leg it actually hurt me not a thing that I watched happen.

Then I thought to myself I cut it when it was on my leg and in one blow it died.

Then a zombie came out with gold armor “How dare you kill our spider I was talking to her about bringing me to her queen.” The intimidating zombie said while pulling it’s own golden sword.

I put Cezch’s sword away and pulled my own sword but mine was iron not diamond.

All the zombies under the oak tree laughed “Ha ha you pulled a weaker weapon on me.” The armored zombie said I between laugh’s.

Nohik pulled back his bowstring and arched an arrow at one of the zombies and fired killing the zombie in one shot.  They all stopped laughing and pulled their weapon’s  “You shot a zombie.” The armored zombie said with it’s sword held tightly. Then I struck at a random zombie not knowing which one I struck.

I opened my eyes and I had killed the armored zombie “Boss?” The zombies all said confused.

Then Nohik finished the talking by shooting both remaining zombies “Glad that’s over with.” Nohik said putting his bow away.

“I know it almost is long enough to make a chapter in my book.” I said.

**Chapter 5**

   I chopped down the oak tree where the zombies had been at then me Nohik went back into the middle of the village.  “Peenike look.” Nohik said pointing at a thief running in a house.

“Let’s see who they are?” I said in suspicion.

We ran into the house they were currently inside. Nohik drew his bow and I drew my sword at the thief.  “Who are you?” Nohik said aggressively toward the thief.

The thief turned around and stared me in the eye then jumped out the window.  They flashed red showing they got hurt.  Nohik shot an arrow out the window where the thief had escaped I started chasing them after jumping out the window.

“Where’d they go?” I asked Nohik.

“This way.” Nohik said pointing to his right.

“Come on” The thief yelled giving away their location.

Nohik was running with his bow trying to shoot the thief before he gave up.

I noticed after ten minutes of running we were going in a pattern.  So I  placed some TNT and hid near the closest house and waited.  When I saw the thief getting close I lit th TNT witch knocked the thief to the ground.

I pulled my sword to them and said, “We asked who are you?”

“Ok you caught me so guess I’ll tell you who I am.”  The thief said removing their mask.  It was Joe! “JOE!?” Nohik said half confused.

“And if you let me go I’ll tell show you where Czech is.” Joe said looking like he was in a lot of pain after the explosion.

“Show us us where he is.” Nohik said calmer.

“Ok I’ll show you.” Joe said with a big grin on his face.

**Chapter 6**

   Joe started showing us the way to where Czech hid.  “So how did you find Czech?” Nohik asked.

“I …” Joe almost  answered.

“What?”  I said.

He didn’t answer.  “Wait?  What is that?”  Nohik questioned staring at a strange dome made out of weird blocks.

“That’s were Czech is.”  Joe said with a smirk on his face.

“I have a plan.” I said “And Joe you’re part of it.”

“Seems good to me.”  Nohik said to my plan.

Joe looked like his legs just got stuck in cement.  His arms were shaking and his face was expressionless.  But he found himself after a pig jumped on him.  “What is this?” Joe said softly as the pig was squealing and running away.

“Let’s see if you can do step one, distract the front guards so me and Nohik can in.”  I said.

**Chapter 7**

   Me and Nohik were hiding in some large spruce trees watching Joe running straight towards the odd dome waving his stone sword.  All of the were shocked at Joe acting like this that they all froze.  “Hey let’s sneak inside now that we have the chance.”  Nohik whispered.

“Ok let’s go then.”  I said as we started.

We snuck up behind the guards and counted down with my fingers down from three then we struck two in the back Nohik used a bow and I used my sword.  “SPYS!” a guard screeched at Nohik and I.

“RUN!” Nohik yelled.

“NOO!” Joe yelled swinging his sword.

“What?”  Nohik said confused.

But before anyone said a word Joe killed the last guard.  “Uhh — that was a close one?”  Joe said very slowly.

“Yeah I guess?”  Nohik said.

“Well let’s go in and save Czech.”  I said with pride and confidence.

“I don’t think that you’ve ever seen what’s in there.”  Joe said.

“Well I don’t think you’ve ever seen what’s in there either Joe.”  Nohik said back at Joe.

Then I broke down the iron door.

Chapter 8

   When I broke down the door I was surprised what I saw.  We were all shaking my throat felt like it was closing up.  Because the Lama King was before our eyes.  But he was in a cage? “Why is the Lama King stuck in a cage?”  Nohik asked.

“Herobrine.”  Joe said.

“But why would want to capture the Lama King?” I questioned.

“Wait? Where is Herobrine?”  Nohik asked.

“Well I know why he captured the Lama King but I don’t know where Herobrine is … He captured the Lama King because he is the only one who can make emerald swords and is the only one who knows Herobrines weakness.”  Joe answered.

“Then let’s get Czech out of there and save the Lama King.”  Nohik said getting a little impatient.

“That might be bad dialog but i’ll add it.”  Joe said with a notepad at hand.

“Wait you’re also writing a story?” Nohik said.

“We’re all writing stories then.”  I said.

“This is awkward.”  Joe said.

Then a random person came down out of a jungle tree that had randomly appeared screaming, “GIVE ME DI…”

CRACK their leg broke on the large dome “RUN!” Joe yelled pointing to all the guards coming at us.

Nohik ran with Joe but stayed and ran to save the person with a broken leg.

**Chapter 9**

   I was running up to save the person from getting captured but instead I get hit in the back and dropped my healing potion I had owned and just now noticed.  It shattered into a million pieces and the healing liquid splashed on the person.  “AHH!” All the guards yelled.

It seems healing turned to harming, I thought to myself.

The jungle person jumped up in perfect shape and ran in the direction of Joe and Nohik.  “HEY!” I shouted.

CRACK CRACK!  Guards were chopping down trees to try and stop me.  BOOM BOOM the trees were all starting to fall BOOM BOOM!  “JUST RUN!” The person yelled out to me.

SMACK a tree fell on one of the guards.  SMACK another tree fell on a guard.  I see the end of the biome, I thought to myself.  CRACK CRack Crack crack cra… I finally got out of the biome.  “Joe Nohik.”  I said in relief.

“Why didn’t you follow us?”  Nohik asked.

“I guess I wanted to save everyone, but I can’t save Igav until we defeat Herobrine.”  I responded.

“Let’s try a different approach.”  Joe said smirking.

Fling fling fling.

**Chapter 10**

“Were are we.”  Nohik said.

“I don’t know but we have to be quite.” I whispered.

Splish splash splish SPLASH.  Water sprayed all over my face.

“What’s happening?”  Joe asked quickly.

Light started to turn on in the room.  We were in a dank cold room with water risen to our knees and we were tied to a pillar in the middle of the room.  The person from earlier was dropped in the room.  They flashed red and asked “How did you get in here?”.

“We don’t know, darts hit Joe and Peenike then me that’s all I remember.”  Nohik answered.

“All I remember is talking and waking up here.”  Joe said.

“I rememer running from the guards, talking, darts flying threw the air and waking up Nohik.”  I said.

“Strange,we all have the same memory but not the same memory.”  The person said.

‘No we have the same story in different words, also what’s your name? Joe asked.

“Uhh … I can’t answer that right now because I had amnesia.”  The person said.

“This seems like a lie.”  Nohik said.

Then the person ran away and ran into the door.  “Quick let’s get untied.”  I said.

We started fighting in every direction up down left and right.  “Everyone run in different directions.”  Nohik said.

We all ran in front of us SNAP the rope broke and I fell face first into water.  “Ow.”  We all said.

“Get the person.”  Joe said.

“You’ll never ow catch me.”  The person said while slipping.

We all caught up with them and tried to pull our weapons but nothing was in our hands.  “What happened?” I asked confused.

BOOM BOOM something was walking but we didn’t know where.  BOOM BOOM “Where is it?  What is it?”  Nohik asked.

“I don’t know.”  Joe said.

BOOM BOOM “Where is it coming from?  Nohik asked very confidently.

BOOM BOOM BOOM BOOM!  “It’s getting to close.”  Joe said angrily.

Beep BOOM!  A TNT monster was right in front of me and blew threw the wall.  “ROAR!”  The beast screamed.

“HOW DO WE FIGHT IT!”  I yelled.

“I DON’T KNOW, WATER!”  Joe shouted.

“I HOPE SO!”  Nohik shouted.

The monster had started throwing TNT at us and all the explosions made it hard to move.  “HEY I GOT A BUCKET!”  I shouted.

“GOOD NOW FILL IT AND DUMP IT OVER THAT TNT MONSTER!”  Joe screamed.

I threw the bucket over the beast then it started to wail in pain, “AHH.”

It was trying to throw TNT but the water stopped it from doing so.  “Joe it worked.”  I said.

“Wait it’s still alive.”  Nohik said.

Herobrine teleported inside the chamber and knocked us all back with his obsidian sword.  “You’ll never get of here now and I can finally make the overworld change to my creation.”

**Chapter 11** 

“What?  What does he mean by you’ll never get out of here?”  I asked.

“I know.”  The person said, “He surrounded this dungeon in bedrock.”

“How?”  Nohik questioned.

“He can build anything,  with a risk… he slowly starts to fade from Minecraft.”  The person replied.

“Well maybe Herobrine didn’t build under us.”  I said confidently.

“Well there’s one way to find out… DIG!”  Nohik shouted.

We all pulled out a pickaxe (but the strange person) and we all started to mine down.  “Hey, I don’t see any bedrock down here.”  Joe said.

“Go mine East.”  I said.

And everyone started mining East.

**Chapter 12**

   “We made it.”  I said to Joe and Nohik.

“But what about that strange person, we can’t just leave them here.”  Joe said.

“You’re right we need as much hand as possible to save Igav.”  I said.

“Oh so it’s ok to follow you now.”  The person said.

“What you were following us this entire time?”  Nohik questioned while pulling an arrow back.

“NOHIK DON’T FIRE!”  I shouted while jumping in between the arrow and person.

But then the entire world stopped and nobody moved.  A felt a creature coming toward me.  Zzzzzzzzz sssss SNAP.  A spider bit my leg and then everybody moved into action as Nohik shot the spider and killed it in one shot.  “I wasn’t trying to shoot anyone.”  Nohik said with a smerk coming on his face.”

“AHH!”  Something from above screamed then we looked up and saw Czech.

**Chapter 13**

Then Czech came flying down on me and CRASH crushed me.  “Ow.”  I said slowly.

“You guys?  I thought Herobrine caught you?”  Czech said confused.

“Well we kinda …”  I tryed to say.

“Thought Herobrine caught me.”  Czech said finishing my sentence.

We all just noticed we were in the open at night.  “Let’s talk later we need to find shelter.”  Joe said.

“I agree.”  The person said.

Then Nohik asked Czech,”Why did you run the opposite direction of us when Herobrine first met us?”

“The reason is, I thought we were going to fight Herobrine.”  Czech answered.

“Why did you think we even could fight him,  we probably would have got killed by the skeletons.”  I said.

“Becau…”  Then three creepers blew up right behind Czech.

“That was shot far off topic Peenike.”  The person said.

“I agree with Peenike.”  Czech said.

“I agree too we can’t hold back saving Igav.”  Nohik said.

And everyone followed me, even the person.  “Hey strange person,  What is you’re real name?”  Nohik asked.

“Cutter.”  The person whispered.

“Cutter?”  Czech questioned.

“We have normal names just go by our job name, but my real name is Hatter.”  Hatter answered.

After the talk we all looked up and saw the back of the dome where the Lama King is captured.  “This is it, Herobrine’s lair.”  Hatter said.

**Chapter 15**

Then we started weird noises from the floor.  So we went over to investigate  the noises.  The floor was covered in dirty carpet but one carpet square felt wierd on my feet “This square feels weird.”  I whispered.

“Smash it.”  Nohik whispered.

I pulled out some shears and cut the carpet.  We looked down and saw ladder going far down a cave.  “Let’s go.”  I said with the same reaction of everyone, scared.

**Chapter 16**

“Well, i’m saving Igav and that’s final.”  I whispered .

“I did this, so if Igav is dead, it’s my fault.”  Joe whispered.

I kept going down.  “WHO IS TALKING UP THERE!”  Herobrine screamed.

I jumped to the end of the ladder and pointed my iron sword at him.  “THAT CAN’T HURT ME!”  Herobrine boomed.

“Maybe not mine, but our weapons will hurt you.”  I said.

We waited about two minutes till finally my friends came down.  I then charged at Herobrine but then tripped on my shoelaces and fell face first right in front of Herobrine.  “I’m not going to waste my time fighting you, so Zhogaxe will.”  Herobrine  growled.

Then a giant creeper hissed at us from behind.  “RUN!”  Hatter shouted.

I ran as fast as I could not stopping but tripped on my shoelaces again.  “RUN, I WILL CATCH UP AFTER I TIE MY SHOES!”  I shouted.

I started tying my shoe and heard the hissing behind me. After my shoe was tied I ran to the group and as I ran I heard Zhogaxe explode the bridge but dared never to look back …
