Trapped craft book 1

By Judah

“Emma left,” said Lucy. Emma turned in that direction to see a zombie pigman raising his sword at her. Quickly she swung her sword hard. The monster vanished leaving a gold nugget behind. 

“Thanks” said Emma “That pigman was about to kill me.”

“Could you guys celebrate later?” said Will.” “Wither skeletons don’t play by the rules.” The two friends quickly helped Will defeat the wither skeleton. 

“Quickly collect the skull,” said Jeff, finishing his duel with a blaze. Emma collected the skull and ran towards the portal followed by her friends. Once they got through the portal Emma finally relaxed and comfortably leaned back against the wall of her room. It was a simple room with a bed in one corner, a door at the opposite side, and a chair in which she was sitting in right now, her computer on her lap playing Minecraft. Today she and her friends had decided to visit the Nether to stock up on supplies from the Nether fortress but they were put under attack sooner than they had expected. Now that they were safe in the Overworld, the four friends managed to get to their mushroom cottage before nightfall. Right before they were about to get in bed thunder began booming outside. Lighting cracked throughout the sky. 

“Got to go,” said Emma reaching for the power button on her computer. Right before Minecraft was about to turn off a bolt of lighting hit the side of the house. Emma heard bees everywhere and then everything went black. When Emma woke up she was sitting next to a red bed. Everywhere around her there was mushroom. Suddenly Emma realized she was in a mushroom house just like the one in – Minecraft. Emma stood up and saw her friends looking around too.  Was it possible that they could be in Minecraft? No no no it couldn’t be happening but it was so real, the blocky sensation, the destination… They had to be in Minecraft. 

“Where are we?” Lucy asked 

 “Minecraft i think,” said Emma. Just then they heard a tiny baby-like shriek nearby.

“Ghast!” Will jumped up, donned his diamond armor, grabbed his diamond sword and his enchanted bow and leaped out of the house bellowing… and crashed into a tree. The others followed slowly. There outside was the biggest ghast they had ever seen. Will shot at the ghast but missed and the arrow rebounded. “Ouch” said Will and promptly disappeared. A minute later he came back out. 

“ How come you did not tell me you had a totem of undying?” Said Jeff.

“Didn’t,” said Will. “It just happened.” 

Jeff stared at him for a second then he took up his own bow and shot. Jeff never missed and soon there was a Ghast tear floating off the ground. Emma walked toward it but instead of floating into her inventory it just stayed there. Emma picked it up and returned to her friends. “How did the ghast get out of the Nether?” said Emma. “The portal is too small for it.”

“No clue,” said Will.” “Usually ghasts

stay in the Nether. It’s impossible for one to go to the Overworld without the work of a griefer.”

“But who could it be?” said Jeff. 

“I do not know.” said Lucy
