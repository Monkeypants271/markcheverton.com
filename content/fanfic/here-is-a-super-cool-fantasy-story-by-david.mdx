## Beating World Records

### By David

Once upon a time on a magical world of dreams people had to beat world records to be king of them all. You had to be brave you had to be smart you had to be kind to become king of the dreams. There was this one group of friends that were named David,Billy and Bob and they wanted to work together so the could all become the king but they were only. 10 years old they thought to themselves how the could beat a record.

One day David, Jimmy and Bob got together after school and they all whispered each other “What if we beat a world record and become king of all dreams.”

They also whispered, “Jinks you owe me a soda.”

They all laughed out loud and their teacher yelled at them to be quiet.  Their teacher was “Mr.Mean,” they whispered to each other.

After school they got together and looked at a book and there read, The World’s Records. They were extremely excited to see what was inside of the book. They looked at the first one with three men in the book. There said in the book these three men juggled the most watermelons with three people.

They looked at each other and whispered quietly, “Do you know how to juggle.”

They all whispered, “No!”

They told each other to go home and practice.

So they did and once David got home in his house, his house was made of Marshmallows and candy canes but he didn’t want to tell his mom that he started eating some of the candy canes from his house. Right when he went on the computer his older brother kicked him off the computer. David yelled, “Get away from my computer.”

His brother yelled back, “Why should I?”

“I don’t know!” Yelled David.

So David ran to his room so he could look at his World’s Records Book because he liked to read it. But just then he remembered that he had to practice juggling, so he did just that. First he started with two balls and threw one after the other. Once he got done with practicing with two balls he went to three balls. He got to starting juggling with three balls so he invited his friends over to play then they all started juggling. They were juggling together with 20 balls! They were good at juggling. They wanted to make a plan for when they become king so they did. There on the plan they wrote on the piece of paper and said “There for us three lucky people who become king will make the days shorter for school.”

They all agreed with it so they went to the person that gets to see the records and did it. Then the person yelled, “These people are incredible! They should become king.”

The three boys got in the van to the kingdom and they became the new king. They were amazed by them becoming king so they whispered into the microphone and they asked what was on their paper. All the people were amazed by kids being the king and all the teachers yelled, “Nooooooooo”

The three kids asked their butler for some food and they got hot gravy with grapes and potatoes with ice cream on the potatoes and chopped up turkey with syrup. They were amazed and almost instantly they ate it all. The butler was amazed by how fast they ate the food. They all laughed, “Hahaha!”

They went to go see how many computers they had and counted 500. They they were thinking it’s the same number as rooms. There were 10 floors and that’s the best part! They were excited to finally live in the kingdom. They thought they were the luckiest people on Dreamland.

The next year, they had been living there for a year now. Then one day they had never thought this would happen. A giant tornado came on a very windy day and they saw where it was heading on the news. They all yelled, “Tornado!”

They all ran into the bathroom to hide and they were scared to death because they could hear the strong winds going through their ears. They could also hear windows breaking and just then the tenth floor collapsed. All they could hear was a big bang and the door of the bathroom fell off and fell through the floor.

The tornado was over and they got up and looked. The floor was completely destroyed. They climbed over the broken stuff to see outside. The windows were gone and half of the city was destroyed and all the rest was flinging away. People had broken bones. It would cost billions of dollars to repair. They were very sad because their parent’s house were gone along with the places they said they would go when a tornado hits. Their parents never answered their phones. All the power was out in the city.

A lot of people  were on the ground and very hurt  and Jimmy Found his parents on the ground not breathing. Jimmy was bawling his eyes out. Then Jimmy and David saw that Bob wasn’t in there.

They were scared because they worried about Bob.

Then they had no words to say because they found Bob on the ground with a piece of glass through his chest.  They started to cry. They were very sad and that was the end of their kingdom.
