A night in the darkness there was a boy whose name  
was CC Rider and he was 55. He rode his horse on the  
countryside. One day he felt like he was being watched  
and he looked behind him and saw a man in black. He  
remembered seeing a guy like him named Sonvill and he  
was chasing him. Soon CC Rider went into the town hall  
and the guy still is fooling him.  
Soon CC Rider was in the library and then Sonvill  
rode his horse back to his evil lab and he took off his black  
helmet and there were really bright lights that shone on his  
helmet in the darkness. Then he heard someone coming  
but no one was there.  
25 years earlier Sonvill was little. He had great  
friends and one day when he was in 1st grade CC Rider  
came and all of Sonvill’s friends got lost, they played with  
CC Rider.  
So he moved away and so since then he became  
evil.  
He has been trying to kill him but at 11:00 he woke  
up and he felt something. He wanted to not be evil.  
So the next day he went up to CC Rider and asked  
him if he wanted to join the dark side with him. Then CC  
Rider went and punched him in the nose and then CC  
Rider rode away on his horse.  
Sonvill was so mad that his lightning struck and  
everybody ran and screamed. He took over land Porter.  
Then all of the bears,lions,wolves and coyotes took it over  
together. But he felt his mom calling to him, “You are  
good.”  
Then in that moment CC Rider came and said, “Will  
you like to join the good side with me? Then we can be  
friends forever and we will not take over the world, deal?”  
“Ok deal.”  
“Let’s go and be good!”  
They went to tell everybody that they can come back  
into town.  
“Look! There is everybody and they are coming.”  
CC Rider said, “Sorry for punching you. Really I’m  
the bad guy after all.”  
Sonvill said, “No I am because I was the one who  
took over the town, so no, it is my fault. So let’s ride off.”  
So they rode off together.
