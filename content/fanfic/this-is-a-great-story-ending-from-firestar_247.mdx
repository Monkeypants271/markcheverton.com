The crafting chamber was filled with NPCs and players. There was much fewer villagers than players, and the villagers that were there were being hacked at by shimmering diamond and iron blades. To Gameknight’s horror, the floor was littered with items ranging from carrots and wheat to armor and bows. “NOOO! STOP!” Gameknight shouted, but everyone ignored him. “GAMEKNIGHT!” A terribly firmiliar voice shouted. “HELP!”  
Gameknight turned and saw his friend Hunter, struggling against a player named CoolDude97. While very skilled with her bow, Hunter was rather clumsy with a sword. “Hunter! I’m coming!” Gameknight responded. A popping noise sounded, and suddenly racing beside him was his friend Shawny. Hundreds more popping noises sounded, and hundreds more players appered. Together they charged torwdes the raiding players. Gameknight reached Hunder and with a few quick hacks, CoolDude97 despawned, leaving behind items that Gameknight didn’t bother looking at. “Thanks.” Hunter said giving him a quick nod. “Lets do this.” Together they continued the fight for life, the fight for Crafter’s village, and although they didn’t know it, the fight to save all of Minecraft.
