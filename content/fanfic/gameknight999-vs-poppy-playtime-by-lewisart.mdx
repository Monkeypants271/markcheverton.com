The circle of light disappeared around gameknight. He looked around and realized he was in the horror game Poppy playtime. “Welp, might as well continue on.” Gameknight said to himself. Walking to the front desk gameknight noticed the green vhs tape. Without thinking he picked it up and inserted it in the vhs. “And while that’s doing it’s thing i can go get the blue grab pack hand.” walking up to the security room door. He did the code to get in. green, pink, yellow, red. Once the door opened up gameknight walked in. “Now to insert the tape and get the grab pack.” gameknight said to himself. After the video played gameknight got the blue grab pack hand and walked out the door. Walking past the fencing thing he aimed the blue hand at the blue hand print and fired. The hand shot out and landed where it should. In the middle.

The door recognized the grab pack hand and started loading. Then it started opening. Once it opened gameknight continued walking down the hall until he saw huggy wuggy. Noticing the door on the left he fired the blue grab pack hand at the hand above the door. Then the power went out, and gameknight saw the power key in huggy’s hand. Shooting the blue grab pack hand at the key he fired and got it. Walking over to the power room, he used the key to get the doors open and walked in. walking over to the voltage box, he used the blue grab pack to rip open the box. Firing the blue grab pack hand he used it to reroute the power. As soon as gameknight touched the other box the power came on. As soon as he walked out huggy was gone.

“Where did huggy wuggy go?” Gameknight thought to himself. Shooting the blue hand at the hand above the door. The door started to load and then opened. Gameknight continued walking down the corridor until he saw huggy wuggy’s arm slither through the door. “I found you now huggy.” Gameknight said to himself. Pushing open the door, gameknight walked in quickly but cautiously. But Huggy wasn’t in the room. “Weird.” gameknight thought. “Huggy was just here.”
