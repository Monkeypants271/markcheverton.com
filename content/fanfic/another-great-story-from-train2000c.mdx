Gameknight and the crafter went down a long ladder. As they went down, Gameknight felt it getting warmer. Is there lava near hear? Wondered Gameknight. The crafter stepped away from the ladder once he got to the bottom. When Gameknight got down, he was puzzled. Instead of a large room, there was just a blue box. The box looked like the tardis from a T.V. show in the physical world but with a blocky texture to it.

“Where’s the Crafting Chamber?” asked Gameknight.

“In that box,” motioned the crafter. “It was crafted by an NPC experimenting with beacon blocks and what they could be used for. He made this.”

The crafter stepped inside and disappeared. Gameknight moved slowly towards the box and saw a handle. He pushed and was teleported to a small but grand chamber with a bridge heading towards a tower with a beam of light going through it. Villagers were moving from one room to another. Gameknight was particularly interested in the strange looking block at the center module. It had a white texture with a black picture on it.

“This is how villages run with the dalek mod,” said a voice next to him.

It was the crafter.

“What’s the dalek mod?” asked Gameknight.

“A mod that allows players (and some villagers) to travel through dimensions and coordinates without portals.”

The crafter then cleared his voice and spoke to all the villagers. “Send the tardis to village number 12!”

Ad with that said, there was a wooshing noise.
