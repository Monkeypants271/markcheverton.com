THE RETURN OF MALACODA!!!!

Deep in the dark land of the Void, the was a tiny island below The End. It was made of cobblestone. On the island, a giant ghast with a look of evil on his eyes was waiting for revenge. He was thrown down here by Erebus and his minions. Little did he know the Gameknight999, the User-that-is-not-a-user, had killed him in the land of dreams. Malacoda gained energy from the purple particles on the island. He flew up tword the portal to the overworld. He found it and went into it. Meanwhile, at a nearby village, Gameknight and Crafter, a young NPC crafter, were rebuilding the crafting chamber. They had set up defensive systems in the tunnels. So if any monster came, flaming arrows would hit the monster, killing it or scaring it away.
