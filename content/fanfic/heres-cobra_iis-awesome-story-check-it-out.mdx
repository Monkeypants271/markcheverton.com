Steve spawned right next to a mountain. Immediately he mined 2 trees and started up the mountain in search of coal. He found 3 blocks of coal and started mining them. He looked up to see that it was becoming night. Then he started digging a hidey-hole. Forgetting he was on an over-hang, he fell through the ground. He lost 5 HP and discovered 2 zombies heading towards him. As fast as he could, he dug into the ground. But then he fell into a cave and found himself facing 4 cave spiders. He ran as fast as he could in the other direction but unfortunately he did not know that spiders are faster than users so he thought he had lost them. But when he turned around he found that they were still chasing him and that they had found a giant spider friend.

Steve kept on running but in the dim light he didn’t see a cliff. He fell off the cliff and into a pool of water and the skeletons that were in the water turned to look at him with their bows drawn. Steve dived down into the water and swam under the legs of the skeletons to get out. He climbed ashore and made it out of there with only one arrow hitting him! He walked a long way and began to see light. He hurried to the light hoping that it was daylight but it turned out to be lava. As he looked at the lava an arrow whizzed past his head. He turned to see a skeleton shooting at him. He tried to run away but the skeleton shot an arrow at his chest and the arrow pushed him into the lava. Then he died and the last thought to go through his head was I am an unfortunate user.
