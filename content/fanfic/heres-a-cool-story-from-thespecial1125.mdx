**The Clash of Two Armies**

Hunter is a diamond miner. He mines diamond to protect his village. His goal is to be promoted to mine uranium, because it is stronger than diamond.

The village has little protection and it needs a uranium miner. Hunter needs to get more experience as a diamond miner before he can be a uranium miner. The only place left to mine is deeper in an old mine shaft.

Hunter has no choice but to go into the old mine that is in danger of collapsing. The old mine was owned by his great-great-grandfather who had to shut it down because some weird things happened. The problems were small at first, but then a few miners mysteriously disappeared.

Hunter finds a side shaft that was hidden in the mine. Side shafts are rare because they are supported by rubber posts that weaken the mine. It has craters from TNT that was set off.

Hunter mines some old TNT and accidentally sets it off opening a passage way. The passage way is dark and someone is in it. He realized it was a zombie and that it was

chained to the roof. He felt so creeped out that he killed it with TNT. The explosion removed a strange stone that reveled a lever.

The lever opened a passage way into a temple. After Hunter opens the door, he sees the ultra diamond on a gold pedestal being guarded by two giant zombies. Hunter kills the zombies by putting TNT in their noses.

Hunter takes the ultra diamond and heads toward home. It’s late and he needs to sleep.

Because Hunter took the ultra diamond two clans awaken at night-fall. The clans are in one of the temple room that is blocked off by ruble.

While searching for a way out, one of them triggers an explosion that kills some of them. The explosion also makes an entrance to the main room witch held the ultra diamond.

The wall that fell down helped them escape from the temple. Before the clans left the temple they looked on top of the gold pedestal and they saw that the ultra diamond was missing. The clans looked for foot steps.

The clans follow Hunter’s foot-steps out of the temple. The mine was very dark but the clans had night-vision. Every second they walked faster and faster and faster until they were at a running speed. The vibration from their steps almost made the mine collapse on top of them. The clans came upon the village and attacked.

The clan attacks only attack at night. They blew up the wall surrounding the village when they first attacked. They tied the pigs tails together and set them on fire. The pigs burnt half of the village and then they jumped into the water putting out the fire on them. They killed the cattle and some villagers in search of the ultra diamond.

Hunter took the ultra diamond to the weapons shop owner to see if it can help protect their village. He tells Hunter that the ultra diamond is what the awoke the clans. They are searching for it, because it is the only thing that can free their cursed souls. To free them, Hunter must find the enchanted book of spells to forge the ultra diamond sword and use it to fight them.

Hunter goes into the temple again to find the enchanted book. He casts a spell on the ultra diamond to form the ultra diamond sword. He must go back to the village to prepare for battle.

Hunter grabbed some TNT to build a trap as the first line of defense . He buried the trap to hide it. He knew he needed a way to set it off, so he made some pressure plates and placed them on the path Hunter sees that it is almost nightfall so he hurries back to the village. Once the clans came out, they charged towards the village and triggered the trap. The village spotted them. The cavalry was sent out with Hunter leading them into battle.

The clans were numerous so the cavalry charges and kills half of both clans, turning the tides of battle. However, the king of skeletons was left untouched. Soon Hunter finds the king of skeletons they lock into battle. In the middle of the final showdown an arrow almost hits hunter but instead it hits the king of skeletons.

Hunter defeats the clan and the king of skeletons. Their souls are freed. From then on that day was a holiday. Hunter was rewarded with gems, goods, and a promotion to uranium miner.
