I’m no one special. I’m just another face in the crowd. I’m a day-dreamer, writer, reader, fan-girl, an artist  and gamer. All that packed into one body, one mind. I’m no one special, just the casual twelve year old girl. Nothing to say much. It’s quite simple really. I live in a small suburban town. With all necessities, of course! Starbucks, CVS, Walmart, Good Shepherd Hospital, Public Library; and of course, a school. The school is huge. We use iPads for schoolwork. (sometimes games) Do you see how simple my life is? I actually kind of wish something interesting could happen,just to make life more intriguing.But, why am I complaining? People are starving, kids are in poverty and, there is tons of violence around the world everyday. I just want to have an adventure. Become some warrior hero with some grand awesome comebacks. Standing up to a powerful wizard or ninja. Fighting for those who I love. I want adventure. I want challenges. As in, jumping off a cliff then,falling into a river to run away from, some evil minions. But, until that moment, it’s just me and my imagination.
