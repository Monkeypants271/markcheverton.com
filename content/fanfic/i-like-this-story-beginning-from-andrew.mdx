Theres many, many Minecraft servers out there. However, there is one server that nobody talks about: MineCrash. Heard of it? Thought not. MineCrash isn’t just any other server. On this server, you fight in PvP tournaments not for fun, but for survival.

Each virtual year 25 new contestants (“Newbies”) are ‘hand-picked’ from servers everywhere, especially popular servers. Thrown into a survival world, they must mine and survive, getting the best armor and weapons they can, until Judgement Day comes once each in-game month. on Judgement Day, all the contestants are thrown into a huge custom arena, with dense forests, jungles and deserts, supposedly built by Him and a whole bunch of hackers. If you die three times (If you lose three games)… Game over. Nobody knows what happens to the losing contestants, but there’s many theories. some people think the losers are deleted from Minecraft forever, but nobodies sure. It’s impossible to leave the server also, so the contestants are forced to play forever for the entertainment of… Well, nobody knows what to call the thing. it’s just a dark silhouette that people call “Him”.

However, our story really starts when two regular, fun-loving players, Stephan\_ and ExTra, are picked among the unlucky people to ‘join’ the server. Immediately, the two realize something is up, and they will try everything to save all the people on MineCrash and destroy Him forever. Making friends, enemies, and fighting epic battles, the pair soon realize this isn’t just a game.

It’s a struggle to stay alive.
