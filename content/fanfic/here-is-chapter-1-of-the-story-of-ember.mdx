Chatper 1 – Ember ShadowChaser

Ember soared through the skies with Blaze at her side, when she looked at her she couldn’t help but notice that Blaze was pale for an AirHorn. There are 6 tribes of dragons, AirHorns can fly faster than any dragon, BlackClaws can shapeshift, Leviathans can breathe underwater, LightFurys can breathe ice, Tunnelers dwell in the earth, ShadowFangs can shoot venom and disappear and are the rarest.  
Ember was an BlackClaw when she transformed.  
They landed in a town called Source where the Airhorns lived and passed by a store that was selling CaveDogs and Baeliths. “I think I should be going home.” Ember said. But when she was about to go she saw a strange dragon out of the corner of her eye.

Chapter 2 – Asher DiamondCrusher

I was at a town called Source just looking around and I saw this BlackClaw looking at me and thought about leaving but decided to stay.”Hello, good morning”She said.’That is not for you to decide whether it is a good morning for me or not.’I said. ‘but for me.’ “um okay,” She said.”Where are you from?” ‘Nowhere.’ I said. “I am not from this place, nor this world.”She said.  
I then disappeared. My heart was hammering as I flew away.’So, there are others like me.’I said. I flew up and spun down and hit the earth and went to my world. I felt a pulling sensation when I got there and I realized that I had took her with me. I still didn’t know her name. Suddenly she fell on my talons!
