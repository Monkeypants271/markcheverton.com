Loud bagging. Kick and punches thrown. Suddenly my brother Andrew screamed. Then chaos erupted when me and my sister Anna ran into the garage. “O MY GOD, are those bodies” I hollered.  Andrew was nodding yes. “Wake up Ryan NOW!” he screamed. “RYAN RYAN RYAN WAKE UP!” I yelled. “Shut up Lily.” He cried with annoyance. “You need to call mom now because it is an emergency!” I roared. We argued for approximately 10 minutes, but he finally agreed and called mom.

When mom arrives after getting Starbucks at a Price Chopper and she sees the hole in the wall and yells at Andrew. “ANDREW WHY IS THERE A HOLE IN THE WALL?” “Because I was a bored mother and you told me to stop chopping down trees.” Andrew calmly said. “That’s not what I meant by that.” she sighed. “But mom look inside of it.” We all peered inside. “O MY HEAVENS ARE THOSE TUBES FILLED WITH BLOOD AND DO THOSE JARS HAVE BRAINS AND ARE THOSE BODYS ON THE GROUND!” Ma screamed. “Yep.” I said calmly. Ryan took Anna back inside because she would get nightmares if she stayed here with us. “Ryan, call the cops.” Mom yelled to him as he was walking away with Anna. “Why me, you’re the adult.” He roared.  “My phone is dead like usual.” mom hollered. “Fine I’ll call.” Ryan disappeared inside with Anna. He got his phone off the counter and called the police.

As soon as the cops came mom got a hotel room. We left a cooler for the police filled with water in the garage for them to drink, but they wouldn’t take any. We got into the car and drove off.  “BYYYYYEEEE” Mom said driving off “goodbye ma’am” The cop said kindly.  “Ok guys I do NOT want you talking or researching about this ok” ma said nervously. “Fine” We all said with an attitude.

We were on the road for three hours before mom yelled. “Ok, we’re here! Time to wake up.” no one moved.  “Lily, Andrew, Anna, Ryan” she whispered trying to wake us up. We finally woke up after she shook us up. After we got to our room and got our stuff up into the room, we ate dinner. When dinner was through, ma with Anna took a nap which was the perfect time to look up history about our house MY GOD, come look at this.” Ryan hollered. “What is it, Ryan?” We all Whispered. “This website says if you go to California there is an insane place, where you can look up anything about your house.” Ryan said excitedly. “Wow I wish we were in California then we can know everything about our house.” Andrew said daydreaming. “There’s a plane ride to California tomorrow, should I get us tickets?” asked Ryan. “If it will help us find what we are looking for, then yes!” I replied.

The next day me, Andrew and Ryan snuck out of the hotel at 2:00am got on a plane to California and off we went. It took 6 hours… but we eventually we landed in LA. After we landed, we went to a huge resource center that has all the information on computers and maps about all the houses in the world. Andrew found information about the owners that used to own the house. He was shocked to learn that a mad scientist used to live there. The mad scientist Victor was eventually found to be doing experiments on people in the house. That explained why we found jars of brains, bodies, and blood.  I told the boys it was time to head back home before mom woke up. We boarded the plane with the shocking news.

Mom received a call from the police that she could come back to the house safely with the family. The police met us there with the wildest news. We were informed that the blood was animal blood, the brains were squishes and the bodies mannequins.  Ma was so apologetic and felt bad for wasting the police men’s time. Andrew, myself and Ryan just stood there in shock that the information that we found was a lie. In the end we are happy that we now have all these fun props for Halloween. Hopefully mom lets us keep them.

THE END
