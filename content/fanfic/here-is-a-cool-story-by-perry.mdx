Gameknight, Stitcher, Hunter, Baker, Digger and Crafter rode off into the desert with their horses.  
Gameknight signaled the others to stop. Gameknight got off his black and white spotted horse and saw a rabbit smile. It was the first time he saw someone smile. Gameknight999 smile back. The rabbit hopped away as if something was coming. Gameknight could the groans of zombies as he jumped back on his horse. He kicked his horse into action and there he went as fast as a speeding bullet.
