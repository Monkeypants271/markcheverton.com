Not your average beach bum.

Kit was a lonely little squid boy who was tired of the undersea world. So when he heard an abnormal boom in the normally silent Atlantic ocean, it felt like a one in a million blessing. But then reality popped his bubble. It was likely a pistol shrimp or a talkative Blue whale. But strangely, the abrupt booming did not ceist. After not much thinking, he knew exactly what was happening. He carefully poked his head out of the murky blue, he caught witness of the abomination that was happening on the coast.
