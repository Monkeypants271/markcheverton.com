**Bad Dream**

   
As the wind blew by. Gameknight999 and his best friend Shawny rode on horses to Crafter’s village.  
“So do you know the tale of Herobrine?” asked Shawny as his horse stop for water.  
“Well Yeah, why do you ask?” Gameknight said.  
“Cause it will be new story!” said Shawny as his eyes change to white.

Shawny jump out of his horse, got out his iron sword. “Shawny what is wrong with you?” Gameknight said  
as he got out his enchant diamond sword.  
“Not Shawny but HEROBRINE!!!!!!!” said Shawny as he ran to him  
The User-That-Is-Not-A-User blocked the attack just as a new sword can out of his friend hand and into his body. Pain came out of Gameknight as his XP was going away.

Soon Gameknight fell to the floor. Herobrine step on him with one leg, his sword high up in his hand.  
“I have finish with Gameknight. Finally I killed the Use-that-is-not-a-user.” said Herobrine  
“I AM NOW THE NEW USER-THAT-IS-NOT-A-USER!!!!” Herobrine cried out

“NOOOO!” shouted Gameknight as he got out of bed. He ran outside, but instead of the village in flames and  villagers killed. It was just peaceful. “Oh what a Dream.” said Gameknight.
