Minecraft story

Darthling562 and Swaglord827 suddenly popped into a Minecraft server named VR ONLY Light-city, and they looked around. “Cool!” Swaglord837 said. Darthling replied,” Wow, I didn’t expect that this 2050 Virtual Reality device could actually get us in the game! Hey Swaglord, do you want to explore?” Swaglord shouted,” Of course! Dude, you can actually feel the blocks!” “Yeah, right.” Darthling rolled his eyes. “No, really try it!” He punched a tree and the piece of wood that he had hit suddenly cracked a little bit, then when he stopped hitting the block of wood, the cracks immediately vanished. He stared at in in surprise and decided to keep hitting it and see what happens. Slowly, the cracks got larger and larger until when the block was covered in cracks, it broke and in it’s place, on the ground, was a small replica of the block of wood he had broken. It was floating and turning in mid-air. He stared at it for a minute and then yelled,” Hey Swaglord! Come over here!,” Swaglord  replied,” What?” Swaglord started to walk over to Darthling and when he reached him, Darthling exclaimed,” Dude, you have to see this! Look!” He pointed to the floating block and Swaglord exclaimed,” It’s just like Minecraft pc and PE! Darthling went close to the item and when he got very close to it, the item flew to Darthling. It hit him and fell to the ground.

He bent down and picked it up. He looked at it, then knew what to do. He banged it on the ground and threw it, then instead of Darthling getting a single block of wood, he got four wood planks. He gave two of them to Swaglord827, then started hitting another tree with his fist. Swaglord found another tree nearby, and did the same. When the had lots of wood blocks, they slammed the blocks on the ground and by the time they were done, they both had a stack of 64 wooden planks. Swaglord threw four of the planks and hit the ground and suddenly, there was a blinding light that made them look away. The light slowly faded away and when it did, a table with tools appeared. Darthling then went and made his own table, while Swaglord made other things. Darthling said,” Man, this is cool! Swaglord nodded and put a plank of wood on the centre of the 3 by 3 squares on the crafting table and another plank underneath the centre plank. There was a sound of something popping, and when the two players looked, there were four sticks floating above the crafting table.

After hours of work later, they had a full sets of wooden tools and sticks. It was turning nighttime so they started to dig. They dug into the ground and made a 8X8 room and put down and chest. They started to mine stone to get better gear. They opened their pockets and shoved as much cobblestone could fit inside of their pockets. Once their second wooden pickaxe had broken, they stopped and rested. Swaglord crafted a furnace in case they found any ores. Darthling, on the other hand, went out to see if it was daytime. It indeed was, but there was a creeper outside, so they both crafted stone tools and  double teamed the creeper, both of them hitting the monster once, then quickly retreating. When they killed it, Swaglord pocketed the gunpowder that it had dropped to craft TNT if they got some more.

They started to explore to find the famous village that inhabited the all of the players on the server. They traveled night and day, at night digging a cave and day, running to try to find the village. Soon, they had found a faint outline of the village. As they reached the gates, they were hit by a potion of weakness, then led into a room. When they had come back to their senses, they were angry. Darthling yelled as a player approached,”Hey! Why did you hit us with a potion of weakness?!” The player, SkullKnight replied,”There are two griefers on the loose, and we weren’t sure if you were a threat or not.”
