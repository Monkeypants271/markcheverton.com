WRANGER AND THE OCTOCRAB

Once upon a time there was a fisherman called Wranger. One day Wranger was going fishing. He had just caught a fish and put his fishing rod in his holder and went to prepare some bait while he was cutting the bait he heard a noise so he turned around and then he was faced to faced with the mythical octocrab. It was half crab half octopus he’d read about before but he had never believed it. Immediately he grabbed his net and caught it. When he got home he started studying it. A week after studying it he heard people disappearing were he found it suddenly he knew what was going on he quickly got in his car and drove to some famous scientists. He banged on the lab door and it opened up he Immediately showed them the octocrab and they let him in 1 day later they went to the fishing spot to go hunting when they were under water they saw it : the king octocrab. They Immediately started shooting it with there harpoons and it died. After that they saved the people and got 5000 dollars .
