INTRODUCTION to the Great whites  
Once upon a time, there was a baby special Great white. But no one knows when it lived or when its parents lived. Maybe you will be the one that guesses right. So why don’t you start reading now? Enjoy 😉

The great whites used to live on a fiery planet with ice and lava-covered land and all kinds of different things. With extremely deep waters that can stretch down 1,000 miles! (Yikes!) Yet millions of animals live there! But the animals need special characteristics for living in such deep waters. There wasn’t just one special shark there were 6 of them. One lava shark, 1 ice shark, 1 lightning shark, 1 magic shark, 1 invisible shark, and the unique shark! They all had jobs but the most important one was to protect the planet from invaders.  
This story is about the unique great white shark. They once thought that their planet would never be destroyed but when they grew up they found out that their planet had no protection and was invaded where they lived. So his (his as in unique shark) family told everyone “you have to leave this reef” and they told their son to go and never come back to this planet because it was going to be in ruins when/if you come back but he didn’t want to. So then he said, “I can’t leave you”.  
Does he stay or leave? To be continued…
