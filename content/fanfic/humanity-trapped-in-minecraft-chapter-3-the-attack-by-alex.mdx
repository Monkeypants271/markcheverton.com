Jackson and most of the parents started to mine down looking for iron, so that they can have a decent set of armor and weapons for the inexperienced parents. Pulling out his stone pickaxe, Jackson started mining down to the area where players usually found the metal. He kept mining until eventually, the pickaxe broke. Pulling out his second pick, Jackson continued mining until he came across a large vein of iron. Jackson then mined all of the iron that was there, collecting a half stack of iron, then mined down a couple blocks and found at least three stacks of iron… odd. Jackson mined all of the iron when his pickaxe broke. Turning, Jackson sprinted towards the surface. When he got up he saw a group of people running towards the innocent parents, with enchanted stone swords in their hands. Drawing his stone sword, Jackson charged toward the incoming griefers and slashed at one of the attackers, making them flash red and backed up. Rookie mistake. Charging toward the attacker, Jackson slashed and slashed at the attackers sword until the opponent’s sword broke, Turning around, the attacker ran off since he no longer had a weapon to defend himself with. Turning, Jackson saw two other attackers, each with leather armor and an iron sword. Knowing that He couldn’t break the attacker’s swords with his stone one. he charged, knowing what could happen to him. “Can I do this?” Jackson thought. Pushing his fear aside, Jackson charged, swinging his sword at the opponents. Seeing this, the enemy slashed at Jackson’s stone sword, causing it to disappear with a pop. Jackson quickly drew his stone axe and blocked the incoming blow. “That was close,” Jackson thought. Looking to his left, Jackson saw the other attacker about to land a blow on him. Jackson then slashed at one of the attacker’s leather armor, creating a scar in the armor and wearing down the armor’s durability. Suddenly, he felt a sword hit his arm and he flashed red, signifying the damage dealt to him. “This isn’t working,” Jackson thought. Turning, Jackson took off running, catching the attackers off-guard. Jackson turned to look back to see if They were following him. They were behind by at least ten blocks. Looking ahead, Jackson kept on running, hoping he’d reach the small town before the attackers caught up. Ahead he saw everyone putting on armor, some iron, others leather, Continuing his sprint, he saw that Max drew an iron sword from his inventory and threw it towards him. Leaping in the air, Jackson caught the sword and turned to face his adversaries. At this time one other person stepped up beside him. The attackers, seeing the extra opponent, hesitated a little. “Now’s the time,” Jackson thought. Charging, Jackson slashed at one of the attackers, dealing more damage to their armor, causing large gouges to appear. Turning, He saw Max slashing at the other attacker with an enchanted gold sword. Continuing his attack, he kept hacking away at his adversary’s armor, until it was destroyed. The attacker tried to get in one last hit, but Jackson brought up his iron sword and broke his opponent’s sword in half, causing it to disappear with a pop. Defenseless, The attacker turned and attempted to run, but was quickly surrounded by everyone in the town. Sheathing his sword, Jackson approached the attacker. “What are you doing here?” Jackson asked. “We were told to find someone and steal their inventory.” The attacker said. “We were also told to steal any armor we come across.” The attacker drew a diamond sword and tried to hit Jackson, but then Someone shot the attacker with an arrow from a bow, causing the attacker to flash red. Realizing he can’t win the fight, the attacker turned and started running back to wherever he came from.
