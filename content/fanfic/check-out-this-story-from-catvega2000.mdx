“Ah why if it isn’t my friend Gameknight999 and his pathetic friends.” spat Herobrine.  
Gameknight shook with fear as did his companions. Herobrine turned his attention from Gameknight to his horsemen, who didn’t seem to move an inch.  
“You fools, attack him!” Herobrine yelled.  
Xa-Tul stood tall, “No, he and the NPCs have done nothing wrong, Gameknight999 here has shown us monsters that we can live in peace, not war. You were wrong, Herobrine and now all of us see that.”  
“We all agree,” Reaper said. “Gameknight shouldn’t be punished, but rather praised for his wonders,”  
The User that is not a user smiled at the skeleton king then smiled at the rest of the kings.  
“Very well” Herobrine said.  
Gathering his powers, the shadow crafter made a diamond sword appear out of mid air. He then charged for the monster kings, his sword ready to harm which ever one he hit first.  
The diamond blade struck Xa-Tul over and over again.  
“NOOOOO!” Catvega2000 screamed.  
The zombie flashed red over and over, the other monster kings were parallized with shock and fear. “WHat are you doing? Leave him alone!” Reaper cried drawing back an arrow and aiming for Herobrine.  
The projectile struck Herobrine in the chest making him flash red. He backed off in pain.  
Cat rushed to Xa-Tul’s side. He laid on the ground in severe pain, his health bar dangerously low.  
“Oh I’m so sorry he did this to you,” Cat said, fear in her eyes. Looking up toe Crafter she asked, “Will he live?” “He can, if we get him to an HP fountain in time, but we must hurry.”  
Cat and Gameknight nodded and turned to Charybdis and said, “you an the others, take Xa-Tul back to Zombie-town and place him in an HP fountain, and we will deal with Herobrine!” Gameknight demanded.  
The flaming monster nodded and he and the other kings carried the wounded zombie home.  
Cat glared at Herobrine. “This is it! No longer will the kings work for you and no longer will you terrorize us! FOR MINECARFT!”  
And then Catvega2000 charged.
