\=Herobrine – The Lost Diary=

So… I found this diary in a cave… somewhere…

I read through it, but I couldn’t make sense of it, other than the feeling that I’m connected to these… events. There were more questions than answers floating in my head.

I have a brother named Steve?

We went on a journey to defeat the Ender Dragon?

I was taken away by my master Entity 303 who calls himself The Shapeshifter?

I shut the book and closed my bright white eyes.

I don’t understand…

Reading the book made me regret all the things I have done in my past life. According to this diary, I have done so many good things, I have helped so many people who needed aid.

And now I’m throwing fireballs at villages and watching them burn to the ground.

Suddenly, there was a knock on my door. I went over to the wooden door and creaked open the door.

It was Ka-Dol, the commander of the zombie army.

He simply said, “You are needed in the laboratory, Dark One.”

“Alright then,” I responded, “Just head back to the office, I’ll be there in a moment.”

“Okay, but you better come here quickly, it’s very urgent.” Ka-Dol said, and with that he strolled off into the hallway.

I looked down in my diary.

Whoever you are, Steve. I thought, I’m sorry for what I would do in the next moment.

I cracked open the chest and threw the diary into it.
