“How about Crafter and his village? They disappeared!” Gameknight asked again. (He had already tried, but the NPC refused to give a direct answer.)

“Herobrine attacked their village first. He was there personally. In his new enderdragon form, he can cause more chaos. However, he seems weaker in the day. The form might restrict him, but when dawn came last night, their sentries say they saw him fly away.” The NPC replied impatiently.

“Wait, their sentries?” Gameknight picked up. “You mean Crafter’s with you now?”

“No, Crafter, Digger and some other villagers went to do… Something.” The NPC seemed hesitant to reveal something. “Crafter told me to keep it secret. But since you are the user-that-is-not-a-user, come with me to a secret location and I’ll tell you.”

Even so, the NPC blindfolded Gameknight (Graft: Paper and Leather Helmet), before leading him on a confusing twisting and turning passage before they were in a possibly underground room.

“Where is this?” Gameknight asked, looking in wonder at the small yet somehow grand room. The NPC ignored his question, telling him. “Crafter went to get the rest of the light-crafters after he heard Grassbrin and Treebrin got infected. The Enderman’s Mark only seem to have taken it’s toll against selective people. Fishers, Farmers and Hunters. They entirely ignored Crafter.”

“Fishers, Farmers and Hunters. What could happen if they all die? What is their connection?” Gameknight thought as the NPC blindfolded him yet again.
