The golden face of the sun shone through stain glass window of the courtyard. Ascending the steps to the alter Skeletoni and Skeletona, king and queen of Skeletonia, carried their new born baby. Skeletoni was robed in a royal purple garment and his queen was dressed in red, both were in full armor. Gently Skeletona laid the new monster on the table. A beautiful girl stepped forward. She wore a golden crown that almost matched her glowing blond hair. She was clad in all red. Before the happy parents was the great Taylor Swift. In her hands was a golden box with what looked to be a red glow surrounding it? “Thank you for coming my friends. Today we welcome a new life into the Boo World” Opening the box, Taylor took some red tinted powder and sprinkled it on the baby’s forehead. The little skeleton sensed the powder and sneezed. Taylor then dipped her finger in water and taking more of the red powder she marked TS on the newborn’s forehead. “I now welcome Skeleton into the Boo World” Taylor said. Turning to the king and queen she said “The Oracle told me that this little one you have here will be the hero of heroes. Skeletona you have given birth to a royal hero”
