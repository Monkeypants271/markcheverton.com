The Warrior Cat series is a story about how a pet cat, (kittypet) meets some wild cats in the forest behind his house and joins them. Over the years he learns about the four different clans, the four different seasons, how to distinguish one cat scent from another, how to hunt, and even defends his clan against the dangers of the forest. These books are funny, heartwarming, with just the right touch of mystery and sadness. These series are like no other animal series. I would recommend these books to you all.
