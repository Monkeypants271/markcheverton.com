Okay, this started out as a pretty normal day. As usual, I was playing Minecraft in the basement. This time, I was recording it for my youtube channel. “OH NONONONO!” I yelled when a creeper snuck up on me and I lost my diamonds. But instead of the usual respawn screen, this time it said something different. The words “Enable Reality Mode?” were on the screen. At first, I thought it was another one of my brother’s pranks. I shouted in the direction he was. “Can you stop this prank? I get it, it’s super not funny. Now just turn it off.” But he replied “I’m not doing anything! I put glue on the toilet lid, but I didn’t rig your computer.” Then why is this screen here? I clicked on don’t enable to see what would happen, but that button didn’t have any effect. Well, I guess the only thing to click is enable. I clicked it, and the next thing I knew, I felt like I was falling down a black hole.
