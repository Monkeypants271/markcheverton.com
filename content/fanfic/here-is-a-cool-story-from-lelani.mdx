# Chapter 1.

## Unexpected Encounter

As she approached, carefully, stealthily, she melee attacked an enemy scout, eliminating the oblivious player immediately. Lelani was playing her favorite game, Transformers War For Cybertron; with her friends: TersePigeon8, joescoescu, ZombieFighter, Marstol, and with her mama. Lowering the microphone on her headset, she said quickly: “Behind you!” A Decepticon soldier was charging at mama’s character, which was Warpath (An Autobot soldier). “What am I supposed to do?!” She said, panicking a little bit. “Shoot his head off!” Lelani blurted out. Now Mama was melee attacking frantically, but shortly she eliminated the Decepticon with 3 swift melee attacks. “That works too.” Lelani said. But forgetting to look around, gave a Decepticon scout the opportunity to sneak up behind her and attack her character, which was Jazz (A stealthy scout). Turning quickly, she shot him with a Scatter Blaster, and then landed a melee attack, instantly killing him. But then, out of the blue, a hacker in the player lobby swoops in and kills the duo with a single strike. Raising the microphone to her mouth quickly said to her friends in the Xbox live party: “There’s a hacker in the lobby!” “Who?” asked Marstol, Lelani’s classmate, “His gamer tag is XxMasterJedyxX!” She replied. “Swat!” ZombieFighter yelled into his microphone, (Lelani’s Gamer tag was swatbklyn) “I could use some help!” “Where are you?” swatbklyn asked; “At the Autobot base!” he replied. And there he was: surrounded by 2 Decepticon scouts. Equipping her sniper rifle, she zoomed in on the two offenders, then landed a perfect headshot on both of them, killing them instantly. All of a sudden, the game and screen froze and crashed. “Oh come on!” Lelani said, exasperated. “I have to go to the supermarket; I will be back in an hour.” Mama said. “OK.” Lelani replied. Then Mama walked out the door. Lelani walked to the Xbox 360 game system and saw that the wire that fed the information into the system had to be restarted- _again_. As she adjusted the wire- suddenly she felt like she had been zapped by the wire, a brilliant light enveloped her in its tight grasp, and it felt as if every single nerve were aflame. Then, as the world around her became illusive, she knew she could see robot workers, lots of them.

Confusion and fear were overcoming her entire being. Slowly opening her eyes, she could see red figures, helping the wounded and making weapons. Where was she? How did she get here?
