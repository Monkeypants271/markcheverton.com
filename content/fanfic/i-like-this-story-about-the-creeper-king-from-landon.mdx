## June’s Big Fight

Once there was a boy named June and  
his dog named Rufus. His parents died by  
the Creeper King and his Minions, so June  
had to live with the villagers.  
One day June got up and every villager  
was at work. He grabbed his armor, bow,  
and sword and went to guard. He asked  
Sam what was going on.  
Sam the guard stated, “There is a king  
coming.” So June looked at the trail and  
saw lots of green things and the tip of a  
huge someone´s head. He got closer and  
saw it was the Creeper King and his  
Minions. June ran to grab his bow and hid  
in the watchtower .  
He hid, then whispered to himself,  
reminding himself that they killed his  
parents. He got up and pulled his bow  
then shot a Creeper and blew up 5  
more. The Creeper King got mad and  
threw a Creeper at June and it blew up  
the watchtower. So June jumped out  
and landed on the C.K. and wounded  
him. June jumped off. The C.K. made  
Creepers go to destroy June. June  
slashed and dashed and headed  
straight towards the C.K. Once he got  
super close, he wounded the C.K.´s. leg  
and blew up a whole pile of stone over  
June. Rufus came and dug up June and  
that night they had a feast for June´s big  
fight.
