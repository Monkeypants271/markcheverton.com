The crafting chamber was full of zombies! Gameknight was dumbfounded; the villagers would never allow zombies into the crafting chamber.  
Gameknight prepared to charge when he realized that half of the zombies within the room were zombie villagers, and many looked just like his friends! There was a thin, lanky one, and a small, boyish one. They looked just like Herder and Crafter! Gameknight almost dropped his sword.
