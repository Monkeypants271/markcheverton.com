## Stuck in a world of Minecraft

One cold winter morning the sad and depressed taco  
killer 2.3.7 was playing minecraft when all the sudden the  
computer started to shake, it made weird sounds. Taco  
killer was scared, then bfffooo!! When taco killer looked  
around all he could see was white, all he could hear was a  
loud ringing sound, he felt like he was going a million miles  
per hour. Taco killer passed out after all the presser.  
When he woke up he saw a black blur shifting around.  
All he could think was, is that an endermen? Taco killer  
said to himself, “Am I going insane?!”  
He saw a lady in a blue shirt and green shorts. She said in  
the friendliest voice he’s ever heard, “Hello, welcome to  
the world of minecraft.”  
At the pixilated horizon he asked the girl, “When will I  
get out of here?”All she said was, “WAKE UP IT’S TIME  
FOR SCHOOL!”  
What do you mean it’s time for school? “Just wake up,”  
she said in a calming voice. When taco killer opened his  
eyes, he was lying on his bed! “This was all a dream?”  
The endermen, the girl! So Taco killer wrote a story and it  
went like that.  
BY. Carson
