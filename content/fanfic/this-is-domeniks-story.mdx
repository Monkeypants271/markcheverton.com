**Chapter 1- First Time Playing Minecraft**

I loaded myself into my Minecraft 1.9.0 server on 3DS. I Had no idea what to do. I went to my brother, and I asked for some advice. “How do I play Minecraft?” I asked my brother.  “Alright,” said my brother. “Press this button to break and press this button to punch” he said.

After a little while, I started to get the hang of it until nighttime. Nighttime is monster time. I nearly died. I was down to a quarter heart. After I managed to survive the deadly night, the only food I had left was 19 rotten flesh and I had one hunger bar left.

I asked my brother to join me. He obviously said yes. He already had full iron armour which made me jealous. It was 10 minutes into the day in Minecraft and not even an auto save, which happens every 20 minutes. This is annoying, especially right in a middle of a fight. After a little while, my brother fell in lava while searching for diamonds.

**Chapter 2 – First Time Playing with My Brother**

Before my brother burned to death, he quit Minecraft to save himself. After reloading in, he managed to autosave himself before that incident where he got into lava. Personally, on 3DS, lava looks like molten cheese with pepperonis on the top. But… what I was doing was destroying forest to get a bigger house.

When it was my turn to go mining, I found diamonds. I was the first person on the server. At least that’s what I thought until I discovered a secret trap door. I opened it and I found diamonds. I did not take them because I was a good person. The first item I saw someone craft was a diamond pick.

Luck was not always on my side. I made the dumbest choice when I fell in lava and obsidian was placed on my head. I didn’t know obsidian was for Nether portals. My brother did not notice me. I was very calm because I didn’t know Minecraft. My brother heard me taking fire damage from the 3DS I was holding. He knew I needed to be saved but it was too late, so I died.

When I respawned I asked him what obsidian is for. My brother did not answer my question because he was playing with my sister… My brother’s house was on a lake and my sister’s house was a mushroom house. My house was on the same island, in a swamp.

**Chapter 3 – The Second Time I’ve Ever Died in Minecraft**

When I was mining, I found more diamonds**!** I was very happy because I found six diamonds. I went to the surface and my brother crafted a diamond pickaxe because I had no sticks. He also crafted a diamond pickaxe for me. But my brother had a different one. It had a blueish purple glow coming from the entire pickaxe. It made me happy when my brother told me I’m going to get obsidian because I was going to mine it myself. The rest of the diamonds I had, my brother used for an extra pickaxe for himself.

We began mining until I fell straight through the obsidian. “Save me!” I yelled at my brother. He tried but it was too late. I died again, but I instantly respawned. I asked my brother if I could have a pickaxe, but he said he broke it. A diamond Pickaxe in Minecraft on the 3DS can break very fast.

I wanted to go to the Nether but I died for a third time and accidentally left the game and crashed it. My brother got mad at me and then I couldn’t play Minecraft on that server. I was sad until I found Minecraft on iPad. After that I never played Minecraft on 3DS with my brother again. I started playing by myself and I got very good gear until I just stopped. And that’s what I did on the first time playing Minecraft.
