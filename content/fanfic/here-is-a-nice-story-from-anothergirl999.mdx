Game knight woke with a start. Reaching into his inventory, he drew his sword as he stood ready for the spider attack that was not coming.

“You have a funny way of waking up” said a voice behind him.

Turning he found his friend Hunter, standing behind him. She stepped towards him and carefully put a reassuring hand on his shoulder. It was dawn and the morning rays from the sun were piercing through the overhead leafy canopy, the golden shafts of light shining down on the two of them.
