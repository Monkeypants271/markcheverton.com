Crafter watched as the monsters and shadow crafters charged through the plains toward the useless village walls. “Archers, fire at the approaching monster army… NOW” Yelled Crafter as the ground shaking footsteps of the monster army. Archers fired at the front line of the monster army, the spider jockeys,  giving time for the sisters, Hunter and Stitcher to fire at the TNT to slow down the army, and it did. Monsters slowed down being delayed by their charge to the exposed wall. while doing that, warriors came out of the open exposed wall with shields in their hands, stepping out 5 blocks away the wall, making noises with the blades of their swords banging against the shield making a thud, but when all did at the same time, it sounded like thunder. The monsters slowed down with a look of uncertainty and fear that were replaced by their hateful glares. Giving the builders enough time to rebuild the walls to the village. “Don’t let them rebuild the walls your fools!!” Ghastbrine said, as he floated in the sky with his fiery hands forming a fire ball getting ready to shoot at the builders of the wall, but that didn’t happen. Hunter shot him in the shoulders, causing him to loose control of his flying ability and fell to the ground with a thud. Crafter smiled a bit then turned to a scream off to his right, it was Morgona, she was badly wounded but healed herself with a potion of healing. “Are you alright?” Morgona stood and nodded her head, “yes, it’s just the idiotic skeletons firing at me” Morgona had a mad look on her face as she threw a harming potion towards a zombie, killing it before it could reach one of the builders. “Look! The Endermen are teleporting the monsters inside!” said one of the NPCs. Crafter looked about the village and could see Endermen teleporting in front of each door of every house in the village and smashed it down with its dark fist allowing the zombies to go inside infecting the elder and children. The horror of this made Crafter feel defeated for the first time in this battle as the monsters went from house to house destroying their children. Digger ran by Crafter with two pickaxes in his big boxy hands and slashed at each monster, Crafter went to help including one of Herder’s wolves and the two sisters to the center of the village gathering as much warriors and archers to the center trying to go to the crafting chamber but was blocked out by the tall dark slender Endermen. Crafter and his friends didn’t give up just yet, for they were a storm of blades and arrows, for those who dared try kill them were destroyed by Herder’s wolves. “Crafter do i do that.. ‘thing’?” this was the right moment for Crafter to think and gave out the command to Hunter and Stitcher. “Do it,” the sisters fired to one of the abandoned blacksmith houses causing them to light on fire. when the wood burned into the inside there was blocks of TNT in there and it ignited them. The blinking blocks began to expand and BOOM.. A chain of explosions rang all through out the village including toward the outside eliminating most of the monster army and shadow crafters. The NPCs cheered and it made them fight harder. Monsters tried to trapp them but they couldn’t. As Enderbrine looked at the view from atop of a hill, he had a disappointed look on his face. “Endermen!” Snapped Enderbrine as a Enderman teleported to his side, but this one looked different from the rest of the Endermen in the monster army, this one had a purple cape with a dark red skin. Looking more taller then any mob in Minecraft except for the dragon was still bigger. This Enderman was 4 blocks tall having a dark red skin giving it a blood red tint on him, his eyes were bright red wearing a lavender crown with a ender eye at the center, he also had a purple staff and on top of it was a Ender Pearl, with a red outline around the orb and looked as if you could see into it. And, at the bottom of his staff was a spear, that had a green blood on it, and what also is different, was his particles. the red and black particles danced around the Enderman. “Go Show them what you can do,” Enderbrine said, as the dark red Enderman nodded in response and teleported leaving black particles and red. The Enderman teleported in front of Herder and his two wolves having a big grin on his face. “It is so nice to meet you all you pathetic little NPCs.” “Who are you?” one of the Villagers asked, “who am I?” said the Enderman, teleporting to the villager who spoke, “Who am I?” “I am your worst nightmare, now who are you I may ask.” The villager was about to speak when the Enderman grabbed the NPC by the neck and lifted him to the air and killing the NPC within seconds, the villagers stopped fighting and looked at the nightmarish creature including Crafter, everyone shook in fear, some kneeling to the ground acting like they had been defeated. “My name is Xeno” Xeno’s voice sounding demonic and you all will be our prisoners, “WE DON”T LISTEN TO ANY MONSTER,” Hunter yelled, her crimson hear flowing, Xeno turned his head to Hunter and smiled. He stood up straight only taking his staff out and lifted it up into the air in front of him then, he slammed it onto the ground letting the pointy spear into the ground, when it did a small wave of purple came all across the village turning it all into Endstone. That’s impossible! That’s what Herobrine used while he was in dragon form, how is that even possible? thought Crafter, Xeno laughed as if he heard his thought. As soon the wave went past the villagers, it turned them into Endstone, then the statues exploded with XP and the XP then flowed into Xeno’s staff trapping it. The villages building melted and terraformed into a different building, the plants turned to purple looking plants and it made them grow 10 times faster then a normal plant would, sprouting like a tree but had no leafs. Animals turned to block like creatures that looked like a shell opening to see a little creature in there looking about, forming a orb inside like it was about to shoot. Then, Gameknight Castle transformed all the stonebricks to look like End Stone Bricks with purple blocks, almost changing the style of the castle. Lastly, the ground shook, feeling like it was trying to be lifted up into the air by itself and it did when all Endermen knocked out the surviving villagers, Crafter, his friends, and some others. This terrified Crafter for the first time in history, why would they do such a thing? At this point Crafter had no idea what to do, he has no help from the mighty User-that-is-not-a-user, Gameknight999, to save them now, it is up to Crafter to save this village. Crafter woke up to a sound of a cling, a sound like a metal bar hitting the ground, and found himself in a prison cell. What will Crafter do now?
