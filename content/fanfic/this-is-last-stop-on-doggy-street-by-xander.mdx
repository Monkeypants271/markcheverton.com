Preface

I was walking down the street with my parents and suddenly I saw a red glow swirling in the air in an alley.  I was thinking my parents were with me, but they weren’t. I looked and saw a portal and it was swirling with doggies I went in through and a blinding red light engulfed me.  

Chapter 1:

I woke up and found myself in a hospital bed. There were doggies everywhere.  The doggies were dressed in doctor’s clothes and everyone all around me were doggies. Two picked me up, put me on a stretcher, and brought me out into the street. Then they took me.

The buildings looked doggies. Some were driving cars.  Some were even driving faster than the speed limit which is 100 km/h. Which is strange. “Ah, the portal!”

“No humans allowed? So strange. Why can’t I walk just out there? Oh no, now I’m gonna have to break a lotta doggy rules, said King.”  “I can’t believe they don’t want humans here!  Humans take them for walks.  Humans help give them their food and humans give them water.  Why won’t they let humans in?  Or humans out…”

Chapter 2:

“Aaaah! A police officer! They’re gonna handcuff me! There are a thousand of them! An army!!! Uh, uh, uh, I have to get outta here!!!”

“They’re chasing me!!!! Oh no no no no nooooooo!”  King thought, they got me and they’re putting me in a jail. Uh how many other humans are there? How am I gonna get out? There are none. I am the first human to come to Doggy Street — and maybe the last!!!!!!!!!!

Chapter 3:

“Yes!” There’s a vent here just enough for the size of a human now to escape.

Yeah! I am out now I must skip out of this weird dimension.  Yay, I see a state police uniform that is left there.  I can put it on and escape and get the key in it then I can go through the portal and back to my dimension but what if there is a special timeline and I’ve been gone for years? Oh no!

I have to focus on the now, not the what ifs.

Chapter 4:

I’ve been only gone for a day, phew.  Now I must find my parents.  Oh no a police officer sees me in a police uniform that is not even part of the state. Oh no, I have to run I’m serious, they are attacking me and almost handcuff me.

My parents, “YES!!!!!!”  I can get out of here.  YEAH, I just found a place to hide the state police uniform weight!

Chapter 5:

I woke up and found that that was just a dream.
