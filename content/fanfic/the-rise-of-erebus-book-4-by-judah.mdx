**The Rise of Erebus Book 4 by Judah** 

Just then Mirthrandos snatched the wand of cloning from Watchers’ hand and pointed it at Planter. At the same time Watcher fired an arrow at a creeper and it exploded. In the confusion, a dozen Planters appeared. “WHAT JUST HAPPENED?” bellowed Erebus. 

“I don’t no sir said a zombie. Erebus teleported directly towards the monster and hit him.  The zombie screamed and ran around the room howling.  

“Now,” said Erebus “get the prisoner and her clones back to the tower now.” The Planters were dragged away one by one. “Now search the Nether” ordered Erebus but when they found the portal Watcher and Mirthrandos were gone 

Watcher stepped out of the Nether  portal everyone cheered as he and Mirthrandos stepped out of the Nether 

“What is this place?” said Watcher 

“its the End,” said Lady Char still writhing in agony “but  you must hide” 

‘why?” said Watcher 

“because’ said Lady Char “to defeat Erebus and save Planter you must take the greatest journey of your lives.” 

“to where?” asked Watcher 

“to the over world” said Lady Char “and there you must find its greatest hero” 

“who,” asked Watcher
