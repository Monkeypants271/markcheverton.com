Hi I’m Gameknight999, Tommy Feynman in the physical world, but when I’m in Minecraft, im the User-that-is-not-a-User, the savior of Minecraft, and I’m happy the way thing’s are. No monsters terrorizing Minecraft and me living a normal life. Going to school, trying my best to get good grades, dodging the bullies that always try to gang up on me, getting my little sister out of trouble, working with my father on our inventions, and using the Gate Way of Light to visit my Minecraft Family when no-one is home…..  
■□■□■□■□■□■□■□■□

But what Gameknight didn’t know that his life was about to be put on pause..

He didn’t know that when his little sister Jenny got the flu, and had to leave school 2 hours before it ended, that his life would spiral out of control.

He didn’t know that was the day of the accident…

He didn’t know that the look of terror on his sisters face would be the last thing he would see…

He didn’t know that the only thing he would feel was the pain…

He didn’t know the last thing he would hear is his sister screaming his name…

He didn’t know that his sister thought it was all her fault…

He didn’t know that it was not going to be darkness that greeted him…

He didn’t know that this was all part of the plan…

He didn’t know that this was just the beginning…

He didn’t know that his enemy could strike, where he was weakest…

He didn’t know that he didn’t take mind of the silent threat…

The threat that was made was by his new and most dangerous enemy yet…

He didn’t know that, waking up was not a miracle……but the most deadly curse…

He didn’t know that when, he woke up he would signal the end…
