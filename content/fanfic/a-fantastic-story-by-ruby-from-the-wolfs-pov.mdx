Wolfing down her mutton she smiled Naraka loved it when her “mom” caught a sheep.  “Naraka, are you going to practice hunting with Shaun?” her mom or Nasha, asked.  “Nah, Shaun is a dope; pretty bad at hunting, by the time I am about to pounce he starts screaming like Herobrine himself is killin’ him.”  She answered. Nasha frowned “He doesn’t seem to understand hunting from your point of view, but still help him learn not to scream, so go hunting with him.”  Naraka groaned, Mom was always doing this and that, telling her to do pretty much everything a normal wolf wouldn’t do.

When Naraka was done hunting all she caught was two feathers of a chicken and some wool from a sheep.  “Shaun, How many times have I told you not to scream!?” Naraka said, clearly irritated.  Shaun replied “Sorry, I keep on thinking that you’re a HUMAN.” Shaun had a point, Naraka surely did not look like a wolf at all she walked on two legs wore a rabbit skin and leather instead of fur, she wondered, do I belong with NPCs?  Her friends Kaki and Meo did not mind at all in fact they liked how she was always walking on two legs and swinging through trees, she found them sunning themselves, chatting too, as she walked in she heard them talking about how the wolves are disappearing one by one “sup Kaki And Meo?” “Hey Nark kark how’d the hunt go?” Meo asked, she liked how Meo called her Nark kark.  “Terrible, even though I told Shaun not to scream he did anyways, so anyway, whatcha talking ‘bout?”  “How that now Meka has disappeared I actually know how she disappeared, but beware it is pretty scary!” Kaki exclaimed.  “I can handle it but Meo will probably wet his place.” Naraka teased. Meo Protested, “Hey! Gross, pleh pleh pleh! I hate doing this.” Meo grumbled.  
“Ahhhh—–Wooooo!”  “What now?” Kaki grumbled.  Their pack leader, Teller of the Pointed Stones always calls gatherings at least twice a day.  “Now, now fellow wolves I know that wolves have been disappearing but now I know why, Kaki? Would you like to tell everybody what happened to Meka, if you do step up on this rock.” He announced. Kaki nervously got up to the rock and told everyone the story. When she was done she leaped down from the rock looking as if she did not care that many stares followed her. Kaki was always brave willing to do things no one else wants to do. She then stopped as an idea came to her head and also announced “who I think should go is Naraka, everyone knows that Nashi found her in the woods, she looks like them so she should go!” Cheers erupted from the crowd, and before she even knew it she was on her way to the NPC village delivering a message.

As she walked in stares burned through her heavy skins, their pack leader walked in with a surprised look on his face “who is this? Girl, what is your name, or what is your job if you even have a village.”  He, in Naraka’s opinion strangely welcomed her. “Naraka, that is my name, and I do not know what this village is and I have various jobs.” She replied sheepishly. With a confused look he asked “so Naraka why have you come to our humble little village?” “My wolves have a complaint about one of your villagers doing; we are having loyal pack members disappear one by one because one of you is grabbing them while they are out on a hunt. We would like you to stop whoever is doing this.” She answered. As Naraka was talking a small boy was blushing and slowly walking away but before he could get away a voice boomed “Herder I told you no more dogs!” herder looked embarrassed and said “I….I’m sorry I…I thought crafter was being bossy so….so I went and got dogs I….I didn’t know I was hurting a pack I’m…I’m sorry Naraka” she saw that he felt horrible “aww I didn’t mean to make ya feel like a wolf who brought the leader zombie flesh I was just saying that my wolves didn’t really like that.” She told herder then continued “so can you return the wolves ya took?”  Herder said with a joyful look “yes, yes I can I will get them to you right now” he then darted away.  Just after he went someone said “hello Naraka I am smithy and I have an idea you live with wolves correct?” she replied “yes…” “Good I know how to make something that might make your hunting easier, I’m gonna make these pointy steel claws that are super long and you hold them together with string and leather! If that’s okay with you” he said so quickly she almost didn’t understand it.  “Umm sure I guess” she replied hesitantly, almost instantly they were in the smithery smithy working away on a project Naraka mumbled to herself “what have I gotten myself into?” he had to fight her to get it on and by the time it was on she was amazed and headed out Herder had all the wolves with him and the village sent them off to get back to the pack

When Naraka got back to her pack what she saw horrified her, it was empty but her two friends Meko and Kaki and the clearing was scattered with…red? In a hurry Kaki ran over to her and yelled “we need to get out of here now!! If we stay we will die and the white…” “Stop!” Naraka interrupted and Kaki fell silent, not a word was spoken “tell me what happened here but say it slow and calm down” Kaki then explained  “all was peace full in the clearing until an arrow flew past my head and hit a tree, then skeletons poured into the clearing and started shooting arrows everywhere teller of the pointed stones ordered that everyone split up and leave, we all did just that but by the time he said that we were hiding in the hollow tree, soon all the skeletons left and we came out to rest because and arrow hit Meko and he was injured. Okay that’s the story” Naraka was shocked and terrified she didn’t know where everyone was and she wasn’t used to being alone with just her friends she was stressed out horribly her mind was racing faster than any horse could ever go her mind full of confusing theories and for some reason it just came to a dead stop. And the world went black. Naraka woke up to dreadful pain and she realized she was back at the village or it looked like the village just fortified with a huge castle and then she realized this wasn’t the village he thought it was a small, young crafter came up to her and greeted her and escorted her to a house with a bed, she got in it then a color, a color she never saw before exploded into her mind and then she was in the woods her hands small and soft like a baby’s she saw a villager running by the looks of it, it was a woman she ran and ran until she could not see her anymore and then her mom, Nasha looked very young Nasha sniffed her then carried her into the clearing where she had lived her entire life and then she woke with a start. Kaki and Meo were sleeping on the floor a little boy with hair black and mop like was on the floor petting Kaki looked up to her and said “you have very nice friends” Naraka was confused and replied “where am I, what happened, how did I get here?” the boy laughed “you fainted silly, the stress was too much for your mind and you went out cold. Then your friends dragged you here I understood them and told crafter then he brought you here and then again you went out cold.” Naraka was utterly confused “uh okay. W-what’s your name?” the boy looked up “Herder, my name is Herder” he said like he has said it a thousand times before. “Uh you don’t look like the herder who captured most of my pack.” She replied “and I’m not. See villager names are different than however wolves are named, see we name ourselves on what task we do, I am herder so I am the one who tends to the animals, farmer tends to the land, warrior fights in the battles that sadly take place very often here. And in every village there is always at least one of each, do you understand now?” herder spoke in a very calm and understanding voice. Now that Naraka saw him better herder had two steel different color eyes one steel blue and the other a bright green, when Kaki had told her the color of her eyes she said steel blue and a bright green, her and herder had the same color of eyes. “Yeah I understand now,” Naraka answered after a little bit of thought to let the words sink in. Herder stood up and shook the fur off his robe “now that that’s settled do you want the grand tour?” he insisted. Naraka felt her face get warm what? What is this feeling for some reason I feel…embarrassed a little bit? Naraka thought “s-sure” Naraka replied. “Okay! Then what are you waiting for this place is really awesome if you ask me!” Herder announced very excitedly.
