As Gameknight999 watched in awe, hundreds of villagers were streaming into the crafting chamber. But what was even more strange was that all the NPCs had iron armor and an enchanted iron blade with them. Gameknight turned and looked for Crafter. He found his friend on a block of cobble, giving the NPCs orders on hat to craft. Gameknight asked “What’s the meaning of all this?” Crafter sighed. He said “Come with me” Gameknight followed until they reached the corner of the room. Crafter said “You see, Gameknight, the reason all these villagers are here is because there have been many strange attackes on the villager’s homes. They all say that the last thing that they can remember is a huge explosion knocking them off their feet”
