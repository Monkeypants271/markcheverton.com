Entity303’s Revenge

     Entity303 worked mindlessly at his task. “This virus will be the greatest ever completed,” he muttered to himself. Putting the finishing touches on it, he slowly stood up. “I need something to make this virus more deadly than any other,”He said. Putting his finger to his head and thinking, he slowly thought of a plan. “AI!” He exclaimed. “When they come together my virus will destroy Minecraft’s AI!”

  As he worked, he tried to think of a name for it. Finishing, he slowly grinned.” I’ll program him to be… Herobrine!”

The End?
