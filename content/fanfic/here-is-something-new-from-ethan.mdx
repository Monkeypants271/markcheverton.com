Chapter 1

                Zombiebrine looked around at his surroundings in his scratchy voice, “This cave is pathetic! Where are you Creeperbrine?” He yelled to no one in particular. He needed this idiotic shadow crafter if his plan were ever to work. He knew Herobrine was exacting his revenge on those pathetic users in the physical world, but he wondered when he would send his minions into the physical world like the maker had said he would. One of the spiders in the cave saw the shadow crafter and walked over to him curious why this mysterious shadow crafter was standing in this cave. “Why hasssss thisssss shadow crafter come to thisssss cave?” Zombiebrine looked at the idiot creature and smiled _this one has some courage, hmmmm this one could be useful. Most of these spiders are cowards after that pathetic user-that-is-not-a-user had killed their queen._ He thought to himself. “I don’t need to explain myself to you spider,” he remarked, “I am doing the makers will.” “You did not hear?” The spider asked. “Hear what? Tell me you idiotic beast!” “The maker…..isssss dead.” The spider stammered, afraid of the shadow crafter’s wrath at hearing this news. “You lie beast!” Zombiebrine spat. Pulling out his diamond sword he prepared to kill this beast for its treachery. The spider seeing this bowed low the ground and spoke in a humble voice, “Gamenight, our enemy, hasssss killed him. Other wise The Maker would have set usssssss free from this prison like he promissssssed.” Zombiebrine paused, he hadn’t considered this as an option. “Spider, what is your name?” “Shatzin.” Shatzin responded meekly. “I will keep you alive because of your intelligence, you shall go and find Spiderbrine, we need to make a new queen for you spiders, but after you find Spiderbrine come back to me immediately. Also, Shatzin, The Maker cannot possibly be dead, but I guarantee that the idiotic people in the physical world have somehow trapped him into a cage, we must free him! Now go!” Shatzin scurried off in search of Spiderbrine. _Hmmmm this does change my plan a little, I will still need to find that fool Creeperbrine though._He teleported back to the source feeling a little winded. _At least The Maker gave me his teleporting powers._He walked into one of the beacons to get to the next server he would go to. He materialized on a stone outcropping with a waterfall behind it, a plains biome in the distance. He turned around and saw a cave behind him and walked into it, he knew he would find creepers there. As he walked deeper into the cave he felt the temperature rise; he was near lava. _Ahh I love some lava, why can’t those pathetic NPCs live in a nice, good cave instead of those houses._ He kept walking until he found what he was looking for, a creeper. “You there, Creeper come here.” The creeper scuttled to where he is and stared at him a dumb look in his eyes. “Is Creeperbrine on this server?” “Yes.” The creeper said starting his ignition process, creepers only way to talk. “Take me to him immediately!” “Follow me.” The creeper said, again igniting his ignition process. When he was back to normal started walking deeper into the cave. Zombiebrine followed the creeper for what seemed like and eternity of mazes. Finally, they came onto a big cave where Zombiebrine could see some figure in the shadows working on something. “Creeperbrine, is that you?” Creeperbrine looked up and nodded. “I see that you are still not the most talkative.” Zombiebrine remarked. Creeperbrine nodded again. “I have received info that The Maker has been trapped by the foolish humans in the physical world, and I have devised a plan to free him, but there is one thing that stands in my way, Oxus the creeper king. For my plan to work we will need the creepers, but that fool always just hides away, we need to dispose of him. Are you able to influence some of the creepers to explode that huge pile of gunpowder under the coward’s mountain?” Creeperbrine nodded and grabbed the creeper near him. Having his hands glow a sickly yellow, he put his shadow crafting powers into the creeper and modified the code to do this. “It is done” Creeperbrine said in a whisper. A moment later there was an explosion that shook the cave but seemed far away. Oxus, king of the creepers was dead.

                                                                                                Chapter 2

                Gamenight looked up from his build to see the sun starting to set. “We should probably get back in the village before dark.” He said to Digger, the muscular NPC next to him. “Yes,” Digger agreed, “but let me finish this wall.” “Ok I’ll meet you there.” Gamenight said. He had used his father’s invention the digitalizer to go back into Minecraft, but for the first time, he was doing it for fun without an army of monsters led by one of Herobrine’s monster kings chasing him. He had come back in the game to work on building another wing in his castle. Just as he was putting away the obsidian in his inventory, Gamenight felt what seemed like an earthquake, but he knew there were no earthquakes in Minecraft. “What was that?” He asked. Digger shrugged, “I don’t know but if felt like an explosion, but it seemed to far away to be a simple explosion.” Now it was Gamenight’s turn to shrug. “I don’t know what that was, but we should go find Crafter.” Gamenight said. “Agreed,” Digger remarked. They both slid down the ladder and ran to the village. When the reached the gates they found Hunter and Sticher, who were sisters, at the gates. “Did you feel that explosion?” Gamenight asked. “Of course, only an idiot couldn’t have felt that.” Hunter remarked. “Hunter be nice,” Her younger sister chided her. “We were going to find Crafter to see if he knows.” Gamenight said as he ran past them. The duo then because four with the sisters following behind. They reached the tower and broke the secret block and slid down the ladder. As they went down there, Gamenight wondered what could have made that explosion. When they reached the end of the ladder, they sprinted down the tunnel that led to the crafting chamber. As they past the small circular room Gamenight paused and thought about that first meeting with crafter, which seemed like ages ago. “Are you coming or are you just going to stand there with a stupid look on your face.” Hunter said with a smile, which got a punch and scold from the younger sister Stitcher. When they made it to the crafting table Gamenight spotted his friend Crafter inspecting the strength of a rope a worker had just finished. “Crafter, do you know what caused that big explosion?” Crafter turned as he saw Gamenight and put the rope in a minecart. “I might have an idea…” Before Crafter could finish, someone came running toward them. “Gamenight,” Stammered Herder who was the one running toward them. “Whoa Herder, slow down” Gamenight said. “My wolves, my wolves,” said the lanky boy, “They ran off, that explosion spooked them I think.” “Herder, I’m sure we can find them.” Gamenight said comforting the boy. “Continue please, Crafter.” “As I was saying, there is only one thing that could have made that big of an explosion from that far away.” Crafter said. “What?” Asked Gamenight. “The creeper hive, I mean all that gunpowder was enough to blow that whole biome around that mountain away. I think a creeper blew up the gunpowder. Because the only other thing would be a million tnt, and I don’t think anyone has that much tnt.” “So do you think the Creeper King is dead?” Gamenight asked. “I think the whole hive would be dead after that.” Crafter said. “So do you think there is something to worry about?” Asked Gamenight. “I don’t think so, but we will probably not see any creepers again for a while.” Was Crafters response. As Gamenight went back up the ladder he wondered if there was something to be worried about.  When he got to the top Herder asked if in the morning Gamenight999 would help him find his wolves. “Sure, Herder I’ll help you. I can finish the new area of my castle later.”

Chapter 3

                Zombiebrine looked at all the shadow crafters that had gathered in Creeperbrine’s cave. “Brothers, soon we will free The Maker from his prison, but first we need to put all our evil powers together to make something that will for sure make the user-that-is-not-a-user tremble in fear!” All the different shadow crafters yelled in agreement. “What will we make?” Asked the newest one who had arrived, Breezebrine. “Something wicked indeed.” Replied Zombiebrine. Using their powers they started to make something truly terrifying. They each contributed ideas and put them to use. Soon the shadow crafters had made something new and never seen in Minecraft. “Hmmmm,” Zombiebrine remarked, “It is missing a name. Fellow shadow crafters, what will we call this monstrosity?” “The Terror.” Spiderbrine suggested. “No, that won’t do, how about The Warden. Who will make war on Gamenight999 and force him to use the gateway of life to free us and free Herobrine!” All the dark shadow crafters nodded in agreement. When they changed the code to make their creature alive, the ground around The Warden changed into skulk and started spreading around. “Soon we will change this green world into nothing but skulk!” Zombiebrine yelled. “Warden, we have made you to destroy Gamenight999, and we will give you all our monsters that we improve with our crafting under your control. You shall blind your enemies, shoot at them with a blast, hear any move they make, and utterly cause them to despair. But first we must prepare, we can’t let that annoying user-that-is-not-a-user mess up our plans before you, Warden, are ready. So my fellow shadow crafters, we shall harass our enemy, Gamenight999 until the Warden has gathered enough forces.” All the shadow crafters started joyously thinking of how they would make the user-that-is-not-a-user suffer for his crimes. “Go now find all the monsters we can, but send some out to harass our enemy, so he doesn’t realize the real plan.” Zombiebrine said. And as the shadow crafters went out to find monsters for their army, Zombiebrine laughed a mechanical, bone chilling laugh. “Soon Gamenight you will be mine!” He yelled at no one. With that he went in search of zombies.

                                                                                                Chapter 4

                Gamenight had just finished helping Herder get his wolves when he heard the music of Minecraft get strained as if something were terribly wrong. He could see that Heder could feel it too, something bad had happened. “Herder I have a bad feeling about something.” Herder nodded in agreement. “We should go see Crafter.” The lanky boy responded. As they went down the secret tunnel Gamenight wondered why these strange things were happening. When they got down Gamenight ran to the crafting chamber to find Hunter talking with Crafter. “Crafter,” Gamenight yelled, “Something bad is happening I think.” “Gamenight, there is nothing wrong. You are always looking for some sort of conspiracy,” Hunter said, “We destroyed Herobrine and all his monster kings are dead. This peace has made you jumpy.” “Gamenight,” Crafter said, “Please continue.” “I think there is something bad happening, I felt a disturbance in the music of Minecraft, the Oracle must be warning us for something.” Just when Crafter was about to respond, Sticher came running in saying, “The village is under attack!” Everyone stopped what they were doing and ran to the ladder and climbed up. As they went up, Gamenight’s mind was whirling of every bad possible scenario that could be happening. When he got to the top he immediately ran to the wall and looked outside, and he couldn’t believe what was happening, creepers, spiders, skeletons, and zombies by the hundreds were marching out to attack the village. “Oh no,” Gamenight said. “The monsters haven’t worked together like this since Herobrine!” Crafter exclaimed. _We can’t fight that many with the few soldiers we have at this village_ Gamenight thought _We will surely be overrun, what do I do? What do I do?_ “Gamenight what do we do?” Asked Digger. “There are too many!” Builder said. “What do we do?” asked another. Right then, Gamenight exclaimed, “We cannot fight this many, we must flee and go to a different village. We need more information on why this is happening.” So the whole village fled from the wave of destruction and hatred that was about to crash down on the village. As the villagers went into the secret mineshaft tunnels in the crafting table, Gamenight could have sworn he saw something blue climb out of the blocks. But as quickly as he saw it, it disappeared. _What was that?_ Gamenight thought _Am I going crazy? What is happening?_ As he sped down the tracks Gamenight wondered why this was happening.
