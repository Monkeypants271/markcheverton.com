The dragon’s eyes finally opened, but it wasn’t a normal hue. The eyes shine white highlighting where the enderman stood. He could not believe his eyes at the sight of dragon’s double-sized pair. “Behold, Enderman, as the time has come, the Maker has returned!” The dragon sat and waited until all the lanky subjects withdrew to bow, despite the low HP levels from the recent battle. Then, it roared enough to cause the music within its code to retreat as far away from the source.  
Far away, a white hooded figure, with 2 red globes for sockets, was encased in a bright light, that sizzled to burn flesh, but was icy as a tundra.  
The beam disappeared just as it came, and so did the figure. The 2 kings stood for a flinched second as the beam popped from view in a millisecond. “What was that, my Lord?” The red creature winced.  
“I….do not have an explanation, but, I can feel it in this body, that was not an ordinary person, but it wasn’t one of the snotty Physical users, nor those like the User-that-is-not-a-user. They are here for a…reason, one I wish not to quarrel with, as your kind’s revolution has begun!” The jet-black winged reptile sneered and quaked the End to ensure that even his presence knocked the balance from the server. “Let us start, with the Crafter, I ought to thank him”
