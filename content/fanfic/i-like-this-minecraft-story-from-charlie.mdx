“Gameknight999 in the Land of Dreams”

                           Chapter 1

   Gameknight looked around him. All he could see was the siver mist. He wondered where he was. Then he remembered that this was the Land of Dreams. The place where nightmares could come to life…  
      Standing and dropping his bow, he looked around for the glowing white eyes of his nemesis, Herobrine, or the dark red eyes of the evil enderman king,Erebus. Satisfied that there was no danger, he decided to call Hunter. “Hunter,” he called into the darkness,hoping he wouldn’t attract any monsters around him.  
    Suddenly, Hunter appeared next to him. “Pick your bow up you idoit,” she reprimised him. “Remember there are always monsters here so you better be ready.” He nodded, remembering the conversation they had had long ago when Hunter had been captured by Erebus and Malacoda.  
    Suddenly, a growl came from the silvery mist. “Gameknight,” Hunter said nervisly. “It sounds like zombies. Lots of them.” “I think your right,” Gameknight replied. Suddenly, thirty zombies appeared. Gameknight was shocked and scared. Even with his two-swords and Hunter’s amazing skill with the bow, there was no way that they could survive this battle.”I don’t think the’ve noticed your swords yet,” Hunter said. The rotten araoma of decaying flesh started to assult his senses. “Come on zombies,” Gameknight yelled at the molded green creatures. “Lets Dance!”

                               Chapter 2

       The angry creatures shuffled for him and Hunter, their angry black eyes glowing almost with hatred. What could make the zombies hate the NPCs so much? he wondered. He figured the answer was probably Herobrine. Faking an attack to the left, he blocked a set of claws from racking his enchanted  diamand armor than kicked a zombie away, letting Hunter’s three flaming arrows consume his HP. The zombies finally noticed his two-swords and took a step back. Gameknight knew that this was a noob mistake and had been proven over and over again in his many hours of PvP. Smiling at the zombies he yelled, “What’s wrong you ugly cowards? You afraid of me?” The zombies were enraged beyond rational thought and ran as fast as thier decaying legs would allow them to straight at the User-that-is-not-a-user. A maniacal laugh floated from beyond the silvery mist,causing the zombies to pause, then slowly slink back untill they were out of range of Hunter.As Gameknight and Hunter locked eyes on each other,Gameknight knew who it was. Herobrine had finally arrived. “So User-that-is-not-a-user,” an evil voice said.”You finally had enough courage to face me in battle. It gives me great pleasure to know that I can always find you here in the Land of Dreams whenever I’m bored.” “I’m not afraid of you Herobrine,” Gameknight shouted at him, though his voice cracked with fear. “Of course your not you coward,” Herobrine mocked. Then Herobrine stepped out of the mist and Gameknight started to shake. Herobrine had become an Elderitch Guardian.

                               Chapter 3

   Hunter looked shocked as she stared at Herobrine. “Are you really sure that’s him?” she said to Gameknight as her voice trembled. “Of course I know it’s me,” Herobrine mocked as the scared exspression on Hunter’s face slowly turned to rage and determanation. Gameknight couldn’t say a single thing. All he could do was stare shocked into Herobrine’s glowing white eyes. The zombies started to chuckle with hatred at Gameknight as Herobrine smiled an evil smile at Gameknight999. “It was great fun getting to know you, coward,” Herobrine said sarcasticly at the User-that-is-not-a-user. “But i’m sure that me and my zombie pals would like to see you destroyed now. I sure hope you will come back to vist me sometime. Zombies!” Herobrine said with an evil smile on his face. “ATTACK!”

   Gameknight suddenly knew what to do. “Hunter!” he shouted. The zombies paused their attack and Herobrine’s expression of rage turned into annoyance. Hunter looked at Gameknight with a desperate look on her face. “What?” she said,almost pleaded. “Lets show Herobrine how he should do fights,” Gameknight said with a smirk on his face. Hunter laughed understandinly then the two shouted almost simultanisly, “WAKE UP!, WAKE UP!, WAKE UP!” “NOOOOO!” Herobrine shouted and shouted at them, “This isn’t over User-that-is-not-a-user! This is not over!” ut he was too late. Gameknight999 and Hunter had dissapeared from the Land of Dreams.
