Items from the deceased were flowing out of chests, while endermen  were forcing the villagers to eat rotten flesh from the fallen zombies. He pulled out water bottles and threw them at the endermen, and quickly destroyed them. He gave the NPCs potions of regeneration, and asked Digger what had happened. “They came like a flood of evil and darkness, and almost killed us, but fed us the zombie remains. Those who refused were killed. Half of us were murdered.” Digger raised his hand and squeezed it, performing the salute to the dead.  
“How could you let this happen?!”, Gameknight screamed, and immediately regretted it. Digger stood as tall as he could, and shoved rotten flesh into his mouth, and walked away. Gameknight999 spit the morsels out and struck Digger in the back.
