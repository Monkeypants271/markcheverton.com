Empty. Empty and dark.  Gameknight999 turned around and was shocked to see Crafter there.  He asked, “where are all the villagers?”.  “I don’t know.”  Crafter said in fear.   Just then, they heard an explosion and cries of pain.  They climbed back to the surface where they found Erbus and Malacoda smiling wickedly.  “Wondering where all the villagers are?” said Erbus.  “Where are they?!” Gameknight999 demanded.  “Let’s make a deal,” said Malacoda, “The Villagers for Crafter.”  
Suddenly, Shawny appeared with Sticher yelling “Hunter, pull the lever!”  
Just then Malacoda dropped to bedrock and Sticher covered the hole with bedrock.
