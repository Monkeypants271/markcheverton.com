### Chapter 1

#### Into Minecraft

Jason got on his computer very excitedly. He was finally home from school and was ready to play Minecraft. He knew about the strange events and rumors about Gameknight999. He heard about the near crash. He was on Minecraft at the time. The ground had started to glow as white electricity ran across the ground. He was on a popular server, so he looked around and saw all the users in the area looking around at the glowing blocks.

But that time was finally over, or so he hoped. The mobs were now unorganized and would not attack. He got onto a private server he had made for him and his friends to record a Survival series on for YouTube. He was trying to work harder and get more subscribers. He got onto the server and started working on some mining. The team was running low on diamonds and he needed to get some. On the last recording he said he would.

Soon the others started to get on. He first saw his best friends of the team, miner\_the\_NPC, or Miner, and CakeyLP, or Cakey. But eventually TriggerFinger got on. They decided not to skype because they wanted to do a diamond hunting competition for fun. Jason, AKA WolfDude55 managed to get 20 diamonds. Lucky day.

His father was an inventor, like Gameknight999’s, according to what he heard. He was working on a project similar to the digitizer, created by Gameknight999’s father, Monkeypants\_271. He was inspired by Jason’s stories of Gameknight999 and wanted to recreate the invention, since he didn’t believe in them. He didn’t understand that WolfDude was in the Battle for the Source. Jason knew it was real. He’s been there.

Later, Jason’s father ran upstairs. “I made a breakthrough!” He shouted. “Come quick!”

_My dad made a “breakthrough”._ WolfDude typed in chat. _I’ll be back._ He got off the server and walked calmly downstairs. “What is it now, Dad,” Jason sighed. His father made “breakthroughs” before. They never worked.

“I think i finally made it so you can get into Mineworld or whatever.” he said.

“_Minecraft,_” Jason corrected.

“Yah, yah,” his dad replied. “Allow me to show you.” He grabbed a sheet on a machine and ripped it off. “The Minitizer 3000, or the Digitizer 2.0.” Jason rolled his eyes. “Can’t wait to try it,” Jason said sarcastically. His dad went to his test computer, which had Minecraft opened on singleplayer. “Let me show you.” He pressed a button on the Minitizer and closed his eyes. The Minitizer glowed white and shot a beam at his dad. Soon his dad’s body was gone along with the light. The screen started moving on its own and eventually Jason realized that it was his dad controlling it from inside Minecraft. _Huh._ Jason thought. _It actually worked._ Soon, the digitizer 2.0 glowed again, until his dad stood there again. “And you can exit whenever you want.” He said, stumbling. “It makes you dizzy when you leave though. It goes away in a minute.”

“Ok well im going back upstairs to play more Minecraft.” Jason said. “I have no interest in entering right now.” When he got to his computer, he got back on the server. _His “digitizer 2.0” actually works._ He typed once he checked if the others were on. All three were. _Cool!_ Miner replied. _The others are getting on soon._ Jason sighed. _Skype?_ Appeared in chat as he realized his sister, TheWolvesHowl528, had gotten on. _Sure._ he replied. He called their skype group chat. Everyone answered after a minute.

“What’s up?” TriggerFinger said.

“Not much.” Jason replied. “Tomorrow’s my birthday, so yay.” Jason continued. “Maybe we can try my dad’s new ‘digitizer 2.0’ then. He got it working, and claimed as I left that more than one person can go in at a time. Like 10.”

“Cool!”, Airagalaxy22 said. “I’m going to get on Minecraft now.” she said. Once everyone was on, they gathered minerals like crazy. Jason couldn’t help but feel a strange feeling brewing on the server. Everyone mined in silence. Mobs were nowhere to be found. He felt a presence and it wasn’t a comfortable one.

Soon, it was the next day. Everyone was at Jason’s house preparing a “lan party”. They were going downstairs to their many computers and were going to play some Minecraft. They were going to set up a new world for some contests. Everyone got on, but soon, noises grew louder and louder. Soon, Jason couldn’t stand it. He looked behind him and saw his father’s Minitizer glowing. “Uh oh.” He said. “I don’t think this is good.”

“What’s wrong?” Daniel (TriggerFinger) asked, looking back. “Oh.” He said when he saw the Minitizer.

“BRACE YOURSELVES!” Jason proclaimed as a beam hit every single person in the room. Jason felt a shock go up his body. He closed his eyes and prepared for what might happen. Everything went black.

He woke up lying in some grass. Soon he realized it wasn’t normal grass. It was blocky. He looked around and saw a bunch of usernames. TriggerFinger, TheWolvesHowl528, miner\_the\_NPC, Triquillus\_Luna, CakeyLP, happyfeet65978, and Auragalaxy22. WolfDude55 felt the grass around him. It was all so realistic. Everything was so realistic. They spawned in some plains with a forest and a dessert nearby. A snowy forest, a tundra, was on the other side of the dessert and a jungle between the forest and tundra. WolfDude looked as the others started to sit up, holding their heads. “Uugh,” said Miner. “What happened?”

“I believe we are in the game.” WolfDude responded. “Inside Minecraft itself, like Gameknight999.” His companions looked around in shock at the real details of Minecraft. Everything felt so alive. “We should get moving before it becomes night.” WolfDude stated as he stood up. “Let’s go use the forest and start a house. We should be able to make one before night.” The others nodded and started walking to the forest, preparing for the physical labor it contained. WolfDude pounded on the logs as they quickly broke. He made a crafting bench and shouted “Crafting bench here!”

Soon, the walls were made. It was large so it could fit all of them. They were working on the roof when WolfDude felt a surge within himself. “I’m going to go get more wood” he lied. He walked into the woods.

Darkness.

#### Chapter 2

#### The Land of Dreams

_WolfDude55 heard leaves in the distance. He saw a mist of unknown origin. He seemed to be where he was, but it was dark and very misty. He walked through the unknown land. The mist seemed to continue forever. He blinked and saw his house. He was in the basement and the Minitizer was glowing. He went upstairs only to hear machinery downstairs._

_He went back into the basement as the Glow of the Minitizer continued to glow. He hid in the corner when suddenly zombies and skeletons started appearing out of nowhere. He wished he had his modified sword from Minecraft that he always made. He looked in his hand to find the unbreakable enchanted golden sword in his hand. He knew what he had to do._

_He jumped out of the corner and stabbed a zombie in the torso. He spun as an arrow flew by his face and took down the shooter. Spiders crawled on the wall and soon endermen started to appear. One, in a blood dark red, came through the abyss of the Minitizer. It looked at WolfDude as he fought the mobs in attempt to keep them out of the rest of the house. WolfDude whipped out his beloved bow and shot into the mob army, killing many. Soon the red enderman had enough. He shrieked into the basement and teleported to WolfDude. He gripped the back of his neck, in the pressure point, and teleported outside._

_“I have waited for you for a long time.” the enderman said. All WolfDude could do was gasp for air. “I am Phobetor, the newest enderman king. You will die, user-that-is-not-a-user.” All WolfDude could do was close his eyes and gasp._

_Darkness once again._

WolfDude jumped to a start. Miner was shaking him, trying to make him conscious. “Ugh. What happened?” WolfDude asked Miner.

“You fell asleep or something.” CakeyLP replied behind Miner. “It’s been a few hours. It will be dark soon.”

“The weirdest thing just happened.” WolfDude said, standing up. “I don’t know what it was, but you guys remember Erebus right?” Miner and Ty nodded. “Well another enderman that looked similar to him attacked me in my dream.” He gripped the back of his neck and noticed that is was sore, and felt like the attack. “He called himself ‘Photebor’, new king of the endermen. He called me a ‘user-that-is-not-a-user’ and when the dream began to fade, he said ‘We will meet again here, in the Land of Dreams’.” WolfDude groaned. “I feel like it was real.”

The trio entered the house. WolfDude walked in and sat in one of the newly made beds. Miner told his story to the others as the roof was finished. WolfDude looked out the window and saw and enderman in the distance, watching the house. WolfDude saw it for a split second before it disappeared. _I’m just paranoid._ He thought, reassuring himself. He noticed it was getting dark and he wanted to go to sleep. He refused to go to sleep now. He was on watch. Soon, Aira sat in the bed next to him.

“Are you ok?” Airagalaxy asked. She looked at WolfDude in worry.

“Yah. I’m fine. Just keeping an eye out for mobs.” he replied.

“Just remember that we’re here for you. We’re safe in here, so you should get some sleep.” Aira said. WolfDude knew she was right, but he was too paranoid. He nodded as she went to her bed. He lied down and felt himself doze off.

_The mist of the Land of Dreams swirled around him. WolfDude looked around and saw the house. He looked as red eyes seemed to run in a blur in the forest. Suddenly, a figure appeared before him. WolfDude jumped._

_This was no ordinary figure, nor was it Herobrine. The creature looked like the new Alex skin set, but with blood-red eyes. Maybe it was Herobrine’s preticessor? WolfDude slowly backed up as the demon-like creature walked towards him. Soon, WolfDude was backed against the house. The demonic creature grabbed him by the throat and said one word. “Thanks.” The voice made WolfDude tremble. It sounded like multiple voices at once. The sound wasn’t very pleasant._

_WolfDude closed his eyes and repeated in his head:_ wake up, wake up, wake up, wake up! _He began to feel himself awakening. He continued._ Wake up, wake up, wake up, wake up, WAKE UP! _He felt himself struggling to wake up, but nothing worked. He couldn’t wake up._

_The creature looked up and smiled at him. “Thanks,” it said again. “I can feel your struggle. You cannot escape me. I am inside you, for now.” The creature faded away, laughing a laugh similar to Herobrine’s._ Was this a Herobrine 2.0 or something? _WolfDude thought._ Did I create it? What about the enderman king? Did that…thing… remake him? _The questions tumbled around his head as he got more and more scared. This was from his nightmares. He tried to understand, but all he got was more confused. He saw a group of creepers walking to the house. These were in the real world. He needed to wake up._

WolfDude jumped up with a start. He looked out the window and saw the creepers approaching faster. He woke up the others as he pointed out the window, saying “creepers incoming!” Everyone grabbed their stone tools. WolfDude wished he had his bow. He knew they needed to go mining soon, but now wasn’t the time. He went outside and stood before the creepers. He knew that the creeper king, Oxus, was basically a pacifist now so he thought this must be a rogue team, making them easier to take care of.

The creepers charged as WolfDude and the others were getting ready for battle. He pointed his sword at the creepers. They continued to surge forward. Before the creepers could reach the users, they all exploded simultaneously. WolfDude just looked at them in shock. He’s never seen creepers blow up before they reached their target before. This was very odd.

The group walked back into the house. WolfDude knew what to do at this point. “Welp, since we have nothing better to do and since we need it,” he said. “Let’s go mining. We still need iron for combat.” The others nodded their heads. “I saw a cave nearby. We go in pairs. I’ll go with Miner, Aira you go with happyfeet, TriggerFinger you go with Luna, and Ty go with TheWolvesHowl. Find all the materials you can. Bring some wood so that you can make torches or crafting tables if necessary.” The pairs began to gather materials as WolfDude met with Miner, who was grabbing wood. WolfDude grabbed the pickaxes.

#### Chapter 3

#### Exploration

Miner and WolfDude walked down the cave. They had gone a different way than the others and found a separate cave. Miner searched and placed torches while WolfDude covered their backs. They had found a spider dungeon, giving them a spider farm to get string for bows. They already had one for each of them, plus 12 more for the others in their own time.

The cave seemed to go on forever. Luckily they found lots of iron and already each had an iron pick just incase they found diamonds or gold. They also made iron swords incase more mobs came. They came up to a room with a lit cavern. Lava was lighting the area.

“Lava leads a likelihood of diamonds.” WolfDude said. They mined around the lava in hopes of spotting diamonds inside the walls. They managed to find a few, and some gold for WolfDude’s sword. He also got some diamonds for another sword so he can dual-wield. That was WolfDude’s prefered fight style.

WolfDude decided not to grab the gold sword yet. He would be tempted to use it, but it’s not unbreakable. _At least not yet_. WolfDude thought. He worked hard on the diamond sword. Soon, he found something. _Designing swords?_ _Impossible for Minecraft._ WolfDude walked over to miner, who had a diamond pickaxe and a sword. WolfDude got an idea. “Let me see those.” WolfDude said. Miner gave him both tools and WolfDude used the crafting table. He made them one weapon and made it smoother.

“How’d you do that?” Miner asked. WolfDude shrugged. He had no idea. “I don’t know,” WolfDude finally said. He chuckled. “Now you can be like the character in your poems and personal storyline.” Miner shrugged back. “I guess I can.” he replied, sarcastically.

The duo finally stopped mining and went back to the house. WolfDude looked at what was once a wood box and now a mansion. _Somebody was busy here_, he thought. He went inside and found separate rooms for each person. He looked in his room and found a crafting bench, a furnace, and series of chests. He walked out of his room and met up with Miner. “What was in your room, Miner?” WolfDude asked

“The basics.” Miner replied.

“Same.” WolfDude said, trying to continue the conversation. They had been very quiet to each other the whole time. Before Miner could respond, Ty and TheWolvesHowl arrived at the house. Soon after, so did Aira and happyfeet. Then Luna and TriggerFinger. Everyone looked at their rooms, surprised.

After they all met up, they talked for a good while, starting with the house. No one had built it. Then they listed what they got. WolfDude and Miner got the most. Full diamond armor for everyone, except WolfDude. Everyone got bows and arrows, even though some of them were better than others. WolfDude was an expert in both. At least now they were ready for war. That would likely come.

Everyone woke up and went outside. They met up and decided on a move. They explore, and maybe find some villages. They walked for days before finding a village. This village did not have walls yet. This was peculiar. Maybe it was a server that had not yet knows about the mob armies from back at the Battle of the Source and all of Gameknight999’s times fighting armies of mobs. The group of users walked into the village. NPCs gasped as they saw no server threads on the group as the users walked to the center of the village. They got to the watch tower and stood at the entrance as NPCs gathered around, gasping and mumbling to each other.

“Where is your Crafter?” WolfDude finally said. The NPCs gasped and looked around nervously. They didn’t know that users knew about the secret lives of the NPCs. _Interesting._ WolfDude thought. He turned to his companions. “I don’t think these guys know about the years of war between Gameknight and Herobrine.” He whispered. He turned back to see an old NPC moving through the crowd. He was wearing a white smock with a black stripe. A Crafter’s smock.

The Crafter got to the front of the crowd and looked at the users. He gulped as he began to speak. “What is it you want?” The Crafter said. WolfDude looked at the poor villager, who was trembling in more than one way. He was old and he was scared for his village.

“We come in peace.” WolfDude said. He heard a loud sigh of relief as all the villagers began to relax. “I’m sure you all know of Gameknight999?” The villagers shook their heads. WolfDude sighed. “It’s a long story, but it looks like you NPCs are going to need to be prepared. There are more mob kings sought on the destruction of all of you, and you need to be ready for war. I am WolfDude. I was at the Battle of the Source as Gameknight999 defeated the first mob king, Erebus. It was a true war. NPCs died. Users fell. However myself and my two main friends, Miner and Ty, were all in that battle and made it through the whole thing. Now it is your time to be ready. This is the only server i know that does not know about Gameknight999 or anything that happened. There are ways to get you villagers to separate your arms. Just destroy a crafting bench while the other person is crafting. We need to do our own planning. Build a wall around your village and make archer towers around. We may return to see progress.”

The villagers were still confused, and WolfDude could tell. “Just go!” he shouted before jumping into the watch tower with his friends. “These guys are going to need help if they are going to survive. The mob army Photebor is likely making will destroy them without defences. They do not have a reason to fight yet.” WolfDude said. “In the meantime, we should make a training ground for these guys. Anyone got sticks and smooth stone?” Aira handed him some. “Ok I’m going to make some dummies out of armor stands for them to hit for practice, but keep some for us to practice with ourselves.”

After making the training ground and starting the wall, WolfDude made sure there was a sign saying “Do not underestimate the younglings,” and the group left. WolfDude took the lead with the others following in a V formation. _This formation was scientifically proven to save energy._ WolfDude thought. _At least, in the air._ The team continued until they reached a jungle. They waded through the tangled mess of trees and bushes. By the time they got out, the group, except WolfDude, was tired. They decided to set up camp there.

WolfDude lied on the ground near the fire. He felt like he was being watched more and more. Eventually he sat up and looked around at the jungle and plains, separated by a river. WolfDude sighed and almost lay back down when he saw some purple eyes in the distance. He looked at the eyes, not directly, until they realized that WolfDude was looking at the possible enderman. When the eyes disappeared, WolfDude concluded that they were being spied on by Endermen. He lay down and slept, but did not enter the Land of Dreams.

#### Chapter 4

#### Through Time

WolfDude woke and looked at the spot where the enderman was. Instead of an enderman, he saw a random splash of color. The colors quickly disappeared in the brush. _What could that have been?_ WolfDude wondered. The group packed up and continued walking South, WolfDude watching in every direction. Eventually Miner came up. He was in the spot behind him

“What’s got you so worried?” Miner asked. WolfDude looked blankly ahead.

“I saw something last night.” WolfDude said. “An enderman was watching us. But when I looked in the same spot this morning, I saw nothing but some random colors that disappeared in the bushes.”

Miner just stared at WolfDude. He was nearly open-mouthed. Suddenly a noise disturbed them. WolfDude looked in the direction of the sound and saw nothing. “What was that?” Airagalaxy said. WolfDude held up his hand in signal to not move as he crept forward. He stared into the forest and found nothing there.

“I don’t see anything.” WolfDude said. He backed out of the forest, only to feel in his mind something trying to drag him back in. WolfDude ignored the sensation and walked back to the group. The group continued forward on full alert.

The group eventually ended up near a village. But there was something with this village. No one was there and the village seemed old and run down. The only thing WolfDude could think of was to go to the Crafting Chamber under the watch tower.

WolfDude broke the block that was supposedly where the crafting chamber should be. All WolfDude found was a dirt block. He dug more and more until he could not dig anymore. The crafting chamber was not there.

WolfDude came out of the hole to find no one. The others were gone. He looked out the window to see if they were standing guard, but what WolfDude saw was a major surprise. Villagers. NPC’s going around. But there was something wrong with them. They seemed ghostly.

WolfDude walked out of the watch tower. When he got to the well, what he saw astonished him. Gameknight999 himself standing there, also ghostly. He was standing below the well, with a villager almost preaching to him. WolfDude recognized this scene from the stories he read about Gameknight. This was when Gameknight first came into minecraft. _But that was in the past._ WolfDude thought. _Unless i somehow went throu-_ WolfDude could not finish the thought. He knew what had happened. He went through some sort of time vortex.

WolfDude didn’t see Hunter anywhere. WolfDude knew that he hadn’t met Hunter yet. Hunter came from the next server on the server plane. WolfDude had always wanted to see Hunter. Unfortunately, WolfDude had more on his mind.

WolfDude walked out of the village, running to the woods. He dodged trees as he ran. He didn’t know what he was looking for to find the time vortex out, but he knew that something had to appear somewhere.

Suddenly, he felt himself flying sideways, a pain in his hip. He slumped to the ground against a tree and looked up to find a pair of glowing white eyes and red eyes staring at him. The new virus charged at WolfDude. WolfDude rolled to the right, standing up. He kicked down the virus before it could recover. WolfDude knew Herobrine was still there at this time period and had seen Herobrine before the other virus attacked. WolfDude knew he was outmatched, since he didn’t have his golden sword, known as the _Soul Blade_. Therefor, WolfDude ran.

WolfDude searched his inventory after he ran a distance. Finding an iron sword, he pulled it out, holding it in his right hand. He turned and saw something white coming at him. _Clash!_ WolfDude barely blocked Herobrine’s iron sword as it came for his face. He rolled to the left, dodging another attack, and stood at Herobrine’s side. He backed up to prepare for another attack, and saw the two viruses standing next to each other.

Then it hit him like a stone slamming into his forehead. _A copy_, WolfDude thought. _This so called “new” virus is a copy of Herobrine. A Herobrine 2.0_. WolfDude chose to ran as he thought. _How can it be? A last stand? A final resort?_ WolfDude determined that Herobrine, to save himself, copied his code and created a new virus with more powers. WolfDude just ran.

One he believed he was finally safe, WolfDude slowed to a walk. He ate some bread as he walked out of the forest into a tundra. WolfDude knew from Gameknight999’s stories that this tundra would be burned and glassed by the blazes that Herobrine modified. He looked at the place and thought about the glassing. He could only imagine how bad it was. It was such a beautiful area. Who could do a thing like this?

_Herobrine would_. WolfDude thought, stopping his mesmerized thoughts. He knew he had to get back to his friends. _How?_ He had to think of something before Herobrine and the other virus attacked again. _The village! That has a time vortex!_ WolfDude facepalmed. Why didn’t he think of that before? He ran to through the forest back to the village.

He heard the sound of pigs and cows nearby and the movement of many people. WolfDude didn’t think about the sounds. What he was interested in was the music of Minecraft. The music was peaceful, yet strained. The Oracle herself was worried. WolfDude didn’t know the Oracle personally like Gameknight did. WolfDude relied on the music of Minecraft to understand the Oracle’s worry.

WolfDude thought about the virus. He wanted to give it a name. _How about Demonbrine?_ WolfDude thought. The virus had dark red eyes and was in an alex skin rather than a steve. Demonbrine was the only name WolfDude could think of. It seemed to suit the virus.

WolfDude finally arrived back at the village. He got to the entrance of the crafting chamber and climbed down the ladder. Climbing back up, WolfDude found himself in the same village. He was not where he wanted to be.

WolfDude was stuck.

#### Chapter 5

#### Kidnappers

WolfDude walked out of the watch tower, alone. He looked in the village and saw the villagers preparing to leave, just like the sounds from before. He looked to the right and saw Gameknight giving instructions next to him. WolfDude sighed. _This must be how Gameknight feels all the time._ WolfDude thought. _I’m willing to bet that he is almost always stressed in situations like this._

WolfDude looked out into the distance of the land. He could see where the tundra would be. As he looked, he began to see a flicker in the light. The land seemed to wave in the sky. WolfDude looked carefully at the waver. _Those must be time vortexes._ WolfDude thought. He looked carefully at the vortex and saw something falling out of the sky. _Aira?_ WolfDude thought. When he focused on the object, he saw Airagalaxy falling out of the sky. _She’s going to land in the tundra!_ WolfDude thought, moving into a sprint.

WolfDude ran to the tundra as fast as he could. He ran track one year in school and got second place on his one and only 1500 meter race, so WolfDude had no trouble jogging at a normally sprinting pace. However, WolfDude was sprinting. He arrived at the tundra and saw Airagalaxy being held up over the shoulder by Demonbrine.

WolfDude’s hand shot out in attempt to meet Aira’s, who was extending hers. WolfDude nearly got there when Herobrine stepped in. WolfDude swung his blade and nearly hit Herobrine in the head. Herobrine lifted his sword and swung at WolfDude, where his blade would meet Herobrine’s. WolfDude kicked Herobrine in the stomach and brought his hilt on Herobrine’s head. Herobrine fell. When WolfDude looked up, Demonbrine was gone. _No_. WolfDude thought in rage. He kicked Herobrine off his hands and knees out of frustration.

Then he felt himself falling.

WolfDude looked up as he fell. The world seemed to turn and waver as he fell. WolfDude closed his eyes so he wouldn’t be nauseous. He felt himself turning, even though he knew he wasn’t.

He finally opened his eyes and saw the blue sky above him. He saw green trees and the green grass of Minecraft all around him. WolfDude heard a loud ringing and felt more tired than ever before. His vision blurred as he looked to his left. He saw three figures running to him.

“WolfDude!” he heard echo through his mind.

“Is he alive?” another voice said.

“Yes. He wouldn’t be here if he wasn’t remember? This is Minecraft!” a third voice replied.

“Oh yeah. I forgot.”

“He doesn’t look good. I think he got in a fight.” The first voice said.

“Let’s get him to-” The voices echoed away as WolfDude fell into unconsciousness.

Miner looked at TriggerFinger in worry. “He’s unconscious.” TriggerFinger said. Miner just moved his gaze to WolfDude and stared at him.

“Do you think he saw Airagalaxy?” TheWolvesHowl replied.

“Maybe.” TriggerFinger replied.

“We better get to a cave. He needs to rest. It’s hot out here.” Miner said sternly. TriggerFinger just looked at Miner in shock. Miner was still staring at WolfDude.

“OK then.” TheWolvesHowl, also known as Ace, said. TriggerFinger looked at TheWolvesHowl and shrugged. Miner lifted WolfDude off the ground in his arms.

They walked only a few blocks before finding a cave on the other side of the hill they were next to. TheWolvesHowl sighed at the sight of it. “There was one right there?” she said, slightly agitated. Miner just ignored the comment and took WolfDude into the cave and sealed it off, with the others inside.

Miner sat next to WolfDude and sighed. TheWolvesHowl and TriggerFinger exchanged glances as Miner closed his eyes and rested next to Miner. “I guess we all should get some rest.” TriggerFinger said.

“Agreed.” TheWolvesHowl replied. TheWolvesHowl sat against the wall and closed her eyes. TriggerFinger took watch in case any zombies came.

WolfDude looked through the darkness. He couldn’t see anyone around. WolfDude sighed. He had no idea where he was. All he could see was darkness. The Music of Minecraft slowly began to fade into his head. WolfDude felt soothed by the presence of the music. But as he listened to the music, the more he noticed the strain within the music. And through the darkness, WolfDude saw a light.

Suddenly, a voice echoed through the darkness. It was an old woman’s voice.  WolfDude guessed the voice was the Oracle’s voice.

“WolfDude.” The voice said. “Your friends are in peril. You will need more power to save them.”

“I don’t understand!” WolfDude said loudly. His voice echoed faintly as an image appeared before him. It was Luna, Aira, Happy, and Ty stuck in a cage. Demonbrine and Photebor came up next to the cage. Demonbrine touched the cage. Everyone flew to the other side with a shock of electricity. Aira just got back up and stood tall. Demonbrine hit them again. WolfDude couldn’t watch anymore.

“It’s time for your. . . modifications.” The voice said. WolfDude looked up as a yellow beam came through the darkness onto his body. WolfDude screamed out in pain as his Minecraft genetic code was reorganized within himself. He felt stronger somehow as the beam of energy and light faded. WolfDude knew what he had to do.

“You’ll find how to use your abilities soon. And just in time.” The voice said. WolfDude collapsed on the ground, falling into unconsciousness.

#### Chapter 6

#### A New Creation
