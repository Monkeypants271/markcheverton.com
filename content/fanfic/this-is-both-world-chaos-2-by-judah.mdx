**Both world chaos 2 by Judah**

In the midst of the panicked  students and the growling monsters Gameknight, his friends, and the oracle fought the real and true final battle against Herobrine that determined whether they would destroy or be destroyed. The oracle planted her staff into the ground and tree vines shot out of the ground and wrapped around Herobrine who cut the vines and stabbed at Gameknight. Gameknight duked and leaped at Herobrine. Herobrine blasted a hole in the ceiling and shot out into the sky. Gameknight followed, running across the sky, his sword outstretched in front of him. Herobrine turned around and leaped at Gameknight. Gameknight ducked and stabbed Herobrine in the chest. At the last moment Herobrine teleported out of the way Gameknight stabbed behind him and got ready for another attack. Before Herobrine could move a flock of doves flew at him in a mad rage. Herobrine was having trouble attacking Gameknight with all the birds in his way. Fianaly Gameknight Stabbed Herobribe in the chest before the virus could teleport away. Herobrine disappeared leaving behind his sword armor and a little box. Gameknight opened the box and immediately he felt wonderful like he had just had a warm refreshing drink. Inside the box there was a note that said _you now have the power of  Overworld A.I_ Gameknight quickly concentrated on a building and it floated into the air. Happy now Gameknight freed the villagers from the curse Herobrine put on them and everyone lived happily ever after.
