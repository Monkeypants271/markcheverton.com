Chapter 1 – John

Once upon a minecraft time there was a guy named John. He didn’t like to travel much,he just stayed home on his farm. Now, you might think John had lots of company on a farm, but he grew crops and the nearest village was 1,000,230 blocks away.  
One day John got lonely and decided to walk to the village. It was a nice walk,John could smell the burning of bones and flesh. When he was half way there he heard an ender dragon roaring! As he got closer he heard the villagers screaming “help us please!”.  
John ran to the village and put on his leather armor! But the dragon was too powerful for him. John respawned in his house. His house was full of creepers. That was the end of John’s house.

Chapter 2 – A Journey

John ran back to the village to see the village destroyed and the ender dragon gone. He had to find out who summoned the ender dragon and creepers! John searched for days without running into any monsters.  
One day he found someone about to fall into lava! He placed a slime block where the person would fall. John said “Hey! Are you okay?” “I’m fine.”said the man “My name’s Steve. What’s yours?” “I’m not sure if I should tell you my name.” said John. “I wasn’t sure I should tell YOU my name either Greafer!” Steve said as he pulled out a Diamond Sword. “Hey, no harm.” said John and threw his stuff into the lava.  
“Is everything gone?” said Steve with a panicked tone. “Ye…umf!” said John as he got struck by an arrow.

Chapter 3 – Skeletons!

“Run!” Steve said and started running toward a house. “What is your name?” said Steve. “My name is John.” he said. “Where do you live?” said Steve as he opened the door and stepped inside his house. “I don’t want to tell you!” said John. “You summoned the skeletons so I would lead you to my house! I just know it!” said Steve. “I live on a crop farm on coordinates 35,40,79.” said John. “I had an old friend that lived on a crop farm on coordinates 35,40,91.” said Steve. “I used to live there, but creepers blew the farm up.” “The farm blew up when my friend was there too.” “Do you think? Yes, I think.” they said at the same time “Your that friend!” said steve with delight.  
The friends got into bed and woke up the next morning to a sad sight.

CHAPTER 4 – ON THE MOVE

When they woke, the front of the house was blown up “Who would do such a thing!” Steve cried “Almost every body.” said John “But why me!” said Steve “Because people in the overworld are downright mean.” said john “You always say that.” said Steve “I know.” said John. “well we should get moving.” said Steve “where?” asked John “North.” said Steve “but that’s the way I ca… owwww!” said John as an arrow pierced his arm  “skeletons!” shouted Steve “no,” said John “something worse.” “What is it!” yelled Steve. “There not skeletons.” said John “There pillagers, with crossbows.” “but what are they doing here?” asked Steve “probably going on another raid.” said John.  
“Well? What are we waiting for? RUN!” said Steve. All of a sudden white eyes were right in front of them then followed by somebody who looked just like Steve.

CHAPTER 5 – HEROBRINE

“Herobrine!” Steve said through gritted teeth “My worst night-mare!” who’s Harry-Bri-En?” asked John “No no no, it’s Hero-brine.”said Steve “Harry-brin?” asked John “That’s so weird.” “Uug!” And all of a sudden they were plunged into darkness.  
They woke in a strange land were it was dark and gloomy. The Ender Dragon roared overhead then John realized that they were in the end and that there was no escape. Suddenly John stopped moving and Steve thought he had gone AFK. But soon he realized  that John was being picked up by an Enderman.  
Steve tried to run and help but he was being picked up to! Then they teleported to the top of an obsidian pillar Herobrine was right in front of  them then Herobrine said “What were you two doing running across my lawn!” “we were running across MY lawn actually.” corrected Steve “NO IT WAS MY LAWN! EVERYWHERE IS MY LAWN! THE END, THE NETHER, AND EVEN THE OVERWORLD! HA, HA,HA,HAAAAAAA!” Then he disappeared leaving only a red smoldering mark on the dark obsidian.

CHAPTER 6 – THE END  

John stared  in horror as the mark became a hole and the obsidian under their feet started to melt. The Endermen teleported away and left John and Steve on the melting platform then they fell and fell and fell then everything went black.  
They woke in a pool of water with a person standing over them. He had letters over his head that said ‘E.S. MINE-WARP’. “How strange.” thought John “Hi, my name is E.s. Mine-warp what’s yours?” asked E.s. Mine-warp  
“Uh, uh, John, yeh John.” then everything went black.  
John woke up in a house by the edge of an end island. The house was made out of purpur. E.s. Mine-warp was standing over him once again. “Would you like some bacon?” asked E.s. Mine-warp “Whats ‘bacon’?” asked John “Oh its another word for ‘pork chop’. Sorry.” said E.s. Mine-warp “sure.” said John  
“So I was wondering, how do you tame an ender dragon?” E.s. Mine-warp  stopped  moving “what do you mean how do you tame an ender dragon?” Asked E.s. Mine-warp “you know, to keep it as a pet.” said John. E.s. Mine-warp did a triple sumer-salt in the air and landed on the ground like you would see in a comic book. “WHAT DO YOU MEAN ‘A PET’!” said E.s. Mine-warp “What!” said John “I want an ender Dragon as a pet, what’s wrong with that!” “Fine! I’ll tell you how to tame an ender Dragon on one condition,  don’t kill yourself.”

CHAPTER 7 – GETTING SUPPLIES

John, Steve, and E.s. Mine-warp went to get supplies for what they were about to do. First they needed to get some ender pearls. That wasn’t too hard because they were already in the End. The hard part was getting the gold there was no gold in the End. so they had to find a way to get back to the overworld to get the gold.  
They decided to for E.s. Mine-warp to jump into the void(since he was a player he would respawn in his house in the overworld) to get the gold.  
He came back later with a lot of gold, enough to make two of what they needed. They needed to make something called ‘The Ultimate Tamer’ it was crafted with two ender pearls (on the side) three gold (along the middle) and four obsidian blocks(one in each corner). They didn’t have the obsidian yet, that was because none of them had a diamond pickaxe. When they sent E.s. Mine-warp to get the gold they had him get a diamond pickaxe as well. They got to work on the obsidian as soon as Mine-warp got back. Soon they had The Ultimate Tamer up and running. Now all they had to do was tame the ender Dragon.

CHAPTER 8 – DANGER

E.s. Mine-warp told John that he had to hit the ender Dragon while he was riding it for it to be tamed. “Just remember, hit it with the curved end of the staff, not the sharp end.” said E.s. Mine-warp “ok.”said John “I can do this!” and then John jumped on to the ender dragon.

CHAPTER 9 – TAMING

John struck the dragon with the curved end of the tamer and the dragon gave a roar. Then it turned blue for just an instant, then it settled on the ground and waited. Then John said “Rise and fly in the air.” and the dragon rose and went soaring up in the dark sky.

John cried for joy as he flew through the air, “Now I have a pet dragon!” when they came to the ground E.s. Mine-warp said ” Let’s go home” “ok,” said John”everyone on the ender dragon! Ok ender Dragon bring us home.” “Ok,” said the ender Dragon ” and call me Tom.”

CHAPTER 10 – GOING HOME

Tom flew up into the air, gave a great roar and then plummeted. downward. All of a sudden they were in a forest biome with (of course) lots of trees. “Thanks for bringing us here Tom.” said Steve “Don’t thank me,” said Tom “I AM tamed after all.”  
When they got home, John and Tom were so tired that they lead down and fell asleep immediately.
