Minecraft Name: dwisawesomw

Story Submission:  
“FISHER!” Yelled Gamenight999 “Help me get this anvil off me!”  
Fisher rushed over to Gamenight and tried to pull the anvil. “I can’t’ I am not strong enough! I am just a Fisher.”  
Gamenight looked up and saw the look of defeat on Fisher’s face.  
‘Is this the end?’ thought Gamenight. ‘Is this really the end?’  
Suddenly Gamenight remembered something the Oracle told him long ago: “Imagine what you can accomplish…then do it!”  
“Fisher Imagine that you CAN do it. Believe that you CAN do it. I believe in you!”  
Fisher looked at him, a look of uncertainty on his face.”Do really believe in me?”  
“YES!”  
The expression on Fisher’s face changed from uncertainty to determination. He grabbed the anvil and pulled it hard enough for Gamenight to crawl out and stand up. Gamenight drew his sword and turned to glare at Erubus.  
“NO!” Screamed Erubus “WITHERS FIRE MORE RAPIDLY!”  
The withers circled around Gamenight.  
Gamenight readied his sword and charged.
