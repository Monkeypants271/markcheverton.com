The path crunched under Mithril’s feet, the sound of gravel creating a small crackle within Gameknight’s ears. It reminded him of eating a candy bar.  
“We’re almost there,” train2000c said. “Just a few more blocks.”  
“So,” Gameknight started. “What does Atlantis look like? I mean, does it have any villagers?”  
“It doesn’t really, but if you saw TheAtlanticCraft’s modded survival, you would probably recognize it.”  
Gameknight and train2000c went on until there was a break in the path. There were two signs, one that said Atlantis and the other that said Fum Carrock.  
“Fum Carrock?” started Gameknight. “What is that?”  
“Oh!” train2000c responded. “That’s a boss battle arena and a faction. It was built inside a terraformed volcano.”  
Gameknight turned towards the path that led to Atlantis and saw two Uruk-hai alongside a player looking NPC. Her name was Evil\_Little\_Kelly. Underneath her name, it said boss battle NPC.  
“Ha,” she laughed. “So you deleted my scout, well, it will be harder to defeat me.”  
“Wait,” train2000c said. “How does she know that?”  
“I don’t know, but can’t you delete her?” Gameknight asked.  
“Another Admin created her for a boss battle. I don’t know if it will affect the gameplay.”  
Evil\_Little\_Kelly took a deep breath and pointed her sword towards Gameknight. “The Maker wants me to bring you to him. I don’t want to put up a fight, though I know I would win”  
“I need to check something on the server source code, be right back.” said train2000c.  
In a few seconds, train2000c had left the game.  
“Ha,” said Evil\_Little\_Kelly laughing maniacally. “The great User-that-is-not-a-user is all alone. How do you feel now, still think you can defeat me.”  
“No,” Gameknight responded. “But I can try.”  
Gameknight tapped his horse and pulled out his mithril sword. He attacked Evil\_Little\_Kelly just as she pulled out her sword and blocked it. The two Uruks helped their leader by also attacking. Gameknight thrusted his sword eventually finding flesh in the Uruk.  
“You fight well,” said Evil\_Little\_Kelly. “But as I said, I don’t want a fight. Come here my Uruks, I’ll finish him.”  
She brought out a potion of weakness and a potion of blindness just to throw it at Gameknight. It splashed and all Gameknight could see was blackness. The two Uruks grabbed him and his horse and walked away.  
“The Maker will be pleased.” Evil\_Little\_Kelly said to no one, to everyone, to the entire world.
