I couldn’t wait to play the violin because the teacher’s was so good at playing it when she showed us the instruments at school. I picked the violin  because I thought it was the perfect instrument. I was so excited about it in the morning before leaving school.  
All morning while at school, I couldn’t hold my excitement for the orchestra meeting at the end of the day. I waited and waited, thinking about the violin that I was going to choose. After P.E, it was finally time to go meet with the orchestra teacher, Mrs. T, so I could finally tell her my choice. I thought about how proud my mom would be about me choosing the violin. When I got to the auditorium, I saw the violins on the side of the room and I knew it had to be mine. Mrs. T went through and showed us all of the instruments, the violin was the best.  
I waited patiently, until Mrs. T finally got to me and asked what instrument I wanted, I replied excitedly “the violin please”. She said of course, that would be perfect for you. I couldn’t believe that I was finally getting the violin. We left the meeting and all I could think about was going home and telling my mom what happened.  
A few days later, I was able to bring the violin home to start practicing for my lessons. I pulled it out of the case and my little brother, Zuko, came over to see what I was doing. I was a little nervous because he is only 1 year old. I didn’t want him to break anything. He walked over and looked at the shiny pieces. I put the violin together and plucked  in it the way that Mrs. T showed us. It made a loud noise that scared my brother. We laughed and then I called my mom over so that she could see the violin. She reminded me to be gentle with it because she didn’t want to have to buy another one. I put the violin away and placed it with my things so I didn’t forget to bring it to school to the next day.  
That night, I continued to play with my brother Zuko and thought about how happy I was to finally have the instrument that I love.
