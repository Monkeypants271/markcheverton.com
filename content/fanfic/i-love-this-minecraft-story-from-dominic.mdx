## **The Battle Between Us**

A minecraft story

### By Dominic L.

Chapter one(The Beginning)

                 It is a normal day in the village. The sun is just rising the wind is blowing. Farmers are harvesting the crops and the animals are making loud noises. In a very small house in a very large village a boy named Mark starts his day like normal.

“Mark come on, you need to help dad with the harvest,” Mark’s sister screamed.

“But…” Mark yelled.

“ You need to do it,” Mark’s sister said loudly.

“Fine,”Mark replied.

Mark got a white shirt, black pants, brown boots and put them on. Mark walked out the door and went to the farm. Mark started working on  his side of the farm.

Chapter two(Wall Down)

Mark finished working and was walking down the path to go to the market.

“MARK OVER HERE!” A mysterious man yelled. Mark looked around and saw the blacksmith.

“Mark follow me.” Mark slowly followed the blacksmith through the alley and passed the dojo, where they train martial arts, and headed for the armory. Mark and the blacksmith go in. Mark was going to grab a bow… but the blacksmith slapped Mark’s hand

“OW!” Mark yelled.

“Grab a sword,Mark.” Mark grabbed a stone sword.

“Hit the target.” Mark swung… the sword flew out of his hands and _SLICE,_ the target’s head fell off.

“Whoa kid! You’re a rookie with the sword!”

“No I’m not, Actually yeah I am.” Mark grabbed a bow and a couple arrows. Mark pulled the string on the bow and _FLICK_…SMACK right in the head. Mark aimed the bow and arrow at the next target. Mark pulled the string _FLICK_ and _BOOM_ the arrow flew out the window _SMASH._

_“_What was that!” Mark said. Mark ran outside and saw smoke coming from the north wall. Mark heard the village’s people saying, “Raiders!” While screaming and crying in pain.

Chapter three(The Fight Begins)

Mark ran to the west sniper tower and climbed the ladder. The sniper had an arrow through the head. Mark pulled the string on the bow _FLICK… FLICK… FLICK…_ the arrows flew and hit a group of raiders. A raider spotted Mark and threw lighted TNT at the tower… _BOOM!_ the tower started to fall. Mark jumped off and crashed onto the roof of a house. _CRASH!_ Mark got up in pain, blood was all around him. Mark started to crawl. Mark finally crawled out the door and he got back up angrily. All of a sudden… _BOOM… BOOM… BOOM…_ Mark ran to the middle of the village. All the raiders died and Mark saw two guys that looked a little bit older Mark.

Chapter four(New Friends Worst Enemy)

“Who are you?” Mark yelled and the two guys turned around.

“I’m Bubba.” The left guy stepped up he had brown hair and a robotic arm. He was wearing a black shirt, black pants, and brown boots. In his bloody and torn hands was a purple glowing iron sword and Mark saw a flint and steel in his pocket sticking out. The right guy stepped up,

“I’m Nate.” He had blond hair and a mask over his face. He was wearing a white shirt, black and white pants, and black boots. In his hands was an iron axe. Mark looked at the village burning away. Then Mark started to think about his family. Mark started to run to his house. Mark smashed the door open and looked around…

“MOM, DAD, SIS!!” Mark yelled.

“MARK, IN THE BUNKER!,” Mark’s dad yelled. Mark ran to the opening of the bunker, and grabbed a chunk of burning wood that had fallen earlier during the explosion. He threw the burning chunk of wood out the shattered window.

“Back up,” Bubba said roughly. Mark walked back, Bubba smashed the bunker door with his robotic arm.

Mark and Bubba jumped in.

“All of you out now or the building will come down on us,” Bubba yelled. They all ran out… _CRASH BANG…_ Bubba looked at the sky and it was dark.

“Oh no,” Bubba whispered.

“What?” Nate whispered back.

“Kask,” Bubba whispered. Bubba saw Kask flying in the air and grabbed his sword and Nate grabbed his axe.

“Hello brother,” Kask yelled to Bubba.

“You shouldn’t be here,” Bubba yelled. A wave of silence gone in the air. Mark pulled out his bow and had it ready. The flint and steel in Bubba’s pocket started glowing purple. Kask had a flint and steel glowing red in his hands…

Chapter five(The Battle Between Brothers)

“Run, all of you RUN!” Bubba yelled. Mark, Nate and the survivors all started to run. Bubba put away his sword and took the flint and steel out, _FLICK_ a spray of purple fire hit Kask and he fell to the ground. Kask grabbed his diamond sword as well as Bubba did the same thing. The two ran at each other and with a big crash. They swung their swords _CLANK CLANK CLANK. CLANK_ Bubba’s sword shattered as it hit Kask in the head. Kask kicked Bubba really hard, Kask eyes filled with anger. Bubba flew and crashed robotic arm first into the wall. Bubba’s robotic arm was smashed and Bubba fell to the ground.

Chapter six(The Magic Diamond)

Nate looked back.

“BUBBA!” Nate yelled. Nate ran back to Bubba. Kask disappeared with red particles left behind.

“You ok?” Nate whispered.

“Yeah but my robotic arm isn’t,” Bubba whispered back. Bubba’s robotic arm was shattered in half, one half on the ground the other still connected to him. Nate grabbed a glowing green diamond and gave it to Bubba. Mark looked back and stopped running and ran back to Bubba and Nate. The magical diamond healed Bubba. Bubba gave it back to Nate.

“WOAH!” Mark yelled.

“Not to loud,” Nate whispered.

“Ok,” Mark replied. But it didn’t heal his robotic arm.

“MARK!” Mark’s sister yelled.

“SIS!” Mark yelled back.

“UUGGGGGHHHH!” Multiple moaning voices screamed . Bubba got up.

“WHERE IS MY FLINT AND STEEL!” Bubba yelled worried.

“Why?” Everyone said confused…

Chapter seven(Defense of The Village)

“ZOMBIES!” Bubba yelled. An alarm went off and screams went up in the air with terror. There was banging on the other side of the wall. About three zombies went straight into Bubba. Part of the wall snapped and zombies came pouring out.

“LOOK OUT!” Nate yelled to Bubba. Bubba turned around and jabbed what’s left of his robotic arm in the first zombie’s head. A zombie pushed Bubba to the ground right next to a burning house a stick fell right in his hands… Bubba threw the stick and hit the zombie in the gut. The zombie fell on the other zombie. Mark pulled the string to his bow… _SMACK_ in the head. Bubba got back up then all three realized they were surrounded by zombies!

“Oh no,” Bubba whispered.

“I’m out of arrows,” Mark said.

“Go ahead of me I’ll hold them off and I’ll be right behind you guys,” Bubba yelled.

“See you on the other side Bubba,” Nate yelled. Nate and Mark ran off the other way. More and more zombies came Bubba was smacking zombies in the face while still running. Then Bubba ran to the left and another left. Some zombies cut off Bubba and all of a sudden… all the zombies died! Bubba disappeared and popped right in front of Mark and Nate.

“What in the world?” Mark screamed. Mark and Nate ran into Bubba.

“AHHH!” Bubba yelled. All three got back up and stared into the horizon.

“We have a long journey ahead of us,” Bubba said…

Chapter eight(The Journey Begins)

Bubba, Nate, and Mark left the village walking into the deep dark forest. They had grabbed more items before they left.

“This forest is called the Forest Of Shadows, whoever enters is never seen again. There’s been a myth about a diamond about six feet tall and about four feet wide is in this forest. “Almost all the best adventurers died or lost in this monstrous forest,” Bubba whispered. All three looked around still walking. Mark saw a sign it said, “Beware a dark force is lurking around in the shadows.”

About six hours of walking they were going in a massive building covered in vines and moss.

“Have you ever heard of H-H-Herobrine?” Mark whispered.

“Yes,” Bubba whispered.

“The king of the underworld, banished from the overworld,” Bubba replied. They were now entering the massive building. Mark looked around and saw a light source.

“Is that a… sea lantern?” Mark whispered. They walked into the enormous room. There was mos and vines everywhere! The sea lanterns are as bright as the sun.

“Whoa!” Mark said. They were now in the middle of the room. _CRACK…_ _CRUNCH…_ the floor fell beneath them. They all landed in the End Portal…

Chapter nine(The End…)

“AHHHH!” Bubba yelled. _SMACK… SMACK… SMACK…_ they got up with terror on their faces.

“The end!” Mark whispered.

“The Ender Dragon!” Nate screamed. It flew straight at them like an angry bull. It was eating the end stone as it hit them. They flew in the air and _SMACK_ as they hit the ground.

“Mark use your bow, Nate distract it I’m going for the head,” Bubba shouted. Bubba ran to a tower and climbed it. The dragon noticed Bubba and flew towards him. Bubba stopped climbing and jumped right on the dragon’s back. Bubba stabbed the dragon in the head. The dragon knocked Bubba off and bit him. Bubba grabbed a stone sword and stabbed the dragon in the eye. A huge roar shook the End. The dragon threw Bubba out of his mouth off the End.

“BUBBA!” Nate cried out. There was a green flash and it was like a flash bang. The dragon hit the ground and a bedrock exit appeared with the End Egg on top.

“When can we go?” Mark said. Nate didn’t reply.

“Now,” Nate whispered. Stepping in the exit portal a arm clinged to the end stone layer…

Chapter ten(Destruction Of The Overworld)

Stepping out of the exit portal they saw their world in flames!

“This is…” Nate cried.

“What happened?” Mark asked. Monsters everywhere zombies, skeletons, spiders, and creepers.

“We need to fight, but with what?” Mark whispered.

“What do you have to craft?” Nate replied.

“Wood, stone, my bow, flint, iron, sticks, feathers, sting, and the dragon egg.” Mark replied back.

“I have diamonds, my axe wait… diamonds plus sticks we could make some diamond axes and a sword,” Nate said with joy. Mark gave Nate the sticks and Nate crafted the sword and axes. _BOOM_.

“WHAT WAS THAT?!” Mark screamed. A shadow shaped man with glowing white eyes flew across the air.

“Is that Herobrine?” Mark whispered…

Chapter eleven(The Final Battle)

Mark got his bow and arrow out and pulled the string _FLICK_. The arrow stopped in the middle of the air and launched back at Mark! Mark jumped out of the arrow’s path and ran to Nate.

“What do we do?” Mark screamed. Nate gave the sword to Mark and pulled the axes out of his inventory. They charged into the field of monsters, swinging their weapons like mad men. In the background a portal opened up. Nate built a dirt tower as Mark put ladders on it. The black shadowy man flew towards them and said, “Y0U D0N’1 TH1NK 1 CAn S1op Y0U!” The shadowy figure screamed. Mark jumped on the figure and stabbed it in the back. It screeched which almost made Mark’s ears bleed! Stabbing It in the back It kicked Mark off, Mark hit the ground with a _SMACK_! Blood covered Mark’s left leg and surrounded it. Mark crawled behind a tree as Nate flew into another tree.

“I think this is Herobrine, also he has bad grammar,” Mark thought. The shadowy figure screamed with pain as Mark looked up he saw Bubba attacking it. As the figure falls to the ground switching it’s form then it exploded. Bubba ran over to Mark and picked him helped him up. Bubba opened up a portal and said, “ let’s go home.” Nate ran through first.

“Let’s go-o home,” Mark smiled. They walked through the portal and disappeared…
