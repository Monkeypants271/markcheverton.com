**Diary of a Friendly Herobrine**

Chapter 1

Most people know me as Herobrine, the “ruthless, evil villain.” In reality, I’m just an unlucky Steve. What you don’t know is that every time a player deletes their account, their avatars remain in the game, except with a mind of their own. We can change up our skin and name as much as we want. I used to be MonterSlayer200, with an ender dragon-themed skin, but I wanted a simple skin, so I just turned myself into a default skin and username. My friend, Max, did the same. Me and Max had many great adventures, such as winning a battle against a full diamond player. We were only full iron (my iron sword was sharpness 2). We also explored an ocean monument and went to the nether. But one discovery would change our lives…

Chapter 2

One day, I went on an adventure in the forest. I found a strange gold block, redstone torch, netherrack, and lava structure. It was covered in vines and appeared to be very old. As I stepped near it, my vision began to distort, and I felt a jolt of power rush through me. Then I fainted.

When I woke up, I was in a strange, iron block building. I saw a villager in a lab coat standing in front of the cage I was in. “MWHAHAHA! I WILL COMBINE THIS POOR, HELPLESS PLAYER WITH A ZOMBIE, CREEPER, SKELETON, AND ENDER DRAGON! I WILL CREATE AN UNSTOPPABLE EVIL MONSTER!” the villager said. I spotted various hostile mobs in other cages. Just as the villager/mad crazy scientist was about to press a lever (most likely, the lever does horrible things to me), I felt a jolt of energy and power. Then, there was a huge explosion.

Next thing I knew, the entire lab, mobs, cages, evil villagers and all, disintegrated. “Wow! What just happened? I’m still alive! Yay!” I said to no one in particular. I looked below me. There was a huge crater right underneath me, leading all the way down to bedrock! And I noticed I was floating right above it! Whatever that structure was, it gave me lots of powers.

I imagined myself pressing F5 on a keyboard (I do that to see what my skin looks like). I half expected to be wearing a superman cape, but, nope, it was just regular old Steve. But something was not right. I had pure, glowing white eyes!
