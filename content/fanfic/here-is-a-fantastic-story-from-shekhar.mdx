“Steve vs. Ender-dragon”  
by A. Shekhar

One day Steve needed to kill the Ender-dragon for it was destined to kill him! But Steve  
needed to make an end-portal. But he had the portals but he needed two more ender-eyes.  
So, he went to the Nether with his enchanted diamond armor, enchanted bow, and sword.  
He fought two blazes to get blaze powder because he already had two eyes. He went back  
and lit his portal.  
He went in a small little island floating in a giant blue ball. There were endermen  
everywhere and he had to get past them to destroy the healers. The healers healed the  
dragon so it won’t die. Steve saw the dragon. It looked fierce, strong and menacing. It  
lunged down at him and Steve attacked! The ender-dragon went up in the air and faced  
him looking enraged that he had been hit.  
The ender-dragon lunged again. But Steve didn’t wait. He was ready to jump onto the  
dragon. He jumped onto the dragon and attacked it without being attacked back. He did  
this for a long time, until the dragon finally swooped down and threw Steve down onto  
the land. Steve was swarmed with ender-men. But he soon found the dragon again. He hit  
it with his bow. And the dragon looked at him and lunged again. But Steve again jumped  
onto it but the dragon knew how to throw him off. It threw him off but Steve with his  
enchanted bow attacked again.  
The ender-dragon finally screeched and all of the ender-men started to walk toward  
Steve. But Steve seeing the dragon finally used his bow for one last shot and this shot  
was fatal to the dragon. Quickly Steve jumped back into the portal and lived on. What  
other dangers will he encounter in the future? …

The End
