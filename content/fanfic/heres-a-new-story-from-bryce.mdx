### Phredric’s Adventure

#### Bryce H.

Phredric was a nice young man who loved to explore. His parents were farmers and owned a 150-Acre lot, much of which had never been explored. One day, Phredric bought a golf cart. He had been saving up for years, and he finally had something to ride in while he explored. Phredric started it up and drove a long way. Past the barn, past the crops, and into the woods. After about half an hour of driving, he came across a graveyard. When he got out, he looked closer and saw this was a graveyard from the 1700’s! He kept on walking and came across an old Victorian mansion! He couldn’t believe his luck! “I’m gonna go look in that house!” Phredric said. He creeped up to it and opened the door….

KABLAMO! There was an explosion inside the house! Zombies started creeping out of the debris. Phredric ran to his golf cart. He quickly found his automatic dart gun that he’d packed just in case, and started firing darts at the zombies! The zombies seemed immune to this and actually gained speed when a dart hit one! Phredric stomped on the gas pedal and zoomed out of there! The zombies were as fast as the golf cart and ran after it! Phredric saw his barn and got off the golf cart and ran into it! He saw the horse and got on it, putting his helmet on. He raced out of the barn on the horse and started trampling zombies! When the zombies got trampled, a purple light source sucked them into the sky. Phredric had won! He went home and decided not to tell anyone about this…..

THE END!
