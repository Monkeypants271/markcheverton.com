I went on to my favorite Minecraft survival world. Boy lets see what this command block, command can do I thought. I was testing another command block command; I liked testing lame command block commands ones that were supposed to send you into Minecraft. So I used the command to get me a command block and I placed it down and pasted the stupid command. I activated it and next thing I knew I was in big gust of wind that was sucking me towards my computer! When I opened my eyes and looked around I saw blocky trees and a blocky house behind me. I’M IN MINECRAFT! I thought. I was freaking out I couldn’t believe I was in Minecraft. I stuck my hand in my inventory and pulled out my iron sword. Don’t ask me how I did because I have no clue. Maybe the villagers can help me I thought. So I jumped on my horse and rode to the village I live near. When I got there I tried asking for help but they kept their arms crossed and just went Huh when I asked them a question. Maybe if I show them I’m not a user they’ll help me I thought. So I went to the village well and shouted “Everyone look I can prove I’m not a user.” They all looked at me. I thought But how do I show them I’m not a user and that I’m actually in the game. So I decided to hit my extra iron chestplate with my sword.
