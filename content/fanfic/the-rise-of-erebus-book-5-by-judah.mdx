**The Rise of Erebus book 5 by Judah**

“Are we there yet?” asked Blaster.  

“Almost,” said Watcher he had been a little shaken up after Planters abduction but now he felt stronger than ever. He would find This Gameknight999, he would defeat Erebus, rescue Planter, and restore peace and prosperity to the far lands of Minecraft but first, he needed to find Gameknight999. Finally, a portal came into view instead of the purple Nether portal or the void black End portal this one was sky blue it looked comforting 

“Everybody ready,” said a booming voice Cutter ran up to them panting with sweat. “I just checked the border and its safe to say we’re in the clear are you ready” 

“as ready as ready can be,” said Watcher “I’m ready to end this war”

 “so am i” said Fencer running up to them. “I am ready to get this over with”. 

“come on,” said Winger “we should get going.” 

“Just a minute,” said Watcher and he took out the Eye of Searching and put it on. Planter appeared but instead of her tower, she was back in the End. Lady Char was fuming. 

“I told them not to complete the ritual but they didn’t listen  Malacoda is  dead, my possessor told that boy wizard information that should be kept a secret, and I’m blamed for everything how could things get any worse.” Just then a Endermen teleported to her and said 

“Erebus is convincing Endermen to join him and the Shulkers are gone and one Ender Dragon has joined.” 

“WHAT” bellowed Lady Char and she started to transform she whipped of her cloak and began to transform from the already terrifying woman she was Lady Char became the most terrifying person Watcher had ever seen. “You Shall die” She hissed and snapped her fingers. Immediately a skeleton appeared and shot an arrow at Planter. Blood dripped from Planters leg and the Endermen Flashed red and disappeared leaving behind an Ender pearl and some XP(experience points). Now said Lady Char changing back into her regular form i have got a plan. I shall paint some TNT grey and say its bedrock and it will explode and it Will Kill Erebus ones and for all. And Lady Char laughed wickedly. She then said “wizards are made out of stars and i shall get those stars for there are omens that the stars have the power of animals, blocks, mods, the void  and servers in the pyramid of servers that include the server where the Withers are trapped and i can free Krale, Kora, the phantoms and the weird monsters. “Yes My lady” one of the Endermen said.

“Good” said Lady Char”now go.”Then dozens of telaportion particles floated around as the Endermen disappeared one by one until there was no won left. Then Lady Char smiled and said “goodbye boy wizard.” Watcher took of the eye of searching and was about to explain what had happened When someone said “ZOMBIES”. Watcher ran away from the portal screaming FOR MINECRAFT . And just like that the battle began.

Watcher charged into battle and leaped at a gigantic Zombie and slashed at him with his sword the Zombie did the same to him in turn. The two swords met shower of sparks Watcher slashed at a weak spot but the Zombie blocked him with his shield and began a series of  vicious attacks of his own. Watcher blocked each of them but he was getting tired. “Everyone to the portal” Watcher bellowed. Soon all the NPCS stopped fighting and ran as fast as they could towards the portal.Watcher kept fighting the Zombies hopping that he could stall the Zombies until all the villagers left through the portal. When the last NPC was through Watcher too ran as fast as he could towards the portal. Finally he made it. Watcher looked at the Zombies and stepped into the unknown
