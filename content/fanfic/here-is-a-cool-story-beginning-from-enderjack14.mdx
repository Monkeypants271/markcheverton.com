Fear clouding his mind he walked in the room standing before him were the 4 horsemen of the apocalypse. Xa-Tul the king of Zombies, Feyd the king of Endermen, Charybdis the king of Blazes and Reaper the king of Skeletons. They were all talking, Gameknight999 ducked behind the cold block of stone. Gameknight999 could hear them talking, ” The crafter and a handful of NPCs escaped through the minecart network but the rest perished” Charybdis wheseed in a evil voice.” Good,” said Feyd smiling ” We will leave now and search for The-user-that-is-not-a-user an obliterate him.”

Once they had left Gameknight999 stood up an looked around the humongous crafting chamber that was mostly destroyed, the ground was littered with items from fallen NPCs. Gameknight999 felt like it was his fault for not being there to defend them against the vile creatures. He had to find Crafter and the survivors quickly. Gameknight999 put his hand in the air and squished his hand into a fist really hard, this was a salute to the dead. He closed his eyes and thought about the horror the villagers must have been put through. He brought his fist down and opened his eyes, he darted to the rails. He dived in the minecart and rode away intent on finding Crafter.
