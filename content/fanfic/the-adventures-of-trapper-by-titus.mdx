## The Adventures of Trapper

“Suddenly, we found ourselves lost in the wilderness without any village in sight. Hello, my name is Trapper and I am a villager. I am with my cat Mittens here in what seems to be a Mega Taiga Biome. “Wait,” I said confused, “I knew if we spoke or used our hands in front of a user we would become one of the lost, but I didn’t know that we would be teleported away so we would become quite literally lost!?!?” Back to the introduction, in my village, I was the one and only trapper (and trappers are very rare). Even though trappers and hunters are different, most of them are the same, except me. I don’t only trap animals, I also like to troll fellow villagers, hostile mobs, and even users!  
   “This feels a lot like a book that I’ve read before. What’s the name of that book?” After thinking for what felt like an eternity, I realized that It was a book that a user wrote. Did it call it a journal? Then I felt something tugging on my pant leg, I looked down and saw Mittens pointing his paw at the dirt. I gasped then congratulated my cat, he drew a picture of the book, and it was 3D! I went to pull out my flask of water from my side pocket then I saw the most fearsome mob in all of Minecraft, Herobrine. Herobrine is one of the many shadow crafters in Minecraft, he is the creator of evil heroes and hostile mobs.  
   Not only is he the strongest shadow crafter, it is almost impossible to kill him. So without a second thought, I started to run as fast as I could in the opposite direction. After running for two or three miles, I tripped over a tripwire and flung into a jungle temple! I can’t believe I’m thinking this but sadly I think someone else is here other than me and my cat. Then I heard it, the deafened footsteps of someone on the floor below me. I blindly crawled to a corner and hid there. Then I saw a monstrous shadow headed for the stairs from the dim light of a lantern. It looked like the zombie king so I started pleading for it to spare me, and then I got hit by two arrows and everything went black.  
   I woke up to Mittens somehow repeatedly slapping me in the face with his paw. “I’m up I’m up,” I said to Mittens with a smile on my face, “Are you okay?” asked what looked like a user with a child wearing a zombie head on his shoulders and with no server thread! “I am the user that is not a user,” announced the user “We’re sorry for startling you. And we need your help, there’s a village in the distance that needs our help. Are you willing to help us save the village?” Asked the user that is not a user. “Yes,” I responded with newfound confidence. “Great so let’s do this!” Exclaimed what looked like a crafter.  
   After running for a bit through the jungle and then into a desert, we found a village. And to our luck, it would be covered in lava in a few minutes! “We need to build a platform above the village that is big enough so the lava won’t hit the village.” I said quickly, “you are a smart kid,” said the crafter. “You heard the kid, we need to start building a platform above the village. Let’s go, let’s go!” Ordered the user that is not a user. “Who are you talking to?” I asked the user that is not a user. “Why don’t you just look behind us and see.” He responded with a smile. As I looked behind me I saw the biggest group of warrior villagers I had ever seen in my life! “Wow,” I said, astonished. “For Minecraft!” yelled the user that is not a user and now I know his name is Gameknight999. “For Minecraft!” all of the villagers repeated as we ran towards the village. Luckily, we had just barely enough (non-flammable) blocks to finish the platform and save the village! And that’s how I became the Trapper of this village.” I explained to the village children. “Thank you for your time and the next villager is right over there.” I said pointing to the house across the street, “No thank you.” As I turned around I saw that it was the Crafter from this village, Gameknight999 (the user that is not a user), and the crafter from Gameknight’s village. “It’s okay,” I responded with a smile. “Bye Mr. Trapper!” The kids said, “Bye” I responded with my heart filled with joy. “Those kids like you a lot” said Gameknight, “Yep, I’m starting to like them too.” I responded with a smile.

The end…for now.
