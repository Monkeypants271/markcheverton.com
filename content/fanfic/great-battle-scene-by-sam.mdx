## Battle In The Golden Palace

It was morning on a cold workday. TIber was using his hammer to get gold from  
a mine for his boss to give to Yeqon.  
“ How long do we have left?” he asked his slavefriend Kipper.  
“About 15 hours left.” Kipper replied. Tiber and Kipper are only two of the people  
who have to mine for Yeqon… There are hundreds. Only a few of Yeqon’s friends don’t  
have to mine in his underground city. Plus, if you’re not a slave you’re one of Yeqon’s  
guards.  
“What are you two doing?” Their boss asked them.  
“We’re talking,” they both replied instantly.  
“Get back to work!”  
After hours of working Tiber walked home. He opened his freezer and pulled out  
a sandwich. He took one bite before his door swung open.  
“We need you to come with us,” they said. They took him to a place with cold  
cells and people. He put Tiber in one with someone with a hood on.  
“Um, hello,” Tiber said.  
“Do you have anything with you?”  
“Well, yes…”  
“What is it?”  
Tiber pulled out his sandwich and his hammer. The person grabbed the hammer  
and started smacking the cage wildly. Tiber took a bite of his sandwich and then threw it  
out the cage. In a few moments the cage was open and the person ran out.  
“Are you coming?” the person who now took off their hood. It was a girl with  
bright red hair. She grabbed some of the weapons from the rack.  
“Come on we have to defeat Yeqon!” Tiber jumped out and grabbed a knife.  
They then started for Yeqon’s golden castle. As they walked, the shadows slowly crept  
up the streets of the city.  
“Let’s wait here,” she said quietly.”It’ll be easier to travel in the dark, there’s less  
guards out then.”  
They waited till 12:00 when only a few guards were left in the dark of the night.  
They walked quickly to the entrance of the golden palace. Suddenly the door opened  
and a few of Yeqon’s guards ran out with swords and armor. Kling! Kling! Klang! Ow!  
The girl stabbed them and threw them in a river with crocodiles and alligators.  
“Just so you know, my name is Linna.”  
“Ok, mines Tiber,” he replied.  
They walked through the long hallway of Yeqon’s Golden castle. When they got  
to the throne room they were instantly attacked by some guards. Linna got beat and  
was taken somewhere.  
“Go get that guy.” Yeqon said to a person in another room. A person walked in  
and replied,” Ok sir.”  
I know that voice, Tiber thought. That’s Kipper.  
“Kipper,” Tiber yelled.  
“Oh Hi.”  
“Shut up!” Yeqon said, glaring at Kipper. His face was as red as a big plump  
tomato. Tiber walked up to Yeqon.  
“You need to stop!” Tiber yelled, slowly Kipper walking toward Tiber. He  
suddenly grabbed Tiber and two guards helped him.  
“Why’d you do that?” Tiber asked Kipper.  
“ Cause Yeqon will torture me if I don’t.” the guards carried him the same way  
they carried Linna. When they stopped there was a boiler where they boil the gold into  
things.  
“Any last words.” the guards asked him.  
“Yes and they are…” before he could say anything they pushed him in…  
It felt like he was falling for years but just before he hit the boiling gold a big rock  
from a slingshot slammed into him and he landed on a ledge.  
When he woke up Kipper was standing right above him.  
“I thought you were gonna turn on me!” Tiber said to Kipper.  
“I realized that I could just run cause he’s a fat slug who always sits.”  
“Thanks!” Tiber said. They started running, determined to defeat Yeqon.  
They didn’t care what was in their way they just killed it and ran off.  
When they got to the throne room Tiber said, “We’re here to defeat you.” The air  
was hot with all the steam coming out of Tiber’s ears. He had a sword in hand and was  
ready to fight. Kipper had a shield and a sword and threw it at Yeqon. He screamed  
when it hit his leg, blood pouring out. Then Yeqon’s number 1 guard came in. He had  
one of those spiky swords.  
Kipper ran at the guard and they started the fight. Tiber was sprinting at Yeqon  
with his sword ready to kill him. But instead of stabbing him he started pushing his  
throne towards the boiler.  
Suddenly he heard a scream and it was Kipper. He had been stabbed and then  
thrown in the boiler.  
“Noooooooooo!” Tiber screamed. But then out of nowhere Linna ran out and  
killed the guard.  
“Linna, I thought you died.” Tiber said.  
“No I just jumped over the boiler and they thought I died.” Linna replied.  
“Well anyway, let’s go get him!” But before they left Yeqon was gone. He went  
into hiding somewhere in the city.  
“Man we missed him.” Linna said. They started walking out of the castle to go  
somewhere to prepare for the next sneak attack.  
“We’ll get you next time!”
