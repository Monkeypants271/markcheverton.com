Catvega2000 was happy to Gameknight999, but she was even more happy to see that the monster kings were good. They turned from their evil ways and now were willing to repair the damages that they and Herobrine caused.

Cat turned ans slowly aprroached Xa-Tul. She looked up at the massive zombie, then did the impossible.  
She wrapped her arms around his giant waist. A look of surprise covered Xa-Tul’s face. He had never felt such a heart-warming feeling. Herobrine definatly wouldn’t have given him the love and respect that all creatures deserved.

After she was done hugging Xa-Tul, she did the same to the rest of the kings, opening them up to the feeling of warmth and friendship.

CRACK!!!  
Suddenly a bolt of lightning shot right out of the clear blue sky.  
“How can this be?” Crafter asked Gameknight.  
“I don’t know but it can’t mean anything good,” he responded.

The bolt shot right in the middle of the village, nearly killing everyone, well that was close enough anyways. There, before them was a deep hole. The villagers began to draw near the the hole. Peering down inside it, they saw a figure… with white glowing eyes!

“No, it can’t be, i thought he was gone?” Gameknight said, fear nibbling at his courage.
