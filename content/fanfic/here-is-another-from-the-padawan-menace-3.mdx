Herobrine’s plan- PART 1  
Everything was black and swirling. Gameknight couldn’t see a thing. He could hear stuff his friends, Crafter, Hunter, Stitcher, and Digger have said in the past. He heard stuff like “It’s sad that everything has to end.” and “This isn’t a game.” But he also heard stuff that he never heard, like, “Bring Gameknight99…” and “This must be serious.” But, soon the blackness cleared away and he found himself in the Crafting Chamber. Numerous NPC villagers were using crafting benches like no user had ever seen. In the middle of the huge room, Crafter, a young NPC, was standing. He had a worried look on his face. Gameknight tried to step forward, but he couldn’t. Just then, The Crafting Chamber disappeared, and Gameknight found himself tied up to a wall of bedrock. In front of him stood Herobrine, the most evil virus. Herobrine puled out his diamond sword and put it sideways in front of Gameknight’s neck. “You have messed with me for the LAST TIME, User-that-is-not-a-user.” Herobrine said as his sword got closer to cutting Gameknight’s neck.
