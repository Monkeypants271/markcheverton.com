Chapter 1

The Castle

”Hey StarShine10,what are you doing?” Greenlighting asked.

”Just building a castle.  Want to help?”

“Sure.” GreenLightning said.

“I need help building the flag.” StarShine10 said.

“Okay.” GreenLightning said.

”Thanks.” StarShine10 said.  “I need help building with the stone too,” she added.

“Will you come to the End with me?  I have a mission to kill the Ender Dragon.  But I need your help because the Ender Dragon might kill me!”

“Oookay.” GreenLightning said neverously.  ”Let’s go to the End.”

”Okay I’ll be brave.”

”I’m glad you will be brave, but why do you need to  go to the End?”

“Well,I need to get an Ender Dragon  head.”

”Why do you need a Dragon  head?” GreenLighting asked.

”Because this guy needs it.'”

“Well, why does he need the Dragon head?” GreenLighting  asked.

“I don’t know, but when we get there he might tell us.”

“Well  .  .  .  okay.” GreenLighting said.

“Green Lighting,why did you sound neverous when you said okay?”

“Well,I don’t like the word kill.”

”Then I won’t say K-I-L-L any more.”

“Okay that would be good.” GreenLighting said. “Hey StarShine10 thanks!  And also you’re a very good friend to me.”

“And do you know what else I need to go to the End for?”

“No.  What?”

“Because I need to get some of the endstone too.”

“Oh, okay.” Green Lighting said.

“Well let’s go now, okay GreenLighting?”

“okaaaaay.”

“Alright Green Lighting, let’s go.”

“Okay let’s go!” Green Lighting yelled.  “Hey Star Shine10,there’s a forest up ahead,” Green Lighting said.  “I hate forests because there are monsters in forests.”

“Its okay I’ll keep you safe.”

“Thanks a lot StarShine10.”

“Your welcome GreenLighting.”

“Ahh!  There’s a creeper over there!”

“Run Green Lighting!” Star Shine10 yelled.  “Come on into this cave!”

“Ahh!  A skeleton!”Star Shine10 yelled.

“Run Star Shine10!” Green Lighting yelled.  “Come on up in this tree.  Good.  We’re safe up here.”

“Hey Green Lighting, there’s the house I was looking for.”

GreenLightning and StarShine10 went into the house.

“Hello StarShine10.” The man said. “I am Ben.”

“Hi Ben.  “StarShine10 replied.  “Do you have the command block?”

“WHAT!?!” GreenLighting yelled.  “He’s gonna give you a command block!?”
