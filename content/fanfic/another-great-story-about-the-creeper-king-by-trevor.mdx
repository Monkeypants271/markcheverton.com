## The Adventure of the Diamond Creeper

### Creeper King Edition

One sunny day in the village when Diamond Creeper was trading with villager #7 for red  
stone and arrows, he was making a defense tower that shot arrows with a click of a button.  
After that he went to the library. While he was looking, he saw a dusty book so he  
pulled it out and it looked like it had rolled around in dust. He read it “The Legend of the  
Creeper King.”  
Diamond Creeper flipped open the cover and blew on the page because of the dust.  
Diamond creeper read the text and it said, “Beware the driest desert in the darkest temple  
there lives the king of creepers.”  
Villager #5 came over and said, “Don’t read that you’ll get nightmares.”  
“Jerk,” Diamond Creeper said. Diamond Creeper went back to reading the book and  
saw a picture. Next to it had a caption, it said, “The creeper king was a fierce warrior and ruler  
of creepers don’t let the size fool you.”  
Then a note caught his eye it said, “Where the book was found, look behind there. You  
will find a little friend.” Suddenly a pig jumped out at him ! He sighed in relief. But then  
Diamond Creeper heard explosions. “Not again… creepers,” Diamond Creeper whined.  
Then he heard multiple villagers shout, “It’s an assault on the village!”  
Diamond Creeper said to Ruben, “Come on.” B OOOOM !  
There was a small silence then Diamond Creeper woke up and villager #20 said, “A  
creeper blew you up. You took a lot of damage, have a cookie.”  
“I must find the creeper king,” Diamond Creeper said.  
Diamond Creeper wiped his head with sweat. “This is the hottest desert ever… shade!”  
Diamond Creeper said. They dived into the temple. “Uuummm I think we reached our  
destination. It’s so dark I couldn’t,” Diamond Creeper said.  
“Oink” Ruben said.  
Then a dark shadowy figure climbed out of a hole . “Darn it! I forgot to delete the mutant  
creatures mod. He looks nothing like in the book.” It was the king of creepers!  
“You will fall like all the users of minecraft from notch to you” the creeper king said.  
Diamond yelled out, “You talk too much” and struck him with a bow and ran off.  
“We have to warn the village,” Diamond Creeper said. “You have to believe me he’s  
real!” Diamond Creeper yelled.  
“NO the creeper king is a myth,” added the prime minister.  
“I’m back,” said a voice.  
“He’s here,” Diamond Creeper sighed.  
Constant explosions were everywhere. Diamond Creeper said in his mind, Why  
creepers, as he climbed in his defense tower and clicked a button and giant bedrock walls  
popped out of the ground stopping the creepers from blowing up the village. With arrows they  
demolished the creepers. The demolition creepers and the king still stood there . Then  
creepers started falling. Diamond creeper clicked another button. It made a bedrock bunker or  
a roof with a door for warriors and towers for archers and it was bedrock to prevent explosions  
from the outside. Diamond creeper was rapidly pushing buttons and cats were spawning  
everywhere and arrows were flying and TNT was exploding. The cats weren’t random,  
creepers are afraid of cats.
