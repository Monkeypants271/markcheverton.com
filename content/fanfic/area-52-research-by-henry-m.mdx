**_Chapter 1_**

I found this on Wikipedia:

**Area 51** is the common name of a highly classified [United States Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Air_Force) (USAF) facility located within the [Nevada Test and Training Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nevada_Test_and_Training_Range). A remote detachment administered by [Edwards Air Force Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edwards_Air_Force_Base), the facility is officially called **Homey Airport** (**XTA/KXTA**) or **Groom Lake**[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area_51#cite_note-3) (after [the salt flat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groom_Lake_\(salt_flat\)) situated next to its airfield). Details of the facility’s operations are not made public, but the USAF says that it is an open training range,[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area_51#cite_note-McAndrewsWaPo-4) and it is commonly thought to support the development and testing of experimental aircraft and weapons systems.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area_51#cite_note-FOOTNOTERichJanos199457-5)[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area_51#cite_note-McAndrewsWaPo-4) The USAF acquired the site in 1955, primarily for flight testing the [Lockheed U-2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_U-2) aircraft.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area_51#cite_note-BBC-6)

The intense secrecy surrounding the base has made it the frequent subject of [conspiracy theories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conspiracy_theories) and a central component of [unidentified flying object](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unidentified_flying_object) (UFO) folklore.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area_51#cite_note-FOOTNOTEJacobsen201211%E2%80%9315,_320%E2%80%93321-7)[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area_51#cite_note-lacitis20100327-8) The base has never been declared a secret base, but all research and occurrences in Area 51 are [Top Secret/Sensitive Compartmented Information](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Top_Secret/Sensitive_Compartmented_Information) (TS/SCI).[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area_51#cite_note-FOOTNOTEJacobsen201265%E2%80%9366,_77%E2%80%9380-9) The CIA publicly acknowledged the existence of the base for the first time on June 25, 2013, following a [Freedom of Information Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedom_of_Information_Act_\(United_States\)) (FOIA) request filed in 2005, and, at the same time, they declassified documents detailing the history and purpose of Area 51.[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area_51#cite_note-cia1992-10)

Area 51 is located in the southern portion of [Nevada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nevada), 83 miles (134 km) north-northwest of [Las Vegas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Vegas). The surrounding area is a popular tourist destination, including the small town of [Rachel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel,_Nevada) on the “[Extraterrestrial Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nevada_State_Route_375)“.
