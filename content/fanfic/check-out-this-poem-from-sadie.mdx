**Basketball**  
By Sadie Marks  
Boom  
The ball punches the court  
Thudding like my heart  
I shoot from behind the 3point line  
It swishes through the air  
A comet about to hit Earth  
Straight into the basket  
A perfect shot  
Cheers go up on the right side  
My arms raised in victory  
Now it’s back to the game  
My feet stamp, my head pounds  
Sweat on my face and blood in my brain  
I jump up for another shot.
