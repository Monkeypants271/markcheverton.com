What is Minecraft? Minecraft is a sandbox game (endless game) where you are in a world of blocks. There are two modes: Creative mode and Survival mode. In Creative mode, you have infinite blocks and gives you the ability to build whatever you want, but in Survival mode (which is the real game) is when you have a health and hunger bar, and you need to mine resources, like gold, iron, diamond, and more. You need to build a shelter to survive the night.  
At night, monsters arrive. Such as: zombies, spiders, skeletons, endermans, and slimes. Though, if you don’t like monsters to show up, then you can turn your world to “Peaceful” mode.

Planning to Invade

He was walking on the moon, with a look of hatred on his face. As soon as his face showed, all the zombies bowed down to him. “My fellow zombies”, He said in a very low and clear voice, making the other zombies shiver. “I am your king, Mazus-Everbleed. We are going to invade the users within minecraft on this server”. He brought out a big globe, as shiny as silver. That globe then showed the only two users on this server, that they are going to attack.  
They are: Batman\_9635 and TBC 44. “But first, we need to build ships for each one of us, so we can travel to the very grounds of minecaft and take revenge on them. They have killed many of our ancestors that used to live on the grounds” Mazus, the zombie king said.  
“But we were the first ones to attack them, that’s why they were killing our ancestors” said another zombie in the very back. Mazus, being outraged, threw a diamond axe at the zombie in the head, bringing down all his HP (health points) and three glowing balls of XP (experience points).  
This forced all the other zombies to take a few steps back. “TODAY, WE WILL BUILD SHIPS FOR EVERY ZOMBIE, AND TAKE REVENGE ON THOSE TWO PATHETIC USERS AFTER WE FINISH!” The zombies cheered, and then started working. “Master” said the zombie commander, “Where do we get all those supplies?”  
“We will take pieces of the moon and put them together, and then we will add a superchip to make it work.” “Yes, your majesty.” Said the zombie commander.  
All the zombies started to dig for resources, mostly iron. Then, the zombie king made a loud evil laugh, making all the zombies wobble, and even make the moon shake too.

Surviving the Night

Batman\_9635 and TBC 44 were walking on the hills, a beautiful day to sit down and talk. “So… what would you like to do?” said TBC, waiting desperately for an answer. “Well, maybe we could play hide and….wait a sec!” said Batman. “What?” “We need to build a shelter, quick.” So they ran up to a tree, breaking and collecting the Oak logs and turning it into Oak planks. Then, they made a crafting table.  
“Quick, craft a wooden pickaxe!” said Batman, worrying when night time will arrive. “Ok” was TBC’s answer. So they were mining stone, lots of it, then turned into cobblestone after it had been mined.  
In about fifteen minutes, they made their whole house out of cobblestone. The size of the house was 20×20. Then, they crafted two doors at the front of their house. “Quick, let’s find some iron, so we can make iron sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe, and full iron armor.  
“Let’s go look for iron in a cave, it’s easier instead of creating a mine.” They found a cave, put torches everywhere, and found lots of iron. “Hey, come over here, there are more than fifty blocks of iron in this section!” said TBC. “Okay, sure, I’m coming!” replied Batman.  
They kept mining and mining iron, until their inventory got full. “We both have 64×27 Iron, so….we mined 1,728 iron each, so x2 will be 3,456 iron! Wow!” TBC 44 said. “Now, let’s go back home.” Said Batman\_9635.  
Right when they came out of the cave, it was already nighttime. “Quick, to the house!” TBC said.  
They made it to their house before the monsters could even reach them. “Quick, let’s make a furnace!” Batman said. They made multiple furnaces, so they won’t have to wait for the Iron ores to smelt to Iron ingots. In about five minutes, all the iron was smelted into ingots.  
Then, they started crafting iron tools, weapons, and armor. By the time they finished, the ground zombies started to bang on the doors, almost breaking them, with other monsters waiting for them behind the zombies. The zombies broke in, and also the other monsters flooded their house.  
“No…..” replied both Batman and TBC. The only thing they could do was slash at through the monsters, and out of their house, and that’s what they did. “My health is dropping, I have half-a-heart.” said TBC. “Me too.” Said Batman.  
They ate cooked pork chops and steak, causing their hunger bar to get full and making their health bar slowly regenerate. They kept running, and saw monsters coming from all directions at them. They killed many, but there were still more of them coming.  
At last, a wolf came with a whole bunch of other wolfs behind him, helping them to attack the monsters. They kept attacking and attacking, but there were still more.  
Finally, the sun rised, and all the monsters were on fire, except for Slimes, Endermans, and Creepers. They ran back to the caves and shadows – hiding from the sun.  
“We did it!” said TBC. “Thanks to the wolves!” said Batman. TBC crouched down and took a look at the wolf that was standing at the front of the pack. “He must be the leader.” said TBC. He looked on the wolf’s name tag that was chained to the leash around his neck in a bone shape.  
He looked at it carefully. “Zachariah is the name of the leader of the pack.” TBC said after a while. “Cool.” added Batman.  
“I think we should use Zachariah as our pet.” TBC answered. “He can make good use of helping us attack the monsters of the night.” Said TBC.

The king of the enderman was sitting on a throne made of diamonds. As others awaited for his orders, Zeek-Lore looked on The Sphere of Pain, and saw Batman\_9635 and TBC 44 running about the land. They were killing some animals for food, but at the same time, they were looking for something.  
“It’s them, again.” Said Zeek-Lore, the king of the enderman. He turned the globe around and looked at the moon, and saw moon-zombies planning to invade. He zoomed in closer, and saw that they were digging up resources, mostly iron, and were building something.  
“Baradikuss,” said Zeek “GO SPY ON THE MOON ZOMBIES AND STEAL SOME RESOURCES THEN COME BACK” Baradikuss, nodded his head in a shiver, then teleported to the moon.  
At the moon, Baradikuss saw millions of zombies digging for resources and are also building. He teleported to other zombies, quickly picking up resources, then when his hand was full, he was about to teleport back, but Mazus-Everbleed saw him, he told him to stop, and drop those resources.  
Without Baradikuss even responding, he teleported back to Zeek-Lore’s castle. The zombie, being filled with rage, screamed very loud, and killed about 10 zombies in the front row, filled with anger.  
Back at the castle, Baradikuss dropped all the reources in front of his king. “Here you go, sir.” Said Baradikuss, bowing down really low.  
“IS THIS HOW YOU GIVE THINGS TO YOUR KING?!?!” said the king of the enderman. “Sorry, sir.” Replied Baradikuss. Zeed threw his globe at Baradikuss’s head, but he teleported.  
Zeek-Lore closed his eyes, and extending out his arms. With his mind, he imaging Baradikuss not having the ability to teleport, and to make him still. He opened his eyes, and it worked.  
Baradikuss, about to cry, was being poured on with water. The enderman was having smoke come out of his body. He was flashing red again and again, until his health dropped to nothing, and disappeared with 3 glowing balls of XP.  
“IF ANY ONE OF YOU DARE TO CHALLENGE ME, THEN YOU WILL END UP IN THE SAME FATE AS HIM.” Zeek said, filled with anger.  
“WE WILL TAKE OUR REVENGE ON THOSE TWO USERS, BECAUSE THEY DON’T HAVE THE RIGHT TO TAKE OUR LAND” and he said, “ALL OF YOU, TELEPORT TO BATMAN\_9635 AND TBC 44 AND BRING THEM TO ME RIGHT NOW!!!  
All the enderman in Zeek-Lore’s castle teleported away, and in a matter of ten seconds, teleported back with Batman\_9635 and TBC 44. “What?” Batman said. “We were just walking in the jungle, and without seeing anything teleported here?!?!” He continued. The king of the enderman laughed.  
“You fools fell into my trap.” He said, “And you two are going to die for taking our land.” “YOUR LAND?!” TBC said with frustration. “THIS IS OUR LAND, AND YOU DON’T HAVE THE RIGHT TO TAKE IT FROM US!” “Shh….you will just make him angrier” Batman whispered in TBC’s ear.  
Zeek, starting to get angry, closed his eyes and imagined that they were burning on fire, and they were. “How did this happen” TBC said. “I don’t know. We can’t let them see us burn to ashes. Get out your bucket of water.” Batman replied. They splashed a bucket of water on them, then after the fire on them faded away, they scooped up the water.  
The same process was happening over and over again, until Zeek-Lore became angry. He brought out his fist, and smashed their faces hard, throwing both of them to the wall, and it literally got a crack on it then fell to the floor, the wall now shattered into thousands of pieces.  
They flashed red, their HP about to drop. “We have to do something about this” said Batman. “We have only one choice – to attack.” “ARE YOU CRAZY?!?!” said TBC “First of all, there are thousands of enderman, and second, you hit one, they teleport next to you and kill you.” TBC continued, shaking with fear.  
Batman, seeing him shake, told him; “STAND TALL AND BE BRAVE! There is only one choice, this is how we should react! Show them that we are ‘The Big Boys’ and that they are nothing compared to us!” “Ok then, let’s do this.” TBC replied.  
They knew that water can kill enderman, and they had a whole bunch of buckets of water in their inventory. “Hey, check this out!” Batman said to everyone. The endermans looked at him, confused. “I have a gift of honour for all of you….AND IT’S A GIFT OF WATER!”  
The two users dumped water all throughout the castle, literally flooding it. All the enderman couldn’t teleport away from that flood, and slowly, one by one, they all died, except for the king of the enderman, who heard their plan, but teleported outside. “WE DID IT!” TBC said with excitement.  
They ran out of the castle and were going back home. They opened their doors, and saw Zeek-Lore in their house. They were shocked, but already were ready for the battle with the king of the endermen.  
Zeek teleported to their side, and giving them a whole bunch of punches. Batman and TBC tried to dump water on Zeek, but he was too fast, and teleported away. And then a whole bunch of wolfs came to attack, but they all died, except for Zachariah, which was Batman’s most trusted wolf by his side.  
They knew that endermans wouldn’t teleport away when you are slashing at their legs, so that’s what they did. Their iron sword was giving him lots and lots of damage, until he disappeared with a blue sphere. Batman, being surprised, picked up the blue sphere and read it. It said: Piece #1/10.

The Search for the Scroll

TBC 44 both had a looks of confusion on their faces. “What is ‘Piece #1/10’?” TBC said. “I don’t know. Maybe we need to find the other nine pieces, then put them together, and it will turn into something.” Batman replied. “It also says two letters, which is ‘TE’.” Batman continued. “When I hold it, it feels like it’s trying to bring me into a direction.” Batman said again. “So, let’s see where it’s bringing you to.” TBC answered. So they kept walking into the direction that the silver globe was bringing them to.  
On their way, they found a village. “Hey Batman, wanna take a break here?” “Sure.” They walked to the village, and saw many villagers. “Hi!” the villager said, “I’m villager #21.” “Batman and TBC were shocked, not because of the name, but because he can talk.  
“Umm…..Hi?” TBC said. This made the villager frown. “Come, I have much to show you.” Villager #21 said. Right when they walked into the village others were saying “Hi, I’m villager #52…..Hi, I’m villager #81…..Hi, I’m villager #67…..” and so on.  
Villager #21 was showing Batman and TBC around the village “Do you actually think that we should live here?” TBC whispered. “Sure! This place is much more protective.”  Batman whispered back. And nighttime approached.  
“…..Or not.” Batman said again. Monsters were everywhere, and all the villagers went to their homes, hoping they won’t die tonight. “Let’s go back to the direction that we were supposed to go.” Said TBC  
“No, not yet. We need to protect this village tonight, then in the morning, we can put cobblestone walls all around the village, so monsters won’t come in.” Batman said. “Sure” TBC replied.  
They ran at the monsters, slashing and slashing, monsters flashing red and red, monsters disappearing and disappearing. A creeper ran up tp them, preparing to explode, but they were too fast for that green creature. They slashed at him three times, then he disappeared with gunpowder on the floor and three glowing balls of XP.  
Then they ran up to the zombie, slashing at him, then at another monster, then another and another, until that whole area was cleared. At that moment, the sun came up, and all the villagers cheered their names.  
That gave Batman and TBC a good feeling inside. “If you want to stay protected for your whole life, then why not build a wall made of cobblestone?” Batman shouted to the villagers. They all looked at each other with confusion.  
Batman and TBC then started building cobblestone walls around the village, followed by a roof. This took them five minutes, since they were rushing. Batman and TBC said bye to the villagers, then continued on their journey.  
“I can feel it.” Batman said. “We are getting closer.” They kept walking, and a few minutes later there was a flash of light that knocked them off their feet. They looked, and saw a scroll on the very top of a really high mountain.  
“I’m not going to go mountain climbing. Why not make a TNT cannon that shoots us really high in the sky, and for us to land on the scroll?” Batman said. “Sounds fine to me.” TBC answered. They got out their dispensers, redstone, redstone capacitors, TNT, and water.

Batman put twelve dispensers in one row, and one dispenser facing a different direction at the end, then another twelve dispensers in one row facing the other dispensers. Then, he put redstone dust and redstone capacitors in front of each dispenser. Then, put TNT all around it, and after that, water.  
“This should shoot us all the way in the sky without taking any damage, since there is water around it.” TBC said. “That’s right!” Batman replied. TBC pulled the lever, and the TNT started to blink. “Ready?” TBC said. “Yes” Batman replied again. Then five seconds later, the TNT exploded.  
Then, they went extremely high up in the sky, then fell right next to the scroll, with lots of damage. “Yes! It’s the scroll!” Batman said. TBC remained silent. Batman held up the scroll, and the whole area flashed bright white. “Hey, TBC, take a look at this!” “Hmm?” TBC answered. “Read it.” Batman said.
