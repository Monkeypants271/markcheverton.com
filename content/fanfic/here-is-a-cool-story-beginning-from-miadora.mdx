As I woke up,I could see a hazy Minecraft stone brick wall in front of me. Was that all a dream? Where am I? I sat up and rubbed my head where it seemed as if I hit it. Is this…..Minecraft? I looked around. It was. It seemed as if I was my Minecraft skin. I think I’m in a dungeon. I’m so confused. What was happening? Then the door opened silently. In stepped a diamond clad warrior. This somehow reminded me of a book series I read. I think it was the GameKnight999 series. As the diamond calf warrior stepped in,he held up a boxy finger,signaling for me to be quiet. Then grabbed me and we ran. I had no idea what was happening. All I remember after that is getting onto a couple of horses,riding to a village and then falling asleep on a bed.
