One evening Jim the Hillbilly was  
eating dinner at the dinner table. As Jim  
was in the bathroom he heard a ¨Crunch,  
Crunch,¨ out in the rain. He grabbed a  
rifle and went quickly but sneaky  
outside.  
Crunch as Jim saw a man behind an  
old car. Jim walked closer and….  
¨Who are you and what are you doing?¨  
Jim walked up to him and…..  
Tackled him and tied his hands with zip  
tie.  
¨I…I…I.. was just trying to get to your  
shed to sleep.¨  
¨You are homeless?¨ ¨Yes.¨  
¨You come help on the farm and I pay  
you 200 dollars.¨  
Later that evening Kyle, the homeless  
man, milked the 103 cows. ¨You just  
earned $100 right there¨.  
¨I´m feeding the cows,¨ said Kyle and…  
One year later …  
Jim went to Kyle´s house and he was  
living very nice and Jim had dinner with  
Kyle. Kyle lived happily ever after .
