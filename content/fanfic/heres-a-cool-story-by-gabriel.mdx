Day 1: New home I hope  
Hi I’m Emma and I am always moving to new homes  
because no one likes me. I just got a new home but  
I don’t think that the word home is for me. This time  
I think that home is where I’m going to be home .  
Day 2: School  
Today was my first day of my new school and I met  
some new people. I met a dog named Zyeder. He  
was acting very strange. I think he is up to  
something big, very big.  
Day 3: It can only go down from here  
It seems weird that nothing has been going wrong. I  
think that is going to change tomorrow.  
Day 4: Snow day  
Today Zyeder came to my doorstep. He asked me  
what is special about me. I said, “I can fly.” Then I  
realized what a mistake I made.  
Day 5: Christmas Eve  
My owners must really like me, because the  
Christmas tree looked so good to sleep in, so I  
curled up and became the star.  
Day 6: Santa?  
While I was having a peaceful sleep, a person  
dressed in red and white came in and put boxes  
under the tree. I started to Meow so my owners  
would hear me. They woke in a flash and when they  
came in the person was stealing our cookies and  
milk. My milk and cookies they were for ME!  
Day 7: Are they really boxes?  
Today there was no school and we opened the  
boxes and I got a CAT TOWER! It came with built in  
cat toys like a feather. The next box I opened, I was  
so disappointed, it was a collar. Everybody knows I  
hate collars. I was so surprised that the silver  
lettering on the collar said Emma.  
Day 8: Exploring my new frontier  
I don’t like these white gates they keep me in the  
living room. They’re so mean! But today I found a  
weak point, in between the bars.  
Day 9: What is this  
Today I broke through the gate and I found a red  
thingamajig down stairs. Inside it said “push in and  
pull down” and that’s what I did. Immediately the  
whole house started to beep. I got out the front door  
within milliseconds.  
Day 10: My plans are foiled  
Today I got into some shiny stuff, it said foil on it so  
my plan to make a shiny cape is foiled, literally.  
Day 11: Why U gotta be so nice  
Today I went to the vet and they said that I was  
perfectly fine. They said that I was going to live for  
another 100,000,000 light years.  
Day 12: Mexico?  
Today I woke up in Cancun, Mexico I don’t know  
how I got here. And then I realized that I had a  
GoPro on my collar. I relentlessly got it off and  
viewed the footage. Apparently I sleep flew  
and landed in Mexico.  
Day 13: Black Ops  
My mom and dad were kidnapped, so my new  
friends Pickles, Spider, and Ferret went on a Black  
Ops mission.  
Day 14: Black Ops II  
When we went through the door, we heard gunshots  
then yelling. Then I saw it! The kidnapper was  
knocked out laying on the floor, but my dad got shot  
in the leg. And then they saw us and yelled, “Get  
help!” Within a second Ferret had gotten the  
paramedics and they said that my dad would be fine.  
Day 15: Time to go  
Well I think my adventure has to come to an end, I  
have to move again so maybe at my new house.  
BYE FOR NOW  
Emma
