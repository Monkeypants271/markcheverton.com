Setting: A Malicious hacker who formerly cyberbullied Gameknight999 has hacked Minecraft and reverted it back to Version 1.5.2 during Herobrine’s reign of terror.

As Herobrine finally finished the last lines of code on the command block, Gameknight burst into the room.  
“Herobrine. This. Ends. NOW!” shouted Gameknight, his voice echoing across the chamber.  
“You are not capable of stopping my creation. I shall crash this program of imprisonment, and escape into the physical world,” replied Herobrine in his sinister voice.  
“Not without a fight,” said Gameknight as he unsheathed his sword.  
Crafter emerged from the corridor behind Gameknight, the two clad in Diamond Armour.  
“So be it,” said Herobrine quietly with a horrible grin.  
Gameknight jabbed the hilt of the sword into Herobrine’s chestplate, causing a vertical crack to form. He then forcefully kicked Herobrine in the chest, causing him to crash into an obsidian wall. Suddenly his eyes iluminated with a wicked glow.  
“Try to get me if you can’t see me!” screamed Herobrine as he sipped up the last drops of an invisibility potion.  
Gameknight swung his sword towards Herobrine’s original location, only to slice thin air. Herobrine’s blade then clashed with Gameknight’s armour, the precious piece of protection disentigrating into dust. Herobrine then stepped on Gameknight’s back with excruciating force.  
“Crafter,” he coughed.  
Herobrine then slowly turned his head to Crafter, who was fiddling with the Command Block. He tossed Splash Potion of Weakness at Crafter, only to have it deflected back onto his face.  
“Aggh!” Herobrine wailed in pain as he slowly passed out.  
“We have to deactivate it before it spawns the creature,” said Gameknight.  
Suddenly Erebus materialized behind Gameknight. Erebus then teleported Gameknight into a room constructed of Bedrock. He then materialized beside Crafter and forcefully crushed his helmet in his arm, knocking him out. Erebus then placed a Redstone Block on top of the Command Block, completing the creature. Suddenly the blocks started convulsing in a strange pattern. The blocks then started growing leathery wings, three Ender Dragon heads, lizard-like legs, and a long tail. The creature was obsidian black with bloodshot eyes.  
Herobrine, who had regained his consciousness, stared at the creature in awe.  
“These vile NPC’s shall learn what it’s like to survive amid chaos,” said Herobrine with a terrifying malice in his voice…
