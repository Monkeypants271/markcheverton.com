Gameknight followed the crafter into the crafting chamber.

I wonder what that creature wanted with us? Thought Gameknight.

They finally approached the bottom of the ladder but instead of a giant crafting chamber, there was just a blue box. The bottom was 3×3 blocks and the top had a light bulb looking thing.

“Your probably wondering what that is,” said the crafter. “That is the crafting chamber. It’s bigger on the inside and small on the outside.”

Gameknight saw the crafter move in close and disappear. Gameknight was confused, he stepped up close to the box and was immediately teleported to another location not in the Overworld or the Nether and definitely not the End. There were villagers everywhere, some were at the center while others were coming out of hallways. There was also a massive beacon looking block at the center.

“Set a course for village 001,” said a soft childlike voice. It was Crafter! He was standing next to the village crafter from this server.

“Hey Crafter,” said Gameknight running up to hug his friend.

“Hello User-that-is-not-a-user,” replied Crafter.

“What did you mean ‘set a course for village 001’?”

“You’ll see,” said the tall village crafter.

All of a sudden, there was a whooshing noise coming from outside. Gameknight stepped out and was amazed at what he saw.
