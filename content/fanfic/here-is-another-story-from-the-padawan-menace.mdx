Gameknight quickly made his way through thick trees and high hills. He was going to find the treasure of Minecraft. As he turned around, he saw Hunter and Monet113 following him. He looked ahead, but all he saw was darkness. He then turned to see Herobrine and Xa-tul, Heobrine’s giant zombie. behind them were Hunter and Monet stuck in tight ropes. As Gameknight pulled out his sword, Herobrine said “That is a foolish option, User-that-is-not-a-user.” Herobrine pulled out a block of TNT. “If you try to attack, your friends will go BOOM.” All at once, Crafter jumped out and attacked, but Herobrine teleported with Xa-tul. So they went back to the village.
