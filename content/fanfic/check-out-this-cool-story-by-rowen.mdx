GameKnight chased after Entity303. His loud breathing from running attracted a few monsters as they rushed through the jungle however he quickly destroyed them. He regretted leaving all Crafter and the villagers behind to chase after Entity303 but he was determined to defeat him. The villagers had been fighting an army of monsters when GameKnight saw Entity303 and chased him. As he ran a spider larger than him fell out of the trees. Claws raked across GameKnight’s armor but the giant spider was quickly silenced by his sword.

As he ran he noticed that he was running slower and slower. He wasn’t hungry so that meant that some  invisible force was slowing him. Then he heard it: the wind. He laughed at his terror and kept moving. Suddenly he felt something wet and cold on his cheek. He looked at his clock and realized that is should be day. More fat rain drops fell on him. Suddenly lightning crackled in the distance. GameKnight was terrified and ran. He knew that if he ran far enough he could escape the storm. It was pouring as he ran through the lush jungle. More lightning crackled momentarely blinding him. At this moment a gust of wind threw him against a tree. He wished that his friends were here but he had left without them.

Then out of the blue a video he had once watched pope into his head. He smiled and placed a crafting table on the ground 2 minutes later he had a weather map and tornado siren. When he placed the siren it began blaring at once. He placed down the weather map. Tornadoes were everywhere on the map. One was headed for him! Under the tornado picture on the weather map there was an EF5. In the physical world tornadoes were ranked on a scale of 0 to 5. 0 being very weak and 5 being very intense winds. This one was very bad.

He quickly picked up the siren and the weather map but when he reached for the crafting table it blew away.  GameKnight ran harder than he had ever run in his life. As he was running he noticed that he could see easily. Suddenly a block of wood slammed into GameKnight’s head. Stunned he fell down. But when he tried to stand he was pulled off the ground. Up and up he went until he was higher than the clouds. As he began to fall he closed his eyes and braced for impact.

GameKnight hit the ground all to soon. He looked up dazed then realized that he was alive! Confused he looked at his surroundings. He was on a mountain that had stretched high above the clouds. He realized that the fall wasn’t very far. As he began to climb down he felt the wind pick up again. Once he was about 50 blocks above sea level. He came face to face the biggest, longest, and darkest tornado ever seen. It rumbled and began to whirl towards him. He grabbed his pick and made a hole in the mountain. Then he hid there. All was quiet for about 30 seconds. Then suddenly all the blocks around him flew into the air. However GameKnight held on to the mountain tightly. The savage winds were blowing the mountain apart.

Then just when the block that GameKnight was holding on to was about to blow away into the vortex. It all stopped. All the blocks fell out of the sky in random places. There was a block of wood in the water, Stone in a tree, and sand everywhere! He climbed the rest of the way down the hill to the beach and sat staring at the evening sun. As he sat he noticed another vortex forming. “What is up with the weather?” GameKnight asked himself. He looked at the weather map and instead of seeing a tornado he saw a hurricane. Suddenly a gust of wind sent him flying int the deep blue ocean…

When GameKnight surfaced it was night. Lightning flashed continuously and winds whipped up huge waves. One wave hit him and pulled hime underwater. This kept happening. GameKnight was getting tired. He searched his eyes for an island and found one! He began swimming towards it but the wind, waves and fatigue slowed him. When he got to the island he lay down shivering. But when he saw a figure coming towards him with a sword he got up and ran. Suddenly lightning flashed illuminating the face of Entity303. GameKnight knew that he couldn’t win and had to think of a plan fast. “What? You scared?” taunted Entity303 as he drew closer…

GameKnight dodged Entity303’s sword as it sliced through the air then counterattacked however Entity303’s armor didn’t even crack. Entity303 used this as a chance to attack. With a huge leap he swung his sword and smashed his armor in one swing. GameKnight counterattacked again but again no damage. His armor was too strong. Entity303 kicked GameKnight hard in the stomach. GameKnight doubled over in pain dropping is sword in the process. He grabbed his sword and got to his feet.

Just then he noticed a block next to Entity303. When he clicked the block the wind got stronger. GameKnight realized that Entity303 was controlling the weather with it. He quickly      thought of a very risky plan. GameKnight ran at Entity303. But before he was in striking distance of his sword GameKnight placed down the tornado siren witch began to blare instantly. Stunned Entity303 covered his ears and GameKnight grabbed the block next to him. Then he kicked Entity303 across the beach. “It’s over.” said GameKnight. “For now…” replied Entity303. Suddenly Entity303 dove into the water and disappeared…

GameKnight used the weather block to change the weather to sunny. He rested until morning then got on a boat. As he rode home he thought about his apology to Crafter and the villagers he did  not notice Entity303’s head bubbling beneath the surface of the water holding a potion of water breathing…
