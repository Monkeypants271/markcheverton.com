### Minecraft Rescue

#### By Darien

I was walking on my lawn. It was a foggy day at noon  
when I saw my ocelot, Fluffy. But there was something  
running in the distance. It was a creeper running away  
from Fluffy. I shot it with my bow and arrow. “Long shot,” I  
yelled.  
Out of nowhere, a griefer came and took Fluffy. I tried  
to shoot him, but missed. “No!” I yelled.  
I saw my friend James walking towards me and I ran  
towards him. “Fluffy has been stolen,” I screamed.  
“I have some bad news,” said James. “The king of  
Creepers, Gorgan, has hired griefers to take all ocelots.”  
“But I must save Fluffy!”  
“But you can’t. You are just a farmer.”  
“No I’m not,” I yelled in anger. “I can be a hero. I’m  
going to save Fluffy, you can come with me if you want. If  
you don’t, I have a friend who will help me.”  
Catalina will help me. She is a brave young treasure  
hunter with brown eyes, brown hair, and also a very skilled  
swordsman. I know, I’ll go to the abandoned mine shaft in  
the cave 2 miles away because she lives 9 miles away. I  
can get to Catalina’s house way faster that way. Hopefully  
her ocelot, Paige, is still there.  
About two minutes later, I was at the cave. I saw a  
cave spider and the only weapon I own is my bow and  
arrow. I slowly walked towards the cave spider. My  
stomach was feeling sick. I was a little bit scared, because  
I have never faced hostile mobs.  
Thwack! I hit it right in the head. When I killed the  
cave spider it dropped some string. I walked in the cave.  
Diamonds, diamonds everywhere! I felt so excited! I  
pulled out my pick axe quickly and started to mine. 800  
diamonds I got in the mine cart! It went so fast that I  
thought I was going to melt!  
Swoosh! Screech! The mine cart stopped at the end  
of the cave. First, before I get my friend Catalina, I’m going  
to get my weapons and armor so I can defend myself  
against hostile mobs. I ran towards the village as fast as I  
could. I burst through the Blacksmith’s shop. “Hey Eric,” I  
yelled. “Give me everything you got! I have the diamonds  
to pay for.”  
As I walked out of the shop, it slowly turned to  
nightfall over the village. I lit a torch and it guided the way  
to my friend. With my new weapons and armor, I felt  
confident that I would win against the mobs. I ran as fast  
as I could to get to Catalina’s house. I was running so long  
I forgot it was nighttime and dangerous. I decided I should  
put on my armor.  
I pulled out my iron sword, and I saw a zombie in the  
distance trying to attack a villager. I ran as fast as I could  
to help the screaming villager. I felt so far away as I ran. I  
hear another cry for help, and I knew I was getting closer. I  
hit the zombie two times before it finally died. The villager  
thanked me by giving me one iron ingot. I was close to my  
friend’s house, so I decided I could slow down the pace.  
When I finally got there, Catalina was not there, and  
neither was her house. Only a few burning bricks  
remained. “I guess I have to do this on my own,” I said out  
loud.  
I saw a horse where a stable used to be. I grabbed a  
saddle out of my inventory and put it on the horse. I tamed  
the horse with a carrot and I jumped on it.  
“Go!” I yelled at the horse I named Bot. We ran to the  
desert temple were the creepers were hiding. I pulled out  
my diamond sword and swung it at the guards.  
Sssssssssssss, BOOM! They blew up and made a giant  
hole in the ground, which most of the creepers fell in. I  
saw a hole. I saw all the ocelots in the hole. I jumped in  
and grabbed all of them because there weren’t that many  
in the hole. I felt good inside like a hero I thought proudly.  
Now the journey home. King Gorgen of the Creepers  
got away and he’s lucky. Until next time, King Gorgen!
