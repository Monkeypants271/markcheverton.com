WELCOME TO YOUR GRAVE  
There was a brave kid named Dipper  
Sanborn. Dipper was 10 years old and  
loved to play hockey and was a naughty  
boy at his house. But the most thing he  
loved was that he was great at drawing  
and loved to play pretend. A lot of the  
girls were in love with him. He had black  
hair and brown eyes. One day people  
were picking on a 5 year old boy and  
Dipper beat them and got sent to juvie  
for beating up kids.  
5 months later he when he got out of  
juvie somebody went over to the prison  
and yelled  
“HELP! HELP! SOME WEIRD THING IS  
IN MY ROOM! GET IT OUT!” So Dipper  
was just about to go when, BOOM! Out  
of the explosion came a ghost, not just  
a ghost a pirate ghost, One Eyed Willy (  
Look on the last page to see Willy)  
“What the heck was that!” yelled  
somebody. Everybody was running  
away……. Besides Dipper. He was  
going to beat Willy then be FAMOUS!!!!  
Here we go. PUNCH! KICK! Willy was  
done for and Dipper was a hero.  
“In 80 years I will return!” screamed  
Willy. So the end was near… for Dipper.  
To be continued in Welcome to your  
grave Willy’s Warning.
