Gameknight walked In and found a chest, unlike usual chests rimmed with obsidian and diamond. Inside was A secret map that lead to A Few temples, a certain treasure in Each. The first location was A desert temple In the Dunes of Azamath. The second temple was Located In the Jungle of Creepers, And Finally the third Lead to the Temple of the Guardians. On the Back of this map Instructions Were attached. They read Backwards: I Evah A emag. Uoy lliw dnif em, Yb gnirehtag lla eht serusaert, neht etavitca Eht meti taht lliw tel ym dneirf tuo. After ahwhile Gameknight Deciphered it, It saying “I Have a game. You will find me, By Gathering ll the treasures, then activing The item that will let my friend out.” Gameknight was Suspicious of who this friend was, but he Decided it would make an Interesting discovery.
