There was only one person in the room. And it wasn’t an NPC. It was a user… A USER! Gameknight thrust his sword towards the newcomer. “Who are you?!” Gameknight demanded.  
“The center of the storm is calm, and it’s easiest to find your focus from there.” The user said randomly.  
“What?” Gameknight asked, confused.  
“It’s a quote. Transformers: Robots in Disguise. Heard of it?” Gameknight slowly shook his head “no.” “Ugh. Whatever. Who were you looking for anyway? Sounded like a battle was going on up there.” Gameknight replied,  
“Okaaaaaaaaaaay… I was looking for my friends. How did you get down here anyway? I’m the only user who knows about this place.” “Well, besides Shawney.” He added in his head.  
“I’ve known about is place for a long time, Gameknight. I’m a hacker. I hacked. It was easy.”  
“So is that why I can’t see your gamertag?”  
“Yes. It’s called a mod, though. I coded it in like, an hour.” Gameknight thought,  
“Okay, this guy is some sort of computer whiz. What else?” Out loud, he said, “This is very fascinating and all, but can we skip to the part where you tell me who you are and why you’re here?” The hacker took a deep breath, and launched into a complicated explanation.
