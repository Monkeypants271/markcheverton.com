As Donnie007 emerges from his safely waterfall hidden, underground cave he went to the tall 40 block tall tower he built himself. Clearly he needed a better view in the area. I wonder if there is a village somewhere… Donnie wondered. He wasn’t so sure about what direction the nearest village is… Until he found a distant tower. Identical to the one he found in the villages he’s seen. “I knew it.” Donnie said. He then went down the ladder, back to his cave, put on his Armour, enchanted bow and sword, and went off. Then looked up at the sky, it was still dark but its just a few scant hours until sunrise. “Since i have my boat i can sail to the village until sunrise.” Donnie said. But as he neared the village, he noticed that there are walls and a moat in front. It reminded him of the medieval defense system. But he is confused. Who could have built these things in the first place. But then he felt a presence behind him. Out of pure instinct, he turned-ready for an attack. But then was shocked by who was right in front of him…
