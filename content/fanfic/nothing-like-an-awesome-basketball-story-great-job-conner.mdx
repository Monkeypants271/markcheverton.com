## Hot stuff

### Author: Connor L.

#### Book 1: basketball

At a very loud NBA All Star game. Where all the seats  
were filled up with 107,312,690 people. The starting  
lineups were Michael Jordan, Larry Bird, Kobe Bryant,  
James Worthy, and Tim Duncan. The rooftop almost blew  
up when Michael Jordan did a backwards corkscrew  
windmill, ( don’t ask what that is……..all I can tell you is  
that is it is a AWESOME dunk!)

#### Chapter 2

Then these spirits came in with their leader Zena! Five  
went into the player’s body and took their talent! At the  
end of the game they almost got fired!  
‘’Why would these spirits take our talent?’’ Michael  
Jordan asked. ‘’Well why,’’ asked the cameraman. ‘’Maybe  
they aren’t good at basketball, so they took our talent,’’  
stated James Worthy. ‘’Then we will challenge them, if we  
win they have to give the talent back, if they win they keep  
the talent.’’ Said Kevin Durant, KarlAnthony  
Towns, Kyrie  
Irving, Russell Westbrook, and LeBron James as they  
walked out. ‘’Sorry, what are you saying?’’ Asked Tim  
Duncan. ‘’Oh, I get what they’re saying.’’ said Kobe Bryant.  
‘’I guess you guys will play them on October 4.’’ Said their  
coach Steve Kerr.

#### Chapter 3 October 4

‘’Are yyoouu RREEAADDYY?’’ Asked the  
announcer. ‘’I want a nice clean and fair game.’’ Said the  
referee. It was jump ball, and Team NBA got it first (that is  
Kevin Durant’s team). Towns was the jumper was the  
jumper, so that means Kyrie Irving is point guard, Russell  
Westbrook is shooting guard, LeBron James is power  
forward, Kevin Durant is small forward, and KarlAnthony  
Towns is center.  
‘’Irving with a deep three….BANG!’’ Says the  
commentator. Kevin is in the corner giving the ‘’nod’’ for an  
alleyoop.(  
Which is where someone throws the ball up and  
the other player on their team dunks it in mid air, they’re  
pretty SWEET!)  
Then the lights turned off and the grand boss of the  
spirits came, he had yellow grinded teeth, sharp  
fingernails, and…a…hairy…MOLE! Kevin Durant had a  
smirk on his face then thought that Nany Mcfies mole was  
ugly.  
Apparently the spirits grand boss was Zarold who just  
came here to see the game also, but came in with a  
dramatic entrance.
