## **Fighting a Titan**

Chapter 1.  
Titan alert

Gameknight woke up in his red Minecraft bed while he scowled to himself, “Oh gee. I wonder if there are more things that we can fight-” Gameknight’s scowling sentence got interrupted by, of course, Herder.  
Herder said, “Gameknight, there are more things to fight. But, there is an alert happening.”  
Gameknight said, “What KIND of alert?”  
Herder said, “A TITAN alert.”  
Gameknight said, “Oh geez! A titan?! What kind of titan?”  
Herder said, “A wither titan.”  
Gameknight felt scared and angry at the same time.  
Gameknight said, “I better tell the crew that there is a titan alert happening.” So the Wither Titan is a giant-sized wither that summons wither henchmen, and The Wither Titan is so powerful, The Wither Titan has the powers of an admin. So The Wither Titan can clear inventories, ban players, erase, talk while it fights, kill you while you’re in creative mode, and The Wither Titan is immune to the kill command.  
And The Wither Titan is called: Witherzilla. So Witherzilla is basically the most powerful mob in Minecraft history, and Witherzilla is the king of all withers.  
Gameknight said, “Guys, there is a titan alert happening! Herder told me! There is a wither titan that is going around and basically destroying everything!”  
Hunter said, “That is literally awful, Gameknight! We have to do something quick!”  
Herder said, “Where is the Wither Titan?”  
Gameknight said, “Herder, The Wither Titan is in a battle dimension. We HAVE to kill that darn titan. We HAVE to.”  
Crafter said, “Let’s go into the battle dimension, but first, we have to get some Totems of Undying.”  
The Whole Crew equips their Totems of Undying.  
The whole crew went into the battle dimension.  
WHHOOMP!  
Gameknight said, “Ok, we’re in the battle dimension, and whoa! That is a really big wither!”  
Herder said, “Wait a second, The Wither Titan is called Witherzilla! The king of all withers!”  
Crafter said, “And The Wither Titan has an eerie blue glow. And it has a crown on its head!”  
Hunter said, “Let’s kill it.”  
And when Witherzilla talks, he makes wither sounds.  
Crafter said, “Hey you big oversized wither! Stop destroying everything!”  
Witherzilla said, “You fools. I’m not called The Wither Titan or You Big Oversized Wither. I am called Witherzilla.”  
Gameknight said, “We will kill you.”  
Witherzilla said, “You fools are trying to kill me?! I am unkillable!”  
Herder said, “Let’s get fighting.”  
Witherzilla said, “ROARRRRR!!!!!”  
Witherzilla fires a bunch of wither skulls at once.  
Gameknight catches one of the wither skulls and throws it back.  
EXPLOSION  
Witherzilla said, “You. Fool. You are a clever boy, but you stand no chance against my wither henchmen.”  
Witherzilla spawns a wither minion.  
EXPLOSION FROM THE WITHER MINION  
Crafter equips a gun  
PFSHH! PFSH!  
Wither minion dies  
Witherzilla said, “You little fool. I am unstoppable.”  
Crafter said to Witherzilla, “Don’t call us fools. Otherwise, we will kill you.”  
Witherzilla said, “Try it. You will-”  
Gameknight types a command called: /superkill @Witherzilla  
Witherzilla gets killed instantaneously.  
Witherzilla said, “I WILL RETURN, YOU LITTLE FOOLS. I WILL RETURN!!!”  
And The Whole Crew (except Gameknight) felt shocked about the superkill command. And Gameknight’s friends never knew about the superkill command. It was completely new.

About Witherzilla: Witherzilla might be unkillable, but he has 100,000 health, and he kills players in a few seconds, even when the players are in Creative Mode! And the kill Creative Mode prevention is Totems Of Undying.  
And just a quick note: /superkill isn’t a real command in Minecraft.  
THE END.
