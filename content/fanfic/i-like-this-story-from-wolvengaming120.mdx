WolvenGaming120 finished up the obsidian wall for the villagers, and he was very proud of his design of the wall, but that wasn’t all. Wolven added 150 redstone contraptions set up for automating farms, transforming buildings, armor switcher, and even the ones for the monsters outside. He called out Builder and Crafter to see if they are impressed with him but then, a ambush of Zombies came, about 2,000 zombies came surrounding the walled in village. Wolven wasn’t afraid of these creatures anymore, he always had faith and hope, most importantly, his friends. As he came up to the walls he found the zombie leader, the one they all feared…. Ze-Bin. One of Herobrine’s evil hero he had created before he was destroyed by Gameknight999, the legendary prophecy. Wolven did have a feeling running down his spin, like a slithering snake of fear trying to crush the courage from Wolven, but he didn’t let it take over his body, only focasing on what’s happening and plan out a design or plan for these pathetic zombies. “ZE-BIN HAVE COME TO THE LAST ORDERS OF THE CREATOR TO DESTROY EVERY VILLAGE ON THIS SERVER”, Ze-Bin said. He stood 5 blocks tall and held a enchanted daimond sword of sharpness 2, he also wore a golden crown with three diamonds on it and wore daimond boots. Ze-Bin also has Purple eyes and glow bright purple with evil intent. Wolven yelled out, “GO BACK WHERE YOU CAME FROM FOOL, YOU DO NOT BELONG IN THE DAYLIGHT!” Things had changed, and Ze-Bin used his last shadow crafter energy to allow all zombies to go through the daylight without burning. Ze-Bin smiled and charged in with his army, Ze-Bin ran faster than any zombie. Ze-Bin did not noticed the traps set up. Wolven commanded the villagers to blow them up and used fire charges and lava to slow the army down, and it did. Wolven opened up  a huge pit towards bedrock and about 500 zombies fell in and left only 100 remaining zombies including Ze-Bin. Wolven came out in the battle field to face Ze-Bin once and for all and the battle began. The thing is, he has a suprise he had been working on for Ze-Bin. It was a mod. Ze-Bin starts to strick but, when it hit Wolven. He began to glow a bright white as he activated his mod, Ze-Bin took a step back and stared at the light, he could see as the light dimmed and could see Wolven in armor that was never in the game, and had wings. Wolven was in complete God Armor looking like a angel. He spun once to strike at Ze-Bin’s legs and telephoned behind to strike at the back and ran so fast to the side about to strike again, but Ze-Bin grabbed the sword and threw it to the ground but it did not use, as it just swung him around to the back of Ze-Bin to strike at the back of the neck. Ze-Bin Roared and stoked at Wolven’s stomach but it deflected it and harmed himself. He knelt to the ground and looked up at Wolven. Wolven flew up and finished Ze-Bin off on a blow to the head. As it did a shine of light shined giving a second life to Ze-Bin and telephoning him and his zombies back to their true home. The caves. Wolven and his village celebrated their victory.
