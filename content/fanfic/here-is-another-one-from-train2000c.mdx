What Gameknight saw when he stepped out was a roofed forest biome. But the strange thing was that there was a village there, but not a normal village nor one with a wall. Tall player looking entities were moving about the area.

“Hello User,” one of the entities said. “I am Elendil, the chief guard of this village.”

Gameknight looked up. The entity looked like something from a mod. The ears were pointed outwards and he wore a green shirt and light green pants.

“Who are you?” asked Gameknight.

“I am a woodland elf,” replied Elendil in a deep voice.

“Can I ask you something?”

“Sure, what is troubling you?”

“Well, Guard sent me with Richard t go to a village and we were attacked by what he calls a Uruk-hai. What is that?”

Elendil pulled out his bow when he heard that.
