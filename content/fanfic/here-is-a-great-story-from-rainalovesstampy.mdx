Minecraft: Inside and Out  
A Minecraft Fanfic/ Head canon written by Raina Mist

Credits to Mark Cheverton for the original idea of putting someone in Minecraft. This is my head canon/ fanfic that I hope you enjoy.

Dedicated to Mark Cheverton, his team/ son,  Joseph Garrett, A.K.A. Stampylongnose, and my family, who put up with me while I wrote this and multiple other stories in secret.

Chapter 1: Into Minecraft  
DJRECNEPS321

Spencer grabbed a croissant off the table. It was probably the only thing he was going to eat in an hour. It was super early in the morning. Spencer woke up earlier than usual, so he was going to get an early start. He was heading to his basement. That’s where his computer was. The reason he was going down there in the first place was that he was going to play Minecraft. He was building a huge metropolis on one of his worlds, and it was almost complete. He just had to make a couple of shops, finish building a skyscraper, build the park, and then it was finished. He was uber proud of it. One, because he built it all by himself, and two, because he made it all in Survival Mode. He gathered the materials in that mode too. It was a huge accomplishment, and Spencer knew it. It took him most of the year to gather and build. But, finally, it was almost complete. He sprinted down the stairs with anticipation, but he almost tripped over some stuff when he got to the bottom. His little sister Yolanda liked to play down here, and there was always something left over from the games she played. Spencer was too old for such, being 16. He usually spent his time coding, reading, and playing Minecraft. (He wasn’t much of a sports person) And when he wasn’t doing that, he was eating, sleeping or at school. But it was Saturday, so he had the whole day to himself. The only problem was, his basement was huge. Now that usually wasn’t a problem, but since it was so large, his dad’s office was down there. And so was his mothers. And, combined with Yolanda’s play area, there was usually always one person down there. He could never have privacy while he played Minecraft. But today, that was going to change. Spencer was going to have one hour of alone time, and he wanted to make it count.

He weaved through toys and stuffed animals and reached his computer. It was the fastest he could buy. That was so the lag when he played Minecraft would be less. But, still, there was always a little bit here and there. Games were always like that.

He slid into his chair and pressed the start button. It glowed green under his finger. The loading screen slowly churned away, the spinning circle of death on it’s forever course to infuriate Spencer. His computer always loaded slower than it should. He wasn’t sure why, but he ignored it. There was nothing he could do about it. When the circle stopped, the screen switched to that of his background; a Minecraft diamond. He clicked on the Minecraft icon, and it started it’s loading process. When that was completed, he pressed play. He switched to the world the metropolis was on and entered it. His character spawned in a building, where he had left it. He quickly got his bearings and went off to finish his city.

One hour later, he was finished. “Finally!” He said as he placed the last block. Suddenly his sister Yolanda came down the stairs and said,  
“Finally what?” she said. Spencer replied,  
“I finished my city! Took long enough.” Yolanda smiled,  
“Cool! Can I see?” He nodded,  
“Yeah. Come here. This is my apartment complex, that’s my hotel, that’s the park, and that’s the mall. There’s a lot of other stuff too.” Yolanda said,  
“Woah. that’s a lot.” She stared at the screen. Spencer said,  
“Not really.” It was weird, but Spencer kinda liked having his little sister’s approval. Also, he watched a lot of videos on YouTube. There were so many worlds bigger than his, and a lot more high tech. They had automated bathrooms and everything. But, this would have to do. “Whatever. It’s not like I’m gonna be judged or anything.” Spencer thought.

Suddenly, he saw a flash of light from the basement’s high window. Following it was a deafening roll of thunder. “Hey, I think Thor wants to get someone’s attention.” Yolanda joked. Spencer gave her a nervous smile, and hit escape on his computer. He hated storms. They reminded him of lightning striking his friend Jeffery a couple of years ago. He had barely survived. Now, he was deathly afraid of them. No one knew of his fear, and he intended to keep it that way. He exited his Minecraft world, but instead of quitting the game, he accidentally entered a different world. “Aw shoot.” Spencer said. Before he could quit, Yolanda asked,  
“What’s that?” Her brother replied,  
“It’s a new world. I only had time to create it yesterday.” Yolanda said,  
“Oh okay. Just wondering.”

Before they had a chance to do anything else, an even louder peal of thunder rocked the house, and it shook Spencer to the bone. He wanted to hide in his closet and stay there forever. But, of course, he would never do that. At least, not in front of anyone. Especially his sister. She would tell everyone at school and he would become a laughingstock.. No, he would stay calm, and do what he always did when something frightened him. He pretended all his fear was contained in a little ball, and he was crushing that ball down so that it practically did not exist. It was a tip he picked up from Anakin Skywalker. At least, the way some writer named Patricia described it. Usually that helped a little, but there was something off… something about this storm that scared him more than anything else. Then he realized what it was. “Hey sis, where’s the lightning? Usually it strikes before the thunder.” She put her hand on her hip and said,  
“Yeah… that is weird.” Suddenly, Spencer saw the lightning. It struck the power lines outside his house! He could see the electricity traveling along the wires, the white line glowing angry and bright. It reached his house, and the lights flickered for a second. “Well, that was a little undramatic.” Yolanda quipped. As soon as she spoke the words, the world started to get fuzzy, and Spencer got tunnel vision. He stood up and tried to steady himself, the chair suddenly feeling too wobbly, but his hands seemed to be stuck to the keys of the keyboard. He tried to pull his hands away from the mouse, but they seemed glued to the controls. His hands were connected to the keys by some kind of energy. He looked at Yolanda, feeling too tired to talk, and she said, “Uh, Spence? Are you okay? You’re glowing white. She put her hands on Spencer, trying to help him, but right as she did, she was enveloped in the same white light his hand’s were. She suddenly looked very tired and sick. The last thing he heard before he and his sister drifted off into unconsciousness, was the mooing of cows, the oinking of pigs, and the clucking of chickens…

Chapter 2: Finding a Familiar Face  
AndiTheAnimalLover

Andi awoke to see a blue sky and strange white clouds floating across it. Was she dreaming, or did they look like Minecraft clouds? Or did her parents find her, took her to the hospital, and she was looking out the window? She lifted her head to check, but all that she saw was green grass, tall trees and animals wandering about. “Where am I?” She wondered. She closed her eyes for a moment, and she rubbed her head. It felt foreign, strange, and… blocky? She felt her fingers. They were square too. “What?” She said weakly. She opened her eyes, and a pig was staring her in the face! The pig oinked, and she jumped up and yelled in surprise. It squealed and ran away. That pig’s face was… blocky? But, that wasn’t possible, unless…

Andi ran down the hill she was atop, tripping on blocks. “What…?” she said aloud. “No. No freakin’ way. I’m… I’m inside of… Minecraft!” Andi just stared at her surroundings. She was inside Minecraft! But how? Oh, of course. The storm. Lightning must have struck her house. But, that didn’t explain why she was inside her brother’s computer. “What if it crashes, or the power goes out, or…” A thousand possibilities ran through her mind, each one making her more afraid. No. She had to stop thinking about the what if, and focus on the now.” It was a tip she learned from reading some Minecraft books written by Mark Cheverton. Actually, come to think of it, this same exact thing happened to Gameknight999. He got trapped in Minecraft too. But, anyway, she and her brother had a habit of taking things from books and integrating it into their lives. And at this juncture, she wished she had read Hatchet, because she could use some survival tips right about now. Andi knew what to do, but she couldn’t call the facts from her memory. Suddenly it came to her. “Wood. I need wood.” She started walking to the forest that was right across from her. She started punching trees down with her fists; she didn’t have an axe yet.

As she cut down multiple trees, memories of her family came to mind, especially her brother Spencer. Or DJRECNEPS321, as he was called in Minecraft. That was his gamertag. Andi’s real name was Yolanda, and her gamertag was AndiTheAnimalLover. Andi was her nickname in real life. And RECNEPS spelled backward is SPENCER. She wondered if DJ was in Minecraft with her. After all, they were both connected to the computer when they got sucked into this whole mess. She wondered if and when she saw him, what skin he was wearing. She wondered what skin she was wearing. Andi looked down at herself and saw extremely long and wavy lavender hair, with a mint streak down the left side. She was surprised she hadn’t tripped on it by now. It reached down to her ankles. Speaking of clothes, she was wearing a neon green crop top, and neon pink shorts. Her black boots stretched up to her knees, and her hands had those kinds of gloves with no fingers. Andi called them texting gloves. On her head was a neon pink headband. She took it off and tied her hair back with it. She would braid it later.

After she had gathered almost almost a stack of wood, Andi noticed it was getting dark. That was bad; nighttime is monster time. She had yet to create a house. She quickly got to work making a crafting table. Opening her inventory was strange. She didn’t know how she did it, but she somehow made her table and placed it down on the ground. She made a sword, a pickaxe, an axe, and a shovel. Hoes were useless, unless you really like farming. She mined up the crafting table with her axe, and went to the side of the hill that she spawned atop. But as soon as she broke the first block, she heard a groan. It wasn’t from a zombie, but a person. “That sounded like Spencer!” Andi thought. She heard it again, and she followed it to the source. Her brother was lying on the ground next to a tree! Well, she had the answer to her skin question she asked herself earlier. He was wearing a skin that made him look like a ninja. (She wondered what he looked like without the mask.) His gamertag was floating above his head in the little green box she had so often seen when she was just a normal user. She quickly knelt next to him and said, “Spencer, DJRECNEPS321? Hey bro. Can you hear me? What happened? Are you okay?” Another moan escaped him, and he opened his eyes a slit.  
“Wha… What happened? Where are we? And… why do you look like you have your Minecraft skin on?” He sat up a bit, and looked around. “Oh… crud. Are we were I think we are?” Andi answered,  
“Yes we are. Now come on. I found a place to build a house.” She helped him get up, and they walked to the hill. She gave half of her wood to him, and set down her crafting table. As DJ crafted, Andi dug a hole in the side of the hill. When DJ was done, he mined up the crafting table and set it in their temporary home. Luckily, Andi found some coal while she was mining, and she made a couple of torches. She placed them in the room, and she sealed the door with pieces of cobblestone.

“Now, can you please tell me what’s going on? Why are we inside Minecraft? And, how exactly did we get here, Yolanda?” DJ asked, clearly ticked.  
“First, I have no idea why we’re here. Second, I think it had something to do with that storm. And third, don’t use real names online remember? I’m not Yolanda anymore. I’m Andi. And your DJ, not Spencer.” DJ rolled his eyes, the only thing that was visible. His ninja mask covered most of his face.  
“Fine, Andi. So… now what…? Do we…” Andi pulled out her pickaxe in one hand, her shovel in the other.  
“We mine. That’s what we do in Minecraft. Right bro?” Andi couldn’t see it, but she felt DJ smiling at her.  
“Right sis.” He said. He got out his shovel and pickaxe as well.

Chapter 3: Mining with Googlies  
DJRECNEPS321

DJ and Andi mined down for a while. Not straight down, mind you, but a staircase. They dug two separate passageways, right next to each other, so they could see each other, but not get in each others way. Andi started humming a song, something DJ recognized as the theme song from Iron Man: Armored Adventures. She watched that show a lot. For some reason, he could remember the words. Something like… “He’s a man on a mission. With armor of high tech ammunition. Trapped on the edge of an endless game, his teenage life will never be the same. In a dangerous world, he does all he can; he’s Iron Man! Iron Man!” Oh great. Now DJ had it stuck in his head. He hummed along with Andi, and she started to sing the words to herself. DJ suddenly switched the song to Call Me Maybe, and they both laughed. As they did, DJ heard laughter. Not their laughter, but someone else’s. It was like it was inside his head, but it seemed like he was hearing it too. Andi apparently heard the same thing, because she stopped mining and asked, “What’s that?” DJ stopped too and shrugged.  
“I dunno. Probably just that glitch messing with our brains. We did just get zapped into my computer, ya know. Actually, I’m kinda surprised that you’re not freaking out right now. Usually, you’d be going crazy.” It was true. If this was any other situation, Andi would probably going absolutely mental.  
“Yeah. It’s weird, but I kinda hear this music in my head. For lack of a better term, it calms me… I guess. I know it sounds strange.” DJ frowned.  
“No it’s not.” As soon as Andi had started talking about it, he heard it. It was somber, soothing, and… strangely happyish?  It was hard to explain. “Huh. Weird.” DJ thought. “Whatever.”  He and Andi were probably losing it, but DJ tried not to think about that. Instead, he focused on mining.

After about two more minutes of digging down, Andi ran into a cave. “Hey bro; a cave.” She said. DJ stopped mining and said,  
“Oh. Good.” They got out their swords and entered the cave. Andi put down her crafting table and made two stone swords and two stone pickaxes. She gave one of each to DJ, and mined up the crafting table. Andi handed eight torches to DJ, and the duo went down deeper into the flesh of Minecraft, placing torches as they go.

After walking a couple steps, DJ pulled out his pickaxe and mined up some coal he found. Andi did the same with the iron right next to it. Together they gathered it all, and they, placed it in their inventories. They went down farther, and gathered up all the materials as they went. After they had been going down for a bit, they heard growls coming from up ahead. Not someone growling, but something. It was a sorrowful wail, filled with hatred with all living things. It would kill someone for only one reason; it lived. The moan belonged to a zombie.  DJ shifted his pickaxe from his right hand to his left, and pulled out his sword with his newly freed hand. It was his job to protect his sister. He had always asked his parents for more responsibility, and finally they had given him some. “Take care of your sister when I’m not around.” He had said. DJ was planning on carrying out the order. “Stay here.” He told Andi.  
“It’s just one zombie. No way.” She replied.  
“Yes way. Dad told me to take care of you when he’s not here, and that’s what I’m gonna do. Now stay put.” Andi crossed her arms, rolled her eyes, and sighed.

DJ took out his torches and placed one in front of him. He kept moving in this fashion toward the the zombie, using the growls and the scuffling of feet as his guide. He eventually reached it, but what he found was not what he expected. The zombie looked real! Instead of blunt stubs for hands, at the end of each arm was five pointed claws, razor sharp and waiting to slice and pierce flesh. It was a sickly emerald color, and was clothed in the traditional zombie garb of light blue shirt and navy pants. It looked like a zombie Steve. (Apparently, when you get sucked into Minecraft, everything is more HD) It noticed DJ and moaned. It charged.  DJ pulled out his wooden sword, and switched it for his pickaxe. Now he had two swords; a stone one in his right, a wooden one in the other. When the zombie saw this, he knew this wasn’t just some user out to mine. It looked right above his head, and then higher. It gasped and looked surprised. DJ didn’t know what the zombie was looking at, but he intended to use it to his advantage. He sprinted towards it, and cut at it with both of his swords. DJ didn’t take any fighting or self-defense lessons, but he seemed to anticipate it’s moves without even trying. Every time he sliced it, it flashed red. But, even with his new-found super power, he still got sliced once. The zombie slashed at his skin, and claws found flesh. DJ cried out in pain. It hurt. A lot. But while it was distracted by the screaming echoes, DJ landed the final blow, and the zombie fell over. It disappeared with a pop, and left behind some XP, rotten flesh, and strangely a carrot. The XP immediately moved closer to DJ without him doing anything, and it flowed into him. He suddenly felt slightly stronger. Picking up the carrot and placing it into his inventory, he turned around only to see Andi peeking around the corner watching him. He jumped in surprise.  
“I thought I told you to stay put!” DJ said angrily.  
“I know, but I heard you scream. I wanted to… make sure you were okay.” She replied meekly.  
“Oh. Fine, but don’t sneak up on me again. Got it?” He said. Andi nodded, but with an annoyed look on her face.  
“I’m not a baby, ya know.” She said as they resumed mining.  
“Yeah I know. But is it okay to protect my sister from zombie slash marks that are actually painful? When I got sliced, it really hurt.” Andi replied,  
“Well, that rules out the dream theory. Are you okay?” DJ nodded.

Suddenly, as they turned a corner, a whole handful of zombies emerged from the darkness and jumped out in front of them! DJ and Andi drew both of their swords. “Oh crud.” DJ thought. For some reason, it seemed like the thought was in the front part of his square mind instead of the back.  
“You got that right.” Someone said. It seemed like it was inside of his head! And, it sounded like Andi! They looked at each other, and DJ knew what happened. But DJ didn’t have time to fully register it. The zombies attacked.  
“Andi, get back!” He said as he chopped at the first zombie within his reach. When he turned around to see if she had done as he had told, he saw her hitting a skeleton that had come to the aid of the zombies. She smiled and said,  
“Fat chance.” DJ was scared, annoyed, and proud at the same time. Scared, because he didn’t want his sister getting hurt. Annoyed, because she had payed no attention to his instruction again, and proud because she was risking her own safety to help DJ. He wondered when she had gotten so brave.

DJ and Andi sliced, diced, and dodged until all of the offending entities were destroyed. They only received minor wounds. Luckily, in Minecraft, there was no such thing as blood. Even the most fatal of injuries would not draw even a single drop of it.  
“Wow. Just wow. It was like, the mobs were… alive?” Andi said. “They moved too accurately for any normal AI.” DJ nodded.  
“Yah. It’s kinda creeping me out. And not just that. It’s like I can anticipate their moves before even they make them. It’s freaky.” Andi said.  
“Yeah, me too. C’mon. Lets get outta here and go raid a village or something. I’m pretty sure we’ve exhausted this cave of all it’s resources.” DJ answered,  
“Agreed.”  He and Andi went back to their tunnel that they arrived there in and entered it. As Andi headed back up, DJ placed four dirt blocks in front of the entrance so any other googlies couldn’t get in and attack them. They headed up the staircase they had carved and arrived in the room Andi had cut out. They took all the torches from the wall and opened up their little hidey hole. When they were out, Andi replaced the blocks. After that was done, they climbed up the hill their temporary house was caved into. DJ stared off into the distance. “There. Look.” He pointed to a village on the horizon.  
“Good. Let’s start there.” Andi said. DJ just realized it, but they weren’t talking like kids. They acted more like adults.  
“Probably the result of us being trapped in a game with no way out.” He thought. Thoughts… DJ remembered something. “Hey sis, any idea how we could like… telepathically talk to each other? It’s like we heard each others thoughts back there in the mine.” Andi just shrugged.  
“Probably a glitch. Cone on, lets go.” DJ nodded, and the two siblings set out for the village.

Chapter 4: The Village Revelation  
AndiTheAnimalLover

The duo made their way down the hill, and they sprinted towards the village. Along the way, DJ killed a couple pigs and cows. They couldn’t see them, but they both felt that their health and hunger bars were low. Andi hated it, since her gamertag said she loved animals, after all. That’s why she made DJ do it. It was ironic, that while she hated animals getting killed, she loved meat. DJ, on the other hand, had no problem murdering defenseless entities. It was creepy.

They finally got to the village. It looked peaceful, with the villagers going about their jobs. But still, as they approached it, they got their swords out, just in case. But only one sword this time. When they entered it, the villagers looked at them. Andi was surprised to see that they weren’t all identical. One had brown hair, one had blue eyes, another had a scar on his right hand, and all of them wore something that was a little different. The NPC’s stared at the space above the sibling’s  heads, and then up, just like that zombie had did to DJ. Suddenly, they had a look of surprise on their face, and they whispered, “The beam, the beam… it’s not there!” or, “Where’s the beam! They don’t have one!” Andi and DJ looked at each other in complete confusion. Villagers could talk? But how? Were they alive? “Hm.” One of the villagers broke free of the crowd, and disappeared into a building. When he came back out, two more villagers followed. One was a woman with long salt and pepper hair, and the other was a man with short, cropped gray hair. They both had the same garb; white with black around the edges. Andi had never seen any villager wear it before.

When the duo reached Andi and DJ, they stopped and looked at them. “Why is everyone staring at us?” Andi said, a little annoyed. They ignored the siblings, and the woman said,  
“DJRECNEPS321, AndiTheAnimalLover, come with us.” Her voice was scratchy, like she lost it not long ago.  
“Yeah? Why should we? And, how did you know our names?” DJ asked with an edge in his voice. The woman laughed.  
“One question at a time. Your names are floating above your head, users. But, you do not have a beam, so, in fact, you are not users. You are something else.” Andi cocked her head.  
“A beam? What’s that?” The man replied,  
“A beam is what connects all players to the server. It looks like one that comes from a beacon. Without a beam, people would not be able to play Minecraft.”  
“Why can’t we see it?” Andi asked. The man replied,  
“You can, but users can’t. Since you are obviously not playing Minecraft, you can see it. Only things inside the game can see them. It is curious why you are not playing the game, but you are rather inside it.” DJ said,  
“Well, we technically are inside it. Lightning struck power lines outside our house in the physical world and somehow, we ended up here. Oh, sorry. You probably don’t know what power lines are.”

The villagers started whispering again. “Lightning… lightning…”  Andi asked,  
“What? What’s so important about the lightning?” The man and woman looked at each other.  
“Come. We will tell you all.” DJ whispered to Andi,  
“C’mon.” Andi pulled him aside and said,  
“Woah, hit the brakes bro. We have no idea who or… what they are, and you’re just gonna blindly follow them somewhere? Am I the only one with doubts about this plan?” DJ answered,  
“Cool it Andi. I just wanna see what the heck they’re talking about. And besides, what reason do they have to hurt us?” Andi said reluctantly,  
“Ok… but if they ambush us, don’t say I didn’t warn you.” Andi was having serious doubt’s about these villagers, but she trusted her big brother. He had to know what he was doing, right?

DJ and Andi pushed through the crowd to reach the man and woman. When they got to them, the whole group went to a blacksmith’s shop. They entered, and the woman motioned for Andi and DJ to sit.

“My name is Beth, and this is my brother, Charles.” Beth said.  
“You can call me Chuck.” Chuck said. Beth continued,  
“Yes, Chuck. Anyway, there is a prophecy. It states that users will come to Minecraft by way of lightning. They will have special lightning powers that they will use to bring balance to the servers by turning back the tide of monsters. Lately they have become more and more aggressive in their attacks, and we have been nearly helpless to stop it. Our arms are linked together, as you can see, so we cannot build defenses or craft weapons.” Andi said,  
“Well, that’s a problem. Wait, did you say powers?” Andi suddenly got very excited. She had dreamed of having super powers ever since she saw Spider-Man and a bunch of other Marvel superhero movies. Beth nodded,  
“Yes, I did. You can call on lightning to set fire to your enemies.” DJ looked at his hands and said,  
“Cool! So were like, real live Thors. Except you’re a girl, sis. Ha, I’m Thor and your, uh… Thorina?” Andi laughed.  
“Yeah, I guess so. But I am not calling myself that.” DJ laughed as well.  
“Excuse me, but, who is… Thor?” Chuck asked.  
“It doesn’t matter. Beth, continue.”  Andi replied.  
“Oh, yes. Well, we cannot make defenses or weapons, so we rely on our wooden doors to shield us. But now, you are here. The Lightning Men will protect us!” Beth said. She sounded relieved. DJ looked confused.  
“Um… ok…?” He said. “Do you have any idea how we control our powers?” Chuck shook his head.  
“Great. We have powers, but we don’t know how to use them.” Andi said, disappointed. She was hoping she could test them out. And, what if mobs attacked, and she didn’t have her weapons? This was a problem.  
“Well, at least we have them.” DJ said. He was always an optimist. Sometimes, it was a good thing, and sometimes it was bad. “Man, if Mom and Dad knew we had powers, they would seriously freak.” Andi knew what he meant. Mr. and Mrs. Davidson always wanted their kids to have a normal life. Something happened in their childhoods that made them afraid of difference, and they never wanted it to happen again. Andi had no idea what, and her parents wouldn’t tell her. The only reason she knew about it was because she had overheard them talking about it one night. It was weird, and a little bit creepy. What had happened?

Suddenly, a flash of light erupted outside of the window. Andi, DJ, Chuck, and Beth looked out to see a figure lying on the ground, just like DJ had. They all rushed out of the blacksmith’s house, and ran towards the crowd that had  gathered. Andi pushed her way through the villagers and reached the side of the person. From what she could gather, the person was a girl, and she had super long, super curly red hair. She almost looked like…  
“Mom?” Andi said in astonishment. She turned her over so she could see her face. Yep, it was definitely her mom. Andi yelled, “DJ! GET OVER HERE!” He pushed through the crowd, kneeled kext to Andi and their unconscious mother, and said,  
“Wassup sis?” He suddenly recognized who was laying in front of him. “Mom?” Andi nodded. “But… how did she get in Minecraft? They weren’t playing it. Oh, they must have been touching something electric, and since my computer was plugged in…” Andi nodded again, acknowledging what DJ said, and comprehending it. Suddenly, their mom groaned.  
“What… what happened? Where am I?” She opened her eyes. “And, who are you two?” Andi said gently,  
“Yolanda and Spencer Mom. We’re inside of Minecraft.” Her eyes widened, and she said sort of weirdly,  
“You mean that game you and Spencer play all the time?” Andi said,  
“Yep.” She sat up. The whole crowd was staring at them.  
“Oh gosh. Who are these people?” DJ said,  
“They’re from this village.” Beth and Chuck pushed through the crowd and approached the trio. Chuck gave a cold stare. Andi said,  
“Hey hey, chillax dude. This is our mom. And, since when did you play Minecraft? You have a gamertag, iHackLife.” She winked.  
“I don’t play. Maybe the game picks a random name or something. Or maybe, if I did play Minecraft, this is what my gamertag would be. I don’t know. But, call me Glitch, I guess. Saying iHackLife every time you talk to me would get really annoying, really fast.” DJ said,  
“Okay Glitch. Where’s Dad? Do you know if he came through or not? Were you two touching anything plugged in?” Glitch answered,  
“Okay, one, your father was in the den, two, I have no idea if he did or not, and three, yes he was. He was writing an e-mail to your teachers on the desktop.”DJ asked,  
“You mean that crappy old dinosaur of a computer?” Glitch, looking a little annoyed and offended said,  
“Yes, that one. It has to be plugged in.”

Suddenly, a flash of light came from outside the village. It looked like the flash that happened when Glitch entered Minecraft. “That must be Dad!” Andi exclaimed. She, DJ, Glitch, Beth, and Chuck all ran towards the flash. When they arrived, they found their dad face down in the grass. Glitch quickly ran over to him, and turned him over.
