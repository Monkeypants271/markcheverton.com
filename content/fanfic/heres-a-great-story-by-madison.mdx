## The Vanishing Villagers

By:Madison Snell

“I was just told that another whole village was spotless, but all the villagers are gone and all their stuff is still there,” Hunter said as she approached Crafter. Crafter started to think.

“Where is the village located?” Crafter asked.

“The next village to the left. But the ones to the right, in front, and behind us were abandoned too,” Herder said as he ran to Hunter and Crafter.

“Oh no! We could be next,” Crafter said.

“What do you mean?” Herder asked.

“Well, think of it, no damage to the walls or the houses, but all the villagers are gone. but their stuff is still there. So they’re getting taking, but all the villages around us are getting attacked so we could be next,” Crafter answered “We need to prepare,” Crafter said.

“Herder, go get all the dogs you have and go tame more. Crafter, me and you will warn the entire village and tell them to be on guard,” Hunter said.

“What do you want me to do?” said a voice next to Hunter. As Hunter looked down she saw Sticher, her little sister.

“Can you go collect wood to make sticks?” Hunter asked.

Then Sticher ran off to get the wood. When night came the village did what they did every night, defend, but then a monster came out of the woods.

“Crafter I need you, Herder, Woodcutter, and Sticher,” Hunter said.

“But Hunter, Sticher is not back yet,” Crafter said.

But just then the monster somehow got in without braking anything. As they took aim at the monster it grabbed everyone. When Sticher got back to the village it was day. She walked through the gates and found signs that said, “Bring the user-that-is-not-a-user to my town.”

Sticher had to find GameKnight999 so he can help find his friends.  
“Monet113 go to the left not the right,”  GameKnight999 said.

“What do I do again GameKnight999?” MonkyPants271 said.

As Gameknight999 surveyed the area he saw Sticher running to them so they all stopped what they were doing. When Sticher got to them she placed signs that said, “a monster has taken ever villager in minecraft and needs GameKnight999, Monet113, and MonkyPants271 to help get every one back.”

As GameKnight999 read the signs he said, “Ok everyone meet me in the basement”.

A minute later Tommy, Jenny, and their father were all in the basement.

“We need to help Sticher,” Jenny said. “I know dad, can I have Shawny over to look after us?” GameKnight999 said,

“Good thinking son.” Their father said,

“Great I’ll text him to come over and tell him what is going on.  He’s on his way,” GameKnight999 said.

As Jenny turned on the digitizer and ran next to the group of heroes a big flash of light spread around the room and when it faded they found themselves standing in the village and in front of them were chests filled with tools and armor. When Sticher ran to the trio, she told them everything that happened. As they set off Monet113 wouldn’t stop making Sticher laugh and smile so she can feel better.

“The first thing we need to do is find the town and figure out what town it is. Sticher did you see the monster at all?” GameKnight999 said.

“No I didn’t, but I did hear the villagers yell zombiepigman,”Sticher said.

“GameKnight999, what about the nether fortress?” MonkyPants271 said.

“Great thinking, but we don’t have a portal,” GameKnight999 said.

Just then a chest appeared and inside was obsidian, flint, and steel.

“Thanks, Shawny,” GameKnight999 thought to his friend.

“Great let’s go save the villagers,” MonkyPants271 said.

As Monet113, MonkyPants271, Sticher, and GameKnight999 stepped out the port and saw all the villagers. When Sticher stepped out of the portal Hunter ran to her.

“I’m so glad your safe”, Hunter said as she hugged Sticher.

“Hey what about us?’’ MonkyPants271 said.

“Hey it’s the hero trio, MonkyPants271, Monet113, and GameKnight999,” Hunter said.

“What’s going on?” Monet113 asked.

“The zombiepigman have taken all the villagers and are banishing them in the nether while they take overworld, the last four people on the list are you guys,” Crafter answered as he and Herder walked to them.

When Monet113, MonkyPants271, GameKnight999, and Sticher heard this they all took out their weapons to get ready for battle. When the pigmen came back they brought zombies with them.

“You cannot stop the invasion user-that-is-not-a-user,” a pigman said.

“Who are you?” Monet113 asked. “I am Zing-Ting?” the pigman answered.

“Villagers GameKnight, Monet, MonkyPants, and Sticher have brought weapons and armor for battle.  Come get some and attack,” Hunter said as she took charge.

When everyone got their weapons and armor they attacked.

“Hunter, Sticher, Crafter, Monet, MonkyPants I have an idea. Hunter, Sticher, and Monet you three distract Zing-Ting, while me, Crafter, and MonkyPants, get everyone through the portal.  Then when it’s just us we attack from all sides and once he’s defeated we go through the portal and go back to the village. Got it?” GameKnight999 asked.

“Got it!”  Everyone replied.

As Sticher, Hunter, and Monet drew Zing-Ting’s extension the others got everyone through the portal. After a few minutes everyone was through the portal.

“Ha! You think you can stop  Zing-Ting ha,’’ Zing-Ting said.

“Come on guys,” Hunter said as everyone got to their stations.

“MonkyPants don’t forget to aim carefully and Monet aim to the left like we practiced,” GameKnight said. “Got it thanks!” Monet and MonkyPants said.

As they were about win the battle Zing-Ting teleported away and next to Sticher then grabbed her then teleported next to Monet and grabbed her with his other hand.

“One shot and Zing-Ting kills two girls,” Zing-Ting said.

GameKnight looked at Monet and Sticher and saw no fear. He knew they had a plan. Sticher motioned to Hunter and then she ran off.  Then Monet motioned to MonkyPants and he started to distract Sing-Ting by aiming his bow and arrow at him. A couple of seconds later Sing-Ting flashed red and disappeared with a pop then GameKInight saw Hunter with her sword in hand behind Sing-Ting’s death spot. It was clear that MonkyPants was distracting Sing-Ting while Hunter sneaked up behind him to make the kill. When they got back to the village, GameKnight asked when they had come up with that plan.

“Last time you were here for the last battle when Monet was held tight in the grip of the ender men king Feyd,” Hunter responded.

After they said their good byes  there was a flash of light and Jenny, Tommy, and their dad were back in the physical world. As the light faded they could see Shawny. Once they said hi, Shawny wouldn’t stop asking what happened.
