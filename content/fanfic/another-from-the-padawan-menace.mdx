Zombie-Hunter book one, part one  
Gameknight found himself on the ground. As he looked up he saw that he was inside of an NPC house. Gameknight realized that is was Digger’s house. He ran outside, only to see that there were armored zombies everywhere. Oh,no, Gameknight thought. He saw a group of zombies run at Hunter. Hunter tried to shoot arrows at the zombies, but they were just to powerful. The decaying creatures piled on Hunter and when they got off, a zombie-villager stood where Hunter was. Nooooo, Gameknight thought. As he ran into the large stone tower, he pulled out his pickaxe and broke the block that covered the Crafting Camber. Gameknight ran into the chamber and saw NO zombies, NO zombie NPCs, and no dropped items. He turned to see Stitcher, Hunter’s sister, and Crafter. “Guys…”Gameknight started. “We know, zombies are attacking.” Crafter said. “But it’s worse than that, Hunter got turned into a zombie!” Gameknight999 replied. Stitcher froze. Hunter was the only thing that Stitcher had left of her family. “NOOOOO!” She yelled. “It’s true, but I have a plan…” Gameknight said as he explained his risky, but possible, plan.
