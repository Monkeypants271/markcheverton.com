Gameknight stepped into the village and was filled with horror when he saw how bad it looked. “I wonder how Crafter is,” Gameknight asked himself. Just then, he remembered the crafting chamber. “I’d better get down there,” he said.  
When Gameknight got to the crafting chamber, he asked Crafter, “Are you OK? What happened?” Crafter replied, “Don’t worry, Gameknight. I’m fine. You see, there was this zombie attack and we weren’t ready, so I got everyone to the crafting chamber. Since mobs don’t know about the chamber, we were safe down here. It’d hurt if they discovered this place.”
