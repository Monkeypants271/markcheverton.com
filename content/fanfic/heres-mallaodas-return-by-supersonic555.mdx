**Malloada’s Return**

    CHAPTER 1 Findings  
    Mallaoda saw endermen teleport out of the void.  Mallacoda threw an ender pearl he had taken earlier.  He arrived on an outer island, just as Erebus went through the portal.  “You have not yet seen my true wrath Erebus!”  Yelled Mallacoda.  Suddenly, a gigantic structure’s shadow loomed over Mallacoda.  It was a huge purple tower, with a floating pirate ship next to it!  Floating over to it  Mallacoda decided to wait for a way to the exit portal on the ship.  He stayed in the ship.
