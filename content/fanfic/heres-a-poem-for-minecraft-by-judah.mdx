## **FOR MINECRAFT**

The battle cry I hear

as the field is overtaken

by Xa-Tull with his broken ear

Malacoda is flying

Through the stormy skies

While Shavalack is searching

with her blood red eyes

Erebus is teleporting

out of Feyds way

while Charybdis is shooting

His fireballs all day

Oxus is exploding

And reforming every which way

While Reaper is shooting

His arrows in May

Oh the battle for Minecraft with the monster kings

But I am their bane.

For I’m the User king.

-   Gameknight999
