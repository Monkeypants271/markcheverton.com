Hey everyone! So a little while ago I got the idea for a Gameknight999 trivia page. Thank you VvMist247 for posting that comment on Why does everyone love to hate Erebus so much?about quizzing you on the books that gave me inspiration!

Ok, so this is how it is going to work. The first ten (10) questions will look normal. These will be easy. Then, on level two (2), they will be in italics. These questions will be medium. Level three (3) will be the hardest and will be in bold. There will be thirty questions in all. When you get your answers to some of the questions put it in the comments below.

Ex.

1.  I ❤️ Gameknight999
2.  Tree
3.  Johnny Appleseed
4.  Four score and seven years ago…

In about two weeks (depends on when Mr. Cheverton can get it posted) I will post the answers.

Rules-

Please, DON’T do all of them at the same time! Do about three at a time. If you are doing answers, don’t do one that ten people have answered. Do one that most people haven’t done. My advice? For answering the first time around, probably just write or type down your answers and then scroll really fast to the comment box so you don’t see anything.

If you want to do your own trivia, then post it below. You can do it over Gameknight999, the Far Lands books, Giants, or even somebodies fan fiction! Just make sure everybody knows what book or series or post you are talking about. And no cheating! You have to already know the answer. No scrambling back to the books to make sure someone is right!

All right! Let’s see how this goes! I think this is going to be really great, and if everyone seems to like it I will post some more! Remember, this is just a game. It doesn’t matter what you got. Just have fun!😁
