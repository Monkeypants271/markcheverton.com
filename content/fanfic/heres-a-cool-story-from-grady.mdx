Prologue

            Lets take it back to when Herobrine was in pig form. Herobrine infected the Ender Dragon egg, but what he did not realize is that he also infected Endermenbrine who was working on a nearby Endermen . The shadow crafter was suddenly struck by a bolt of lighting so hot and harsh he felt like it was burning his entire being. After the lightning bolt struck, Endermenbrine felt strong, almost as powerful as The Maker himself. Endermenbrine watched and waited to see what Herobrine would do in his war against The-User-That-Is-Not-A-User. On the shore of The Great Lava Ocean, when the Final Battle was unraveling, Endermenbrine was watching from a distance. After the maker entered The Gateway of Light, he new he would finish what The Maker started, to rid this world of the filthy NPCs.  Endermenbrine fashioned a new name for himself, one that would make his enemies cower in fear. He named himself Enderbrine, and he would finish what The Maker started.    

Chapter One

 As the light faded around The-User-That-Is-Not-A-User, he found himself in his familiar spawn. The waterfall flowing down behind, the mountains looming all around him, like  ancient guardians. Gameknight999 headed for the tower of dirt blocks with his torch on top. As The-User-That-Is-Not-A-User ran past the water fall he grinned remembering how it had saved his life the first time he had been pulled into the game. Gameknight999 dug up the blocks of dirt revealing his hidey-hole as he expected. The-User-That-Is-Not-A-User opened the chest inside and took out some food, iron armor,  an iron sword, and some tools. Now he was ready, Gameknight999 stepped out of his hidey-hole, put the blocks back and headed towards the village. As the sun went down The-User-That-Is-Not-A-User saw that there was a lot more endermen then usual. Gameknight999 frowned thinking the last thing he wanted was another war. With the familiar watch tower in the distance, Gameknight sprinted towards the village. “The-User-That-Is-Not-A-User is here!” one of the sentries cried. As Gameknight999 walked into the village, he saw that there were still two watchers in the tower.
