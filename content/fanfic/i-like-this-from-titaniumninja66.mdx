What he saw was this: there was an npc in the corner holding an enchanted diamond sword in 1 hand and Gameknight’s friend Crafter in the other! There was a moment’s silence. Then, all of a sudden the npc was gone! And taking Crafter with him. There was no trace where the mysterious npc had gone except a book.

Gameknight picked it up and read it. It said:  
Dear Gameknight,  
I have taken your friend Crafter and put him in the custody of 1 of my generals who is looking forward to seeing you again. You have 5 days to rescue your friend but if you fail, Crafter will die.Herobrine.

Gameknight froze. He thought back to when he had to fight Herobrine. He had almost lost but he had learned that when Herobrine killed something he morphed into it. So they had turned him into a pig. But apparently Herobrine had escaped. Then he heard the banging of an iron sword on a chestplate. The invasion alarm. He thought, “Why would there be an invasion alarm? The war is over.” He ran to the cobblestone ramparts and was shocked at what he saw: thousands of armored zombies and skeletons in enchanted gold and iron armor attacking the main gate. Fear ran down his spine but reluctantly he drew his enchanted Diamond sword and lept into the battle.
