**Intro**

**Herobrine.**

Lots of people say he’s real.

Others say the opposite.

The people who do believe in him believe in a way that is not true. These people use photoshop and other apps to make pictures that have him in it. Most of these are fake.

Many people believe that he is the demon of Minecraft. This much is true, but the story of how it came to be this way is false. At least all of the other ones on the internet that people make up.

**The True Story of the Herobrine Origin**

This is the true tale of how Herobrine came to be the way he is today.

It all started with him and Notch. 

Many people say they were brothers, but they weren’t even distantly related, let alone having the same parents.

The two lived in different dimensions entirely.

Notch was the one and only person who actually created Herobrine.

Of course, Herobrine’s name wasn’t Herobrine.

It was Steve.

There were two original Minecraft characters. Steve and Alex. Steve was cautious about his surroundings. He didn’t trust anyone. Not a single soul. He didn’t trust anything.

One day, Something changed. A new person was in the world. Steve’s own cubic world that he shared with Alex. Minecraft had finished construction. Notch had introduced it to the world, and people were playing the game.

After four more users had joined the server, it was too much for him to handle. Untrustworthyness took over Steve and he fled. 

He ran from Notch, Alex, the users, everything. He ran from fear, trying to get himself to safety – if there was any at all. 

After Steve had ran, Notch – in fear people would notice the mishap and confront him – created a new steve, who was much better than the old one. This Steve was confident, strong, and trusted everyone he met, giving him friends and allies.

However, the first Steve – Herobrine – had run away to a cave – a cave that nobody knew of, including Notch. He stayed in that cave for many years before anyone noticed him. 

The first Herobrine sighting happened when Herobrine went out of his cave for the second time to scavenge food, and was seen. The User who saw him gave him the name, Herobrine, which goes off of the way the original Steve attacks. He attacks exactly like the current Steve does, but with hatred and fear instead of hope and confidence. 

Herobrine attacked the first user he saw out of fear that he would be exposed and lose all safety in his life. But a few years later, Herobrine encountered Steve, and realized Notch had replaced him. He attacked out of rage and pure hatred. Steve and Herobrine have been enemies since their first encounter with each other to this day. Herobrine today roams the multiple servers in Minecraft, trying to stay hidden. Few people have seen him, but some screenshots of him are real, but only very few specific ones. He attacks a user if he appears on the screen, knowing he’s been seen when he doesn’t wish to be.

_**The True Story of Herobrine’s White Eyes**_

Upon staying in his cave for years, Herobrine’s eyes adapted. Instead of his pupils growing larger to let light in, they produced their own light, shining like flashlights. This light that emitted from his eyes allowed Herobrine to see in the dark. This happened because there was no light for his pupils to let in. Herobrine’s pupils and irises disappeared so that the white light could fully shine from his eyes. Many people believe that he has no eyes at all, that his eye sockets glowed for an unknown reason, but he had pupils, they just disappeared to let light out so Herobrine could see in the dark.

_**The Story of Herobrine’s Powers**_

After Herobrine was sighted for the first time, he began to wander the server, afraid of being seen again. Scavenging for weapons, he began to connect with nature. He slipped himself into the elements in hope they could help him. And help him they did. Herobrine can summon lightning, vines, ice, rain, anything you could think of that has to do with the nature around us. 

When Herobrine slipped into nature, he disappeared for quite some time. When he came back, he was different. After all that time, Herobrine was an element of his own. He was a Spirit, a God, a Demon. The elements of Earth, in Minecraft and out, were changed.

Before Herobrine existed, the elements were Fire, Ice, Water, Wind, and Earth. Now that Herobrine has messed with things, the elements are Fire, Ice, Water, Wind, Earth, and Herobrine himself. 

Herobrine is the center element. The center element is the ruler and controller of all elements. Herobrine is using them to protect himself from the world when he does not need to. But he is also using the elements to bring revenge down onto Steve, attempting to wipe him from existence. Herobrine is furious about what happened when he ran and Notch replaced him. He wishes to destroy Steve. Steve the second.
