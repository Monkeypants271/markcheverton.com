Gameknight struggled to get out. But it was helpless. The pull was getting stronger and stronger, choking him.

Then, Gameknight felt a strange shift. Like a slither.

Gameknight thought, then pulled out his sword and stabbed his chest!

But, Gameknight felt nothing but a jolt in the pull. A translucent red coil flashed red on him, Erebus, and Grieferking The red coil jumped off them and revealed it’s true form. A giant snake!

The monster reptilian camouflaged again, and Gameknight gripped his blade.

“You want some of this?” Gameknight yelled.

“COME ON! LETS DANCE!”
