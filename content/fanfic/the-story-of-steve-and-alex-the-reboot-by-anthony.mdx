Steve and Alex ran to Steve’s house to get some supplies. They each grabbed an iron sword, a full set of iron armor, a bow with the Infinity enchantment, and an arrow. Then they headed out of the village with Watcher, Baker, Digger, Smithy, and Stonecutter. The villagers with them also had iron swords and full sets of iron armor. They proceeded to the hole where Steve had fallen and built steps down. Once everyone was at the bottom of the hole, Steve opened the secret door and followed the tunnel until he reached a cave. He pressed a block sticking out of the wall, then gave each of them a potion of invisibility, except for Alex.

Steve said, “Alex, you need to make sure some of the skeletons see you, but don’t make any noise. We’ll follow behind with our potions of invisibility and attack the skeletons when they get close. Once most of the skeletons are destroyed, we’ll leave two alive and ask them if they know anything. If the first one doesn’t know anything, we’ll ask the second. If the second one doesn’t know anything, we’ll destroy all the skeletons and blow the whole cave up with TNT.”

“Got it,” said Alex. Then Steve and the villagers splashed their potions of invisibility on themselves. The secret door in front of them opened, revealing an area filled with houses and skeletons walking around. Ten skeletons saw Alex and charged at her, but as soon as they were three blocks away, Steve and the villagers destroyed them. Then three more skeletons charged at Alex after noticing her. Steve and the villagers quickly took care of them.

After a few rounds of skeletons charging at Alex and Steve and the villagers destroying them, only one skeleton remained. However, this last skeleton didn’t look like a normal one. It was probably twice the height of a normal skeleton and wore a crown that looked like it was made of golden bones.
