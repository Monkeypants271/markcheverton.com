**Welcome to Minecraft** 

Chapter 1 – A Regular Day

Aiden was just a regular kid, he loved Minecraft, loved food and loved his family, but one day that would all change. He finally finished school, got home and turned on his pc and he started to load Minecraft, while he waited he grabbed a bag of Doritos, and ate them. Then he looked to his left and saw his father’s failed invention; the pixelator it was supposed to pixelate whatever it was pointed at and put it on the computer, Aiden tried using it to make a Minecraft skin once, he pointed the machine at a stick he found one day but all it did was put the stick into the computer’s code it almost worked. Aiden redirected his attention to his computer, which had finished loading Minecraft. He went to his computer, moved the mouse to hover over the play button, and pressed it. It brought him to the menu page, he put his headphones on, connected them to the computer, and decided to make a new world, he set it to hard mode, and named it Welcome to Minecraft. But when he pressed create the pixelator activated, it was plugged in, and the pixelator was aimed towards Aiden, his last thought before he blacked out: Oh No!

Chapter 2 – Trapped in the past

When Aiden finally was able to open his eyes he saw a green grassy blocky world, after a second glance and looking at a tree he realized he was trapped in his favorite game, Minecraft, but there were tons of animals, too many. He thought to himself; wait am I in Minecraft’s past. He then realized he was, he knew that soon there would be hostile mobs out soon, and being in the past they were more powerful than usual, he used his fist to punch some cows for leather and beef, after 3 hits one of the cows died, and he repeated that process 3 more times till he had 5 leather and 6 beef, he would need a base, so he punched some trees till the wood broke, he picked it up after it broke, after that he made a crafting table. He dug down into the ground, made a 3 by 3 wide room and made the ceiling 2 high in one corner he put the crafting table and made a wooden pickaxe in another corner he started mining diagonally, he knew it would be night soon so he sealed the entrance to his hidey-hole. With the cobblestone he had gotten he made a stone pickaxe, sword, and shovel, he then continued mining then he found some coal for torches. “I hope I can get home,” Aiden said to himself, with his crafting table he made a furnace, put some coal in it and put some beef in and it started cooking, with the remaining coal he made torches and placed one in the center of the room. Aiden didn’t realize but he was 400 years back in Minecraft’s timeline, before iron even existed, so he was going to have to survive as long as he could. He used the 5 leather to craft himself a leather helmet, it would have to do. He wondered why he couldn’t find any iron. Then his attention was turned to his hunger, only 3 bars left, he remembered that there was beef in the furnace, he walked over to it and took the beef out. He ate 2 pieces and his hunger regained backup to its maximum 10 bars, he decided to check if it was day yet when he went out he saw a sign that read a troubling message.

Chapter 3 – Entity71

The sign read: Now do you know what’s happening, well if you don’t I have zapped you into the past of Minecraft all the armour you can craft is leather, and the only tools and weapons you can craft is stone and wooden. Also the reason this area is so full of animals is the fact that users and villagers don’t exist, try to get past this age fast, my ‘little friends’ are coming! Also take my pet pig Wilson, he doesn’t like me he may like you better though. Oh and the dinosaurs are coming. -Your captor Entity71 Aiden felt chills down his spine as he read the message, and when he finished he saw a pig come out from behind the sign, then he heard a roaring noise it sounded like A  pterodactyl, he had heard what scientists thought it sounded like, apparently, Minecraft agreed, Aiden grabbed Wilson and hopped into his hidey-hole he covered up the top, and cowered in fear, he was going to need to figure out how to get past ages, to escape the dinosaurs. He then realized this ‘“age’” was pretty much compared to real-life happening 65 million years ago he thought maybe this was based on real-life, and if he progressed through all the ages he could get home! He then turned his attention to the oinking pig next to him, he patted the blocky creature on the head. The pig seemed to enjoy that, at least now he had a pet pig so he wouldn’t be alone.

That is my Minecraft story
