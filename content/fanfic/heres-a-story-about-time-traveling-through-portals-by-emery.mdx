## **_The Portal_**

### **_By: Emery_**

**Chapter one Discovering**

One day a girl named Amira, with nice golden blond hair and blue eyes and a green shirt, and Laura, with silky brown smooth hair with green eyes and a blue shirt, were at the park swinging. Then they went to the jungle gym.  Laura pulled a lever by mistake and they noticed tunnel, so they went down the tunnel and Amira always had a flashlight with her. When they reached the end of the tunnel they saw two round things and two levers. They walked over there and Amira and Laura pulled the silver and gold levers. It was a …PORTAL!?! They freaked out and were being sucked into different portals. Amira was sucked into the future, and Laura was sucked into the past.

**Chapter 2 Amira’s Future Time**

Amira was worried when she turned around she was surprised to see all the flying cars and the weird shaped buildings. Amira was wondering about Laura. But then she saw something in the distance it looked like a person so she walked up to it. It looked like her future self. She asked if it was her future self. She said yes.

Amira was talking to her future self for a while, and she could not stop talking for how awesome it was to be talking to herself.  Amira wanted to leave but she could not find the portal.

Amira saw the end of the ocean and started walking towards it. Amira started to float as the water got  deeper. She saw her best friend Laura was walking toward a little hut then stopped walking and turned and saw Amira. She came running toward her with joy but they could not touch. It was not Laura, it was… V-Tex? He had a black mask with a red X across it. He was standing at the end of the past and future. Amira realized seeing him in the intersection would mean something for her and her friend. Amira was backing up and started running. She saw the portal and Amira ran through it. She wanted to save Laura, but it might be too risky. Amira said, “Thank heavens I’m back home.” She went through the tunnel and back to the jungle gym.

**Chapter three Laura’s Past**

Laura was worried and scared but she saw a little hut. She walked in and there was just a bed, she laid on the black and blue bed and fell asleep.After two hours of sleep she was bored so she walked out of the hut and. It looked like there was something round in the distance walking toward it. It was the portal so she walked through it, and V-Tex crept up to the portal with a daring laugh and shut it off. Laura was in middle of space able to breathe with a little water and very little food. Amira walked down to the portal room and turned the portal back on.The portal turned back on, so Laura walked through the portal, and back in the portal room seeing Amira with happiness.

**Chapter four Together**

When they were back together in the portal room. They were as happy as can be. But they wanted to go in the past and the future together so they did. When they were in the future Laura was screaming for how cool it was. Amira said, “I know it’s really cool because I got to see my future self.”

Laura said, “You have got to be kidding me you should have been sucked in the past cause I wanted to see my future self,” Amira laughed.

Laura yelled, “STOP!”

Amira said, “Fine.” They were walking by the side of the bay and they were talking. Then they went to swim in the sparkling blue shiny water.

When they were done swimming they were completely wet so they went to go dry off. They walked back through the portal and would not stop talking to each other. Amira said, “That was such a great time.”

Laura said, “I know right. We should do it some other time.” But they were not alone.

**Chapter five Not Alone**

Amira and Laura were not alone they were being watched. It was V-Tex he was walking quietly behind Amira.Then he snatched her and went through a portal to the underworld that they did not know about. Laura said, “Amira where are you? You better not be playing around with me.”

Then she saw the underworld portal. Laura said, “OH NO!!!” She ran through the portal and saw Amira tied up she was crying. Amira cried, “ Please help me!”

Laura ran to go get a knife to cut the rope. She ran back through the portal. She ran to go get her knife. When she was back at her house her mom asked where she had been. Laura said, “Mom I need to go. I promise I will be back.

She ran back into the tunnel and back in the portal room. Back through the portal and ran to Amira, and Laura cut the rope and Amira was free.But… V-Tex saw them trying to escape so he ran after them but they ran. But Amira and Laura were too quick and then they escaped. Amira said, “Thank you so much.”

Laura said, “ No problem.” Then they hugged.

**Chapter 6 The Sleepover**

Amira and Laura were having a sleepover they were really excited. They got on their minion pjs. Laura said, “ I am so excited!”

Amira said, “ Me too.” They jumped on Laura’s bed, and then the did a pillow fight.Amira kept laughing Amira said, “ You are the best friend ever!”

Laura said, “You’re the best friend too!”

They got really tired and fell asleep on Laura’s bed.

Then Laura’s Mom came home Laura’s mom said, “ I’m home. Then she saw them sleeping. Laura’s Mom said, “Oh,” then she yawned and fell asleep on the couch.

At 12:00 A.M V-Tex was in the portal room walking through the tunnel and

to the playground. V-Tex said, “Finally I’m back on earth!” He did his daring laugh,and he saw Laura and Amira. He ran through the window and grabbed them. He laughed and Amira and Laura were screaming for Laura’s Mom. Her mom woke up she screamed and called the police.

**Chapter seven Kidnapped**

The police saw V-Tex with Amira and Laura. Amira and Laura both yelled, “HELP US!!!”

The police told him to surrender, but he ran through the tunnel, so the police ran through the tunnel too, but they saw the portals. They were confused so they walked through the future portal but Amira, Laura, and V-Tex was in the past portal. The police saw V-Tex with Amira and Laura in the past when they were by the ocean so they ran through the portal,but V-Tex shut it off, so the police were umm… stuck in space so they were gone. Amira forgot she had a knife with her so she got out the knife and cut his hand V-Tex screamed, “AHHHHHHHH!!” He breathed very heavy, and Amira and Laura escaped so they ran through the portal but the wrong one.

 **Chapter eight Candy Land**

Amira screamed, “OMG SO MUCH CANDY

I THINK I’M GOING TO DIE!” Laura said, “O-O OMG ME TOO!” All they saw was lollipops, Hershey bars

Crunch, and a lot more. So they walked around and then they saw… a cow. It was not just any cow it was a candy cow. It was made with licorice lollipops and Twix, but they did not know that that cow was a camera being controlled by V-Tex. They wanted to eat it but… they didn’t want to hurt a cow, so they walked away and V-Tex shapeshifted into his normal form. Amira and Laura walked through the portal and shut it off. But V-Tex was walking through the portal and now he was stuck in space forever without no food or water, and the candy land portal vanished.s

**Chapter nine End Of Summer**

The next day they went down the portal room and they didn’t see the any of the portals , so they think they defeated V-Tex and they did. They were so happy, so they went to go play at the playground. They went on the swings. Then they just remembered that was the last day of summer. They’re going to 5th grade, but luckily their parents remembered to get their stuff for school. So they went to her house and saw Amira’s mom with news. Amira’s mom said, “ Sweetie I got a new job in New York.”

Amira sadly said, “ Mom I don’t want to move because moving means new schools and new friends.” Amira was so sad and started to cry and hugged Laura.

Amira’s mom said, “ I know but this will help us because we will make more money. ”

Amira yelled, “ I DO NOT WANT TO MOVE!” She ran up to her room opened the door and then slammed it. Amira’s mom told Laura that she could leave, but Laura wanted to stay and talk to her so she did.

Laura said, “ I’m sorry it had to be like this.” Amira didn’t say anything. Laura said, “ why don’t you wanna talk?”

Amira mumbled, “ Because I don’t want to talk cause this is all my fault. Laura asked why? But Amira didn’t talk.

Amira said, “ just get out.”

Laura asked, “ why?”

Amira yelled,” I SAID GET OUT!” So Laura walked out. And Amira asked herself what is wrong with me?

**Chapter ten Packing and Leaving**

Amira started packing to leave but she was still upset to be leaving her home. Amira asked her mom why she wanted to take the job, but Amira’s mom said, “We need to get more space and it would be nice to get a new job.”

Amira walked back upstairs and kept packing. When they were ready to leave she wanted to say goodbye to Laura but Amira’s mom said no.

Amira and her mom got in the car and drove to the airport. They paid the plane tickets for Amira and her mom.  The plane ticket was $200. She was surprised but she still paid the tickets. When they were on the plane Amira was thinking about Laura.

**Chapter eleven Our New Home**

When Amira and her mom got off the plane they drove to their house that they bought. Amira was surprised Amira said, “ Woah this house is huge!”

Amira’s mom said, “ I know it is big and the inside is modern. “ Amira ran in and she just freaked out and ran around the house. Her mom showed Amira her room. Her room had white walls a bed her bed sheet had anchors the anchors were white and it had a blue background. Amira jumped on her bed then after Amira was tired of jumping she sat on her the bed after jumping and then she called Laura.

Laura answered and said , “ Hi Amira how is your new home?”

Amira said, “ IT SOOOOO PRETTY AND MY HOUSE IS HUGE!!!”

Laura said ,” Oh that’s great.” They talked for about 20 minutes. Then they finally hanged up.

**Chapter twelve Amira’s New School 5th Grade**

Amira just remembered that tomorrow was school so Amira got her bag ready and she went to bed

In the morning Amira brushed her hair, brushed her teeth, and got dressed. Amira was nervous to see all the new people. Her mom drove her to school and Amira said, “ Goodbye mom. “ Amira’s mom said goodbye to her too, and drove off to work.

Amira was nervous to even go inside. Amira went inside and everyone looked at her and people were talking about her, so she went into her classroom. Mrs. Klen said, “ Hello students.” Everyone said hello except for Amira.

After several weeks of school Amira made 3 new friends Kalani, Mckenna, and Belle. Amira always played with them at recess. Alway sat by them at lunch. Amira trusted them so did her friends.

Kalani said, “ Oh those girls? They’re the mean ones.”

Amira said ,” Oh. “
