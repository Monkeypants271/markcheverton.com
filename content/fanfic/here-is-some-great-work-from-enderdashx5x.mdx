Prologue

“Stop trying Hunter, were never gonna get out of here.”

Tommy looked at his friend who was just walking into the void of darkness. There was nothing here except a black floor, nine kids, and complete darkness filled with nothing.

“B-But..” Hunter muttered, tears in her eyes. “W-We can’t stop f-fighting f-for…”

“For what?” Topper asked, reapplying the bandages on his left hand. He was given the bandages ever since his animatronic was throw out. Turns out if your animatronic body gets hurt, you do as well.

“For our freedom, Tops,” Filler answered, looking into her twin’s eyes.

“See! Someone knows what we gotta do!” Hunter exclaimed to no one…but to everyone.

There was silence after that. Hunter continued walking into the darkness, but always ending up where she started. Topper and Filler made up more stories to tell themselves, Stitcher and Jenny were talking about older siblings, Digger and Herder were together talking about many things in their lives, and Tommy and Crafter just sat down, waiting for Hunter to stop.

Eventually, she came back, tears blurring her vision. Tommy stood up and walked toward her. He saw tears running down her face, her face red.

Suddenly, she ran into Tommy and broke down in front of everyone. This was rare because was the second oldest and always kept herself together.

“I CAN’T DO THIS ANYMORE!” Hunter sobbed, rapping her arms around the only boy she had different feelings for.

“H-Hunter…” Stitcher stuttered and paced toward her sister.

“I just can’t…” Hunter stopped her heavy breathing and calmed down when Tommy hugged back. He was like a brother to her, and will always be there for her.

She eventually feel asleep with Tommy, Crafter, Stitcher, and Jenny, leaving the other four to talk.

Digger, Herder, Filler, and Topper were the misfit animatronics. Digger’s body allowed him to be seem as a hallucination, Herder’s body doesn’t do much, just heads out balloons to kids, Topper’s suit got thrown out, so no one knows where it is, and Filler’s animatronic is a literal mangle. Her body got torn apart by the children again and again until she was forever broken and left for the children to destroy.

“I can’t believe it’s been seven whole years.” Herder blurted out.

The three looked at him and sighed. It was true, seven years since their lives had been corrupted, destroyed, taken away.

“It still feels like it was yesterday day,” Topper said.

They didn’t talk much after that, just thought about what happened…

\~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The nine kids unlocked the front doors to the pizzeria and walked on. It was 11:30 at night on a Saturday and they were sneaking into a pizzeria.

“This is insane and stupid,” Hunter said looking at Tommy as he put the keys away.

“Well it’s my dad’s place, so it doesn’t really matter,” Tommy clarified and turned to the front desk of the pizzeria. It was so lifeless at night. The only sounds were their foot steps which made the place a lot more creepy.

“Common, let’s look around,” Digger said grabbing Herder’s hand and walking toward the right hallway. The everyone agreed that the two were cute together and would make a great couple.

“Ah, wait, DIGGER!” Topper exclaimed and chased after his older brother with Filler skipping behind him.

Crafter chuckled at the kids. Even though they were two years younger than most of the group, they were more matures than anyone in their grade.

“Tommy?” Jenny asked, bouncing on the balls of her feet.

“Yeah?”

“Can I go and explore with Stitcher?”

“Sure, just don’t get in trouble,”

Jenny and Stitcher squealed like fangirls and ran off to one of the party rooms.

“Guess that leaves us three.”

Hunter, Crafter, and Tommy walked toward the empty stage. They were always curious why there was a huge stage in the pizzeria holding no mascots. Tommy’s dad said that they were panning something special for all the kids, while Tommy’s brother, Hero, said to stop thinking about it and stop sticking his nose where it doesn’t belong. No one liked Hero, he was always the older kid who boss the kids around.

“So…” Crafter said as he stopped and looked both ways. “what do we do now?”

Hunter smirked and ran to the backstage. She found her way up on the stage and yelled, “EVERYONE, I THOUGHT OF SOMETHING TO DO!!”

Eventually the other kids came to the main stage. The all looked at Hunter in confusion.

“Well?” Stitcher asked. “What did you “amazing” brain think of this time, sis?”

Hunter rolled her eyes and put her arms on her hips. “Red Light, Green Light.”

Everyone stood there for a few moments before Digger and Herder went to the back of the room.

“You guys coming?” Digger boomed.

The rest of the kids made it to the back of the room and looked at Hunter.

“Okay, here we go!” the red-head stated and turned around.

“GREEN LIGHT!”

Digger, Herder, and Crafter ran to their left, Jenny and the twins ran to their right, and Tommy and Stitcher ran forward.

Hunter quickly turned around. “RED LIGHT!”

Herder jumper when she yelled and moved from his spot as well as Jenny.

“Back to the back!”

The two went to the back of the room where they started.

“GREEN LIGH-”

Before she could finish “light”, Digger raced past everyone and tagged Hunter.

“No fair!” Hunter pouted and folded her arms.

Suddenly, they heard the front doors of the pizzeria open. They stood in their places frozen, they didn’t know who it was. A thief? Tommy’s dad? One of the employees? They didn’t bother talking about it, they just ran and hid from whoever the person was. Digger and his younger siblings rushed to the back stage, Stitcher and Jenny went into the kitchen, Crafter hid in one of the Party Rooms, Herder ran down the hallway, and Hunter and Tommy hid under a table.

As soon as the two made it under the table, the person’s footsteps could be herd. The two kids held their breaths. Hunter grabbed onto Tommy’s hand and held it tight, he could see fear in the girl’s deep, brown eyes.

The foot step got louder, and soon the person walked down the hallway. Both Hunter and Tommy gasped, it was none other than Tommy’s other brother, Hero.

Hero stood there for a while, then brushed his black hair out of this face and headed down the hallway Herder was in. The two kids looked at each other then silently got out from under the table and followed the older kid.

The Tommy was going to enter the hallway before Hunter grabbed his short and pulled him to the wall.

“Gotta be stealthy,” she said and hugged the wall. Tommy nodded and did the same. The two went down the hallway and we’re going to make a rig turn before Hunter grabbed Tommy’s hand.

“Tom?” Hunter asked, her voice cracking.

“Y-Yes?” Tommy asked, a little scared.

“If anything happens…” she stuttered and went up close to Tommy.

“Nothing will happen,” Tommy said with a smile. “Hero just probably saw Herder and will tell him to leave, the wort he would do is yell at him…and us.” He then grabbed Hunter’s hand back and kissed her on the forehead. She blushed and smile. Tom smiled back and the two continued down the hallway.

After going right, the two saw Hero staring down at something. Tommy noticed something in his hand, but couldn’t make it out in the darkness. He was going to ask Hero what he was looking at before Hunter grabbed Tommy’s sleeve. Tom looked at Hunter to see her left hand covering her mouth and tears in her eyes. She let go of his sleeve and pointed to the ground next to Hero’s feet. Tommy looked into her direction and gasped, tears forming in his eyes.

Hero then turned around and smiled. “You ready for your turn?”

Hero didn’t wait for them to act, he ran toward the two.

Tommy couldn’t move before Hunter grabbed his shoulder and threw him down the hallway.

“Run. Please.” She mouthed, tears in her eyes. Tommy couldn’t bare to she this happen, what she was doing. He shook his head, tears streaming down his eyes. As he started running back, he could hear her voice, saying the one this he always wanted to hear.

“I love you Tommy.”

\~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

“FILLER!”

Filler shot upward from her sleeping position and looked around. She was still in the dark, never ending void. She stood up and looked around very quickly.

“TOPPER?!” She yelled, wondering where her twin brother went. She ran around looking for him and luckily found him on the ground, shaking, with the others running in.

“T-Topper?” Filler asked and crouched to the ground. She looked at her brother to see his eyes almost turning white.

“M-My suit…” Topper stuttered.”…i-it’s being re-repaired…a-and I-I can’t control m-myself…”

Filler shook her head. This only meant one thing. He was being dragged back into his old body. This old suit. The springlock suit.

“No…fight Topper…FIGHT PLEASE!” Filler begged, tears forming in her eyes.

“I-I’m sorry…” He stuttered. He then jumped onto Filler and talked her to the ground. Filler screamed as Topper started beating her up. Digger grabbed his younger brother’s arms, trying to hold his back, but Topper just for fully got them loose. He started fighting Filler again before she bent her legs and kicked him off of her.

Topper flew off of her and fell onto the ground with a thud. He sat up and started laughing. “That was fun.” He chuckled, but it wasn’t his voice it was a voice they all dreaded.

“Hero.” Tommy said, fear in his tone.

“GET OUT OF OUR FRIEND!” Crafter yelled and Hero.

Topper just laughed and stood up. “Well,” he said and stretched his arms. “Say bye to your friend Topper,”

“W-What?” Herder stuttered before Topper disappeared.

“TOPPER!” Filler yelled and fell to the ground. She stared at the spot where she was last at and sobbed. It was all too much. First it was that night then this. She first had her family, and life taken from her, and now her twin brother who was always with her.

“Topper…” She sobbed, before they heard a grandfather clock ring. They all sighed and got ready for another day, living in the suits of Gameknight’s Pizzeria.
