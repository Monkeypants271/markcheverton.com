The next day, Gameknight and a few other NPCs visited Morgana.

“What’s the cure?” Gameknight asked Morgana.

“There is no such simple cure.” The witch, now NPC, replied. “I get what you mean, though. This is the Curse of the Enderman, caused when an Enchanted Enderman comes into contact with a NPC. Enchanted Endermen are stronger, more proficient endermen formed using the crafting powers of Herobrine. The only cure is Phoenix Tear. Phoenixes are now extinct, but using the diet of the Phoenix, the tear of the descendant of the phoenix, we might be able to make it. We’ll need pufferfish, melon seeds and ghast tears.”

Gameknight formed that with an earlier fact. “But all the Farmers, Fishers and Hunters are infected. That’s what the endermen are trying to achieve! They don’t want us to get the cure.” Morgana sighed, “That’s what I’ve been trying to tell this dumb NPC here but he doesn’t get it. Only skilled Fishers can catch pufferfish, skilled Farmers can grow melons, and skilled Hunters to kill ghasts!”

“That’s true.” Gameknight conversed. “I have a pufferfish from the Elder Guardian. For the rest, melons are found easily in dungeons – Shawny can get that for us. Ghast tears, on the other hand, a hard to get, and very dangerous. We’ll have to venture to the Nether – the home of Charybdis, the blaze king! As for dungeons. there’s Reaper, Xa-Tul and Feyd to be seen.”
