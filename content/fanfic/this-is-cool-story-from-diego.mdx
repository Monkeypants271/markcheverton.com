Prologue

HE walked down the corridor of his underground castle. Suddenly a figure approached him looking battered and worn out. The figure said “Sir, there… In the the blade room… Armed thief… Sword…robbed.” HE was surprised his most guarded room being robbed?  
HE quickly hurried to see what was happening. Then, there in the room stood the thief the thief wore a black coat, a black Bandana, with a red and black hat on there head, and grey pants. The thief said “Hey there king or should I say King Strife. Huh, what’s with the shocked face, oh right, I’m stealing your precious sword the Chaven Blade well I would have asked but well you know this is way better so yeah and now with the sword I own this kingdom the Kingdom of Felarun so yeah now you’re going to have to die, bye bye now” The thief grabbed the Gun one of the guards  and shot King Strife.  
Now the the thief owned Felarun…  
But somewhere a child in a blue and white T-Shirt and baggy grey shorts was about to become a legend this child was named Derek he was Felarun’s next hero.
