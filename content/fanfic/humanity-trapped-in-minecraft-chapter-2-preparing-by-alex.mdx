**When the sun came up, everyone that was in a home came out to see if all the mobs were gone. “Is everyone ok?” Someone asked. “Yeah.” Everyone said in unison. “Ok, here’s what we’re gonna do,” Jackson said. “Some of us experienced people who have played minecraft will mine for stone to make tools, while the rest of you need to look for sheep and kill as many as possible.” Jackson explained. “You need three wood planks and three wool to craft a bed.” Jackson explained. “You will have at least two experienced minecrafters with you, so you know what to do.” After he was done talking, everyone did what was told and went to find sheep. Jackson and seven other experienced Minecrafters started mining til they hit stone. “How much stone do we need?” Someone asked. “As much as you can carry.” Jackson replied. Everyone went to work immediately, each person mining stone. Eventually, Jackson’s wooden pickaxe broke. Drawing another pickaxe Jackson continued to mine stone. “Alright, that’s enough.” Jackson said. Everyone headed up to where the homes were. They were small, but they would be made bigger and better eventually. Right now, they were focusing on what they needed to do. Once they were by the homes, Jackson ran to the nearest tree. Pulling out his wooden axe, Jackson chopped down the tree and turned the oak logs into oak planks and then crafted a Crafting table. Running back to the others, Jackson saw a nearby creeper approaching the others and he drew his wooden sword.** _“Oh no you don’t”._ **Jackson thought. Running faster, Jackson collided into the creeper, pushing it away from the others. The creeper tried to ignite, but Jackson was too fast. He kept slashing at it and backing up, until the creeper died. Jackson picked up the gunpowder the creeper dropped and returned to the others. Jackson placed down a crafting table and started crafting stone tools. Once he was done, he realized he had eight blocks of cobblestone left. He decided to craft a furnace for one of the homes. “Why did you make a furnace?” One of the parents asked. “I know we’d need a lot more stone for a furnace for every house.” Jackson explained. “So, I’ll just place it somewhere that’s really close to the homes and build a little shelter around it.” That’s a good idea.” One of the parents said. Jackson turned around and placed the furnace. He then built a shelter around it, and a door in front. All the people that went with Max to collect wool returned, and went into each home and crafted a bed and then changed the color. “Hey max!” Jackson shouted. “How’d it go?” “It went well”, Max said. “Everyone is coming together and helping each other out.” “That’s good.” Jackson replied. As soon as everyone crafted their beds, they started working on improving their homes, having people who were architects help make the basic huts look more like a modern home. As the homes became more modern looking, Jackson looked away from homes and into the wilderness.** _“Where are you mom?”_ **Jackson thought. Sighing, Jackson went into his basic hut and crafted a chest with the remaining oak planks that he had. Jackson looked outside and saw some of the parents finishing their improved homes. Jackson opened the door to his hut and noticed he was getting a little hungry.** _“Food bar’s getting low,”_ **Jackson thought. Looking up at the sun, Jackson saw that it was starting to go down. “Attention Everyone,” Jackson said. “It’s almost night, so we should be preparing for the mobs to spawn.” Upon hearing this, everyone went inside their finished houses. Instead of going inside his basic hut, Jackson saw a pig nearby and ran towards it. Drawing his stone sword, Jackson killed the pig in two hits. Picking up the pork, Jackson put it in his inventory and ran to the little shelter he built for the one furnace. Putting his old wooden sword in the furnace for fuel, Jackson placed the pork on top, and a minute later the pork was done. Jackson ate it quickly and felt his hunger bar increase. Jackson then went back to his hut and closed the door right when the moon came up. Jackson got in his bed and fell asleep instantly. When it was light outside, Jackson got up and went outside and started looking for Any animals that might be nearby. Jackson started walking when he saw two cows and one chicken. Running to them, Jackson drew his sword and killed the animals. Putting all the raw food in his inventory, Jackson sheathed his sword and walked back to his hut. At this point everyone had killed some animals and cooked their raw food. “What should we do today?” Max asked. “Well, we should start mining for iron or gold, maybe diamond,” Jackson explained. “On it,” Max replied. Turning, he started issuing commands to the people in the area.**
