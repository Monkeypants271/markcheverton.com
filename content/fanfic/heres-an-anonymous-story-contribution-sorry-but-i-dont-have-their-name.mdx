Chapter 1: Jurassic Island  
“Gameknight!” shouted Digger.”Isn’t this a bit too dangerous? I mean by this.”They sat in a circle surrounding one single campfire. With no protection or walls surrounding them to keep them safe, well except for the Stegosauruses wandering nearby.  
“its okay, Digger.As long as we don’t go into the woods or the water, everything will be just fine.” replied Gamekinght, he sighed. The NPCs are still used to think in the logic of Minecraft.  
Some Builders asked Gameknight how to place down the wood in their inventory….. thats a bit too ridiculous. That’s the logic of Minecraft, in this game you only use them for crafting walls and stuff to build structures.  
“Get some shut eye, guys. Have some sleep.” Crafter instructed while Gamekinght was thinking about all these things.I should get some sleep as well. He thought.  
As he lie down on the cold, wet and sandy beach, he saw a silent Monet watching him.He reached his hand to pat Monet’s head and said:”Everything will be just fine, get some sleep.”  
She sighed, and replied:”Night, Tommy.”  
“Night, Jenny.”Gameknight replied as he was overwhemed by darkness.
