Things Not Seen, by Andrew Clements, is truly an awesome story. It is about a young man, and how he copes with waking up one morning and finding himself…gone. He has to struggle with the new boundaries and rules that come with him…being gone. He tests out his new limits, and while doing so makes a friend, a blind girl named Alicia. Alicia and her parents eventually learn about Bobby’s…problem, and try and help. Meanwhile, Bobby and Alicia are growing closer together, and team up to try and find a way to get Bobby back to normal. This book, while at times a frustrating blend of science and mystery, also has times of excitement, adventure, and heartache. This book is the perfect book for all readers from 10, to 100.
