Steve and Alex came out of the village with some more villagers. The names of the villagers that just joined them were Fisher, Builder and Woodcutter. Steve told Woodcutter to cut some wood and Stonecutter to get some stone. Steve gave Baker some sugar and some bread and told him to bake a cake as for food and said to cook the fish Fisher would bring to him. He told Fisher to find some fish to fish and bring them to Baker. Then Steve told Builder to take the wood and stone Stonecutter and Woodcutter would dig up. After everyone was done they looked for any caves that had a point were lava and water would meet. The first cave they found was just a cave that only had stone blocks in it. Stonecutter dug up a few blocks of stone then they continued to look for caves with a point where lava and water met. The second cave they found had a glow in it that the thought might be the light from some lava but it was only the light from torches in a old jungle temple behind the cave. Then the third cave they found was only a cave with stone blocks and a few zombies in it. The fourth cave they found was just a spot were some TNT exploded that they were sure was from some miner that came here in the past. The fifth cave they found was actually the remains of a watchtower built onto a hill of stone that got blown up with TNT. “Are you sure that we will ever find a cave with a point in it where lava meets water?” Baker asked. “I am already getting tired of searching these caves” Fisher said. “We will find the cave soon” Digger said. After a while of walking around Steve saw a cave that had the sound of splashing water and a glow that likely came from lava. “I think I found it!” Steve said. They raced toward the cave and quickly found the secret button that would open the secret door that lead to zombie town. Steve pressed the secret button and said “We will use the same plan we used in skeleton town” then he passed invisibility potions to the villagers and saved one for himself. They all drank their invisibility potions except for Alex who didn’t get a potion of invisibility then they walked into zombie town and noticed all the zombies were in the center of the town listening to a person with glowing white eyes and next to the person with glowing white eyes was a massive zombie that was maybe three times the height of a normal zombie. The massive zombie had red eyes with a faint white glow. “I guess that giant zombie is also one of Herobrine’s creations” Steve said. They made a turn all the way behind the massive zombie and half of them charged at the massive zombie while the other half charged at the person with glowing white eyes. They destroyed the massive zombie after the other half decided to charge at it instead. Then the person with glowing white eyes noticed them and said “I am Herobrine and you are not allowed to disturb our meeting”. This time as soon as Herobrine said the second e in meeting they charged. By the time Herobrine finished his sentence they hit him, knocking him to the ground. Then all the zombies saw them and charged. They ran as fast as they could, carrying Herobrine’s body with them. They made it back to the village and all the zombies burned under the sun so they decided to go back to their zombie town. They dug a very deep hole and put two layers of obsidian around it and put Herobrine in it. “I bet Herobrine is going to be stuck in there for a while” Digger said. “That hole is pretty big. It is fifty three blocks deep and forty blocks wide” Stonecutter said. After a while of checking for any gaps they went to tell the villagers to build defenses in case there would be any attacks.
