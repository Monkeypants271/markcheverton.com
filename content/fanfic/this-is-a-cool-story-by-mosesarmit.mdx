“Aha!” cried Malacoda. He was with Erebus, who was holding a dirt block in his hand, quivering. Malacoda threw a fireball at him, and Erebus immediately died. “Raah!”  
Gameknight ran all over the cave like a maniac, as Malacoda kept throwing and missing fireballs at Gameknight. Gameknight, who was finally into position, stopped, fired an arrow, and another, and another, all into Malacoda’s flesh. Malacoda was weak by now, and ran straight at Gameknight.  
Gameknight lured Crafter to him, as Crafter shot an arrow at Malacoda.  
“You can’t fail me!” roared Gameknight.  
“You are a twit, you little Minecraft player!” yelled Malacoda furiously. “I will beat…”  
Malacoda fell to the lava floor, dead as a dinosaur. Gameknight and Crafter shouted in victory as they walked the caves together, when Malacoda rose up and threw a fireball at Crafter, as Crafter died.
