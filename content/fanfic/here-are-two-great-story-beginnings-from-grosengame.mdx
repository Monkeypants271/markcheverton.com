One day, gameknight was walking to Crafter’s village. On his way, he ran into an iorn golem! The iorn golem just stared at gameknight. “That was weird” gameknight said. He contiued walking. The iorn golem followed gameknight. Then, the golem stopped to think whether he should  follow gameknight more. The iorn golem thought that would be unreasonable so he walked away, so gameknight did not have the iorn golem after all.

It was one rainy day. Gameknight waited in his hidey hole. After a while, it stopped raining. He came out of his hidey hole for some food. He realized it was night when he came out. “Oh, whatever” he said to himself. He was really hungry. Without being carful, gameknight headed a random way. He didn’t find any pigs or cows. “By the time I find a pig, I will be dead” Gameknight said impatiently. Then, he heard something. Zombies!!! Then he found a pig. He ran to it. The giant crowd of zombies were only 20 meters away from gameknight. One of the zombies touched him. “I wish I bought my diamond sword! It is better than 3 iron swords” he said as he started running away from the group of zombies. He was really starving. He found another pig. He hit it as many times as he could before another annoying and hurting zombie touched him, but he was too late. He tried to avoid the zombies. Then he got luck. The pig was now a raw pork. Uh-oh, he forgot, the ZOMBIES!!! The only way he could get back to his  hidey hole would be to confuse the zombies and loop around them. Gameknight remembered a tip; only sprint in emergencies. He started sprinting. That worked. He arrived at his hidey hole, he jumped in, placed a torch and covered the entrance with some dirt. He put the porkchop in the furnace and waited. When the porkchop was done, he ate it really quickly. Then he got a little hungry. Uh-oh.
