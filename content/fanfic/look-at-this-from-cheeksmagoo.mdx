Once upon a time, Monkeypants, Monet, and Gameknight were sitting on a hill. Then, Crafter snuck up on them and scared the living daylight out of them all. The three of them jumped up with their swords pulled out, and saw it was Crafter. He laughed nervously as he backed away, but soon everyone was dying of laughter, until Crafter backed up into the box Herobrine was in and released him. Herobrine came out stronger than before in the form of the Witherstorm from Minecraft Story Mode and started eating everything he could get his arms/tentacles on. Then Crafter, Monkeypants, Monet, and Gameknight ran. And ran. And ran and ran and ran! Until they saw something. No, not something. Someone! It was Cheeksmagoo! Clad in diamond armor and carrying an enchanted diamond sword, she ran up to the Herobrine Witherstorm…And got eaten by it. Or that’s what everyone thought. While she was inside Herobrine, she stabbed his Virus’s center and saved the day. Crafter gave her a paper crown and Monet gave her screenshot. Monkeypants put her in his series, and Gameknight gave her a tombstone for her poor dead pig, Reubin II.  
The End!
