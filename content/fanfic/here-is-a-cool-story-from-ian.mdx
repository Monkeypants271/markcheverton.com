DAY ONE: LIFE ON A FARM

Hello! I thought I should introduce myself. I am CREEPERKILLER123. This is a story about my adventures in Minecraft. So now that I have introduced myself, let’s start the story… or should I say… _ADVENTURE?_

So there I was, minding my own business and tending to Moomoo (my best friend and cow) when I heard a high pitched scream coming from the direction of the village!

I raced home and  smashed down the door (a repair for later) and whipped open my emergency chest. I grabbed my diamond sword and armor and smashed through my window (another repair for later). As I raced towards the village I heard another scream. “HELP!!!” I ran faster, determined to help my only friends in the world. My blocky heart pounded in my blocky chest. As I got closer, I saw smoke coming from the direction of the village! With renewed determination I ran even faster. When the village peeked over the top of the hill, I saw every user’s worst nightmare…… **_THE ENDER DRAGON_**_!_ I instantly knew I was in over my head… WAY over my head.

    I sat there staring as hell in minecraft unfolding in front of me. out of the corner of my eye I saw the village priest praying. “RUN!!!!!” I yelled. He looked at me and ran. When the Ender Dragon saw a possible victim getting away, he opened his mouth and everything went into slow motion. I sprinted at full speed. Even though I was only a few blocks away, it felt like an eternity. I tried to run faster but my body wouldn’t respond correctly. The villager’s face looked pleading, like all he wanted in the whole server was for me to save him. Then, the world returned to normal speed and I burst forward. A blinding pain erupted in my side and it came to me that the ender dragon used the villager as bait in a trap, and I fell for it. I was a total imbecile. the pain felt like thousands of knives stabbing me all over my blocky body. Then my vision blurred and I passed out.

I woke up after what felt like days but was probably weeks to someone shaking me. my eyes opened and I did not believe what I saw. I blinked twice and passed out.
