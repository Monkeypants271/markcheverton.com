### Prologue

###             Lets take it back to when Herobrine was in pig form. Herobrine infected the Ender Dragon egg, but what he did not realize is that he also infected Endermenbrine who was working on a nearby Endermen . The shadow crafter was suddenly struck by a bolt of lighting so hot and harsh he felt like it was burning his entire being. After the lightning bolt struck, Endermenbrine felt strong, almost as powerful as The Maker himself. Endermenbrine watched and waited to see what Herobrine would do in his war against The-User-That-Is-Not-A-User. On the shore of The Great Lava Ocean, when the Final Battle was unraveling, Endermenbrine was watching from a distance. After the maker entered The Gateway of Light, he new he would finish what The Maker started, to rid this world of the filthy NPCs.  Endermenbrine fashioned a new name for himself, one that would make his enemies cower in fear. He named himself Enderbrine, and he would finish what The Maker started.

### Chapter One

###  As the light faded around The-User-That-Is-Not-A-User, he found himself in his familiar spawn. The waterfall flowing down behind, the mountains looming all around him, like  ancient guardians. Gameknight999 headed for the tower of dirt blocks with his torch on top. As The-User-That-Is-Not-A-User ran past the water fall he grinned remembering how it had saved his life the first time he had been pulled into the game. Gameknight999 dug up the blocks of dirt revealing his hidey-hole as he expected. The-User-That-Is-Not-A-User opened the chest inside and took out some food, iron armor,  an iron sword, and some tools. Now he was ready, Gameknight999 stepped out of his hidey-hole, put the blocks back and headed towards the village. As the sun went down The-User-That-Is-Not-A-User saw that there was a lot more endermen then usual. Gameknight999 frowned thinking the last thing he wanted was another war. With the familiar watch tower in the distance, Gameknight  sprinted towards the village. “The-User-That-Is-Not-A-User is here!” one of the sentries cried. As Gameknight999 walked into the village, he saw that there were still two watchers in the tower. A red blur sprinted  towards him. “Gameknight!”, Stitcher, Hunter’s younger sister said as she tackled him with a hug. “My friend I see you have returned” said a high pitched voice, Crafter stood at the entrance to the watchtower. “we need to talk.”

###             Gameknight stood at the entrance to the crafting chamber. “Crafter what’s wrong” Gameknight999 asked, as they walked through the chaotic crafting chamber.

### “I’ll tell you when we’re alone”. Crafter replied. They walked into a back room Gameknight had never noticed. “We added this room after The Final Battle”, said Crafter. “we were going to try to grow plants under ground, but now it serves as a meeting room”.

###             When they were inside and the door shut Gameknight began with rapped fire questions. “Crafter why were there so many endermen out, why were there still two Watchman in the tower, and why does everyone seem on edge.” I’ll answer your questions in order, crafter replied. “We still don’t know why there are so many endermen, but they have been attacking us more frequently lately. that should answer your other questions too. “So does this mean that there is another monster king” Gameknight asked shuddering.  “No, Crafter said, I think we’re dealing with shadow crafters”.

### Chapter Two

###             “Shadow crafters Gameknight said, I thought we destroyed them we destroyed Herobrine.” “I thought so too, crafter replied but Herobrine must have made them part of minecraft, not a virus but actually part of the game”. A popping sound distracted him as A character with green eyes and blue hair appeared out of nowhere. “Jenny, Gameknight said, don’t tell me you used the digitizer too”. “I used the digitizer too, Monet113 Gameknight’s sister replied, I saw you downstairs using it and I thought I haven’t had a Minecraft adventure in a while”. Gameknight sighed, “Well apparently when we destroyed Herobrine we didn’t destroy his shadow crafters” Gameknight999 said, “so now they are trying to get revenge, is that right Crafter? “Yes but I think only one in particular, for now……..”, Crafter replied “So which one is it” Gameknight asked. “Enderbrine, Crafter said, or it least that’s what he calls himself”. How do you know The-User-that-is-not-a-User asked. “there was a battle last night. “WHAT Gameknight interrupted , I thought the battles were over”. “This was the first battle in a while,” Crafter replied. “At the battle last night it was just endermen, we didn’t provoke any of them they just attacked”. What Ba-Jin saw was much worse then she’d imagined, a green  figure was fighting there leader Qa-Nay. Qa-Nay got punched from behind, wait the figure was just in front of him, the mysteries figure moved with lighting speed. Qa-Nay stood no chance I had to tell the-User-That-Is-Not-A-User.

The user slowly backed away before turning and sprinting away. The mysteries figure chuckled softly, he loved scaring users like that. He then set out to rejoin his brethren somewhere in a far off village a boy spotted a blue figure near the ocean. The boy ran to tell everyone what he saw but no one believed him.
