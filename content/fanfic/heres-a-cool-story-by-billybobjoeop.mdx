A blinding sphere of light burst outwards onto the grassy plains as Gameknight999 stepped into his surroundings. He always loved the feeling of coming out; it reminded him of home. However, as soon as he stepped out, he knew something was wrong. First off, his inventory was empty, with no reassuring embraces of safety from his enchanted diamond armor, which was gone. Second, he saw the sun was blue, and the sky was yellow. Why would the sky and sun switch colors like that? he thought. Even glitches wouldn’t do that. There was something he didn’t understand here. “Whatever,” he told himself. “I’ll go to Crafter’s village.”

When he got to the village, there was something different, very different around it. There were two big, gigantic spiders roaming around the fields. When they turned around and their purple eyes met him, they charged. At first, he freaked out, thinking it was Shaikulud and Shavilak reborn, but their eyes represented something more evil….ten times worse than that vile Herobrine virus. He needed some information. Another thing he noticed was the trees. They were so huge….how were they like this? Obviously Herobrine couldn’t have done this, as his shadow-crafting modifying powers were limited. Someone more sinster had done this.  
I’ll talk with Crafter, he decided.

Before he could walk to the gates, an elephant greeted him with a honk. This bewildered the User-that-is-not-a-user. Elephants were not in Minecraft, unless someone was using a mod. He opened the gates. No one was there. Something was very wrong. He decided to check the crafting chamber. As he slid down the ladder and walked into the vast room, he was shocked.

Before him and his eyes, was Herobrine and a mysterious stranger with glowing red eyes of a thousand infra lamps staring at him. Gameknight expected Herobrine to laugh at him, but instead choked out his last words before falling unconsicous onto the floor. “Find the prophecy. We must work together, my enemy.” And then, his glowing eyes closed and dimmed until only bright red light filled the chamber.
