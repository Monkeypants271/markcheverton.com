Hey guys, Anthony (aka maxx) here, just wanted to apologize for not posting sequels to my books in a while, anyway, here’s the story.

Poof! My brother appeared in the game. “Why did you select enable reality mode? You shouldn’t have done that. Now all three of us are trapped!” I was furious at my brother.“Guys, chill”, my brother said. “Let’s stop arguing and just focus on survival for now”, my sister said. I was still furious at my brother, but I decided to listen and stop arguing.

A while later…

“Now we finally have enough wood to get basic tools for all three of us!”, I exclaimed. “Now time to get some food.” We quickly killed a few animals with our swords. We got enough leather for three leather boots, and enough food to last a week. We also had just enough wool to craft three beds. All of us got to work digging dirt and mining stone. Then, we put on the leather boots we had crafted earlier. It was almost night, so we built a small dirt house, complete with three beds, a crafting table, a furnace, a door, and several torches. Then, we went to sleep. I wondered how we would ever escape from Minecraft.
