### **The New and Evil Oxus**

(as requested by Enderpriest)

        **The story:** As many of you know, Oxus is the creeper king, who was famous for:

1#: Deserting Herobrine (in _Attack of the Shadowcrafters_)

2#: Being one of the only monster kings that wanted peace (in _Attack of the Shadowcrafters_ and _Overworld in Flames_)

3#: Making a creeper hive under a mountain (in _Overworld in Flames_)  
The last that anyone heard of Oxus was when he let Gameknight999 and some of his friends escape from his creeper hive.

But after Gameknight999 left what do you think happened?

Do you think that Oxus continued his life of peace or do you think that something else happened?

**The portal:** “Hurry up!”

Oxus looked at the villagers that were slowly building the portal that he hoped would solve the problem of creepers dying after they exploded.

The new block that the villagers were making the portal out of was a Creeper Block.

It was made in jungle biomes when a creeper exploded and killed a cat, this was extremely rare, because when a creeper is confronted with a cat the creeper freezes up, or runs away.

_Thunk!_ The villagers had finished placing the last block in the portal.

Oxus came to look at it.

It was shaped like a nether portal, but it was all filled in.

“If this works, will we be set free?” one of the villagers asked.

Oxus ignored the question and thought, _if this works then we can throw them into the lava!_

He turned to look at the lava, and imagined the villagers being thrown into the lava; he imagined their screams of terror as the lava consumed their HP and smiled.

He then turned to the old creeper that was standing close by him.

“Well,” the old creeper said, pausing to let the ignition process stop, “it looks like it is time.”

Oxus nodded solemnly.

The only way to start the portal was with the death of a creeper.

The old creeper walked up to the portal and ignited.

_Boom!_ The creeper exploded and suddenly all the blocks disappeared.

A swirling and undulating green portal replaced them with black spots moving through it.

Oxus smiled and screamed, “It worked!!”

Suddenly one of the black spots started…growing.

Larger and larger it grew until it was about the size of a creeper, then a young creeper walked out of it.

The other creepers in the cavern, as well as Oxus, recognized the creeper as the one who started the portal.

The creeper that stepped out of the portal looked around, astonished.

“But…but…” the creeper started to say, “How,” he paused, “am I alive?”

Oxus smiled and said, “The portal works.”

Then he turned to the villagers that were huddled in a corner, just 5 blocks away from the lava pool.

“Kill them!” Oxus said.

**The plan:** Oxus hurried toward the meeting room where all his generals were waiting.

He walked into the room and all the creepers turned to him.

His entrance put flashes of red on the walls that were already lighted with streaks of blue.

“I am confused,” one of the creepers, said, “Why are we calling,” he paused, “a meeting?”

Another creeper answered, “Because we still need to,” he paused, “decide if we are going to,” he paused again, “kill the NPC’s.”

“There is no,” another creeper said, “question about what,” he paused again, “we are going to do!”

“I agree,” Oxus said softly.

“We now have the power to destroy all the villages in Minecraft!”

“I suggest we use it.”

The last creeper turned to Oxus and said, “With all due respect,” he paused, “king, I thought,” he paused again, “that you were the one,” he paused still again, “that created this place so we,” another pause, “wouldn’t have to fight the NPC’s?”

Oxus smiled and said, “Yes, I did.”

“But now we don’t have to hide, we can strike out with all our power and once all the villages are gone we will roam freely!”

Most of the creepers hissed in delight, but 2 of them didn’t.

Oxus glared at them, then they reluctantly started hissing their approval.

Oxus smiled and screamed, “After we are done, there will be no more villages in Minecraft!!!!!!”

**The users:** Suddenly 4 users dropped down from the ceiling.

All the creepers and Oxus looked at them in surprise with their mouths open and as the users looked around Oxus observed them.

One of them had: Snake, floating above him, his skin was part fire, and part ice, he had headphones on his head, and he had glasses on.

Another one had: Cobra\_II floating above him, and his skin was a Steve skin but with white eyes.

The third one was a girl who had: Geokimasn135, floating above her head, and her skin was an Alex skin.

The final one was also a girl, who had: Zombola\_\_\_\_12357 floating above her head, and her skin was part zombie and user.  
“Holy monkey mother!!!!” Snake screamed.

Cobra\_II launched himself at the creepers; wielding a diamond sword and screaming, “DIE CREEPER SCUM!!!!!!!!”

Zombola\_\_\_\_12357 gave a yelp and tried to throw a splash potion at the creepers, but ended up dousing herself instead.

She quickly died, but then Geokimasn135 started shooting arrows at the creepers, while jumping onto blocks to get higher.

_Pop!_ The creeper right next to Oxus died with one shot.

_Wait,_ Oxus thought, _that wasn’t from Geokimasn…_

Snake was drawing arrow after arrow after arrow and sinking them into creeper bodies through his enchanted bow that killed with one shot.

Oxus hissed in anger, and sent a message to all his creepers in the room that were still living to get out of the room.

All the creepers started retreating out of the room, but it was very hard with Cobra attacking their backs screaming, “COME AND GET ME MONSTERS!!!!!!!”

Once all the creepers were out of the room Oxus looked at the users that remained.

Geokimasn was aiming at him with her bow, Cobra was looking at him warily and Snake was looking confused.

“Can I kill him?” Geokimasn asked.

Suddenly Snakes eyes widened in recognition, “Holy Mother of Monkeys, how did we find you?!”

Then Oxus started to blink, Snake tried to scramble away screaming, “Look out!! He is going to…”

Oxus exploded.

Dark.

Dark.

That was all that Oxus could see.

Then…it was green, creeper green.

Oxus looked down at himself and saw that he was black.

He saw a light and started to move towards it, expanding as he went.

Suddenly…he was back in the room that held the portal.

He looked around and saw hundreds of creeper faces looking up at him in awe, adult creepers, baby creepers, nurse creepers, old creepers, all of them looked at him in awe.

Oxus felt as young as when Herobrine first created him.

“Start the destruction of the villages!!!!!!!!!”

**The killings:** Oxus smiled as he saw the creepers coming out of the portal chamber.

That meant that another village was destroyed.

As the creepers walked up to the platform that Oxus was on they all bowed respectfully, and waited for instructions.

Oxus said, “Good job, now you can rest.”

“We won’t be having anymore attacks now that it is day.”

As the creepers headed towards the sleeping quarters and Oxus headed towards his, he couldn’t help but remember what the user Snake had said.

_How did we find you?!_ Did that user know him?

Oxus certainly had never seen him before.

As Oxus entered his chamber, and walked over to his sleeping place he wondered, _how did that user know me?_

When Oxus woke up he immediately went to the portal room.

Already a creeper army was emerging from the portal and heading towards the platform to receive instructions.

Oxus pulled one of the creepers aside and said, “How did it go?”

Just as the creeper was about to answer, Oxus thought, _just tell me with you thoughts._

_It was terrific! The villagers on the walls saw us and started firing arrows down onto us, but we dodged most of them._

_Once we made it up to the wall the front line exploded causing the wall to be destroyed, so now the villagers on the wall couldn’t warn the other villagers._

_Then most of us went in between the houses and detonated._

_But before that, one of the villagers woke up and he woke up the rest in his house._

_The cries of the doomed were sweet, sweet, sweet, sweet!_

Oxus smiled and called out to the creeper on the platform, “I will be taking this army to the next village!”

**The stop:** Oxus walked out of the mountain and towards the village that he knew was there.

The creepers that followed him made a _swish swish_ sound that sounded like chickens flapping their wings.

“Yeah, yeah, bro, I got it.”

Oxus suddenly stopped.

“Hey sis! Do me a favor and stop throwing those potions in my direction!”

Oxus slowly looked over the hill that was in front of him and saw Geokimasn135, Cobra\_II, Zombola\_\_\_\_12357, and Snake.

_Well,_ Oxus thought, _now we can take out these users!_

Oxus sent a message to 3 of the creepers in his army to sneak around the hill and draw the user’s attention.

3 of the creepers rushed out of his army and slowly began making their way towards the users.

“Look, we need to head back to that mountain,” Snake said.

“Ha! With all those creepers, we don’t stand a chance!” Cobra replied.

_Hiss…Boom!_ Two of the creepers exploded, killing Geokimasn.

“Hey! That was my sister!!” Zombola\_\_\_\_12357 yelled.

She pulled out a splash potion of harming and lobbed it at the last creeper, killing it instantly.

“You see! You see!” Cobra yelled pointing at the items of Geokimasn135, “We have disturbed the creepers! They will stop at nothing to kill us!”

Oxus stood up and screamed, “Attack!!!”

All the creepers from the creeper army rushed towards the users, scurrying as fast as they could.

Cobra looked at the creepers and screamed, “Ahhhh!!!!! Good bye!” then he ran away through the woods.

Zombola\_\_\_\_12357 was quick to follow, but Snake stayed there.

He drew his bow, and franticly shot arrow after arrow at the creepers, killing them with one shot.

“Ha! Ha! Ha! Ha!” Oxus laughed.

“Now you will die Snake!”

Suddenly the creeper right next to Oxus died.

Oxus turned to see 3 other users coming fast.  
One of them had: LavaLady above her head, and she was wielding a bucket of lava.

Another one had: Spear\_me above her head, and she was shooting arrows as fast as she could.

And the last one had: Eagle-Eye above her head, and she was throwing potions of harming at any creeper that she passed.

Oxus’s eyes widened as the 3 users ran towards him, suddenly he felt pain, extreme pain.

He fell to the ground, and the last thing that the king of the creepers heard was, “If only you didn’t make that portal.”

**The end:** And so ends Oxus, king of the creepers.

Most remember him as a secondary character in the GK999 series, but given the chance, he could’ve been so much more.
