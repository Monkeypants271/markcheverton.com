Chapter 9: bargain  
   Gameknight charged in the castle in the fortress, then, he gasped, there is a lot of wither skeletons on both side of the room, before him, sat the wither king, he is clearly talking something to all his subjects, an annoying expression was presented when gameknight entered the room, he was about to shout at gameknight when gameknight spoke first  
    “Wither king, i am not here to fight, i am here to bargain with you”  
    “Oh… bargain?” the wither king is now listening to gameknight, he looks like he is interested in it “spit it out, ‘friend’”  
     “Where are my friends?” gameknight asked  
     “Oh… they are living… rather not happy, but rest assure, they are all alive” the wither king replied with a sneer  
     “WHAT DID YOU DO TO THEM?” gameknight roared  
     “Oh… nothing… i guess” the wither king replied, the assuring smile of his seem to always hang on his face, his eye shining with interest, “what do you want to say?”  
     “I, want you to release them!” gameknight roared  
     “Ah… i see, so you want me to give your friend to you with no cost…” the wither king smiled,  
     “Nah, that’s not bargain, that’s surrender”  
     “I can give you anything if you release my fri-”  
     “Shut! I have heard a lot of this thing before, but… if you said so”  
     “What do you want then?”  
     “Your soul… you said you can give me anything if i released your friend” the wither king smiled again, the reassuring smile turned into a evil grin  
     Gameknight shuddered, then replied “can you change it into something else?”  
     “Yes… you can be the one to be in the cage instead, and they will be released, they are useless to me… but you… you are useful… very useful… heh…heh heh” the wither king smiled again
