Minecraft’s Glitch Part 2

(I’m changing my Alternate Universe:  
 Herobrine and Glitch are siblings  
 Entity 303 is a glitch not a hacker.  
 Null is… Null)

  Glitch frowned. Where were they? She was standing in a clearing with only grass and the random chicken to be seen.  
   “I don’t get it” Glitch muttered “We followed them here but they’re nowhere to be seen”.  
    Frustrated, she started glitching like crazy.  
    “Gurl” said Zoey, leaning against a tree “Calm down”.  
    Glitch took a deep breath and slowed her breathing a little. Slowly, her glitching stopped.  
     “C’mon Zoey” sighed Glitch “There’s nothing here, let’s go back”.

       “Glitch? A-are you awake?”  
       Glitch opened her eyes. Entity 303 and Null were standing beside her bed. She sat up.  
        “You’re back! Where’d you go?” she asked, cheerful. Entity smiled slightly at her.  
        “We have something to tell you” Null mumbled. Glitch nodded, looking around.  
         “Where’s Hero?” she asked, expecting one of them to say that he was setting up a prank or in the basement.  
          “That’s thing…” Null said, uncomfortably “He’s, um, well…”.  
          Something’s wrong, Glitch thought, Null never stutters.  
           “Herobrine’s d-dead”  
           It hit Glitch like a thousand bricks. She stared at them for a moment.  
           Then she smiled.  
            “You’re joking” she said “Y-yeah! It’s just some silly prank you guys set up!”. Her voice was breaking.  
             Entity and Null shook their head, not daring to make eye contact with her.  
             Glitch shut her eyes. She knew what he did when he was not around. He had a war to handle. But that war killed him.  
             No. The-User-that-is-not-a-user killed him. Gameknight999 killed him.  
             She opened her eyes. Her warm, friendly blue eyes were replaced with cold white glowing eyes fulled with rage and hatred.  
              Null and Entity took a step back. They had never seen their friend like this. She was always so kind and forgiving.  
              The room started glitching, including Glitch. She started laughing.  
              “WELCOME TO THE DESTRUCTION OF MINECRAFT!” she shouted, her voice distorted.  
               She would escape. Escape from the prison she once called home. But first…  
               “Get ready Gameknight999” Glitch smiled “I’ll find you, whether you’re in Minecraft or the real world”.

               The Oracle shuddered. She didn’t know what, but something evil was about to endanger Minecraft and its inhabitants.
