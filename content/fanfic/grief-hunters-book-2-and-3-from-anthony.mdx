Book 2

Until that guy, well, somehow managed to break through their security system and steal all their supplies. Also, he left a dirty surprise, which happened to be TONS OF TNT. Yeah, you can guess what happened next. BOOM. BOOM. BOOM. Yep, all that’s left of their legendary fort is some stone, wood, and various other blocks. “How are we going to hunt griefers now?” Chris, the TNT expert, asked. “I don’t know” replied CoolGuy123 (expert trap designer). “Don’t ask me” said TotalPro (loves building stuff that happens to attract griefers, joined Griefer Hunters when his 18th house was destroyed by greifers). “How about we mine some blocks to fix our fort?” asked buildmaster (propropro builder). So they spent months digging for materials.

                              Meanwhile…

“Those Griefer Hunt guys got soooooo beaten by dudethatlovestnt” said ProGreiferBlowsupStuff. “Yeah, he totally put them out of business” replied troubleiscomming3225. “Our base is pro times infinity level now” said greiferdude, motioning to the pure diamond wall. “Get ready to plan a second raid against those noobs in five days. Otherwise…” explosion123 pointed to the lava pit.

Book 3 (4 days later)

“Oof!” yelped TotalPro. He landed in a 12 block deep pit. MrTNT, who is one of the griefers, snickered. While TotalPro tried get out of the hole, MrTNT quickly dug a hole in the wall and placed some diamond ore there. He also placed a stone button on the ore. Then he placed some TNT behind the diamonds and returned to his hiding spot. When TotalPro saw the diamonds, he jumped for joy. But when he tried to mine it, he accidentally pushed the button, which activated the TNT. BOOM!! TotalPro lost most of his health from the blast. Then, MrTNT set TotalPro’s game mode to hardcore, jumped out of his hiding spot, and was just about to hit TotalPro with his enchanted netherite sword. But TotalPro was really smart. Right before the sword hit him, he dropped all his items and teleported away. Also, he sent a message to MrTNT saying “TotalPro was killed by Mr TNT”. MrTNT wasn’t very observant and didn’t notice that TotalPro sent him the message. Once TotalPro safely teleported to the base (or at least what used to be the base), he set his game mode back to survival (somehow the server wouldn’t allow anyone to enter creative) and ate some food. Then, he went to a different mine and continued mining.

                           An hour later….

“We have enough materials to fix the base and make new tools!” exclaimed Chris. CoolGuy and buildmaster got to work.

                      At the griefer base…

“Those Griefer Hunters just fixed their base!”  furiously shouted troubleiscoming3225. “We are raiding them tonight instead!”
