Dinosaur hatching  
\-By Tim

For Mark Cheverton And Gameknight999

Chapter I.  
Dinosaur evolutionary tree  
Gameknight and his friends have been tired of fighting monsters themselves, so they thought of an  idea. The idea was that they would hatch dinosaur pets that are going to be on their side. They were in a dinosaur egg hatching habitat. And they probably time traveled back to the dinosaur age. So the 1st dinosaur they encountered was an “Archosaur”. Now, an Archosaur is a kind of Dinosaur that was a clade of dinosaurs that were the ancestors of most reptiles and crocodiles and alligators. And the Archosaur was a carnivore (I think.), it was a quadruped which meant it walked on 4 feet, and it lived in the Triassic Age when there were dinosaur ancestors. And the crew bought 6 eggs of Archosaurs for $0 from a dinosaur egg dealership. They waited 8 minutes for the eggs to be ready for hatching.  
8 minutes later…  
After the 8 minutes, Gameknight and his friends used their microwave as their dinosaur egg incubator. They brought their microwave in their time machine.  Then, they set the microwave timer for 8 minutes. Then, they started the microwave.  
8 minutes later… again  
After, the Archosaur eggs hatched.  
CRRRRRRRKKKKKK!  
Then, little baby Archosaurs popped out of the eggs. An Archosaur is brown with orange stripes and a long tail and sharp teeth.  Now, an interesting part of Archosaurs is that in this story that you’re currently reading, Archosaurs bite your finger.  But what’s interesting about that is that it is an evolutionary trait of Archosaurs after they are born. But after a while, once they get older,  they stop biting your finger. And the reason that Archosaurs bite your finger is to know that you are the adopter of the baby dinosaurs. But, as Archosaurs grow, they get hungry, so there is a Golden Pterodactyl Valley. The Golden Pterodactyl Valley with the pterodactyls is also food for the dinosaurs. The Golden Pterodactyl Valley is a field filled to the brim with golden Pterodactyls. The Pterodactyls are hostile in the field there, so it’s best advised you get a blaster when you have one. And one of the pterodactyls could peck you if you’re too slow. But Gameknight and his friends are always fast when they fight somethin’. So they got a blaster from out of their inventories.  The blaster was a Gamma-ray burst shooter. So Gameknight and his friends encountered a golden pterodactyl in the valley. The pterodactyl that they encountered was pretty hostile and dumb. So the pterodactyl was flying around freely until,  
PEWSHUN!  
The pterodactyl got shot by the gamma-ray and it said, “SQUAAA! SQUAAAAAAA!!” The reason the pterodactyl said those squaaas is that it did a few death squawks as it flew out of the flying control and the pterodactyl hit the ground violently.  
SPLAP!  
When the golden pterodactyl hit the ground, its body got all messed up. Gameknight and his friends also discovered something else besides the pterodactyl. It was an ancient mollusc called Ammonoidea. When Gameknight and his friends collected the Ammonites and collected the pterodactyl for the Archosaurs, they realized that the ammonites were coins for buying new dinosaurs in the evolutionary tree.

Chapter II.  
Buying new dinosaurs  
After Gameknight and his friends went to their dinosaur hut, they fed the  Archosaurs.  
CHOMP! GULP! GLIPGULPGULP!  
Gameknight said to Treebrin, “Hey Treebrin, ya sure those Archosaurs are on our side?”  
Treebrin said, “Well, of cours they ar! Why would they not?!”  
Gameknight said, “That’s a really good question…”  
Gameknight and his friends got 75 Ammonite coins from the dumb pterodactyl. And it turned out that they had enough Ammonite coins to buy another dinosaur in the evolutionary tree called a “Ornithischia.” An Ornithischia is a kind of dinosaur that was an herbivore, it was a biped, which meant it walked on its hind legs, and it probably lived in the Triassic Age. And the Ornithischia is gray with an orange crest that sticks out of its head. So Gameknight and his friends bought the Ornithischia for 75 Ammonite coins in the evolutionary tree,  
KA-CHING!  
And then the Ornithischia was bought.  
Gameknight and his friends bought 6 Ornithischia eggs for $67 from the egg dealership. Then, they went to their Microwave Incubator and put the 6 Ornithischia eggs into the microwave and set the timer for 6 minutes.  
6 minutes later…  
CRRRRKKKKKKKKKK!  
Ornithischia eggs hatch.  
And the evolutionary trait of a baby Ornithischia is slapping your body to know that you’re an adopter of the Ornithischias. The slapping stops at some point after the trait phase.  
5 years later…  
The Archosaurs and Ornithischias are 10 and 5 years old. The Ornithischias were hungry, so they fed the Ornithischias some plants, (because the Ornithischias are herbivores) then the plants were gobbled up by the Ornithischias.  
CHOMP! CRUNCH! MUNCH MUNCH!  
Gameknight and his friends are going to stay in the dinosaur age and hatch new dinosaurs later on.
