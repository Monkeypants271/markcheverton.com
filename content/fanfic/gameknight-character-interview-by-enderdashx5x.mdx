Gameknight Character Interviews!

~Entry #1 – Gameknight/Tommy~

Interviewer: Hello Tommy, great to meet you! I’ve heard so many things about you and your adventures. So why don’t you tell us your name and just about you?

Tommy: Well, my name is Thomas Feynman, better known as Gameknight999, and I’m a socially awkward geek who lives to play Minecraft and stuff like that.

Interviewer: Nice to know Thomas, nice to know.

So what are you’re thoughts about cyber bullying?

Tommy: It’s a bad thing, a really bad thing. Kids or teen just don’t see it coming and then – BOOM – their life is ruined forever.

Interviewer: Have you had any cyber bullying problems before, Tom?

Tommy: Cyber bullying? No, not unless Herobrine and Entity count. But I’ve had real life bullying experiences before. But you get over it. But cyber bullying is on the internet, and it can never go away.

Interviewer: …interesting.

So, avoiding that topic, what did you think of Feyd’s sacrifice?

Tommy: Oh, Feyd, yes, yes, Feyd. Well I mean, it was a cool thing to do and stuff, I’m thankful he did it, but he’s a monster king so I can’t really trust him or like him.

Interviewer: Did anyone say you couldn’t like him?

Tommy: I…um…no. No one said I could or couldn’t.

Interviewer: So you basically mean your biased–

Tommy: No–! Well, yes, but, it’s a monster king. A monster king! How can I trust him?!

Interviewer: you did when it was for Minecraft.

Tommy: …yes I did. But he’s evil–

Interviewer: He saved Minecraft.

Tommy: …

Interviewer: …

Anyways, back on track, do you have any attractions to anyone on your team or in general.

Tommy: Well, yes, but…

No one is going to hear this, right?

Interviewer: Yes sir, no one besides me.

Tommy: Good. To be honest I’ve been really feeling more and more attracted to Hunter.

Interviewer: The Hunter on your team?

Tommy: Yes, her. She’s just so different from everyone else and I just…love her.

Interviewer: But you do know she’s just a program.

Tommy: No she isn’t. She’s a living, breathing organism stuck in a body in a game.

Interviewer: So your saying is we brought her out she would become a living person.

Tommy: Probably and hopefully.

Interviewer: Interesting, so if someone could would you want them to bring her out of the game?

Tommy: Yes, defiantly!

But not just her, all of them.

Interviewer: All of them?

Tommy: All of them.

~Entry #2 – Hunter~

Interviewer: Hello Hunter, glad I could sleek with you today.

Hunter: Um, yeah, pleaser’s mine?

Interviewer: I’m guessing you’re not into these “formal” talks?

Hunter: Formal would be the last thing to describe me.

Interviewer: Oh, I see.

So here’s just a random question but have you any interest in literature?

Hunter: What is this? A book report?!

Interviewer: Answer the question, please.

Hunter: Fine. Yes, I like to write…a lot. That’s what I do in my free time, go off into the forest and write. I don’t go hunting I just write to my heart’s desire.

Interviewer: What do you write about?

Hunter: Everything. Experiences, thoughts, troubles, hardships, relationships…especially relationships.

Interviewer: Could you possibly read a section to me?

Hunter: Yeah, sure, just don’t tell anyone.

\*pulls out book\*

This is really personal but as you don’t tell anyone–

Interviewer: My mouth is shut.

Hunter: Thanks. Here we go.

‘There he goes again, back into his world. I couldn’t tell him, I just stood there. Why couldn’t I reach out and grab onto him? Tell him how I feel? What it my conscience, telling me that it was impossible to love him? Or did I just want him to be feee from guilt?’

Interviewer: Wow, that was amazing. M-May I ask you something?

Hunter: Sure.

Interviewer: Was that about To- I mean, Gameknight?

Hunter: Maybe.

Interviewer: Okay then, what about the monster kings.

Hunter: What about them?

Interviewer: Do you have an attraction for anyone of them?

Hunter: …

I mean Feyd’s a cool guy but…

Interviewer: But…?

Hunter: I don’t know how to choose between him or Gameknight.

Interviewer: Oh, I see.

Hunter: It’s just some confusing stuff, don’t worry about.

Interviewer: Okay, okay.
