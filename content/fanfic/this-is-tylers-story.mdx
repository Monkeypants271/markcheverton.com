**Chapter 1**

Four curious parrots Echo, Blue, Delta, and Charlie were flying about. Blue is the most dominant of the bunch, Echo is the more skittish, Delta is the more daring, and Charlie is the most loving. The four boys loved causing mischief together.

All the four birds land on a busted Xbox and they all think to themselves “What a weird-looking nest. I wonder what type of thing lives here.” Echo, the most curious of them all, pressed a button, and all of a sudden, the four birds got sucked into the Xbox and into Minecraft. All of them fell 3 feet out of the sky and got stunned by the fall. Then all of them hear a strange noise.

**Chapter 2**

Echo, Charlie, Blue, and Delta were laying on the ground frozen in fear from the noise they heard.  Suddenly, out from the bushes jumps a cat named Garfield who had light brown stripes with a yellow-orange body. Garfield circled around the birds, but they can’t do anything until they can fly again, but then an instinct to run coursed through their veins. So, the birds ran for their lives, each one going in different directions.

They were able to lose Garfield and finally they could rest, all of them tired from the chase. The four went into a hollow log for the night. In the morning, Blue & Delta were the first ones to get up. Blue and Delta went searching for food. Echo and Charlie woke up and wondered where Blue & Delta are. After a while, they realized that they were gone to get food. Blue found some berries and Delta found some seeds. “I think that’s enough food to last us a week,” Blue said. “Yeah, I think so too,” Delta said.

“Come on, Delta” Blue said being impatient. “Coming. Just give me a minute.” Delta said grabbing some weird berries. “Ok, coming,” Delta said. After Delta grabbed the weird berries, Blue and Delta flew back to the log. Echo and Charlie were exhausted from working on the decorations in the log. “Look,” Charlie said, looking at Blue and Delta. “Oh, Blue and Delta are home.” Echo said, excited to show them what they had done to the log. After Blue and Delta landed, the two went to go look at what Echo and Charlie had done to the log. “Wow!” Blue said while walking into the log. The log had a storage room and massive bedrooms.

Blue left the log to go exploring. When he was exploring, he found someone. “Oh no…” Blue said remembering who it was. His name was James. James had a bad history with birds. Birds would make nests in his chimney which annoyed James. The local birds called him “the Falcon” and Blue quickly realized why they call him that. A bird flew overhead and James whistled. Then a peregrine falcon came out of nowhere and hit the bird. It didn’t even realize what happened to him. Blue estimated the falcon’s speed. He estimated its speed to be 242 mph.

Blue went back to the log and told everyone what he saw, and everyone knew that they needed to stop James now. The four birds went to go put a stop to James. When they found him, James whistled. “Oh no…not again!” Blue said scared as he expected the falcon to show up and hit them. But the group dodged the attack. The group surrounded the falcon and started to dive into it, the falcon tries to attack but the group is too fast for the falcon to attack and the falcon gets exhausted from trying to attack one of them. With one final dive into the falcon. It dies and the group dives into James and he gets overwhelmed by the speed of the birds. And with a pop, James disappears the group passes out and wakes up in the real world and started to live their normal life’s once again. 5 years passed and Blue, Charlie, Delta, and Echo have mates. And its breeding session.
