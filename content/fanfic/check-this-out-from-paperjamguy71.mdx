PROLOGUE:

PaperJamGuy71 smashed open his bedroom door, and fell onto his bed. He was exhausted after his day at school. He grabbed his laptop from underneath his bed, and turned it on. He spent the next few hours playing Minecraft, on GameKnight999’s new server, which just updated to 1.9. “Hopefully Jay1536 and SkyHetalia have done some updates to our castle in survival,” he muttered to himself. Suddenly, a brilliant white flash enveloped his screen. PaperJam was nearly blinded by this, as he covered his eyes.

It suddenly felt like he was being chomped on by an army of snakes. PaperJam opened his eyes only to close them again. It was so bright, he couldn’t even look. When all the flashing stopped, he opened his eyes, only to find himself inside his castle- the on the GameKnight999 network! PaperJamGuy71 then suddenly looked down at his hands; they were blocky. “Oh crap,” he said. He knew the only reason why everything was blocky, and he was not in his real home; he was in Minecraft. Literally. PaperJam then rushed outside, and saw them. His 2 friends: Jay1536 and Skyhetalia. But they weren’t alone. they were with GameKnight999 and Monkeypants\_271. “What’s going on here?”
