NAME: Twak

MOB, USER, OR NPC?: actually, parrot.

JOB/TITLE: messenger, she can fly faster than most of her kind and is great at reciting voices. Although when she relays the messages, don’t be surprised if she includes some background noises from when the message was being said, such as other voices, monster sounds, or random pickaxes clanging against stone. I’m not sure if she will work for the zombies or NPCs but she is currently friends with a zombie.

APPEARANCE: she is a yellow and blue parrot, a bit larger(and clumsier) than most of the messengers, has excited sparking black eyes.

PERSONALITY: Twak is used to being called upon to provide her services at all hours, and nothing pleases her more than delivering a message. She is loyal, and will not(unlike some other messenger parrots) give the message to anyone else. However, you cannot send her to someone she hasn’t met or a place she has not been, unless you gave extreamly specific instructions. She is popular among her group of deliverers, but remains modest and a little shy.

HOME: The creeper’s jungle, in the far lands.

MOST OFTEN SEEN: delivering a message

FRIEND OR MENTOR: Fy-dua, a young zombie who is kind and rescouseful. He lives not far from the creeper’s jungle, and she often visits his zombie town.

ENEMY OR RIVAL: On-dua, Fy-dua’s older brother. He shoes her away whenever he catches her and Fy-dua conversing and orders Fy-dua around.

FAVORITE OBJECT IN MINECRAFT: Pumpkin seeds. She will do almost anything for this special treat.

MOTTO OR FAVORITE SAYING: “News!” She doesn’t completely understand the meaning of this word, but she likes how it sounds. To her it is sophisticated and important.

STRENGTHS: loyalty, fast flying, and imitation(duh, she’s a parrot)

WEAKNESSES: sometimes her shyness can make her a pushover around other parrots, they take advantage of this, and so her pumpkin seeds are usually divided between friends. She doesn’t really mind, though. Also, she’s not that great at landing. Once she was trying to perch on a shoulder and ending up colliding with the receiver’s horse! Besides that, whenever she’s nervous she has a habit of fluffing her feathers out a bit, so you can always tell when she’s lying.

Hope you like Twak! She was fun to come up with! I really hope parrots will become a bigger part of these Minecraft series!
