## Hands Up

Despite its location, Dots Donut Shop was about to get  
famous……  
\*RING RING RING\* Annies phone rang.  
“Hello…” Annie answered.  
“Hi we have a situation…” Maggie replied back to Annie.  
“Ok I’ll be right there…. Oh wait where are you?” Annie mumbled  
back.  
“I’m at the treehouse,” Maggie replied.  
Annie hung up the phone, grabbed her jacket, and ran out the  
door.  
“Oh, hey Annie…Where are you going?” I questioned.  
“Oh I’m going to the treehouse, we have a situation.. Come  
on Meg,” Annie replied back.  
So now Annie and I were Sprinting to the treehouse….5  
minutes later…… “We are here..” I told Maggie breathing heavily.  
“Ok someone painted on the back of the treehouse and we  
don’t have enough money to pay for the new wood….What are  
we gonna do?” Maggie rushed through pacing back and forth.  
Earlier that evening…. “Hahah” Chuck quietly laughed while  
spray painting a picture on the back of the treehouse then Chuck  
thought “ Why am I doing this even though they are mean to me  
and they are nice to everyone else, two wrongs don’t make a  
right. Oh well they deserve this for being mean to the best bank  
robber in the WORLD” “HAHAHA” Chuck laughed evillike.  
Chuck  
saw a light turn on in Maggie’s house “What is going on out  
there?” Maggie sleepily wondered walking out to her back yard  
“UH” Maggie Screeched.  
“Ok..ok calm down….WAIT I have an idea…” I shouted WAY  
too loud.  
“Ok… What is it.”Maggie and Annie screamed at the same  
exact time.  
“Wait do you know how to make donuts?” I asked Maggie  
and Annie.  
“I do,” Annie answered.  
“No” Maggie confessed  
“Ok we can work with that…” I exclaimed.  
“WORK WITH WHAT…?” Annie and Maggie Screamed.  
“Oh yeah I forgot to tell you my idea..My bad… but we can  
set up a donut shop from that old building down the street from  
here.” I finally explained. “Oh and Maggie you would be the  
cashier.” I finished.  
“Wait.. how will we deliver the donuts?” Maggie asked me.  
“Oh I have a friend that just moved to the neighborhood.. His  
name is Chuck…” I answered back.  
“Ok let’s get started” Annie quietly murmured.  
Annie, Maggie and I headed to the old market that was down the  
street from the treehouse, suddenly there was a stop.  
“We need to get baking supplies, chairs and glass for the  
display.” Maggie told us.  
“Ok, I will get that stuff.” Annie Answered.  
Maggie and I headed to the shop, while Annie want to the public  
market…  
“10 minutes have passed and we still don’t any customers”  
Maggie angrily exclaimed.  
Chuck arrived to the shop with a bag of money, “Here we  
can use this money I “saved up” for the repairs. Chuck mentioned  
nervously.  
“Ummm….ok sounds good let’s get to wo”  
“COME OUT WITH YOUR HANDS UP!” The police called out.  
“What did we do?” Annie questioned walking out the door  
putting her hands up.  
Chuck didn’t come out the door and at that moment I knew  
that something was up.  
“We didn’t do anything,” Maggie told the police repeatedly.  
“Wait where did that chuck guy go?” The police asked us.  
Chuck was running toward the end of the city.  
“STOP CHUCK WAIT….” I yelled.  
In a few seconds….BOOOOOOOM!!! Chuck was hit by a  
train. Everybody was shocked and the cops were disappointed in  
him because he didn’t stop before the train tracks and look to see  
if anything was coming.  
“Well our work here is done we shall collect the money he  
stole from the bank and help you guys with your donut shop,” The  
officer nicely mentioned.  
“I wanted to grab a donut but Chuck committed a crime and  
we had to chase him down, so now it’s time to grab a donut.. And  
remember if you need the money to repair your shop we will help,  
oh and we will tell the mayor too.” the other officer exclaimed.  
Chapter 2  
The Mayor Repairs  
The officers, Annie, Maggie and I walked back to “Dots Donut  
Shop” to grab a donut and because the officers are giving us an  
award we are giving them each a free donut.  
“Hello sillvill I have an announcement Maggie, Annie, And  
Meg are town heroes they saved the banks money from CHUCK”  
“HUH” the town screeched “I know I know, It was a disaster  
especially because he wasn’t new to the neighborhood” ‘HUH” the  
town wailed again “I know because he was disguised as an old  
lady” the mayor explained. “HUH” The town yelled again.  
1 year later….. “Ok we need 500 chocolate with honey  
donuts.” Maggie yelled to Annie in the back of the shop.  
“Ok when do we need that done?” Annie and I asked  
Maggie.  
“By Monday….. And remember today is Friday.” Maggie  
replied back to us.  
And on that day I now know Dot’s donut shop was the most  
famous location in SillVill history…..Despite it’s location.
