###  The Cats And The Cute

One morning I was sleeping and my mom yelled, “ Time to get up!”

I got dressed and went downstairs to eat breakfast. After I ate, my mom came in the room and she said, “You’re going to be late!”

So I got on the bus and went to school.

When I got to school I put my things in my locker and went to class. After class my teacher told me to go to the principal’s office.

I went to the office and my dad was there and I told my dad, “Where are we going?”

He said, “We are going to the doctor.”

I said, “No!!!!!!!!!!!!”

He said he will let me get a cat.

I said, “Ya!”

So we went to the doctor’s office. At the doctor’s office he said I needed to get a refill on medicine so we got my medicine and went to the pet store .

At the pet store there were cats, dogs, BIG wild cats, and wild dogs. I asked if I could go over by the big wild cats and he said, “Yes.”

I looked at a lion and I said, “I want it!”

Dad said, “Yes.”

So I got the lion and it was free. Then mom called and Dad answered it.

She said, “Do not buy a lion!”

I said, “Too late I got it!”

Mom said, “Let me talk to Dad!”

So my mom and dad talked for awhile and finally Dad got off the phone and we went home.

At home I played with my lion and fell asleep and that night I thought about what we would do tomorrow.
