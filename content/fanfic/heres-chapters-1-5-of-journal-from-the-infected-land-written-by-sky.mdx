Intro:

Hi, my name is Flame, I used to be one of a commander in the great war between monsters from the shadow and the humanity, I  was one of the greatest warriors in that battle, everyone, especially my friends, were all proud of me, but, I failed in an important mission, the mission is to protect the chests with resources, but, there is an army of creepers ambushed us, and, most of us got killed, only I survived, but, the city leader thought I am a coward and run away when the creeper attacks, so, I got sent to the most dangerous place in all the overworld: infected land…

Chapter 1:

On this day, I need to find a place to stay, at least a cave, but, I am not living inside a cave like a caveman, I can remember the time we have survival training with my partner: blaster. I wish he was here with me, so at least I have someone… anyone to talk with, I can remember the city leader said there are possibly villages with some decent defenses, so, I am thinking about going there… if he is not lying about it. I am gathering supplies like food, planks, stones, and coal, I haven’t found any irons, welp, I am admitting it, I hate wearing armors, so, I only have an iron sword with an amazing enchantment with me with I came to this dead place, the city leader told me there are caves with creepers, called creeper hives, I definitely never want to go there, I can still remember all my friends died due to those dangerous “moving TNTs”

Chapter 2:

I have traveled pretty far during the night, I am not afraid of the mobs in the dark, remember, I am the best warrior… I used to be the best warrior in my city. In the afternoon, I reached a desert, and I could see a big, grey figure from far away. At first, I couldn’t recognize what these were, and then, a material suddenly popped into my mind: cobblestone! The city leader was right about the village thing, I ran toward the cobblestone wall with every ounce of speed, but then, I stopped: what if the villagers won’t let me stay, what if they think I am infected with the new zombie virus? What if…  I decided to go back to the nearest place that can spawn cows and maybe make myself some leather armor. And that’s exactly what I did, I dyed my leather armor red and black, my favourite colour, the red meant for bravery and determination, black meant for skill. Then, I mine for some iron, and I even came across a few diamonds, which I made into a diamond pickaxe. When everything is ready, I am going to bed, sweet dream, blaster…

Chapter 3:

When I came back from the hole I dug, I walk cautiously toward the village, I can hear yelling on top of a tower, it looks exactly like the watchtower we have in my city, but a lot smaller, when I get close, I heard a voice yelling at me: “ show yourself or you will be the next beehive”  I yelled back: “ wait, I am not a enemy” “identify yourself then” the voice yelled again, I reached into my inventory and grab out a newly enchanted shield blaster get me, and with the other hand, I grabbed the iron sword, the blue and purple light reflect off the cobblestone wall,when I am satisfied with my defense, I yelled back: “ I am no threat of you, I am here to seek for a place safe enough for me to stay” the commanding voice was sounded again “fine, come in, but put away your weapons” when he finished his sentence, the gate opened a crack, just enough for me to get in, I put away my flaming sword and walk straight in the village, a villager sprayed some liquid on me, I can only guess that it was the disinfectant from my city, and, I saw a person with full diamond armor and both diamond sword and iron sword came up to me, he introduced himself: “hi, my name is Gameknight999, the user-that-is-not-a-user” “a… user what?” I am totally confused with the name, who would have such a long name? I thought, I hesitated for a moment and introduced myself too, then, when the tension grew, I excused myself to go to make myself a house, geez, I thought, that was a dangerous village…

Chapter 4:

my day started in a loud “bang”, I jump off my bed and grabbed my flaming sword is a smooth motion, I saw 2 red hair girls, each with an enchanted bow on their hand, running toward my right, one of them shouted to me: “are you going to just stand here with your fire sword, or you are going to help us defeat the zombie army?” without hesitation, she moved out to catch up with her sister, then, I snapped out of my trance and run toward the direction they are going when I move up the wall, I saw a massive horde of zombies, I move to Gameknight and asked: “so, what are you going to do?” “We are going to defeat them, of course” the red-haired girl replied with a scowl, “what do you think?” I move up to her and stare into her eyes “I need a bow, do you have one to spare?” she shrugged, “don’t you know how to make one yourself?” she said sarcastically, then, her sister punched her in the arm: “ hunter, be nice for once!” she then smiled at me: “just maybe ignore my rude sister, she still can’t learn to be nice, I think she is just that way.” I smiled, then told her that one of my friends is literally the same as a hunter, but… I can never see him again because he died during the creeper attack. Then, I paused for a moment, “let’s just focus on the problem before us and forget the past” “I agree” she said. I can already see archers firing arrows at the zombies, I light my sword even brighter when the sword is at its max brightness, I jump down the wall, and slashed at 4 zombies in front of me, they died instantly, due to my sharpness V and fire aspect II, when Gameknight saw my action, he jumped down beside me and slashed at the zombies trying to get behind my back, the archers are doing a good job keeping most of the zombies back, so, me and Gameknight can take a break on all that fighting. In the dinner, hunter patted me on the back and said: “Wow, your fire sword was really awesome, but why can’t you make a diamond one?”  
“The iron one has a magic spell on them, as you can see, they can burst into flame if I want, that’s how I got my name: Flame, the flames on the sword has wither, poison, and weakness III effect”  
“HELP, SOMEBODY, MY VILLAGE NEED HELP!!!” someone screamed while I took a huge gulp of water, the water sprayed on hunter’s face, hunter glared at me and scowled, “eh, sorry?” I said meekly

Chapter 5:

Gameknight runs toward the crafting chamber, with a bunch of villagers behind them, I look at hunter, confused, she just shrugged “just go follow him.” she said to me, I did what she told me, I jumped down stairs and ladders, finally, I reached around room with a bunch of furnaces and crafting benches, I walked toward Gameknight and asked: “what’s going on?”, he pointed to a new villager. I walked toward the villager and handed him a potion of healing. He grabbed it eagerly before I could say any words, by the time he finished, I asked: “what made you wounded? Is it zombies, spiders?  Or maybe creepers?” he shook his head and replied: “no, it’s none of them, it’s a shadowy figure, with a pair of bright, blazing eyes…” I considered his description, and suddenly, I remembered “Herobrine” I hissed. That villager stay silent, for some reason, I turn to Gameknight, he replied: “no way, my dad killed Herobrine, I saw him smashing the computer with a rusty hammer!!! There is no way Herobrine is still alive!!!”
