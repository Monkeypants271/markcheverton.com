## **_The Doomed Adventure_** 

### **_By Emma_**

**_Chapter 1 –_** **_The fight of the ender dragons!_**

Thursday, Aug 22, My mom and dad are going out on a date night the problem is at night the ender dragons are always here so hopefully I don’t get eaten by one. My mom and dad said if I get scared I can go over to my friend’s house. 

At 10:30 my mom and dad were not home yet so I went to my friend, Lynsey’s, house. Lynsey said I can stay the night. Then just at that moment, there was an ender dragon at her front door! We screamed and we got into her house and slammed the door shut! We were so scared we had butterflies in our stomach. We ran up the stairs to her room and locked all the doors and windows.

Then when we went back to her room we just started talking and then we heard two big booms at the top of the house. We slowly walked outside to see what was going on and then we saw two ender dragons on top of the house. Then we heard the dog barking over and over, then we saw a zombie in the house. Lynsey quickly ran outside and threw it out of the house. Then this big group of zombies ran down our street.

When they found the baby they ran back. We saw a car so we ran inside to get in the bed. At 9:30 we both woke up. Lynsey said she saw something weird in the ender dragon’s hand. She said it was like a crystal or a gem. 

We ate breakfast quickly and then headed for the library. We both found a book about the ender dragon crystals so we checked it out. Then we went home, read more into it, and then my tummy exploded! We found out that the crystal was in the middle of the forest, the scariest part of the forest.

We did not want to go but to find out what was happening in our neighborhood so we went. We brought some weapons in case anything happened. We got our tent and set off.

**_Chapter 2 –_** **_The Adventure Begins_**!

We got to the forest about three hours later. We got tired so we set up the tent and got ready to go to bed. We woke up the next morning and heard this weird sound like a hurt wolf. We went to the area where we heard the sound and we saw the wolf so we rushed over to it to see if it was ok.

Luckily Kinsey brought a bandage kit so we helped the wolf and then we mentioned the middle of the forest. The wolf just started jumping up and down and he stole a piece of paper out of our backpack and started to write something. Once he was done we read it and it said, _my parents are stuck there and there are two guardians that were pig zombies and ender dragons._

He said he got hurt by one of the guardians. We went to go see what was going on with the ender dragons so we ran all the way there.

Two hours later we made it there and now we knew what the dog was talking about. We were very quiet and then Lynsey had an idea that we were going to distract the beast so we can get the gem. We brought a flashlight, and the book said the only way to distract a dragon is a flashlight, so I quietly got the flashlight out of the bag turned it on and threw it out in the middle of nowhere but the dragon spotted it. Good thing it did not spot us. Since I was the fastest runner I ran out and stole the gem and then I heard barking noises.  I figured out that it was that wolf’s parents so I said, “Quiet down because the dragon is going to spot us!” We were all back with Lynsey and we were all safe so then we walked to a safe place where the dragon could not find us and settled down for the night.

**_Chapter 3 –_** **_Coming Home at Daylight!_**

We woke up at 10:00 am. We wanted to go home and luckily we still had the dogs and they knew where we were going. We walked half of the way, but the bad thing was that we were running out of food and we were really hungry. We started running until we ran out of breath. It was like three more hours until we got home.

Two hours later we were almost home and then we reached a different neighborhood. We found out that our friends lived here and they helped us get to our houses. We found out that our parents were worried sick because they could not find us. We lied and said we were in that fort that we made. We went home and had a good night and I had the best sleep I have ever had in my bed alone.
