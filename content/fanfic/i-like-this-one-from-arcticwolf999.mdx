Gameknight looked at their prisoner, a Zombie. Everything about the monster was strange, the way that he talked(Zombies normally have a rough voice, this ones voice was smooth.), the way that he moved was almost identical to a user and, above all, the speed at which he fought. Gameknight had come across him whilst patrolling around the Oracle’s temple, when he calmly walked out in front of Gameknight. Gameknight and his warriors had surrounded him, but he was impossible to defeat, as his blade seemed to teleport, and yet, he was playing with Gameknight, thus, they had been forced to take him prisoner. And Gameknight now stood in front of him. He was in a Obsidian cell, with iron bars on the front. Gameknight grew impatient of his continued silence:  
“Okay, speak up. Who are you?”  
The zombie turned his head, revealing glowing eyes like the ones that Herobrine wore, but his glowed purple. Strange.  
He laughed,”That’s what they all want to know, Gameknight999″  
“TELL ME!”  
“Very well, I am the Glitched XP, the one mob that’s been here since the Awakening.”  
“You’re a Zombie.”  
“Not exactly. I am a Zombie now, but last week, I was a NPC, and before that, a Skeleton.”  
“How?”  
“Let me explain. a long time ago, my minecraft body, evolved, eventually being able to infect others with my Glitched XP.”  
“I still don’t get it.”  
“When I die, a purple XP orb get’s released. Any mob that picks up the XP becomes my next body.”  
“Okayyyy…”  
“You’re confused. Always happens.”  
Suddenly a NPC arrives in the chamber.  
“Sir, A zombie horde has been sited traveling towards us,” The NPC said.  
Gameknight responded, “Very well, I’ll be up there in a moment.”  
“the have lives too, you know.”  
“What?”  
“The monsters. the have hopes, dreams, family. The only ones that I would willingly destroy are Herobrine’s creations.”  
“Uh, sir, Herobrine is leading them.”  
“Release me, and I’ll fight Herobrine for you.”  
“Ha. As if I’m going to fall for that one.”  
Suddenly, he teleported out of the cell and next to Gameknight.  
“That wasn’t a offer. It was a statement.”
