## Cold hard days

### Chapter 1 The meeting

One beautiful day two best friends {Macy and Jacob\] were walking to school,  
stomp stomp stomp…  
Once they got to school they sat down as usual and saw the most worst  
terrifying horrifying words anyone can ever imagine. 3 NEW KIDS WILL BE HERE  
TOMORROW. Then my teacher, Miss Hiem said what the board had written on it.  
I was scared. I hate new kids because that could mean new bullies. Most of the  
time they get bullied when new kids come.  
THE NEXT DAY  
Stomp stomp went footsteps. we stopped but the footsteps kept going.  
Stomp stomp stomp we looked over and we saw 1 girl and 2 boys. One boy  
had blue and black hair the other boy had black and green hair and they both  
had a tattoo on their arms saying ‘Swag,’ The girl had blue, pink and black hair  
but no tattoo. “Omg’” I said with a surprised face. “‘I have never seen 7th  
graders look like that” Jacob told me.  
Once they got to school they sat down all 5 and all the extra kids. They sat in  
a row then put their feet up on their desk like they were the most talented  
coolest kids in the whole school.  
“Welcome” yelled my teacher miss Hiem. “There are 3 new kids here. would you  
three like to come up here”? ‘No” they all told her at the same time. She got real  
scared, and then it became contagious and it spread to the rest of the class.  
Now all of us were scared. But there was one girl who was not scared.  
“PHEEEEEE” went something strange. Now 7 kids were complaining about some  
kids spitting spitballs at the back of their heads. “Ewww,” I said even though  
nothing happened to me.  
“STOP!” our teacher told the kids. “No” they told her. Then our teacher did  
not do anything. After a little while they noticed that I had one terrible flaw. 1  
of my eyes is bigger than the other, I did not want them to stare at me. They  
started staring at me. I got nervous so I asked to go to the bathroom. Once I  
walked into the bathroom I sat right in front of a hard bumpy wall. It hurt just to  
sit there. Then all the sudden I heard stomp stomp stomp “It can’t be,” I  
whispered to myself.

### Chapter 2 The Miracle

Stomp! It was quiet at first then stomp STOMP STOMP STOMP. Then she  
was there, that rock-n-roll crazy girl and the new kids. My tears came out like a  
waterfall. I didn’t want her to see me cry since I have a terrible flaw. I was  
shaking even more than ever. Shaking, shaking. She walked right in front of me.  
“Listen” I told her. “Don’t ever in your entire life make fun of me because of my  
eyes. I am no different than you. If you do you will regret it. I like my style…”  
Then I got interrupted. “We like your style. Well, I like it, the boys don’t  
understand fashion.”  
“Oh,” I told her with a warm smile.

### Chapter 3

#### The Ending That Changed Everything

I walked into the classroom boom boom boom. Then I read the whiteboard.  
“Everyone make fun of the little girl coming back from the restroom” the  
whiteboard had written on it. Tears were coming from my eyes again. I asked  
Jacob what happened when I was gone. He replied, “ I don’t know I just woke” he  
looked like he got hurt.  
Then stomp stomp stomp the new girl Jenaxe came in. “STOP DON’T MAKE  
FUN OF THIS CHILD!” she yelled in disappointment.  
“Make me,” Vince said, then Jenax slowly stepped in front of Vince. Slowly and  
even more slow, then you hear little boys screaming and running out of the  
building like elephants looking at a mouse. I ran to her and I told her about how  
I was sorry because I yelled at her. Then I thanked her. She responded with a  
warm friendly smile. “They won’t ever bother you ever again.” Then I thanked her  
again and again over and over time after time after time. Now I don’t care if  
new kids come because I have a Friend that will help me out.  
And that’s the story of kindness.
