The Minecraft Diaries – Secrets under the Surface – ???

(This story is a transcript by FrozenBoy20 from one of the most mysterious and disturbing recordings in Minecraft. Takes place one decade after the disappearance of Steve’s brother, Herobrine.)

I run.

I have no idea who is chasing me, and whoever it is I do not want to meet him.

The sound of my footsteps echoed against the walls of the tight cave, lit up by a torch in my hand. The backpack I’m wearing lightly bumped my back repeatedly as I sprinted through the maze of the cave.

And the sounds… not sounds, whisperings… HORRIBLE whisperings surrounded me as I run. The whisperings got louder, as if it was getting closer.

Finally, it fades.

Safe, for now.

I took a right corner in the cave and was mystified at what I saw.

What… is this place? I thought.

It is a room I’ve never seen before.

“Room” is probably not the correct word to use. It is more like an ancient stronghold of some kind. Iron bars arched over the entrance connected to the mossy stones. As I strolled into the stronghold, I can see lava contained in stone blocks I have never seen before on the two corners near the entrance. There was a staircase made of the same material as the surrounding area, but the top layer has a spawner. I tried to make sense of the mob inside the spawner, the problem is that I can only figure that it is silver and is tiny. I slowly climbed the stairs avoiding the spawner, but what I saw didn’t make sense.

There were the strangest blocks I have ever seen, or maybe they’re probably not even blocks. Each of those “Blocks” had a marine blue top with an Ender Eye in the slot, then the rest of it was tan coloured “Blocks”. There are twelve of them arranged in a three by three square excluding the corners, and in the middle was an ominous void… It seemed like a portal to… somewhere…

I sat down on the top of the stair and took out a small device and a book. I flipped through the book trying to find a breakthrough to activate the device.

The sound of the pages should’ve been lost in the sound of the burning, hot lava. Instead, it echoed through the stronghold and out into the dark hall.

Flap flap flap…

Flap…

Flap…  
I saw something in the pages that caught my eyes.

There was a picture of the same device that was sitting next to my side, I quickly scanned through it…  
And found the code to activate the device.

Excited by the discovery, I quickly threw my book to the side and picked up the device. Gave it a spin, and pressed it.

And regretted it.

The whispering of the ghosts begins to echo in the stronghold all around me. It keeps on getting louder, like a speaker getting louder. Wind blasted me in the face, pushing me back into the portal. I caught myself just before I can get even an inch closer to the endless void behind me. I stood back up quickly to get off the staircase.

Then the sound of the footsteps paralyzed me.

It’s not my footsteps, I wasn’t running anywhere.

I gazed at the dark hallway in front of me, and I saw my end.

In the darkness, I saw two white lights…

Not lights.

White, glowing eyes.

I stood there paralyzed in fear.

The Minecrafter came running towards me.

Eyes growing brighter, bigger.

I can only scream one word.

“HEROB-”
