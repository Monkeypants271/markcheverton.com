Subject: A fan made story of “Attack of the Shadow Crafters”

Herobrine was sitting on a peace of stone having a discussion with the queen of spiders, Shaikulud, and the king of creepers, Oxus, Herobrine was disappointed of the last battle and the failure of Vo-Lak, his eyes were glowing bright white nearly as bright as the sun. As he sat there in a huge chamber of Shaikulud’s nest and the idiotic monster mob surrounding him, he knew he had to make something better then the foolish monsters. He needed his own shadow crafters, each one that can craft things that lives in the shadows. He stood and teleported to the nearest village right outside of it, watching the pathetic villagers walking around aimlessly, it was night time and it was the perfect time to strike. Teleporting to every cave and taking every monster he sees to the village including the monster king and queen and yelled at the top of his lungs with his harsh bright white eyes glowing dangerously. About 50 mobs went through the village sweeping through it like a tidal wave. “KEEP THEM ALIVE I NEED TO DO SOMETHING TO THEM,” Herobrine yelled as the mob cornered every villager to the side of a building, killing some making them suffer a bit. Herobrine made it to the crowd of villagers and smiled a toothy grin. He used his crafting powers of a creeper and dragged 10 creepers to one villager looking like a farmer but he won’t be for long, as he dragged them into his crafting powers, his hands slowly turned from a sickly yellow to a dark green. His hands working in a blur, damaging the villagers and creepers at the same time and putting his hatred and evil thoughts into his creation. When he finished with the formation of the villagers and creepers together, the villagers stood slowly trying to stand, and green aura surrounding the villager, as he stood, the villagers opened his eyes showing a bright green in his eyes and slowly faded to dark green eye sockets like normal villager eyes.

“Stand Shadow-Crafter, for I will call you Creeperbrine, u can craft creepers and make them stronger including the creeper king,” said Herobrine as he had a slight grin on his face. Creeperbrine smiled and started to craft something, his hands glowing sickly green and he crafted a small little creeper, testing his dark shadow crafting skills. Herobrine laughed a demonic laugh.
