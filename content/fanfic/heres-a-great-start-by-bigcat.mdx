Before him stood one little NPC. She had long black hair, and by the color of her smock, Gameknight knew she was a planter.  
“What happened?” He softly asked as he approached the NPC  
“They took him,” she struggled to say, her HP dangerously low. If she didn’t eat soon, then she would probably not make it.  
Gameknight999 knew he had no food, and he may not have time to go get some from the surface, so he ran towards one of the many chests that sat nearby. But it had very little in it, only a couple of apples.  
He thought for a second, then turned and ran towards another chest, reaching in, he grabbed eight pieces of gold, then ran towards a crafting bench.  
He could still remember the first time he met crafter, he had been so overwhelmed of the fact that he was in minecraft for real, that he lost his temper and broke a crafting bench while an NPC was still using it, as it shattered, it had freed the NPC’s hands, giving them hope of defeating Erebus and his army of monsters.  
A few minites later, he returned to Planter with two golden apples, she ate hem up quickly.  
As her HP began to rise, she was able to tell Gamkenight what had happened  “Tbey took crafter,” she said.  
Gameknights eyes grew wide with shock.  
“Who?”
