Gameknight and Richard were walking in the colorful Mesa when suddenly a creature came out and attacked them. Gameknight quickly drew out his sword while Richard notched an arrow in his bow. Gameknight circled the creature and whacked it with his sword. Richard tried to pull back his bow but the creature’s short sword found flesh and slashed at it. Soon, the creature was dead.

“What was..”

“A Uruk-hai,” replied Richard.

“And the sword, I’ve never seen it before?” asked Gameknight, a worried look on his face.

“That is a dagger. It is from the Lord of the Rings mod. Doesn’t do as much damage though.”

Richard kept on walking with Gameknight following close behind. They walked non-stop for an entire Minecraft day only stopping to eat. They were finally there, the village. The village had the normal look, a well at the center, a watchtower, but no blacksmith.

I wonder what that two block high black structure is. Gameknight thought to himself. All the villagers had the normal look except one. That villager had a blue smock and two eyebrows instead of the one unibrow. The villager looked at Gameknight suspiciously from the window of a long and wide wooden house.

“Hello User-that-is-not-a-user!” said a voice in the crowd.

Gameknight turned to see a tall villager with a black smock with a white stripe going down the middle.

“So, you’ve met the Dunedain,” the tall village crafter said to Gameknight.

“Um… what is a Dunedain?” asked Gameknight.

“Richard!” answered the crafter.

Richard didn’t have the normal villager look, but the look of a player without a nametag and server thread.

“We have to discuss the issue of the Uruk-hai,” staggered Richard. “Me and the User-that=is-not-a-user saw one on the way here. I’m afraid there getting closer. Gameknight will go with you to the crafting chamber while I look around the perimeter”

Without a word, the crafter walked towards the watchtower with Gameknight close behind.

“I wonder how that Uruk-hai got from that village to the path near here?” Gameknight asked to no one.
