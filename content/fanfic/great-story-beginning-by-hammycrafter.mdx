When GameKnight got through the portal, he instantly felt hot, sweat trickling down his face. A giant nether fortress loomed above, blazes patrolling everywhere, blasting any intruders to ashes. GameKnight pulled out a pickaxe and dug at the base of a pillar. He dug at it until he had made a staircase upwards into the fortress.

He snuck along the walls, watching for any patrolling blazes. Fisher followed GameKnight along the wall and that was when they came to a chasm between two sides of the fortress. “Fisher, give me your fishing rod,” GameKnight motioned. Fisher did as he was told. GameKnight swung the fishing rod with all his might and swung it so the hook hit the other side of the chasm. He climbed across, unti his grip started to slip. “Fisher! HELP!!!!” GameKnight screamed.

Then he realized his big mistake. He yelled, giving away his position. Blazes rushed across the room, hurling fire charges at GameKnight. Fisher realized he had some snowballs from once fishing in an ice biome. He pulled them out of his inventory, and chucked them at the blazes. They wheezed in pain and fled the scene.

“Phew, thanks, Fisher.” GameKnight said, finally on the other side of the chasm. GameKnight threw a single ender pearl to fisher. “Throw this,” he said. Fisher threw the ender pearl and landed right next to GameKnight. When they were almost done celebrating, something hit them both on the head and knocked them out . . . cold.
