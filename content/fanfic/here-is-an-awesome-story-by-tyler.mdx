Gameknight and his friends, warriors, and wolfs were heading back to

the village when Herder asked, “ Was that the last battle for Minecraft?” “

Boy, I sure hope so, I hate always battling and losing NPC lives.” said

Gameknight. Then Gameknight had an fun idea. “ Crafter, Herder, Hunter,

Sticher, Digger, Cobbler, I think we should all build a big boat for the whole

army and us for a nice relaxing adventure so we can fish and relax like

never before, literally like never before.” said Gameknight and everybody

laughed. So when they got back to the village, they announced the plan and

everyone liked but Gameknight added something, “ Everybody with leave

their weapons and armor here so we can relax.” Everybody seemed fine

with it so everybody put their armor and swords and bows away and got

their axes to start chopping wood. It took a two days to get enough wood but, they did it.

Gameknight hired some builders to help build the ship, while he sent

the others out to get string, sticks, food, wool, glowstone, pumpkins, coal,

furnaces, crafting tables, chest, and glass. When they finished the boat it

was huge and awesome!!! Once he assigned everybody a bed with a

chest, furnace, and a crafting table, he made some fishing rods and put the

enchantments, Lure III, Luck of the Sea III, and Unbreaking III on all of

them. Once everybody had what they needed and were ready, the set off.

Herobrine just finished his new monster king, Stray Monster King, he

took him up to the surface to get the feel of the sun. Thats when he saw the

pathetic Gameknight999 and his army set off. Herobrine told his new king

to follow him and attack when they got close enough. Herobrine built him a

ship out of Nether Brick with fire on the top. The king gathered his army

and set off after Gameknight999.

When Gameknight found a spot with a lot of fish he set an anchor

down and told everyone it is time to fish and get some food, but remember

to cook it first. everyone caught a fish right away and went to their furnace

to cook it. After everyone caught twenty fish the picked up the anchor and

headed off, again.

Only little did Gameknight999 know that Shredder was following them

( the Stray Monster King ). He was giving a speech to his army, “ Fellow

warriors, we were sent out here to find Gameknight999 and kill him and his

army once and for all.” said Shredder. The army cheered. Shredder

continued, “ We will not stop until we killed his army, and when we rule the

Overworld over those NPCs!”. Once again the army cheered and they

continued forward.

Gameknight was just cruising when he heard, “ Monster boat! “. He

leaped into action and handed everyone an enchanted bow with, Infinity,

Flame, Unbreaking, and Power that he kept in a secret chest. After he got

everyone a weapon, he gave everyone iron armor, once again in the secret

chest. When that was done, he looked onto the boat and saw a monster he

didn’t recognize at first then realized it was a stray mob! Gameknight yelled

out, “ Everybody, those are stray mobs if you were wondering and they

have a king which I don’t know who he is.”. Then right on cue Shredder

yelled out, “ My name is Shredder and i am here to take you and your

pathetic NPC army down!”.

Gameknight thought, “ Great another battle, just what I need, more

NPC lives that I am responsible for. Just great.”. Hold your fire, go for the

front ones with bows. “ Fire, Stray Mobs, fire now!” said Shredder. “ NPCs,

fire now!” said Gameknight, “ Shoot and duck NPCs, shoot and duck! The

Stray Mobs don’t work together they shoot at their own rate pick a target

out and fire and kill the move on, knock out the archers!” They had the

odds in their favor until swordsmen came out of the stray mob boat. Gameknight

panicked and didn’t know what to do, fear was nibbling away at his

courage then he picked out Hunter and Sticher to shoot the swordsmen

coming out of the boat. They built little towers and started to shoot. They

were doing great but Gameknight didn’t think they could keep up with the

constant flow of the swordsmen, so he picked out some of the back archers

and brought them downstairs to shoot at the closer ones with him.

They were pushing the mobs back until Shredder yelled out, “

RETREAT!!!”.The monster went back the boat but they still shot to make

sure next time they came back they have fewer monsters they were able to

knock out 50 more stray mobs. Once they were out of sight Gameknight

said to his warriors, “ I didn’t know Herobrine made another monster king

so we need to move and find another village to stay in and get more

warriors. Sadly, we lost a few warriors so look around and lets honor the

fallen for heir sacrifice.”. Gameknight along with the rest of the army, raised

their hands in the air and squeezed their hands until their knuckles were

red. Now lets move guys so Gameknight999 went up to the wheel and went

for land and saw a village along the coast.

When Shredder got back Herobrine didn’t like what he heard, “ What

do you mean you retreated?!” asked Herobrine, “ Gameknight999 and his

army was kill ours, if we stayed a minute longer we would all be dead.”

responded Shredder. Hero brine said, “ I’ll give you move troops if you don’t

retreat again and if you do, you will meet your fate, do you understand?” “

Yes master.” responded Shredder. “ Good and just to make sure you don’t

retreat again, I will be coming with you and will not fight.” Hero brine

gathered more troops and set of after Gameknight999.

When they arrived they found out that this was Hunter’s and Sticher’s

old village. They were greeted by the village crafter and a baker and Hunter

asked about her parents and the village craft said they recently just died.

For once Hunter and Sticher both cried. Game knight tried to comfort them,

“ I am so sorry about your parents Hunter and Sticher.” Hunter said, “

Thanks Gameknight but lets say what we came here for.” and both of them

stopped crying. Game knight asked the crafter to have a private meeting

with him if its OK. So he lead them down to the crafting chamber and told

everybody to get out for a second. Game knight and the village crafter sat

down and Gameknight said, “ Do you think your village could help us fight

against the stray mobs?”. The village craft replied, “ I don’t know, will we all

die?” “ No” said Gameknight999. “Then we will do it.” said the village

crafter. “ Oh one more thing, do you think we could stay here for a while?”

asked Gameknight. “ Sure why not, anyway we need help training.” said

the village crafter.

They sleep through the night and at the break of dawn, they started

training, archery, sword skills, and deflecting arrows. They got 15 archers

and 15 swordsmen/women. so they hoped on with all of their goods got

them assigned beds and everything they set off after the stray mob ship.

Herobrine gave Shredder 200 more warriors to fight with and

Shredder is doing the same thing with the archers and the swordsmen.

They found each other and the battle begun.

This time Gameknight was the first to attack.Archers fired rapidly and

knocked out a lot of archers and thats when the swordsmen came across

but Gameknight was ready. he had TNT cannons ready and shot, not only

did it knock out all of the swordsmen but it put a hole and it killed all of the

other swordsmen waiting to go out!Shredder almost yelled retreat but that

was when he remembered Herobrine was aboard so he added his bow to

the battle. Game knight and some other swordsmen snuck over and

destroyed all of the archers and Gameknight and Shredder had a face-off.

Gameknight told his warriors to go back and Shredder told his 5

remaining warriors to stand back. Game knight was the first to strike, he

slashed at a leg and Shredder howled out in pain swung his gold sword

Gameknight but missing by a mile because Gameknight was already

behind him hit him repeatedly in the back until dropped to the ground on

both legs and struggled to breath and Gameknight couldn’t understand

what he said then he disappeared with pop he left his gold sword, armor,

XP, and once again Herobrines ender sword. Game knight picked it up and

dropped it into the water but once again Herobrine was waiting. Gameknight

headed back over to his boat and Digger asked, “ What did

Shredder say to you?” “ It sounded like, Herobrine will get you or something

but I’m not sure.” Then they headed back home to the village safe and

sound, or so they thought. . .
