## **_Mos and the Mad Zitz_**

### **_By Tucker B_**

**_Chapter 1_**

**_Mos and Zitz_**

Mos is the hero of future town and a person named…Zitz wanted to take over future town

but he kept failing. Today all of that is going to change.

In future town Mos went to a bar and Zitz shows up to try to take over future town again.

But Zitz brings a robot and Mos thought that he could beat Zitz.

But Mos lost to the giant robot and Zitz took over future town.

**_Chapter 2_**

**_Took over_**

Since Zitz took over future town Zitz made Mos his slave and Zitz named it Zitz town!

Zitz made a huge huge pit for Mos at night. Mos tries to escape every single night and he can not get out of the pit.

So he tried to do his flying ability but it did not work but Zitz didn’t take Moses super jump and he got away.

**_Chapter 3_**

**_Escaped_**

When Mos got out of the pit he went back to his tower home but Zitz took that over as well.

But he did not lock it so Mos went in it and a alarm went off!”eeeeeeerrrrrrr.”

Then Mos got out as fast as a tiger and he got away.

Then Zitz looked outside but Mos was hiding and Zitz did not see him and Mos didn’t know Zitzes secret plan.

**_Chapter 4_**

**_Zitzes secret plan_**

Zitzes secret plan is to destroy the HERO League!

The people that are in the league are Mexico,Zeno,and… Amber.

Mexico is in Mexico.

Zeno is in Past town,and Amber is in the good land town. So watch out all of you!

**_Chapter 5_**

**_Mos with the Stop_**

Mos heard Zitz in his room saying,”This will be the best plan ever to destroy the HERO League. HaHaHaHaHaHa.”

So Mos went for a spy mission and he gets to the beam. He grabs a wrench by the beam and destroys it and Mos whispers,”Good bye massive beam.”

**_Chapter 6_**

**_My powers are back_**

Since Mos saved the HERO League he went to get his powers in the backyard underground. Then he heard,”I will never ever let Mos take these powers.

They are to good.”

Since Zitz was distracted Mos went to try and get the powers but Zitz turns around and Mos stops so he watched till Zitz left.

Then Zitz left and Mos got the powers.

**_Chapter 7_**

**_The real fight_**

So when Mos got the powers he wanted Zitz to be gone for good so he went to find Zitz and Mos hid found Zitz and Mos said,”You’re now going to get what you’re going to need from the past 4 days.”

So Mos went to fight Zitz then Zitz turned around and started to say,”You think you can beat me.

I am invincible.” “Look at what you did.

You destroyed the town now,I am going to destroy you.”

They fighted for a half hour and Mos used his fireball for the win.

All shall be controlled in 3 days.

Muwahahahah!” Zitz yelled to everybody.

**_Chapter 9_**

**_Why isn’t it back to normal_**

“Why isn’t it back to normal?”

Zitz town is gone forever and the fake Zitz.

But it’s not back to normal.

A nice nurse came to help Mos and did not care about Zitz but the world changed.

Mos made a tournament for a sidekick and a person named,Lonil won. Then Lonil was Moses sidekick.

So whenever a villain came there would be Mos and Lonil,and fake Zitz was crushed by Mos so fake Zitz will never be seen again.
