Joker Vs. Batman

By Hayden

Joker Fights Batman

One night it was a foggy evening in Gotham City. Shadows fell all over the streets. Big buildings towered above. Joker was wandering around Gotham City. The Joker wasn’t nice at all. He was very mean, even though he looked like a clown. The Joker had tried to kill Batman in the past, but didn’t kill him. While Batman was trying to fight Joker, Batman heard somebody yell, “Help!”

It was TV’s superstar, Adam West.

When Batman got to Adam West, Adam West said, “Na Na Na Na…Help!”

So Batman helped Adam West.

After that Adam West said, ”Thanks for your help.” Adam West said, “I’ll help you.”

Batman said, “Thank you.”

While Batman and Adam West were fighting Joker, Batman saw Green Loontern.

Green Loontern said, “You know what this place needs? More crystals.”

Batman said, “You’re thinking of Zamaron.”

Green Loontern said, “Oh yea, sorry. Anyway I’ll help you guys.”

Batman said, “Thank you.”

So Green Loontern and Batman set up the trap. The trap was like a mouse trap with sticky stuff so Joker walked over to the trap and then felt sticky stuff on his legs Joker thought to himself where is this stuff coming from. After that Adam West threw a boomerang at

Joker. But they missed Joker dodged it after Adam West threw the boomerang at Joker. The police came to get Joker and he tried to run away. Green

Loontern said, “Too bad Joker, we got you.”

After that Joker was back in jail.
