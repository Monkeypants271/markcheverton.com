Northern Storm (Part 2)

Chapter 5: High command

Date: November 27th, 2017

Location: South-eastern minecraftia 

Time: 3:46 pm 

Soldier had just turned off the TV. He glanced at his phone. He couldn’t believe the message he just received.

\== ROBLOXIAN HIGH COMMAND==

TO BE BROUGHT TO THE IMMEDIATE ATTENTION OF SOLDIER\_235

GRIEFING OPERATION BEGINS ON NOVEMBER 28TH. PRIMARY TARGETS 

STRIKE 1: MINECRAFTIAN EMBASSY IN GEOMETRY DASH

STRIKE 2: SOUTHERN COMMAND

STRIKE 3: MN BASE 3

STRIKE 4:(TBD)

TNT OBTAINED FROM 34 FALONEY STREET, ROOM 7

NEW TNT TYPE “C4”

WAIT FOR FURTHER INSTRUCTION

END 

Soldier stared at this news with his mouth wide open. He scrambled to his feet and left. Before he left, he noticed something was off. He put on his mask. Little did he know the wi-fi was tampered with. The RZSDC now have an extra piece of evidence against him.

Date: November 27th, 2017

Location: South-eastern minecraftia 

Time: 4:52 pm 

Grevich was flying in a quiet stealth RAH-66 comanche helicopter. His pilot, a battle-worn vetaran was very skilled. The autocannon of the helicopter was armed with mini-tnt shells. The could be crafted by putting 1 iron ingot on top of 3 gunpowder to create 27 pieces. They had loaded he helicopter with 81 shots. They were well armed for possible attacks. After the Robloxian army attacked minecraftia, the mobs have agreed to help the humans fight the robloxians. Zombie motorcades were on the ground, escorting VIP members of the government to south-eastern minecraftia. They had been attacked twice already. Endermen were used as communications, as they could teleport, warning the zombies of the attack. Skeletons helped the zombie motorcade fight flying units. Because of the route they took, they didn’t get attacked as frequently as the other routes. Once they got there, they were greeted by Xa-tul, the  recarinated. They then went into the southern high command. Skeleton snipers were placed around the base along with creeper grenadiers. The meeting started to take place. “I think we are all aware of the current situation with Robloxia” Grevich stated. Xa-tul spoke up. “Sir, we have information that shows that the Miecraftian embassy in  Geometrydashia just got attacked.” Everyone stared at him.

Date: November 27th, 2017

Location: West-central, Gemoetrydashia

Time: 3:46 pm 

Bacon-pal had just read the message that Robloxian high command sent him. He knew that Soldier\_235 also received the message. Although the message was only supposed to be received by Soldier, he wanted to impress the officials by doing better that Soldier. Because of him, Bocon-pal got knocked out of the Top 10 best Robloxian soldiers. He needed to impress the officials and attack the embassy before soldier. He quickly crafted some TNT and walked up to the embassy gate. “Name?,” Asked the Geometrydashian guard. But before there was a response, he saw that a chunk of the gate was blasted missing and so was Bacon-pal. All that was left of Bacon-pal was a massive pile of loot and documents.

Xa-tul started talking again. “Documents turned in by the Geometrydashian government show that there was a Bacon-haired attacker at the embassy. He blew up a chunk of the gate, but blew himself to pieces in the process. Turned out he was Bacon-pal, long time rival of Sodier\_235. Agents, come in!”. Two Geometrydashian agents came in through the door. “According to the guard at the embassy, the attacker dropped a mountain of documents written in a traditional Robloxian language. They are documents that exactly reports the details of previous attacks, such as the Shipyiozineck school attack. Many mysteries that have been associated with Robloxian operations in minecraftia have been solved, such as the 2011 disappearance of the minecraftian arliner. It turned out to be that is was disassembled and the passengers were held captive until they got released to an unknown location. That proves the innocence of the 23 border crossers in 2015.” The other agent started speaking. “Also, there were hand-written documents that showed the location of the 56 lost miners. Apparently they are in the deepest cave of the mojang cave system. The document says that they were left with 5 years worth of rations before an artificial cave collapse supposedly killed them”. Grevich spoke up. “We have to get to where Soldier is buying the TNT. Let’s send creeper grenadiers and skeleton snipers to the location of the shop, 34 Faloney street. I will make some more grenades”. The recipe for grenades were simple. 4 iron surrounding a TNT. That creates 4 grenades. He crafted 64, a stack. He gave 8 to each of the 8 creepers. Their explosive ability was a last resort now that they have teamed with the humans. The skeletons were given harming V arrows, 15 each. They were also given crossbows since pillagers didn’t trust people.” 

Xa-tul then stated “Let’s send them before it is too late!” They were too late.

Chapter 6: Darkest night

Date: November 27th, 2017

Location: South-eastern minecraftia 

Time: 4:34 pm 

Soldier had arrived at 34 Faloney street before they even started talking. He went into the shop and said ‘Room 7”. The Attendant nodded slowly and lead him to the room. Inside was first-quality experimental C4. “The C4 is crafted by putting clay around 1 block of TNT, multiplying its power by 10. Once ignited, it has a 15 second timer. We suggest you use 10 pieces of C4 since clay surrounding TNT creates 5 pieces of C4”. “I see, I see…” Said Soldier. Suddenly, his phone buzzed. “Hold on a second,” he said. His phone had this message.

\==ROBLOXIAN HIGH COMMAND==

BACON-PAL KILLED BY TNT ACCIDENT AT MINECRAFTIAN EMBASSY

END

Soldier stared at the message in shock. How did Bacon-pal die? A TNT accident seems too immature for Bacon-pal, as he was a trained soldier. Suddenly, a thought struck him. Bacon hair was a rival of Soldier! He probably tried to do better than soldier, but died from being to cocky. Soldier smiled. He was going to do better. “Alright, I have to leave”. “Good luck!” Yelled the attendant.

Date: November 28th, 2017

Location: West-central Geometrydahsia

Time: 1:43 am

Things were tuning down at the minecraftian embassy in Geometrydashia. The employees were dozing off. The guards were in the armory, unequiping themselves. Soldier had arrived. He had arrived at the embassy on a bus. Because he knew the driver was suspicious of him, he shot him with a crossbow with power XXVII. He stepped outside the bus. He stepped off the bus. He knew that the guards would be suspicious of the noises. One guard had heard the sound of leaves scrunching. The guard grabbed a Geometrydashian Mini-wave shooter and went outside. Soldier saw him. He was hit by a stone spike and died with a depressing “Biump”. Soldier then went behind the embassy and planted the C4. He set a fuse for 30 seconds. He ran away before it exploded. A pebble scraped him.

Chapter 7: Chaos

Date: November 28th, 2017

Location: South-eastern minecraftia 

Time: 7:52 am 

Xa-tul woke up. He went to the dining room of the compound and ordered a coffee. The attendant nodded and slid him a coffee on a coaster. He received a message on his phone. He read it. Here is what it read.

\==TO BE BROUGHT TO THE ATTENTION OF MINECRAFT HIGH COMMAND==

SKELETON SNIPERS AND CREEPER GRENADIERS DID RAID OF 34 FALONEY STREET, NO TRACE OF SOLDIER. CAN BE RELATED TO ATTACK TODAY. ALERT STATUS 7/10

END

Xa-tul dropped his coffee cup. “Can I speak to Grevich?,” he asked. “I will call him. Ahem…” He cleared his throat and turned on the microphone “GREVICH REPORT TO THE DINING ROOM”

. Xa-tul clenched his ears as the feedback was deafening. “Is there anything wrong with the mic system?,” he asked, annoyed. “Sorry!,” the attendant said over the microphone. Xa-tul fell off his chair this time. “ARGGGRGHHHH!!!!,” he yelled, clearly annoyed. “I will-”. He got cut off by grevich entering the room. “Hello, you wished to speak to me?”. “Yes, the minecraftian embassy was bombed’.

\==TO BE BROUGHT TO THE ATTENTION OF MINECRAFT HIGH COMMAND==
