### The Rejected Iron Golem

CHAPTER 1 – How it started

The Warden grabbed a splintered part of an iron door. Why had this iron nugget triggered something in his dull brain? Then it started to come back. He remembered that one fateful night when he had been beaten up by an old villager. The NPC (non playable character) had told an iron golem to leave. How would that village protect itself? Then the once-iron golem thought that he would find lots and lots of players and more iron golems. So he set out. Far and wide he searched. But he couldn’t find anyone! So he accidentally fell into a cave.

Then he found out that it was a deep dark cave. He searched far and wide for any player but he couldn’t find anything. He got intrigued by the Black Blocks with blue flecks in them so he got rich by them with wandering Traders and then he got so haunted by them that he started to turn blue and got a permanent scowl! He started to get blind bit by bit.

CHAPTER 2 – Blinded by The iron golem blues

The iron golem walked over to the black blocks with different blue flecks. “What are these?” He wondered. Are these… Diamonds!? He was so excited! He broke the blocks with super-golem strength. Diamonds! They were really diamonds! He was so excited! But… wait… why was there an emerald? Just floating there? Bonk! The warden fell face forward with a bump on his head.

CHAPTER 3 – The zoo

The cursed iron golem woke up in a glass cage. His head swiveled right to left, but there was no escape. Then he noticed people looking down at him. Then he saw other mobs. He was in a zoo! “How

can this be?” He wondered. He looked in his inventory. He still had the diamonds! He crafted a pickaxe, grabbed some blocks, and started building. “Hurrrr?” Some of the villagers were confused. “What is the monster building?” A baby villager asked. The warden roared! He was free!
