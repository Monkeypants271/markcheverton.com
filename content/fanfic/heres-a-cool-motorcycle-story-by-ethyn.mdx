## Mudding with my Brother

Joe was taking a nap and had a dream. The dream was when he was 12, him and his brother Jeff were riding four wheelers. They were racing and hitting all the mud puddles they could,  and they hit one and it was a soft one and Jeff sank. Joe woke up and woke Jeff up.

Jeff said,  “Let’s go racing and hit all the mud puddles we can and get really dirty.”

Joe said,  “Sure, why not? Let’s do this. What are we waiting for?”

They took off like a freight train. They got stopped in front of a old guy’s house to talk. The old guy came out with a shotgun and said,

“Stay off of the rocks! You are getting too muddy.”  Jeff and Joe just off and the old guy was chasing them. Finally he stopped chasing them. Jeff and Joe went home and told mom what happened.

They got home and Jeff yelled, “Mom where are you I need to talk to you about something.”

Mom said, “well honey what did he say to you?”

Jeff said “ l hate to tell you this but he said to stay off the rock you are getting too muddy. And he said that with a shot gun in his hands.”

Mom said, “ I am sorry to see you get embarrassed because of that. “

Jeff replied, “Ok mom I am going back out to ride some more again.”

“Do I have to ride down that road or can I go the other way instead?”

Mom said, “no you can go the other way that will be ok with me.”

“Ok mom.”

Jeff and Joe went to ride four wheelers and went to a creek. They got there and walked and  rode their four wheelers in the water too. It was time to go home because it was getting too dark. Joe realized that his four wheeler would not start.  He looked in his gas tank and it was empty.  He called Jeff to see if he could run home real quick and  get some gas so they could come home.

He called him just in time and Jeff said, “Good thing, I was just going to get it because I thought someone was getting low.”

He grabbed the gas tank and Jeff forgot he had a little trailer that he could pull with his four wheeler. He hooked the trailer up and put the gas in that so it was easier to take the gas to joe so we could go home.  Jeff left the house and went back to the crick and took the gas to the creek  so he could fill up. He filled up and continued the adventure.

They went home because it was dark and it was dinner time. When they got home they both gave each other a huge hug and said, “Thank you for riding four wheelers with me I appreciate it a lot. I am very happy you are my brother other wise I would be very bored.”

Jeff and Joe had an idea to wash their four wheelers since they could not ride because it was dark. Jeff got soap, the hose, and a scrubbing tool. They got all the stuff ready and got started.

They did one at a time because they like to do things together.  First thing they did was they got it wet then put soap on it then took the scrubbing tool and scrubbed all the mud off of the four wheeler. Joe moved Jeff’s four wheeler and pulled his up so they could wash it too.  They washed it the same way they washed Jeff’s four wheeler. They got done and put all the tools that they used to wash they four wheelers away in the shed. They also put the four wheelers away in the shop where their dad has all his equipment for his job.

As you can tell Jeff and Joe are brothers and like to do stuff together and not independently. They do not like to do stuff without each other or by themselves. Jeff and Joe were very good brothers to each other. Jeff and Joe are very good kids and their favorite thing to do is ride four wheelers of course. They also like to explore and hunt too.

Jeff and Joe have a very good family and very good friends. Jeff and Joe are the only kids in the family and they are very good kids. Jeff and Joe raced with each other for a while then they didn’t because they thought it would be more fun just doing whatever they want with their own four wheelers. They just like to be outside and to do better stuff than sit on the couch and play video games and eat NOT healthy food they like to eat healthy.

Jeff and Joe were very good siblings. Jeff and Joe had a great time hanging out with each other. Jeff and Joe are great brothers with each other and both have great friends and a good life with each other.  Jeff and Joe’s birthday are in June and July. It was June and his birthday was on the 22 and it was the 21.

For his birthday he wanted a dirt bike so he could race in races. Now Jeff and joe both want dirt bikes for their birth day so they both can race together. The boy are very picky about what brand they get for dirt bikes and four wheelers just like their dad. Their dad is very picky too they like Yamaha racing equipment.

So they got the boys tickets to go see a dirt bike race so they could see if they like dirt bike racing. They got told that the race was on July 22 right after joes birthday. So the day came and they got everything ready and hit the road. Their family got there and found seats and got food and waited until it started.

The guy that was the announcer said, “Hey guys it’s the 2017 dirt bike race.”

The guy said, “Are you ready, get set, go.” You could hear all the engines go reeeeeeeen reeeeeeen reeeeeeen reeeeeeeeen. Jeff and Joe told their mom and dad that they already like dirt bikes from the way they sound. Their mom and dad said that they like them too.

Finally the number 10 dirt bike went over the finish line and just kept going around the track. You could see all the mud,dirt, and rock fly everywhere from the dirt bikes tires. Jeff and Joe were getting thirsty so they walked up and got some water and went to watch the races again.  When they got back they saw that number 21 got in front of number 10 so know number 10 is in 2nd place not first. Jeff and joe were wanting number 10 to win because it was a cool dirt bike and a Yamaha also.

Finally number 10 got number 21 on the jump he got back in first place. I bet he got him by a meter on the jump. The jump looked scary at first when 10 went up and over. Number ten was the big man to win. The number twenty one  was the big guy trying not to lose the race. The men are really trying  to win the grand prize.

The boys just kept passing each other. Finally the boy hit the last lap of the night. One of the boys jumped a hill on the track and accidentally one of the boys knocked one of the boys off of his dirt bike. Can you guess who it was? It was number 10. Jeff and Joe were like, “let’s go home because the race is over.”

Dad said, “Wait, I want to see who wins. Maybe 10 could come back and win.”

They stayed and watched the last 3 minutes of the race. Number 10 was right up on 21s tail. He got up next to him and he put it in high gear and took off and all the mud from the wheels throwing it up and went in is face and crashed him. So 10 was 10 feet from the finish line and he did a wheelie across the finish line. Jeff said, “Totally I want a dirt bike from you and dad mom.” The family went home and had Jeff and Joe’s birthday and both of the boys got dirt bike for their birthdays. Jeff and Joe were so happy they got dirt bikes.
