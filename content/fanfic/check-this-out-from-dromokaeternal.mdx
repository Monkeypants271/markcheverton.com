Outside of the door there was a man. His skin were made of scales like a dragon’s, and he wore a green cloak, and carried an enchanted diamond ax and a shield with a scale painted onto it. On his back were the Elytra, the new item added to Minecraft. His eyes were not glowing like Herobrine’s, but he did look evil. He swung his ax at Gameknight, and Gameknight, not expecting an attack, fell to the ground, stunned. As the man prepared to swing his ax again, a user jumped in front of the ax, and was killed. Behind him was about twenty users, all wearing monkey skins. They carried simple weapons, but they looked like they had won many battles. They attacked the dragonscale man, and they were all teleported into a plain, and the dragonscale man was flying in the sky with five EnderDragons. This battle could get deadly.
