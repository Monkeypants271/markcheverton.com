Gameknight had found the Nether portal, only it looked different. Instead of the shimmering purple portal, it was a dark red, like the color Erebus was.  
Was Erebus back? Gameknight thought. Nah, he couldn’t be, he was destroyed by me in the land of dreams.

Taking his precautions, Gameknight999 stepped into the portal. He was shocked at what he saw: Red fiery endermen, a giant fortress- A giant fortress? Gameknight could see a throne and something sitting in it.

Oh no, Gameknight thought. The something in the throne saw him, and teleported over to Gameknight. “Hello, old friend,” said a crackling voice.”Erebus? You’re alive? But- but I killed you in the dream land!” Gameknight shouted.  
“Indeed you did, but Herobrine was able to restore me, and I am now in his debt. I am here to wreak my revenge.”
