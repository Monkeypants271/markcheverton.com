## Something Strange

### Chapter 1: Fallen

It was a beautiful day outside, perfect weather, the sun was high in the sky and there was a calming summer breeze blowing. Yup, perfect day to play chase with your friends, which was exactly what, Maddie Christie and Kaelynn Morimoto, were doing.

Maddie and Kaelynn were closer than ever, but even though they were so close, they had almost nothing in common. Maddie was older, a bit more mature, and sometimes took caution too much, but even though she was like this, she did like playing with her friends. While Kaelynn was a bit younger, and more free spirited, which is what made those two fit together like a puzzle.

The two girls were playing chase at the foot of Mt. Ebott, they saw a bit of a small cave, they both knew the legend about the great mountain, **whoever climbs the mountain are said to never return.** Even though the legend may be true, they didn’t believe the part where monsters lived under it, which is why they weren’t scared to play chase there.

“Hey Kaelynn, come check this out!” Maddie called out. She was standing right by the cave in the mountain, then Kaelynn came over.

“Woah, do you think this is the cave the legend talks about?” she asked when she ran over.

“Yeah, hold on,” Maddie responded picking up a rock. She then threw the rock into the cave and listened to see when it would hit the ground. After about a minute or so, she heard a loud thump.

“We should probably play chase a bit further away, we wouldn’t want to fall down, it seems like a long enough drop to hurt us pretty bad,” Maddie said.

“Ya, and if we survive, we wouldn’t want any monsters coming after us, oooooooooooooooooh,” Kaelynn said weirdly.

“Aww c’mon Kaelynn, everyone knows that story about monsters is just a myth,” Maddie said walking away.

“You’re right, I know, I was just messing with you,” Kaelynn said following her.

Then the two girls played the rest of the day. They then came up with an idea to play blind chase, which is where both people have a blindfold or they close their eyes and they play chase. So Kaelynn was the one being chased, and she ran towards the cave but didn’t know, so she then tripped and fell in, but she was able to hang onto a vine. She howled and screamed for her best friend to come and save her. Immediately Maddie came rushing over to save her. She grabbed the vine and tried with all her strength to pull her friend up, then the vine started to snap.

“Ah, Maddie grab my arms!”Kaelynn shouted

“I can’t you’re too far away, I can’t reach you,” Maddie responded.

Maddie then pulled harder on the vine, but wasn’t strong enough and she too started to slide off herself, and before they knew it, they were plummeting into the underground.

### Chapter 2: The Underground

It was dark, there was a small glimpse of light shining through the hole and down onto the two girls. They had landed on what appeared to be a bed of buttercups, which was the one thing that had broken their fall.

They both woke up, only a little bit soar.

“Well this is great, I told you blind chase was a bad idea,” Maddie yelled as she stood up and brushed the dirt off of her pants

“Hey, I’m not the only one to blame here, _you_ wanted to do it just as much as I did, and you should’ve grabbed my arms, not the vine,” Kaelynn shot back at her standing up as well.

“Ok, fine, how about we both say sorry since we’re both to blame here, and stop fighting, so we can find a way out of here together, it doesn’t look like we can get back the way we came down,” Maddie said.

“Alright, I’m sorry,” Kaelynn said.

“Me too,” Maddie responded.

Then they hugged, and started walking. There was a small pathway leading to a purple doorway, so they went over there and went through. They didn’t see anything but a patch of grass, it was a good thing they were holding hands, they wouldn’t want to lose each other in dark rooms like the one they were in. They cautiously walked towards the patch of grass, then suddenly a golden flower with a face, came out and began to speak.

“Howdy, I’m Flowey, Flowey the flower, hmmm two humans, that’s strange, there’s never been more than one human that’s fallen down here at the same time, anyways that doesn’t change anything, because you’re still new to the underground aren’tcha?” Flowey asked.

They both nodded slowly, because they were really scared, seeing as how apparently, the legend about monsters was true.

“Well someone oughta teach you how things work down here, I guess little old me will have to do, you ready, here we go!” he said, then suddenly, two hearts appeared in front of their chests, both were a deep pink.

“Huh, well that’s interesting,” Flowey said.

“W-w-what is?” Kaelynn asked shakily.

“Your souls, they’re pink, I’ve never seen a pink soul before, at least not in person but I do know what it means. First, how do you two know each other?” he asked.

“W-w-we’re best friends, and we have been for a very long time,” Maddie responded.

“Well that makes sense, you see girls, each living creature has a soul, it’s the very culmination of a being. Each soul has a different trait, the ones that are known are, kindness which is green, integrity which is blue, patience which is turquoise, justice which is yellow, bravery which is orange, perseverance which is purple, and determination which is red, but yours are pink, the souls of friendship, but that’s besides the point. Your souls start off weak, but can grow strong if you gain a lot of LV, what does LV stand for why LOVE of course,” Flowey said with a smile on his face.

The girls didn’t know why but for some reason the talking flower made them less scared and made them want to believe what he had to say, so they listened.

“You want some LOVE don’t you, don’t worry, I’ll share some LOVE with you. Down here LOVE is shared through little white ‘friendliness pellets’, move around, catch as many as you can,” Flowey said winking as these little white seed things came out from behind him.

Then as the ‘friendliness pellets’ came towards them, they did as the flower told them and went for them, then once they hit the girls right in their souls, an agonizing pain shot through them, they had both collapsed on the ground, feeling the worst pain they’d ever felt in their lives. They looked up towards Flowey and his face had changed to a big creepy grin, staring at the girls.

“You IDIOTS, in this world, it’s KILL or be KILLED, why would anyone pass up an opportunity like this?” he said. Then the seed things that hurt them before circled around them.

“DIE!!!!” Flowey shouted as the seeds neared toward them, and he started to laugh and cackle at their downfall.

The girls hung onto each other tightly, feeling so scared and betrayed. Then, as the seeds came closer, they suddenly had disappeared. The girls were a bit confused when they disappeared and they were suddenly feeling much better, than a fireball came hurdling towards Flowey and he was knocked up against the far wall. The room then lit up and two figures appeared, one tall goat lady in a purple dress with some ancient symbol in white stitching on it and another human with a pink and blue sweater, and short brown hair.

“Oh my, there goes Flowey trying to hurt another child who has fallen down here,” the goat lady said.

“Mmmm,” the other human said tugging on the goat lady’s dress and pointing at the girls.

“Yes, what is it my child?” the goat lady said, then she looked over at the girls, “(gasp) oh my, two children, that’s different, uhh hello girls, I am Toriel, caretaker of the ruins and this is my child Frisk, we came here because we heard a thump, so we came to look, but it seemed like nothing happened, there was just a rock, then we left, we were close to the entrance when we heard your screams of pain and we came as soon as we could,” she said.

“Are you ok?” Frisk asked walking over.

“Yeah, we’re fine, thanks to you,” Maddie responded.

Then Frisk and Toriel grabbed them by the hand and helped them up.

“That’s good, well follow us, we’ll show you around the Ruins,” Toriel said.

The girls followed Frisk and Toriel through another purple door, then the next thing they knew they were in a completely different room. It was all purple everywhere, and they saw a little gold star, that allowed them to ‘save’. After that, they walked into the next room and there were plates on the floor and a switch on the wall.

“Welcome to the ruins, there are a lot of puzzles and traps here but do not be afraid, we’ll help you get through the ruins safely until we get back to my home,” Toriel said as she stepped on 4 of the plates, and flipped the switch.

As the girls kept following Frisk and Toriel they began to grow a bit suspicious after what had happened with Flowey. After a while they grew a bit more comfortable, but right when they were feeling safe, they got pulled into a fight, it was a good thing Toriel had taught them what to do in a fight. The monster that had encountered them was a frog looking thing, then Frisk called out to them, “that’s a Froggit, they don’t mean harm, just compliment them and then spare them, you’ll be alright,” they said. So the girls did what they said and even got a little hug from the Froggit after they forgave him for attacking them.

“Good job children, I’m very proud of you,” Toriel said smiling.

After a lot of walking, puzzles, and encounters they finally got to Toriel’s house.

“Well we’re here I’ll show you where you’ll be staying, follow me,” Frisk said.

Frisk showed the girls to a small room with two beds, a toy bin, a wardrobe, and a bin full of shoes.

“This is a nice room, thank you,” Kaelynn said.

“You’re welcome, mom and I will be in the family room, come and ask for anything if you need something,” Frisk said leaving.

The girls looked around the room, then after looking  at everything, the girls were starting to get really tired so they decided to go to sleep. They slept for a couple of hours, then when they woke up they saw 2 slices of pie, one was cinnamon and the other was butterscotch. It was a good thing they each had a backpack, they put the slices in their bags and headed out of the room to ask Toriel when they could go home.

### Chapter 3: A Mother’s Love

The girls walked into the family room and saw Toriel sitting on a big arm chair, reading a book about snails.

“Hello Miss. Toriel?” Kaelynn asked.

“Oh, hello children, did you have a good rest?” Toriel asked, putting her book down.

“It was fine, thank you for the pies, we didn’t eat them yet since we’re not really that hungry,” Maddie responded.

“That’s alright, oh I’m sorry, did you need something?” she asked.

“Ummm, well uhh, we just wanted to know umm, if we could uhhhh, know what your book was about?” Maddie lied. I mean how could they ask how to go home after Toriel and Frisk had been so nice to them.

“Oh, well this book is called 72 uses for snails, would you like to hear an interesting snail fact?” Toriel asked.

“Ooh yes please,” Kaelynn said joyfully. She really liked learning about nature.

“Ok did you know, the largest snail recorded was 12 inches long?” she asked.

“Cool, umm if you don’t mind me asking, where’s Frisk?” Maddie asked.

“Oh they were very tired after they had some of the pie, so they went to sleep. We probably should stay quiet,we wouldn’t want to wake them up,” Toriel answered.

“Oh, ok well we’re going to go look around if that’s ok,”

“Oh, yes that’s, fine bother me if you need something else,” Toriel said getting back to her book.

Then the girls explored around the house, it was pretty small, other than their room and the family room, there were two other bedrooms, one must be Frisk’s and the other Toriel’s. They cracked the door open a bit, and saw Frisk sleeping soundly, then they left, they didn’t want to disturb them. So then they looked around in Toriel’s room. There was a small room in between the bedrooms and the family room with a staircase leading to the basement, they didn’t decide to go down there, then they looked in the kitchen. After they were done looking around they were finally gonna ask Toriel if they could go home.

“Hello again Miss. Toriel,” Kaelynn said.

“Hello again girls, did you need something?” she asked.

“Umm yes, listen we really appreciate everything you’ve done for us thus far, but how can we go home?” Maddie asked shyly.

“Oh umm, excuse me, I need to do something,” Toriel said putting her glasses and her book down and leaving.

“Miss. Toriel wait!” They called out to her.

They followed her down the stairs, and chased after her to see why she didn’t answer their question.

“You wish to know how to return home do you not. Ahead of us is the exit out of the ruins, I am going to destroy it, go upstairs children,” Toriel said, then she kept walking and the girls kept following. “Almost every child who falls down here meets the same fate, they come, they leave, they die, you naive children, go upstairs,” she continued on, and they still followed. “If you leave here, he, Asgore, will kill you, I was barely able to save Frisk’s life, now let me save you, this is your final warning,” she said.

The three of them had reached a purple door, that must’ve been the exit, Toriel turned back with a stern face, but with sadness in her eyes.

“You wanna leave here so badly, fine then prove yourselves to me, prove that you are strong enough to survive,” she yelled.

Toriel then pulled the girls into a fight, they were more scared than before, and they didn’t want to fight Toriel.

“Please Toriel we don’t have to fight!” they shouted, and she didn’t respond, but instead balls of flames came hurdling at them, then all of a sudden right before the flames hit them, the fight was interrupted.

“Mom, stop!” Frisk called out.

“Frisk, go upstairs they need to prove they are strong against you know who,” Toriel said.

“No, we know who’s out there, if they’re just like me, then I know they can make friends with whoever’s out there so please, I’ll even check up on them,” Frisk said.

Toriel took a moment to let her child’s words sink in, then spoke, “(sigh) you’re right Frisk, I’m sorry girls, I was wrong, thank you for keeping us company, and letting us get to know you, call me whenever you’d like to come over or if you need Frisk to come and help you on your journey,” Toriel said.

With a final hug from Frisk and Toriel they said they’re goodbyes and bid them farewell.

“Bye Toriel, bye Frisk, thanks for everything,” they called out as they walked through the door.

All of a sudden everything got really really cold, and they were standing in a foot of snow.

“Brrrr, it’s super cold, we should probably keep our sweatshirts on and our hoods up,” Maddie said.

Maddie and Kaelynn wore similar sweatshirts, except Maddie’s was deep green at the top and it got brighter until it was a bright yellow at the bottom, and then Kaelynn’s was the exact same except it was purple at the top and turned blue going down.

They walked through the snow, feeling really nervous once again, for who they were going to meet and if they were just as nice as Toriel and Frisk.

All of a sudden a shadowy figure jumped out from the forest and attacked Maddie, and wrestled her down to the ground, he was a skeleton, wearing a blue hoodie, and one of his eye sockets was glowing a deep light blue, and he had some sort of dog skull thing behind him looking like it was just about to blast her to pieces.

“I’m not letting you hurt anymore monsters you little demon!” he said in a deep voice.

### Chapter 4: You Look Familiar

“AAAAAAAAHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!! No please noooo what did I do, please don’t kill me,” Maddie screamed in utter fear.

“Hey get off my friend!” Kaelynn shouted. She then ran over and tackled the skeleton so he would get off of her friend.

“Ack! Frisk run I can’t let Chara possess you,” then he pushed Kaelynn off his chest and went for Maddie again. She quickly stood up and started running, but was immediately stopped when she saw her soul in front of her but it had turned blue and she came flying towards the skeleton. Once she was face to face with him, it only took a second for Kaelynn to, once again, jump in to stop him, but by then it was a little too late, a bone came hurdling towards Maddie and it hit her shoulder, so hard that it started to bleed. Kaelynn managed to pin him down so he could see that they were obviously not who he thought they were.

“Hold on skeleton, first off I’m not Frisk, and my friend never killed anyone, she’s innocent, and who is this Chara you’re talking about?” Kaelynn asked.

It was by then, the skeleton had finally realized what he did, and his eyes turned into normal eye sockets with white pupils, he looked super sorry for what he did, then Kaelynn released him, and he stood up.

“Oh my gosh, I’m so sorry, I thought you were someone else, someone who was a killer and it wasn’t just to us monsters it was to Frisk. I’m guessing you met them and Toriel in the ruins, you just looked like them so much, that’s Chara, uhm matching sweatshirts,” he said.

Maddie then came over limping and holding onto her bleeding shoulder, she had almost fainted, but Kaelynn caught her.

“Uhh, again I’m really sorry, uhh, well this is awkward let me start over, hey, I’m Sans the skeleton, uhh who are you?” he asked nervously.

“I’m Kaelynn, and this is my best friend Maddie,” Kaelynn responded.

That’s when Maddie stood up and then fell face first into the snow, still bleeding.

“MADDIE!!!” Kaelynn shouted, then Sans bent down and picked her up.

“Look, let’s head back to my place, there’s a small town called Snowdin, that’s where me and my brother Papyrus live, he can heal her if we don’t run into him on our way there, but don’t worry if we do he’s very kind,” Sans said.

“Umm ok, oh sorry for tackling you, this is all still pretty new to us,” Kaelynn said as they started walking.

“Eh there’s no need for you to apologize, it was my fault,” he said.

As they started walking, they walked past a station and a dog with a black mask, orange boots, torn shorts, and a pink jersey, smoking what appeared to be, dog treats?

“(Gasp) Sans, what are you doing here, and with, two humans?” the dog asked.

“Doggo, these humans mean no harm, and I accidentally attacked this one, so I’m taking them to Papyrus, Undyne’s not around is she?” he asked hesitantly.

“No, but Papyrus just passed through here, he was actually looking for you,” Doggo said.

“Great, thanks Doggo,” Sans said, then Kaelynn bowed as a thank you, and they continued.

After a bit Kaelynn finally said, “umm, mind me asking, but who’s Undyne?”

“Oh, nobody, you don’t have to worry about her,” Sans said.

“Ok,” Kaelynn said surely, but she knew she couldn’t trust him after what happened.

After more walking and a few puzzles, they ran into another skeleton, he was tall, had red boots, a white battle shirt, and a red scarf.

“Hey bro, we’ve been looking for you,” Sans said.

“SANS, WHERE HAVE YOU BEEN, AND, ARE THOSE HUMANS?” the new skeleton, who seemed to be Papyrus, said.

“Yes, but something happened to this one, and we need you to heal her, can you please?” Sans said. Kaelynn got pretty upset that Sans didn’t mention it was his fault.

But Papyrus did agree, and they went back to their house. It was in a place called Snowdin, it  seemed like a nice town.

Maddie woke up to Kaelynn, Sans, and Papyrus looking down at her. She quickly sat up once she saw Sans, and she scooted over to the other side of the couch shaking in fear, “please don’t hurt me,” she said covering her face.

“Whoa, hold on there Mads, he’s not gonna hurt you. There was a bit of a misunderstandment, but he apologized and we walked all the way to his house and his brother healed your shoulder,” Kaelynn said walking over to her.

“Oh, uh, sorry it’s just this is something I’ve never experienced before and I was scared,” she responded.

“Heh, I know and again I’m really sorry about you know what, anyways, Maddie was it, the name’s Sans, how are you feeling?” he asked.

“Umm I’m fine and who’s that?” Maddie asked in response, pointing to Papyrus,

“That’s my brother, Papyrus, he’s the one who healed you when you got here,” Sans said.

“Oh, thank you Papyrus,” Maddie smiled.

“YOU’RE VERY WELCOME HUMAN!” Papyrus said, then he turned and headed into what appeared to be the kitchen.

“Alright Sans, tell me right now why you lied to Papyrus about what happened in the forest,” Kaelynn said angrily.

“Shhh! listen my brother can’t ever know ok, at least not until he’s gotten to know you ok?” Sans whispered.

Papyrus soon returned with a cup of tea.

“HERE YOU ARE HUMAN, A CUP OF GOLDEN FLOWER TEA, THE KING’S FAVORITE!” Papyrus said giving Maddie the cup, “IT’LL MAKE YOU FEEL BETTER.”

“Thank you Papyrus,” Maddie said taking the cup.

“Hey bro, can I talk to you?” Sans asked.

“OF COURSE BROTHER!” Papyrus said as they walked into the kitchen.

“So bro, are you going to try and capture them?” Sans asked.

“I DON’T KNOW BROTHER, YOU KNOW THE WHOLE ROYAL GUARD THING HASN’T BEEN GOING TOO WELL, AND BESIDES THEY SEEM REALLY NICE, AND I CAN EVEN HELP THEM GET PAST UHM WELL YOU KNOW,” Papyrus said.

“Great, I mean hey you’re still holding up, and even if the guard thing doesn’t work out that’s ok, you still got lots of friends and your cooking has been getting better, and as far as I’m concerned, they’ve been through alot,” Sans said.

“So what do you think, can we trust them?” Maddie asked.

“ I don’t know, I mean, I kind of like Sans, he’s sweet and Papyrus seems sweet too, and he looks pretty cool,” Kaelynn said.

“Whoa wait, back up did you just say you like Sans? the skeleton that nearly got me killed!?” Maddie questioned.

“No, not like that, I just mean he seems like a cool guy, so yeah I think we can trust them,” Kaelynn said.

Then the two skeletons re-entered the room.

“SO UH HUMANS, I NEVER CAUGHT YOUR NAMES, WHAT ARE THEY?” Papyrus asked.

“Oh well, my name’s Madelyn, but all my friends call me Maddie,” she hinted for Papyrus to call her that, since it seemed like she wanted to be friends with him.

“Yeah, and I’m Kaelynn,” she said.

“OH, WELL, DO YOU HAVE CELL PHONES?” Papyrus asked, then the girls pulled out their phones, and handed them to Papyrus. He then inserted his number into them. “THERE YOU GO, YOU TWO ARE GOING TO RUN INTO A COUPLE OF THINGS, SO IF YOU EVER NEED ANY HELP, JUST GIVE ME A CALL AND SANS WILL BE THERE TOO,” Papyrus said giving them their phones back.

“Thanks Papyrus,” Kaelynn said.

“Is it ok if we look around, since I wasn’t awake to see your town?” Maddie asked.

“Sure, I can come with, and show you around,” Sans offered.

“Oh, um ok,” Maddie said nervously.

Sans made a face that kind of screamed regret.

Then they left.

“OH, UHM MADELYN, YOU LEFT YOUR JACKET HERE!” Papyrus called out, he then ran over to them and handed her the jacket.

“Papyrus you can call me Maddie,” she said taking the jacket.

“BUT YOU SAID ONLY YOUR FRIENDS CALL YOU THAT,” Papyrus said.

“Yeah, my friends can, and, well, I want to be friends with you Papyrus, it’s the least I could do for the one who healed me, and you’re very kind,” she said with a gentle smile.

“(Gasp) REALLY?!? OH THANK YOU!!!!!” he squealed. He then picked her up and started spinning her around, when he put her down she couldn’t even breath she was laughing so hard, and she was so happy she made him so happy.

“You’re welcome Papyrus, I promise we’ll come to visit,” she replied.

“OK, I’LL BE AT HOME BEING A COOL FRIEND!!!!” Papyrus shouted running home and waving.

“Hahahahahahah,” they all laughed.

“Anyways, Sans, now that we’re alone, can you explain to me why you straight up attacked me out of nowhere!?” Maddie said a bit angrily.

“Ya, I figured that would be the first topic that would hit conversation. Listen kid, there were two humans that fell down here, that was Frisk, and another named Chara, the first fallen human, I don’t know their entire story, but it would be best to ask Toriel or even Asgore,” Sans said as they were walking around Snowdin.

“So Sans told me that Chara was an evil ghost that possessed Frisk, and made them kill all of the monsters in the underground multiple times because they had this power to RESET,” Kaelynn said.

“RESET, what’s that?” Maddie asked.

“Well it’s basically a way to turn back time and erase data back to your last save point, have you guys seen these star things around the underground?” Sans asked.

The girls did remember that there were these little star things that allowed them to ‘save’ that must’ve been what he was talking about. They both nodded.

“Ya, so anyways, Maddie your sweatshirt looks almost exactly like Chara’s, with the green and the yellow, they had the same colors,” Sans said.

“Wait, Sans, if resets erase data, wouldn’t that mean like erasing monsters memories of before the reset happened?” Maddie asked.

“Yup, so you’re probably wondering how I know all this, well first, I’m different from any other monster, I remember these resets, I suffer reliving all those memories, and every human can do it, sometimes they have the choice, and sometimes it happens uncontrollably, like uhm well I don’t want to worry you guys but, when the humans die,” Sans mentioned.

“Wait, what, death?!” Kaelynn shouted nervously, “are we gonna die?!”

“Woah, hold on, calm down, I’m sure it won’t happen, listen, before Frisk came here, I talked to Toriel on the other side of that door in the forest, and she told me to promise to protect any human that came through there, and I think I did pretty well,” he said, and at the end they notice he made a bit of a face looking like he was nervous, and then there was a bit of an awkward silence.

“Well I…. uh we promise not to reset for your sake, and we will never break that promise, especially Maddie she’s really serious about promises, right Mads?” Kaelynn told him.

“Yup! I also don’t take to lightly to broken promises,” she said. “So, uhm, is there anything else we need to know?” Maddie asked.

“Uhh, no just that if you go that way you’ll hit Waterfall soon, good luck,” he said pointing a certain direction.

“Wait, you’re not coming with us?” Kaelynn asked a bit scared.

“Nah, I think you girls will be ok, but just in case, I’ll be up ahead,” he said, then he walked away waving, and then all of a sudden he disappeared into the wind. The girls were really confused but they did look around. There was shop, an inn, a libraby, and a restaraunt. Then they headed for Waterfall, and they were filled with friendship.

### Chapter 5: Feeling Homesick

The girls walked the way that Sans told them to, and when they were finally back on grass and didn’t see any snow, they knew they were in Waterfall. They kept walking, until they saw another monster leaning against a wall next to what appears to be a blue flower.

“Um, excuse me, may I ask, what type of flower is this?” Maddie asked, because she was curious of course.

“This here is an echo flower, it repeats the last thing it heard over and over again,” he responded to her looking down, since he was tall.

“Oh thank you, they’re very pretty,” Maddie said, then she listened and heard her voice,\*oh thank you, they’re very pretty\* it repeated.

“Woah, that thing is far out, you know the two people who’d love this thing?” Kaelynn asked.

“Natalie and Erica?” Maddie responded.

“Ya, you know I kind of miss them, and I miss home,” Kaelynn sighed, that’s when Kaelynn felt a familiar hand touch her shoulder, it was Frisk!

“Frisk!” They both yelled excitedly.

That’s when they noticed Sans was next to them, listening to the whole conversation, sitting at his sentry post.

“Hey kid, haven’t seen you in a while, how have you been?” Sans asked walking over to Frisk.

“Good, how’s Papyrus?” they asked.

“Fine,” he responded back.

“Oh, I see you met Maddie and Kaelynn, have they gone to Grillby’s yet?” they asked.

“No not yet, I was just about to offer, and Frisk if you want you can come along, you know for ol’ times sake, right? What do you guys say?” Sans asked. The girls looked at Frisk, and they had this cheeky smile like they knew what was going to happen if they said yes, so they said agreed.

“Alright then this way,” he hesitated, then he said, “I know a shortcut.” So the girls followed Frisk and Sans, and before they knew it they were in Grillby’s, and they were really dizzy.

“Uhhh woah, how’d we get here?” Maddie asked.

“Sans, can teleport, don’t tell anyone,” Frisk whispered, and Sans nodded.

The girls zipped their lips, and promised not to tell. They sat down at the bar after everyone said hi to Sans and Frisk when they walked over.

When they took a seat, they heard the sound of a whoopie cushion and they couldn’t stop laughing. They all ordered, Sans and Frisk got a burger and the girls got some fries.

“Do one of you girls want ketchup for your fries?” Sans asked.

“Sure,”

“No thanks,” they responded. Maddie was the one who said sure, so Sans gave her the ketchup, and before she squeezed it onto her fries the cap on the bottle fell off and the ketchup went everywhere.

All of them were laughing except Maddie. She just stared at her food, then Kaelynn turned to her and asked if she wanted her fries, but instead Maddie just laughed too and said, “that’s ok, a lot of ketchup never hurt anyone,” and then she picked up a fry that was drenched in ketchup.

When they finished their food, Grillby came out and took their empty trays and left.

“So Kaelynn, I heard you talking about two other girls, what were their names?” Sans asked.
