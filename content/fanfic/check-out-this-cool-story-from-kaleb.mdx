### Battle of the city

#### **By: Kaleb J**

Me and my friend Jeremy were walking to school together.

We finally got to school When the bell rang and we went inside.

It was recess and we went to our ground tree

fort. When we got there there was a pile of ashes and hot coal. “Jeremy what happened to our fort we spent months working on it”I said.

Then we seen what burned it so badly The Ender dragon was soaring above the town. The Ender Dragon in one blast of scorching flames the school was destroyed. Luckily nobody was in the school.

Then Freddy Fazbear popped out of nowhere. After Freddy appeared more Fnaf animatronics started to appear out of the blue.

Eomonn came  running from the remains of the school, Eomonn said “I see that you met  my animatronics. I brought them here to help defeat the dragon and they are really strong so if they hit the dragon once it could kill it”. Just then the animatronics started attacking the dragon. Freddy hit it once and it started to glow. All of a sudden the dragon disappeared leaving a **pop**. then all of Eomonn’s animatronics started disappearing then the remains of the school started to glow bright enough to blind. Then the school was back to normal. And everyone went back to class.
