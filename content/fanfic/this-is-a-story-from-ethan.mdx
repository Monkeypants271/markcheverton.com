Chapter 1-  Entering Minecraft

Tommy looked outside. It was storming, so he couldn’t go into minecraft. Last time, he had been sent back in time to the time of the Great Zombie Invasion, and met smithy of the two-swords.  
He thought of Hunter, Stitcher, Crafter, and all his friends. He wished he could see them again.  
His dad and mom went away for their anniversary, and they wouldn’t be back until tomorrow. His sister, Jenny, was at a sleepover with her friend, so Tommy was alone in the house.  
He stared at the digitizer and sighed. He’d promised his dad he wouldn’t use the digitizer until the storm passed, and he couldn’t break his promise.  
Then Tommy decided to play minecraft, to cheer him up, when he realized the storm had passed. He leaped up from the chair and walked over to the digitizer. He pressed the button, walked over to the computer, and turned on minecraft a split-second before the digitizer sent a bright shaft of light at him. Tommy felt boiling hot, and at the same time, terribly cold. And then, he was in minecraft.

Gameknight looked around for the mountain with the waterfall, and, sure enough, there was the hidey-hole. He also saw the post on top of the mountain where he lived.  
“I wonder what Crafter is doing now…” he thought to himself. He looked around for a tree, and when he found one, he broke it’s leaves until he had found some apples. “Now I need a horse,” he said to himself. He found one quickly, and then after he fed it some apples, he got on the horse and rode off to find Crafter’s village.

Chapter 2- Crafter’s Village

After a while, Gameknight reached the end of the hill. He looked down. There was Crafter’s village. The high stone walls were now made of diamond. The watchtower was taller, the buildings were wood and iron, and the village was two times bigger.  
“I guess I’ve been gone for longer then I thought,” the User-That-Is-Not-A-User said, gazing beyond the village.  
“Uh oh,” Gameknight said, as he saw a group of at least 100 zombies going towards the village, some with iron pickaxes, meaning they could break the wall.  
He ran towards the village, reaching it in seconds. He knocked on the now-iron doors saying, “Can I come in?”  
“Look,” said a villager. “It’s the user-that-is-not-a-user!” The villagers cheered and opened the doors, then quickly closed them when they saw the army of zombies.  
Gameknight looked around for his friends. There were lots of villagers, making it hard to find his friends. Then he saw Crafter, now fully grown, Stitcher, also fully grown, Hunter, and Herder.  
“You’ve grown!” Gameknight said to them. He glanced around for Digger, Topper, and Filler. He couldn’t find them. Then everyone stopped cheering when they heard clanking coming from the walls.  
“Zombies!”
