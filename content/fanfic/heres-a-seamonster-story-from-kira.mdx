Introduction  
My name is Glustyx, and I am a viking sea monster. I  
am 268 years old, which means I am still young for a sea  
monster. I am roughly 100 feet long, but I have never  
actually measured. Even though vikings call me Jormungandr,  
that is not my true name. Vikings were like sharks. They hunt  
and travel in the ocean and seas. The vikings all had small  
farms, and they loved skiing. Viking women also had almost as  
many rights as men, one of those rights is being able to own  
land. This is the story of how my viking friend Vigi Loptsson  
and I saved the world from the mischief maker god Loki. But  
first we need the story of how Vigi and I met.  
2  
I lived a life of constant danger. Either the Vikings  
were trying to fish and accidentally got me with their spears  
or net, or sharks smelled my blood because a viking  
accidentally nicked me with his spear. Also, they are  
sometimes setting boats on fire with dead loved ones in it  
and setting the boats assail. Can you imagine what that does  
to the environment? I was the only sea monster of my kind  
left as far as I knew. My mother disappeared shortly after I  
hatched and I was the only egg that was not a dud. Anyway,  
one day I was wandering close to the shore. I heard a couple  
of voices. It was a young boy and his father.  
“Son,” the father said, “Never go near the water  
without a strong man or Jormungandr will get you.”  
“Oh Dad,” the boy replied. “I have no fear of that sea  
monster.”  
The dad replied, “Vigi, if you go in that water of your  
own free will I will not cry at your death.”  
“Fine!” Vigi said. Then they walked away. I knew how  
to speak the vikings’ language because I had grown up around  
them.  
That night, just as I was settling down in my cave at  
the shore, I heard footsteps on land. I came out of my cave  
to investigate. I heard Vigi talking to himself.  
“I will find Jormungandr and ride him!” Vigi declared.  
“Then my father will know how powerful I am.” I finally got a  
look at Vigi. He was tall, had blonde hair, and was very stout.  
Most vikings had blonde hair because they would bleach their  
hair to respect the viking culture ideals. Also, it helped with  
head lice. He was very clean, because vikings actually have  
3  
very good hygiene.  
The last thing about him was that he wore a symbol. Then I  
had a flashback.  
I was swimming around, and I was just a baby, only,  
like, 3 years old. I was laughing at something, then my  
mother screamed. “Swim away! Swim away!” she called, but I  
didn’t know where to swim. Then a boat appeared with that  
symbol on it, and there were vikings on the boat, vikings that  
were throwing nets all around. Then the boat rushed past,  
sweeping my mother and I in different directions. I got  
swept into a nearby cave but my mother was thrown far out  
to sea.  
Then, I came out of my trance and Vigi was right  
there! I growled at Vigi. Now, I had never shown any  
aggression towards vikings but this was different. Vigi  
pointed up and then started swimming to the surface.  
When we got to the surface he said, “I know you can  
talk. I heard you yelling for your mom.” I sighed.  
“Why are you wearing that symbol?” I asked.  
“It is a symbol called The Triple Horn of Odin. The  
Triple Horn of Odin is used as a sign of commitment to  
modern Asatru faith. It is always either worn or put on  
display.” He replied proudly. Then he said, “I need your help.  
If I do not prove myself to my father, and we do not stop  
Loki, then all is lost.”  
“Don’t try to trick me.” I said. “I heard you talking  
about proving yourself to your father, but you said nothing  
about mischief maker Loki.”  
4  
Vigi said, “I just wanted to prove myself to my father  
so that I could be chosen to go on the journey to help stop  
Loki.”  
“Fine.” I said.  
Vigi got me up very early the next morning. We had  
stayed up late planning for our attack on Loki. First, we were  
going to borrow Vigi’s father’s boat, which was named Alptr,  
and Vigi and I would start traveling to the dangerous Island  
of the Gods. Loki was there, so that’s where we wanted to go.  
Second, we would lure Loki to the shore, so that I could join  
the battle. Third, we have to get Loki’s weapon and stop him  
or he will destroy us all!  
… We arrived at the island, and Loki was nowhere in  
sight. “Stay here,” Vigi whispered, “I will find Loki and bring  
him here.”  
“OK,” I whispered back. Then Vigi walked away. An  
hour had passed and still no sign of Vigi. Then a eagle burst  
out of the trees and started circling the trees. There was  
something different about this eagle though. There was some  
sort of shimmery effect that made him seem powerful. Then,  
it dawned at me. This was Loki! I had forgotten all about his  
shape-shifting!  
Loki swooped down and shifted into his normal shape  
again. “Hello Glustyx,” he said.  
“H-h-how do you know my name?” I asked.  
“Because. I am your grandfather and your father,” he  
replied.  
I screamed, “WHAT!?”  
“It is true. I shape-shifted and mated the first  
Jormungandr, and then I shape-shifted again and mated your  
5  
mother. I am afraid there is a curse that makes it so that  
when I mate with the Jormungandr family, only one of the  
eggs survive.” said Loki.  
Then I said, “Let’s get down to business. Why are you  
threatening our world?”  
“I am not! I was trying to help a friend but then, I,  
uh, made a mistake,” he said.  
I said, “What did you do?”  
“I kind of made the goddess Frigg mad.” Loki  
explained.  
Then I heard yelling. Vigi came charging out of the  
lush bushes, swinging his sword like a crazy man. That’s when  
I realized that Vigi was a berserker. A berserker is a viking  
that usually wears bear or wolf skins and is a lethal fighter.  
Berserkers also go crazy in battle.  
“VIGI, WAIT!” I cried, hoping to stop him before it  
was too late. Vigi skidded and then stopped. “He doesn’t mean  
any harm!”.  
Vigi was like, “huh?”. I told Vigi our entire  
conversation. “How do you know he is not lying?” Vigi said, “It  
could be a trick.”  
“I trust him,” I said.  
“Fine.” Vigi grumbled. “But I am going to keep an eye  
on him.” Then we turned to Loki.  
“Do you have a plan?” I asked Loki.  
“If we can just get all the vikings together, we could  
make our point to Frigg,” Loki said.  
Then Vigi said, “Vikings never were a unified group  
6  
and they never will be.”  
“Then I guess it’s just the 3 of us then,” Loki said.  
Then I said, “What if we just tried? I mean, we have  
a berserker, a god, and me, the sea monster.”  
“I guess it might work,” Vigi said.  
“Fine,” Loki said, looking like he was not happy, “but  
we probably will not make it.”  
“We must have hope and faith,” I said. Then I looked  
at Loki and flashbacked to him in eagle form. “Loki, where is  
the goddess Frigg?” I asked.  
“At Prophecy Peak,” replied Loki.  
“I have a plan,” I said, “but it will be risky.” I  
explained my plan.  
“Let’s do it!” Loki and Vigi said in unison.  
Here we were, at Prophecy Peak, where the goddess  
Frigg changed the future for everyone. I snuck closer to the  
top, because, as I had recently found out, I could survive out  
of the water and slither like a snake to move.  
“Loki, have you shapeshifted into a wolf?” I asked  
quietly.  
“Yes,” Loki whispered.  
“Vigi, are you in position?” I asked.  
“Yes sir!” Vigi replied.  
“Vigi, be quiet.” I said back.  
“Sorry,” he whispered.  
“OK, everybody go……Now!” I said. Then we sprang  
into action. Vigi started yelling at the top of his lungs,  
jumping up and down, and waving his sword around all at the

Loki!”
