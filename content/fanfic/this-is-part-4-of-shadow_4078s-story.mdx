#### PART 4:  
Glitch laid down on the ground, staring at the night sky. What was that saying? That “Evil never rests”? Well, that had to be lie. She was resting right now.  
“Yo” Null appeared next to her. A few months before, she would’ve screamed. Now she was pretty much ready for anything.  
“Hey” she replied “What’s up?”  
At that moment, Entity appeared and poked her with a stick. Glitch flinched and all of a sudden Entity was hovering in the air that was glitching slightly.  
Glitch watched, amused, as Entity struggled in the air. Null laughed. Feeling sorry for her friend, she put him down.  
Falling flat to the ground, Entity banged his head. Glitch glanced at him and facepalmed,”Imbecile”.  
“So” Entity asked, stopping “How’s ‘holding Gameknight captive to question him’ going?”.  
Glitch was silent for a while.  
“You don’t really wanna kill him do you?” Null said. Glitch frowned.  
“Let’s not talk about that”
