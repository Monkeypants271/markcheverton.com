### Chapter 1

The new coach seemed peculiar,and it  
wasn’t just because of his third eye. So I  
secretly followed him to his office when,  
he shut the door. My name is Sally, and I  
am in 8th grade. I was very curious about  
our new coach so I peeked through the  
window. He took off his face,and I realized  
it was a mask. But then I saw why he was  
acting weird. He was an alien! He had a  
green gooey face!  
The coach saw me peek at the window, and  
he ran so I could not see him. I saw the  
rest of my class going to the classroom.  
Then suddenly my friend Sara called from  
the 8th grade classroom.  
“Sara,” said Sally, “come to class.”  
So Sally went to class although she was not  
capable of overcoming her fears. She still  
went to reading class.  
Their math teacher, Col Mustard, was  
waiting for all the students to come back,  
but Was always the last one.  
Col Mustard shouted, “You’re late! You  
must sit in the mustard corner. When they  
dumped the mustard on Sally, she dived off  
the stool and watched the mustard melt the  
chair. “Knew it!” stated Sally. “Acid!”  
She jumped up and took off Col Mustard’s  
mask.

### CHAPter 2

Col Mustard dashed at Sally. She  
sprinted out the door. The janitor had out  
her broom so Sally grabbed it and threw it  
at Col Mustard. She missed and ran outside.  
Col Mustard stopped right before he  
went outside and said something weird. It  
sounded like a curse. Sally looked in her  
curse journal and saw that he cursed  
everybody in the school and made them  
aliens. She ran home and looked up how to  
make an alien human. She saw how to cure  
them with acid and lollipops in plasma  
coma? “COMA? What is that?” Sally wondered.  
So she left to find that she saw An alien  
spaceship it was as big as the sun. She  
went inside to see if that comma was in it.  
She pressed a button and did not open the  
door, but it handed her a blaster. She  
blasted open the door and saw coma but an  
alien was guarding it. She shot at it and  
it was as small as a flea. She touched the  
coma to see if it was safe.It was making a  
noise that sounded like kids yelling,  
¨Help!¨  
She touched it. It felt and smelled  
like a cloud with water in it, but it look  
like a pearl. She grabbed it out, and she  
stuffed it in her bag in her sack. She had  
to steal one thing though, a lollipop. So  
she did but where would she find plasma?  
She looked at her list:  
Coma \*  
lollipop\*  
Plasma

### CHAPTER 3

What Sally did not know is she had  
plasma a strap on her shirt all day, but  
now she figured it out. She looked in her  
blaster and took out the plasma, put it in  
her bag, and ran home.  
When she got home she mixed all of the  
ingredients together thinking it would  
explode. When she got to school she climbed  
on the roof seeing all the school children  
were walking like zombies. She dropped in  
the mixture in the vent and ran inside  
yelling for everybody chasing her to go to  
the main vent. And BOOM! They all switched  
from aliens to humans.  
Sally ran straight to Sara and slapped  
her to see if she was alive. She screeched  
in pain. You could see her hairs pop up.  
Sally gasped in relief and did the same  
thing to everyone else. Then she went to  
save the rest of the school.  
Next was the middle school. So she did  
the same thing, but this time it was  
harder. She had to use her blaster. She got  
it out, put in the mixture, and shot her  
sister and it worked.  
But she still was mean, so Sally shrunk  
her and ran to help the rest. Sally snuck  
around the corner to see a bunch of the  
alien children. She fought her way through.  
It was like a zombie apocalypse, but it was  
an alien school apocalypse! She had to  
shoot as fast as possible. She shot her  
last bullet. The last alien was switched,  
she thought.  
They all were in pain. But she did not  
care she was so scared her face turned blue  
and she was out of breath. She stared at  
the biggest alien of them all, the gym  
coach. She started to shoot but it did  
nothing, so Sally ran, grabbed out a pocket  
knife, poured the mixture on it, and cut  
his arm. CAPOW! He turned back into a  
human. He had a scratch on his arm but he  
was ok.  
She did the same with her brother, and  
the aliens were no more. When they got home  
they had a party.  
What was that? A green asteroid came.  
it was on fire. We ran at it. I had my  
blaster but it was not an alien. It was a  
baby. Right when we opened it the baby  
disappeared and came back flying. We were  
wondering if this would cause trouble?  
I walked up to it, and it was holding  
something. I asked what it was and the  
mysterious baby said something in alien  
language. I listened.  
He said, “ela edmodo sinca.”  
I realized that is what Col Mustard had  
stated. So right before he finished the  
curse I made him human again by blasting  
him. He was happy I saved him, so I asked  
what his name was.  
He said, “I am Xenomorph. I have come  
from planet/galaxy 117.”  
“Xenomorph needs our help,” Sally  
announced. “We need to get him home but  
there are guards and I can’t do it without  
you. Who is in?”  
They all yelled, “Me!”  
I gave them all a blaster and we went  
to help Xenomorph. Once we got to his  
planet there were predators. I did not  
believe what I saw. A war was going on. We  
joined in, blasting every last one of them.  
We got Xenomorph home safely.
