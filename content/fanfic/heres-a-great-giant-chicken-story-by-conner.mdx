## Dawn of the Giant Chickens

### By Connor V

One time in the great mountains of minecraft CONNOR and  
GAMEKNIGHT were getting ready for the battle against the  
Giant Chickens, because they have been taking out all of the other  
villages. They needed to be STOPPED!! But Connor was the strong  
one and Gameknight was weak and he always did not want to  
fight.

#### Chapter 2

This battle needed to be in the Mountains because they could  
drive them off a cliff and they had a high advantage so they could  
see the chickens coming..

#### Chapter 3

Gameknight looked worried when they were heading up the  
mountain.  
Connor said, “Gameknight there is nothing to be afraid of.”  
But Gameknight didn’t answer, he just kept walking up the  
mountain.

#### Chapter 4

Gameknight and Connor were at the top of the mountain and  
getting their swords and bows ready, but Gameknight couldn’t find  
his sword so he told Connor.  
Connor replied, ”Just fight with your bow.”

#### Chapter 5

Connor said, ”I think i see the chickens and the one in the  
front looks like the captain.”  
“I thinks he is the captain too,” Replied Gameknight.  
Connor said, ”We are going to have to drive them off the  
cliff.”

#### Chapter 6

When they were in position Gameknight found a horn to blow  
in but he said to Connor, ”can you blow into it i’m too scared?”  
“Fine.” replied Connor.  
So he blew in it and it was as loud as a freight train.

#### Chapter 7

All of the chickens ran toward the cliff and all of the  
chickens, except for one, fell off the the cliff, and that was the  
leader, and it looked like he was running the other direction to  
regain his army.
