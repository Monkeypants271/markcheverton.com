So What Is Minecraft?

Minecraft is an open 3D world sandbox video game where it has no required goals to accomplish, allowing players a large amount of freedom in choosing how to play the game. The style is called “sandbox” because it provides a creative landscape with no fixed goal and endless possibilities. Its blocky design is rather kid-like, too: Characters’ heads are square, colors appear in chunks, and even trees look like they were grown in a Lego lab. It was originally made by Markus ‘Notch’ Persson. It was run by a company called Mojang before being sold to Microsoft in 2014 for 2.5 billion American dollars. It is the best-selling video game of all time, and over 200 million copies of the game have been sold. It can be played on computers, Xbox, PlayStation, Wii U, Nintendo Switch, and Mobile. The game is where players can build anything. The game which has been described as like an ‘online Lego’ involves building blocks and creating structures across different environments and terrains. Set in a virtual world the game involves resource gathering, crafting items, building, and combat.You can also play with friends. In Survival Mode you are put in a life or death situation. You start by destroying trees to make wooden tools and end by defeating the Ender Dragon. Though you can also go to the End City to get cool stuff. Some people think that is where the game really ends. But when the game ends you can skip the credits and keep playing. You don’t leave the world and make a new one. You can keep building, exploring your world. You choose when you end. And Creative Mode lets you go into your imagination and build whatever you want! From small houses to  GIGANTIC Castles with many blocks to choose from. The sky’s the limit. Plus you can make people go wow with Redstone machines! Minecraft is for most kids the first online game they play. And it was my first too. And there are different types of Minecraft, there is Bedrock Edition, and there is Java. So yeah they are obviously different, though it depends on what is cool about each of them, Bedrock actually in my recommendation is pretty cool. You might think it’s just like the first version of Minecraft, but it isn’t. Java/Vanilla Minecraft was the first version of Minecraft. Now you might think it is just very old and outdated. But Minecraft is updated. From 1.8.8/start of Minecraft to 1.19/Wild Update. It might actually take years, maybe even decades to stop Mojang. There are so many things you can do in Minecraft.
