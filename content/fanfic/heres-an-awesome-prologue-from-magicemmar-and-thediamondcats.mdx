Black.

Blacknees.

Blackness covering the world.

The same Blackness that killed it.

The Blackness that a young boy unknowingly unleashed.

The same Blackness that was consuming all light.

The Blackness hiding monsters from nightmares.

The same Blackness that was being controlled.

The same man that the young boy unleashed.

The man was the Blackness.

But…..

He was darker than the depths of the void.

Darker than the Blackness that consumed the world.

Stronger than the monsters, the nightmares of old.

The man was a God in the world that he was unleashed upon.

His name…..  
 

HEROBRINE.
