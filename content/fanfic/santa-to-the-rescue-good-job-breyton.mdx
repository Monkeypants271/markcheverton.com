### By Breyton

### Chapter One

## The North Pole !!!!!!!

One morning at the North Pole there were three good elves, Santa Claus, Mrs. Claus, and two  normal elves. One night the two normal elves went to Nebraska. They wrote bad words on trains, on store walls, and on cars. Then Santa found out that the two elves were becoming evil elves. The good elves’ names were Bob, Mark, and Emma.  Emma is greedy. The bad elves’ names were The Vader and King Of Viruses.

Later when Santa Claus found them he kicked out the two bad elves. Then Santa and Mrs. Claus didn’t have enough elves at Santa’s Workshop . Santa Claus was as red as a cupcake. Mrs. Claus didn’t like that the two elves turned evil. Santa found the two bad elves again so he tried to make them good again. Santa said, “ I’ll get you back.”

Santa said, “ We have to catch the two bad elves before they ruin Nebraska.”

Mrs.Claus said, “They’ll hurt all the children and parents.”

“Or will they do that?” said Santa. “The way I will catch them is I’ll make a wall, I’ll get the bad elves here, and then I’ll catch them in a net.”

“Ok,” said Mrs.Claus.

Santa said, “I’m going to make the wall out of brick or metal.”

The way Santa was going to make the two bad elves good again, is he put a wall for the bad elves to draw on the wall in spray paint, and he would catch the bad elves in a net. Then Santa will erase all the bad stuff in their brains.One day later Santa Claus and Mrs. Claus caught and erased all the bad stuff in the two bad elves’ brains.

### Chapter Two

### The Night Before Christmas Eve!!!!

There once was a family who lived in Louisville, Ne. Hello, my name is Breyton and there’s one more week until Christmas. “I can’t wait until I get the presents from Santa!” I said. This is my family.  My dad is Brandon, my mom is Andrea, and my sister is Aleaha.

On the day before Christmas Eve two elves went to our house and the elves were The Vader and The King Of Viruses. They were drawing on our house wall, so we had to call 911 for the police. We waited and waited then the police saw the evidence. They put a camera inside the door to catch the two bad elves. We caught the two elves and we called 911 again, and they came and took the two bad elves to jail.

That night the elves tried to escape over and over until they realized that they can’t get out. More prisoners came in the prison yard one after another but they wouldn’t stop coming. So the guards put security cameras all around the prison yard. They also put sound in the security cameras and we heard the prisoners trying to make a plan to get out. That night they put all the guards out by the gate so no one could get out.

“Soon it’ll be Christmas Day and all the kids won’t have presents,” said Santa Claus.

That Christmas day we did give the kids what they wanted. After that we finally got the two evil elves good again.
