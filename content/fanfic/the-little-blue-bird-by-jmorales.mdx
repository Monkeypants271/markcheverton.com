Hello little blue bird in the sky,  
Soaring so free, and I wonder why,  
Flying from your nest each and every day,  
I’m stuck here in my bed left to decay,  
One night you paused by my window,  
And I, in curiosity, stepped out to see  
you were so beautiful, way more than me  
Looking in the mirror, I saw a sight,  
My reflection was like a dead bird, falling from heights,  
When I looked into your eye, I saw something true,  
Your freedom, my dear bird, is what I wish for too.
