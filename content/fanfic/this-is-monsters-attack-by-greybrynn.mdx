MONSTERS ATTACK!!

Chapter 1 – The prophecy

Watcher walked down to his village and saw that his village was on fire! “Noooooo!! Not again!!!”Said Watcher as he ran into the village. He saw a giant ghast floating away from his village, with his Son, Cobbler! “No!! Cobbler!!!” Yelled Watcher. “Is that you, Watcher?!” Yelled Smithy as he extinguished the last house on fire. “Yes, Smithy, and they took Cobbler!!” Watcher Replied as he started to cry. “I know. I know.” Smithy Said as he was trying to comfort Watcher. Smithy was a big stocky NPC(Non playable character) with red hair and green emerald eyes. Cobbler was just a kid, and was Watcher’s son, and he had blonde hair, just like his mother’s and had brown eyes like his father’s. A villager walked up to Watcher crying. “Baker!!”said Watcher as he gave his wife, Baker, a hug. “Why would they take Cobbler?!?” Asked Baker. “Let’s go talk to Crafter, but I think something about the prophecy had a Cobbler in it.” Replied Watcher. They walked to the Watchtower and removed a block of cobblestone, inside the Watchtower, that fell down a hole revealing a iron door. As they climbed down the ladder, someone came over to help them get in. “Where is Crafter?” Watcher asked. “Here,” Replied an NPC. “What do you need?” “Crafter remember the time you told us about the prophecy? Well…..we ran into the forest to find Cobbler and we missed that prophecy” Said Watcher. “Wait what?!? Well. The prophecy is that one day the king of the ghasts, Maclonce, would take a Cobbler,  from a random village and take it back to the nether, to build a 10 by 7 portal out of cobblestone and fill it with corn, berries, and use flint n’ steel on it. Then the portal to all the source will be activated. I fear your son,  Cobbler, is that kid, and he will be forced to make the portal, also when the Cobbler makes the portal and uses the flint n’ steel, he will have to use all of his Cobbler powers, and might umm… well…die.”Crafter Said. “There is another prophecy that we Crafters haven’t told the other villagers, but I have a feeling you need to know it, so here it is. The legend says that there are five villagers who will become the guardians of Minecraft, and they will rise up the day when Maclod takes the Cobbler, and they will save Minecraft.” Crafter Said. “That is amazing.” Said Baker. “Zombies!!!” A random Npc Yelled. “We better get up there and help.” Said Watcher and pointed up to the exit. “ Come on let’s go!!!” Smithy Yelled as he was climbing up the ladder.

Chapter 2 – The Attack

“Guys, I think we should put a wall around the village, that is about the height of the watchtower.” Watcher said. “Guys once this wave of zombies is over build a wall around the village, that is around 8 blocks high.” Instructed Crafter. Smithy started to run toward the zombies and slicing into them with his iron sword. “Archers!! Fire at the zombies!!” Yelled Watcher. The zombies kept pushing forward even though they were outnumbered. weird thought Watcher. “Guys I think The zombies are up to something.” Said Watcher. “Guys check the perimeter of the village!!” He ran towards some zombies and started attacking them with his iron sword, and that was when his sword broke. “Guys!!!! Does anyone have a spare sword?!?!!” Yelled Watcher. “Here!!!” Yelled Smithy as he threw a iron sword towards Watcher. “Thanks!!” Watcher Yelled. “Zombies are on the other side of the village!! And there is a lot and I mean A LOT!!!” Yelled an NPC. “Villagers!!!! Some of you come with me to the other side of the village!!!” Yelled Watcher. They ran to the other side of the village and found that there are to many Zombies. “Retreat to the crafting chamber!” Yelled Watcher. They all ran to the crafting chamber and Watcher jumped off the ladder and placed a bucket of water were he landed. “Everyone start crafting minecarts!” Instructed Watcher as he started to give some villagers some iron. Watcher walked over to Baker and handed her a minecart. “Please take it and go, I need to stay back and make sure everyone is safe.” Watcher Said. “OK, but what about you?” Asked Baker. “ I live to protect my village, but I will come I promise.” Watcher Said as he pushed her down the Minecart network track. He ran up, with a handful of minecarts, to some Villagers entering the crafting chamber  and started handing them to everyone entering. “Go down the rails on the right, but make sure you go on the far right rail to get to the desert village.” Instructed Watcher as he gave a villager a minecart. A large explosion rocked the crafting chamber as a giant ghast came out of a giant hole and headed towards Stonecutterand Watcher. “Get in a minecart, Watcher!!” Yelled Stonecutter. “No, you need to go.” Watcher Replied as he shoved The last minecart into Stonecutter’s hands. “No!!! They need you more than me. Now go save minecraft!!” Said Stonecutter as he shoved Watcher down the minecart network tunnel.
