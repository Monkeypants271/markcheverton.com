Subject: Meeting GameKnight999 and Crafter

Flutters is in a deep sleep as she lays on her blocky bed, twisting and turning at ever living second. Morning daylight arose as the blocky god in the sky shined it’s bright light into her glass pain windows. She has woken up, rubbing her blocky hands on her face to progress to the daylight. She grabbed a hold of herself and jumped out of her bed, going over to her chest to grab her basic necessities for the day. “Golden apples, Diamond sword, Pickax, Ax, Yup, Everything is in good order!” Flutters said to herself as she put all of her items into her inventory. She then fled her house and went searching for more diamond. She went to her usual mineshaft she had found a couple nights ago. She gathered a total of 7 diamonds and headed out of the mineshaft, but then, bumped into a strange looking young fellow, who she recognized in a instant. “OH MY CELESTIA, GAMEKNIGHT999!!!!” Flutters screamed to the top of her lungs as she jumped around everywhere, almost falling into her mineshaft. “Um, hello? Do I know you?” GameKnight questioned the young, excited, bubbly female. “Oh, no. I am just SO happy I can finally meet you, my hero!” She cried, pulling him into a hug. ‘Um….’ GameKnight pondered in his mind. ‘I didn’t know I had fangirls already…” He thought as he looked down at Flutters. “GAMEKNIGHT, GAMEKNIGHT! WHERE HAVE YOU GONE, BOY?” A peculiar squeak voice called. “Crafter! I’m over here!” GameKnight shouted. The young boy came walking, and as stunned as GameKnight was. Um, May I not intrude”, Crafter said with a pause “Do you know this young lady?” The boy asked GameKnight. He mouthed the words ‘No.’ By this time, Flutters had opened her eyes and seen the boy, Crafter looking shocked to see her sky blue eyes. “O.O, C-Crafter?” She asked as she tried to grasp the words to speak, but that had came out. Then, she passed out form all the cuteness she was seeing in just 10 minutes, Night had fallen quickly and the monsters were closing in the the gang of 3. ‘Well, we can’t just leave her out here.” said Crafter. They both grabbed Flutters and started picking her up, she was surpisingly lighter then the both thought, carried her into her shelter. “It is too dark to go outside in search for our own shelter” said Young Crafter, making himself comfy with a mini bed he had brought for himself. “Lay in the double bed with the girl, we will leave at daybreak.” GameKnight sourly looked at Crafter, and then at Flutters, laying sound asleep in her queen sized double bed. GameKnight999 then sighed and climbed into the bed, but as far from the girl as possible. The next morning, GameKnight found himself wrapped around Flutter’s arms in the bed and she was nuzzled on his chest. As he woke up, he was shocked, his cheeks turning pink as cotton candy. “Crafter, CRAFTER!” GameKnight yelled quietly, being sure not to wake her up. Crafter woke up, and to his surprise, was holding in a laugh. He then finallt said, with a grin, “Let it happen.” And then curled back into a ball, for it was too early to get up now and leave. GameKnight then layed against his pillow, and put his arm around Flutters, all the while, She was smiling. The End.
