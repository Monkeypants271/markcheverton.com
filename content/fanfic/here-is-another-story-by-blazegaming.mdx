Trapped in Dinoland(New name XD) Chapter 2: Ambushed! By… Dilos?  
Gameknight was woke by a sound. It’s something like an elphant trumpeting, but deeper. It’s a mammoth. He thought to himself.  
He opened his eyes and saw his new room all done and a beautiful balcony sticking out of the cliffside. THe whole room is blue at the side Gameknight is sleeping at and purple at the side Monet’s bed is.  
Yesterday, the team, well, the group, or the villagers and Monet, followed Gameknight to a beautiful cliffside with a beautiful lake under at the north-west side of the map and built a magnificent house on top of the cliff.  
He saw a note beside his bed.He grabbed it and read it.  
——————————————————————————————————————————-  
Got you some new clothes! It’s in the chest, since you look like in real life, I’ll just call you Tommy, no one else is here anyways. 😛  
\-Monet  
——————————————————————————————————————————–  
Gameknight wandered arount the house after putting on his brand new hide set of clothes. It is really comfortable and quite warm because the air that morning is cold. The house is shockingly big and it is divided into many sections, all thanks to Monet’s creative mind. It is located at the north-west section of the island, beside a cliff with a lake under it which is a location suggested by Gameknight since he always live in this strategic location and know it very well. But it was going to change, sooner or later. Gameknight thought.  
Monet went to sleep after finished drawing the floor plan last night. Gameknight stayed up to help building the house until crafter told him to go to sleep during midnight.  
Now, it is 6oclock in the morning. The misty fog outside is beautiful and the temperature is 18 degree Celcius. The air is full of the smell of water and the scent of trees.  
Gameknight walked outside, going towards the fireplace which is in front of the house under the protection of the double high wall surrounding the house. He saw Monet sitting beside it, just her, no one else in sight.  
“Jenny.” He called out.  
” Oh hey, Tommy.” She said as she turned her head around to look at Gameknight. ” I’m cooking some breakfast for us. You can come grab your serving.”  
He walked towards the fireplace, then Monet handed over a cooked meat and a varient berries.  
“Where’s the others?” Gamknight asked curiously.  
“Sleeping.”  
“What? How can that happen? They usually are up during 6 in the morning! It’s already 8 now!”  
“Well, they slept late in the night, remember? I saw Crafter going to bed just now.So stop freaking out.”  
Gameknight’s face turn red, embarrased.

“Whatever.” He said. “Just curious anyways.”  
“So what are we doing today?” Monet asked.”I suppose we’re not going to do nothing right?”  
“We’re going out searching for more food.”  
“Yeah, we have only 50 piece of meat and 5 of them is spoiled.”  
“Don’t throw the spoiled meat away though.”  
“Why?”  
“We need them to tame a Scorpion also known as Pulminoscorpius and a Spider A.K.A again Araneo.”  
“Jeez, you’re so obsessed with ancient creatures aren’t you?”  
“Nope, all my information about ancient creatures are experiences from this game.”  
“What are you guys talking about?” A voice came from behind them. Gameknight and Monet turned around and saw Stitcher standing behind them.  
“Morning, Stitcher.” Gameknight and Monet said at the same time.  
That day, about 4p.m., Crafter,Gameknight,Monet,Hunter,Stitcher and Herder went out.  
“Why did you want me out here?” Herder complained. “There’s not a single wolf here, I hate this place now.”  
“You shouldn’t though.” Monet said. “Because there’s a lot of Dinosaurs you can be friend with, even a hostile dinosaur.”  
“Really? Where are they?” Herder asked, his eyes now flashing with excitement. “I wanna tame them.”  
“Do you even know what dinosaurs are?” Gameknight said. “They’re these weird animals around us, they’re all grouped together to be called dinosaurs but some are not dinosaurs such as mammoths, scorpions, spiders, titanomyrmas and etc.”  
“So how do we tame them?” Herder asked. “Which one of them are the best because there’s a lot of dinosaurs here.”  
“Woah, woah, Herder, calm down.” Hunter finally can’t hold her sarcastic nature and started.  
After a while, a smart Herder who knew really well about dinosaurs, and Herder’s teacher-Gameknight and the others return camp with lots of food including some Savoroot, Rockarrot, Longrass, Citronal and Narcoberry seeds for Planters and other Farmer type NPCs to plant.  
The next day, they went out hunting again but this time, they’re hunting for pets.
