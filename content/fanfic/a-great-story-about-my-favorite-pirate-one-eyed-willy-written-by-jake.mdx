Prologue  
Just because Dipper beat Willy does  
not mean he won. Dipper did not see  
the label that was on the back of the  
book because his hand was covering it.  
It said….. WARNING if you put the spell  
on me I will return in 80 years! I will  
return!  
So Willy went back to the underworld  
and Dipper was a hero….. Wrong.  
Remember, Willy gave out his  
chilling warning that in 80 years he  
will return!  
After that Dipper just left and made a  
house and moved in and then made a  
shop of how to make swords, how to  
fight people, and how to defend  
yourself. After that they were happy, but  
that only lasted for 70 years.  
Chapter 1  
80 years later  
“Welcome to the other side of the  
village and the only Dipper Sanborn’s  
Memorial!” said the tour guide.  
“Welcome to the secret underground  
treasure room!” There was all of the  
adventure’s Dipper had gone on. There  
was the time he captured “Dipper”, the  
evil replica, and the time he went to help  
an astronaut, and the time he beat Willy.  
“So what would you guys like to  
see?” asked the tour guide. After that  
there was a noise…. Somebody who  
would not return in 80 years…. The  
Return of OneEyed  
Willy.  
“OH MY GOSH!” said the tour guide.  
“Everybody go to the exit and find your  
home.  
“It’s here,” said the creepy man.  
“What’s here?” said the tour guide.  
“A pirate with only one eye, One  
Eyed Willy is here.”  
“What can we do?”  
“The only thing we can.”  
“What’s that?”  
“Summon Dipper Sanborn. We need  
all of the people that Dipper knew, but  
we have a pirate’s chest in the  
room.There’s a key behind the chest, so  
I’ll get it and you just keep everybody  
from getting severe injuries, or death.  
Now go and gather everybody up that  
Dipper knew.”  
Chapter 2  
“Dipper” said the creepy old man.  
“We need you to be alive so you can  
beat Willy for us, and so you can save  
the world one last time.”  
Then the ground stopped  
shaking….. then BOOM! Everybody was  
wondering what was going to happen.  
Dipper Sanborn has returned and to kick  
some pirates booty! “What is going on!”  
Said Dipper.  
“We need your help,” said the creepy old  
man. “We need you to kill Willy again.”  
“No way! Willy? As in OneEyed  
Willy?! Yes!!! Let’s go! First let me get  
my gear and let’s roll!” So the battle of  
the century was about to begin.
