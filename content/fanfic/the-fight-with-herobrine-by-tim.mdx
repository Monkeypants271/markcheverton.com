## **The Fight with Herobrine**

Chapter 1 – The fight with Herobrine

Gameknight and his friends discovered something mysterious in the Nether. The mysterious discovery was that Herder had the Herobrine curse.  
Herder said, “Ah, Hello, Gameknight. I see we have a lot to catch up on.”  
Gameknight said confused, “What?!!!!!!?”  
Hunter said, “Just ignore him, Gameknight. Trust me.” Gameknight did not want to focus on Hunter saying about ignoring Herder that had the Herobrine curse.  
Herder said, “I see that you have your friends with you. I think it’s time for you to get bombarded by blazes.”  
“BE QUIET!!!!!!!” Gameknight snapped. “Don’t you dare make me fight you, Herobrine.” Gameknight said again.  
Herder said, “Try it. I will win.” Then, the duel was on. Brang! Dang! Klash! Chop! Herder said, “I will never go easy on you.” Then, Gameknight did a secret technique where he brought Herder’s sword down to the ground violently. KLASSSSH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!  
Herder said with the Herobrine voice, “You may have brought my sword down, but I have infinite swords!!!!! HAHAHAHAHAAHAHAHA!!!!” Gameknight said, “I don’t believe that you have “infinite swords.”  
Herder said, “You don’t believe tha– Wait a minute, why is the ability disappearing?!” Gameknight said, “Well, I have triggered a curse cure that made your “infinite sword” ability disappear.”  
Herder said, “Grrrr! I have had enough of your cure—”  
GrrrrrzzZzZzZzZZZzZZzZzZz!  
Hunter said, “I think there is a curse glitch between the Herobrine Herder and Herder himself. Good thing that’s curing the curse.”  
Herder himself said, “Wait, I think I’m glitching the curse betwixt myself and the curse of Herobrine–”  
GrrggrrrrrzzzZzZz!  
Herobrine Herder said, “No, this cannot be happening. I’m glitchi–”  
Ggrgrgrgrgrzz!  
Herder himself said again, “Yes. I think the cure is working hard–”  
Ggrgrgrgrgrzz!!!!  
Herobrine Herder said in anger, “I will be back. I WILL BE BACK!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!”  
RGRGRGRGRGRGRGRGRGRGRGRGZGZGZZGZGZGZZGZGZGZGZGZZGBZGZGZGZGZGZGZGZGZGZGGGZGZGZGZZGZGZRZGRZGRZRGZRSZGRG!!!!!  
Then, Herder was back to normal. Herder said, “There goes the curse of Herobrine.” Herder felt relieved.

Chapter 2 – A dream

Gameknight, Tommy, in the real world, was dreaming the whole time about that fight. Tommy  opened his eyes and grumbled quietly as he woke up. He was on some sort of red bed that was comfortable.  Then, Tommy thought, “My friends!” Tommy had been dreaming this whole time, and he did actually fight Herobrine once recently. But now, Herobrine himself and the curse were destroyed. Then, Tommy heard a hammer smashing metal. Tommy went downstairs to the office and saw MonkeyPants271 destroy the computer that ran the digitizer. MonkeyPants271 said, “This computer is offically borked.” MonkeyPants271 continued to destroy the computer, while destroying the cover of the computer, causing it to pop out. Then, he got into the CPU, and smashed at it until he made it split into 1300 pieces. Then, he hammered at the memory cards and BIOS memory chips, chipping them apart with each smash. Parts of the BIOS and memory cards got into the wires of the computer, making the components and wires tear apart.  
Tommy said, “Why would you destroy the computer if you’re destroying it for no reason, Dad?”  
MonkeyPants271 said, “Well, I’m destroying it because Herobrine got into the software.”  
Tommy said, “But you know that Herobrine was destroyed, right?”  
MonkeyPants271 said, “I don’t. And I didn’t check on you when you were playing with your friends.” Tommy said, “Are you going to replace the computer, Dad?”  
MonkeyPants271 said, “Definitely.” Then, Tommy felt relieved. Then, Tommy did his real life chores with MonkeyPants271.

THE END.
