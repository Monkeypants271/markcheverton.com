Chapter 1

As the morning sun rose up above a 12 year old boy Max, he rolled over. But the sleep mask wasn’t doing its job as it kept falling off his nose. As Max grunted, sat up, and opened his eyes, he saw the rising, shining, bright sun and shielded his eyes from its really bright rays. He then got outta bed, and went to his closet to pick out some clothes. As he opened the closet, he grabbed his “Gaming Shirt” which had the Minecraft logo, Alex and Steve, and the sky. He then smiled, and put it on, along with his Minecraft sweatshirt, which had the face of a creeper on it. He then heard his phone beep. As he walked over to it, he picked it up and THIS:

New Update From Minecraft,  
The 1.22 Update Is Out Now.  
Go And Update It To Finally See What’s Been Added!

He grinned a HUGE smile, and turned off his phone. “Good Morning sweety! Since It’s now 8:00 A.M, you may be on Minecraft!”  Max’s mother yelled from downstairs on the couch. Okay!! Be there in a moment! He yelled. As he got on all his Minecraft clothes, He turned on his PS4, and started Minecraft. His username read “Maximus#1”. As Max clicked on the “create new world” button, he was taken to the usual settings screen for his world. He then clicked “Creative” instead of survival mode, just to test out the brand new features.
