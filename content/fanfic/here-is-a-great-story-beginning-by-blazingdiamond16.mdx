What GameKnight999 found was probably the worst site he has ever seen… What he saw was all the villagers tools scattered.  
There wasn’t a single villager in sight. But, what he did see were a bunch of Endermen in the distance with the Ender Dragon circling high above. He thought that the Ender Dragon was to never leave the domain of the End. But, when the Dragon turned so that GameKnight could see his eyes. he didn’t see the purple eyes an Ender Dragon normally had, he saw the white eyes of Herobrine.  
That was when he realized, Herobrine is still alive
