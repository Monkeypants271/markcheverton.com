Mark was writing a brand new story on his PC.

“Alright I am almost done!” exclaimed Mark. Mark’s new book was called Minecraft Noob Guide. It was supposed to be about a Noob who discovers Minecraft and gets trapped inside the game just like GamerKnight999

All of a sudden his computer went black and then came back on.

“Huh? What was that. Well it’s ok at least it’s back….” then the computer went black again.

“What the?” questioned Mark.

All of a sudden a strange Creeper face with red eyes appeared.

“Hello there Mark,” said the strange Creeper, “I am Desmond. The terror of all Minecraft Players.”

“Go away!” shouted Mark. Desmond stayed. “GO!” shouted Mark again. “Well Mark if you want me to go away…well fine.” The computer went back to normal.

“Yes!” Mark shouted. All of a sudden the ground shook. His PC fell to the floor. A large Creeper beast arose from the floor.

Mark ran to his room. He found his diamond sword. When he ran back out. Desmond was even crazier. He had 3 heads and lazer fire eyes.

What was Mark gonna do?
