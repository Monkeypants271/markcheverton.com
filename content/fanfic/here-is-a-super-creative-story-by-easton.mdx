Chap.1 Boxing Tournament

I was sitting in the alley one day resting in my box bed then a man with skull tattoos and big muscles picked me up and threw me in a large potato sack. I twisted and turned like a snake in a container but I was too weak to get up then out. When we stopped moving he threw me out and on a bench.

“Put these on, Seth. I will be waiting!” he said in a low menacing voice.

So I put on my shorts and shirt, my shoes, and my boxing gloves. Yes that’s right! A boy as weak as me, that has arms and legs replaced with noodles is boxing with big men with big muscles and big punches.

Big, big punches! Oooooo.

So I walked out of the tiny room and met the man that right after, threw me in the ring. I met a man that was skinny. It looked like all his weight went to his muscles. The bell rang. He threw a punch in my gut and then to my face.

Blood dripped out of my mouth. Then it spilled out of my nose. I had no

strength to tap out so he jumped on me.

“Ahhhhhhh!” I shrieked.

His sweat dripped into my mouth.

Yuck!

Chap.2 OPPS

What if a man had a snake tongue?

What would they do?

They were experimenting on a man named Jo Fool. So they took snake DNA and poured it into his mouth. The man screamed with pain. He became larger and his arms and legs disappeared and then….. He became a LARGE snake, tongue and all.

“What ss did sss you ssssss do sss tooo ssss me ssss!”

“I foforgot what your name was,” A scientist quivered in fear.

“MMMMssssss ZZsssZOKOOLsssss,” he answered with a menacing look.

Everyone started running. He ate the slowest person first. The rest of the scientists jumped into their rocket with a guest that had a rattle and no meat on his bones following them back to earth.

I was with the only one person that understands me, Zeth, the street cat. We were sitting in the alley packing up to go to Elmers town to get away from wrestling.

“Ok, Zeth, let’s go hit the streets,” I said sighing. “Elmers town is just two blocks away. Let’s go!”

Chap.4 Elmers town

“Finally! Ha ha!” Seth exautanilly said. “I am wore out! Let’s find a place to stay.“

So they went and explored Elmers town.

There were shops and apartments and tons, I mean TONS of street cats. Seth found a 10 dollar bill in the wrestling ring so they went in the cheapest apartment, paid 5 dollars rent and slept on a little tiny mattress.

Chap.5 Arrival

The space shuttle reached the NASA station. The scientists arrived and hustled out the door.

¨ Wa wa we AHHHHHHHH!” One of the scientists said while being crushed by Zokool.

Zokool slithered his massive body over to find a street sign that said, ELMERS TOWN ONE MILE.

His body was about one mile so he jumped his body length, and there he was Elmers town!

The mayor spotted him and rushed to the towns sirens. WE WO WE WO. That got the attention of most people, but Seth. He slept through all of this.

¨ S.S.S. S.S.S. S.S.S. !!!!! ¨ The mayor said loudly in the town microphone. S.S.S. means Scouch so people can hide with you, Scatter run,run,run,run,run,run, and Scream and run because there’s nothing else better to do.

So everyone in the town flocked off and ran away.

Chap.6 I See You!

Zokool used his snake vision to find more humans and found a boy lying in bed. Zeth was awake so he jumped on Seth to warn him that a big snake was rushing towards him. Seth woke up in shock so he ran out of the apartment building with Zeth.

Zokool spotted them easily and slithered after them.

Chap.7 The Battle

¨I guess I´m done for,¨ Seth said.

¨Use your power, Seth!¨ Zeth said strongly.

¨Whaaaat you’re like a cat poster!¨”Seth said. ¨What power?¨

“Look inside you!” Zeth said.

“OK.”

Zokool wrapped his body around Seth. Seth then was terrified and turned into a rabbit. He fell to the ground escaping the massive snake.

“WOW!”

He used this advantage to turn into a mouse. Just kidding, just kidding, he turned into a tiger! He jumped on Zokool scratching and biting. Then he turned into a bird to poop from above. Zokool was almost defeated, you could see it. To finish, Seth turned into a lion and bit him in the neck. Zokool was defeated.

Chap.8 Come On!!!!!!

Seth was proud of himself with that big accomplishment. The bad part was the man at the beginning of this story saw it all. He was impressed. That was a bad, bad thing. He wanted him to go back to the alley and fight with the other wrestlers again.

The man picked him up by the T shirt. Suddenly Zeth ran up and scratched the man in the face! “Good job Zeth!” Seth said with his eyes popped out!
