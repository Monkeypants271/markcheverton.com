Story Submission:  
Gameknight999 walked through the portal, and to his surprise, he found the blaze king standing right in front of him. “You are an invader in my land,” the blaze king said. “I only came here to save Minecraft,” Gameknight999 said. “Ha, I dare you to try,” the blaze king said. “I accept,” Gameknight999 said as he slashed at the blaze king, continuously making him flash red. “NO!!!” the blaze king said as he flashed red one final time, then disapeared with a pop. Gameknight999 picked up the warm blaze rod, and he noticed that instead of being yellow, it was the color of dried blood mixed with yellow. He cringed at what he remembered was the same color. Erebus. He walked right back through the portal, and Erebus was gone. I should have expected that, Gameknight999 thought. Erebus reappeared and Gameknight said, “I have the blaze rod.” “That quickly?” Erebus said, startled.
