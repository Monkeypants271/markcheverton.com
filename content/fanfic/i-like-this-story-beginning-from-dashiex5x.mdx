Hunter opened the door to meet her parents. “Hunter dear,” Hunter’s mother said.”We need to talk.” “What is it mommy?” Hunter asked and sat down on the floor.”Hunter, me and your father,Catcher,have something to tell you.” Hunter gulped wondering what would it be. Would they be moving villages? Did she have to move schools? Did they have to go on a trip to the forest? “Yes we do,” Catcher said.”Me and Painter (Himter’s mother) have been wanting to tell you this for awhile. Hunter closed her eyes and put her hands into fists waiting for what-ever they needed to tell her.”Hunter,” Painter said and put her arms on her shoulders,”You’re going to be an older sister!” Hunter’s eyes widened when she heard the word”sister”. “Mommy,” she said and looked and Catcher then Painter really fast.”Are you having a baby?” Painter nodded. Hunter was son crying in joy and jumping around the house. “It’s going to be a girl too!” Catcher said and hugged his wife and daughter.”Can I name her?” Hunter asked. Both parents nodded.”Ok then,” she said.”How about,Stitcher?”
