**_Tutorial_**

If you craft a pickaxe you can find a cave and go down it. Wait, you can’t go down there yet, you need a sword! So craft a sword and go down there. You can find many things down there. Including mods. Mobs are deadly, they will attack if they see you. So that’s why you need a sword. So kill all the mobs and start mining, I mainly go for coal, iron, diamonds, and emeralds. _Tip-_ find diamonds and emeralds down deep by lava, and find iron and coal up high by the surface. So now you know how to mine.

Plant some crops on the line you made with the hoe and wait. Now you know how to farm plants. Now it is getting dark, quick build a house, get wood fast make a line of wood blocks four times. Build it higher and add a roof. Now craft a door and a bed and go to sleep.

So do you know how to play the game? I‍f not, there is more story right below this sentence.
