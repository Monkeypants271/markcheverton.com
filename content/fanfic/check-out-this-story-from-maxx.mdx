Gameknight999 (AKA Gameknight) sat down on the chair and logged into Minecraft. “Now let’s go to the Battle of zombies world” Gameknight said. As soon as he spawned he saw himself surrounded by zombies. “There are only a fifty zombies in total” he said. Suddenly, the phone rang. “Hello?” Gameknight called into the phone. “I am Entity303” a voice replied. Of course Gameknight was only playing as a player, not literally in Minecraft. “Really, Entity303 again?” asked Gameknight. “I thought all you wanted me to do was finish the fuzzy rainbow” Gameknight said. “I have another quest for you” Entity303 said. “What is it?” Gameknight asked. “I want you to find the Guardian’s Eye” replied Entity303. “You have three days in Minecraft to find it” Entity303 said. “You must go INTO Minecraft and find it” Entity303 added. “I will await you to complete my quest” Entity303 said. “If you don’t find the Guardian’s Eye in time we will have some destruction in the village” Entity303 said. Entity303 moved the screen to show Gameknight that there was TNT under the village and an army of mobs ready to charge at the villagers. “I added a few charged creepers for extra destruction” Entity303 said. “Go into Minecraft, NOW!” Entity303 shouted. Gameknight hang up the call and really quickly drank some water, tell his parents he was going to use the Digitzer, and then finally press the button to activate the Digitzer and go into Minecraft. He arrived in Minecraft a while later and put on the armor the villagers left him then he started his adventure.
