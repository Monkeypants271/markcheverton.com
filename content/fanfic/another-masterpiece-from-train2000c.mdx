Gameknight walked out of the blue box. to his surprise, village 12 was a village in a roofed forest.

“Wait,” said Gameknight. “there are no roofed forest villages.”

He looked around and saw villagers ant tree houses. The treehouses were basic rectangular shaped with wooden walls and a brick roof. Just then, an NPC walked toward him. The NPC was taller than villagers and the player, yet had 4-pixel player arms.

“Hello!” the NPC said. “I am Elondil, the leader of the village. I am an elf of the woodland realm from the LOTR mod.”

“Hi, I’m Gameknight, the User-that-is-not-a-user.” Gameknight replied.

“Follow me.” Elondil ordered.

Gameknight followed the tall elf through the dense shrubbery. Some iron golems were moving about checking for mobs. Elondil stopped at a wooden wall. The wall was taller than trees and was made out of dark wood planks.

“This wall keeps villagers in and orcs and monsters out.” Elondil explained.

Elondil moved from the wall and walked toward a fancier tree-house.

“You must go to the kingdom in order to fulfill the prophecy.”

He threw Gameknight a map. The map showed a path that led to a gray line. On the side, it said that Steve’s brother, Herobrine, will be free of the virus when the User-that-is-not-a-user brought the four elementals.

“The first elemental water. You must go to Atlantis to receive it.” said Elondil.

“What are you talking about?”

“I am talking about you freeing the NPCs of Minecraft.” replied Elondil.

“I’ll try.”

Elondil led Gameknight to the gate and told him to sit on the horse that was tied up to it? Gameknight left the village to seek out the elementals in order to stop the virus once and for all.

Meanwhile, in a dark cave, Herobrine woke up.

“Where am I?”

“In a cave,” sounded a voice in his head. “I need you to do a favor. Kill Gameknight999 and Steve. Stop them from reaching the prophecy!”

“Steve, no not my brother, I beg you, don’t do anything.”

“And why not? He was trying to destroy you!”

“I can’t, not him. I want to be free from you Virus!”

“And how can you do that if I am in your code? That’s right nothing! Now, destroy them or else, YOU will see entity 303’s power!”

Herobrine left the cave and entered a dark alley.
