The Minecraft Diaries – Secrets Under the Surface: Interrogation

(This story takes place after the three series from Mark Cheverton, followed by one unpublished book from FrozenBoy20)

She was tied to a wooden chair in a room with no windows.

There is only one source of light – a redstone torch, it emitted just enough light for me to see the outline of the dark room.

And enough light for me to see the fear in her eyes.

As I walked towards the women, the sounds of my footsteps echoed against the walls. I can hear her breathing rapidly out of fear, her blue hair draped all over her face, and drops of blood dotted her battle outfit.

What’s once a beautiful girl is now going to be a rotting corpse.

Soon.

Very soon.

I stopped in front of the women and she looked up to me. Her long, blue hair parted, revealing her bloody face. Her sky blue eyes that had once held hope is now lost in the midst of fear. Her hand has been tied around the back of the chair, and her feet struggled against the string that went around the legs of the seat.

“What the HELL do you want!?” She shouted.

“Oh, nothing much.” I simply replied, “I just want to ask you a few questions.”

“Whatever the hell you’re asking, I’m answering none of those.” She spat to me and I walked around her, observing her.

“Oh, that’ll be easy to fix.” I said calmly in her face, “I’ll just torture you over and over again, it’ll be a very, very long time until you die.”

And I laughed an evil laugh.

\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*  
The laugh echoed in the room.

The sound was so horrible it made me flinch.

As the laugher died off, I can feel my ears ringing.

“Okay, now I am going to ask you.” He said.

The air around me began to tense, as if waiting for something bad to happen.

“Where is the Polarite?”

\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*  
“I don’t know what you’re saying…” She said with a shaky voice.

“YES, YOU DO!” I bellowed as loud as I can, “You’ve SEEN the Polarite, you’ve HELD the Polarite. Heck, you even KNOW what it does. Surely, you know WHERE IT IS!!!”

My voice echoed loudly against the walls in the room.

“I’m not going to…” She tried to speak, but her shaky voice turned into a whisper.

“TELL M-“

“OKAYOkayokay…” She interrupted.

And I waited.

“FrozenBoy… he has it… he has the Polarite…” She said with a shaky voice.

“Is that all? He is the one who has it?” I asked.

She nodded.

“Well, thank you very much.” I said as I pulled out my knife.

Carried the knife towards her stomach.

Slowly.

“Thank you, WaterForest.” I said calmly in her face.

There was a horrified look on WaterForest’s face.

Dug the knife deep inside her.

And she screamed.

And I cackled.

\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*  
I heard the scream from my girlfriend.

And I cried in my cell.
