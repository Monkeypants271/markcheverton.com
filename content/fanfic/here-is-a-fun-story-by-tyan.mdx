As Gameknight999 ventured through the oppressive heat in the Nether with his NPC friends, his senses were on high alert for blazes, zombie pigmen and…

A loud shriek pierced the air. Ghasts.

Ghasts are gigantic white blocks with tentacles attached to them. They looked and sounded like they were in great pain, and could fling flaming meteorites hurtling through the air, headed directly for users.

“WATCH OUT!” Gameknight shouted, sprinting behind some netherrack to avoid the flaming meteorites that would explode on impact unless you knocked it back to them. Hunter, grippign her bow firmly, swung her weapon and hit the meteorite with a loud crack, sending it back to the unsuspecting ghast.

BAM! the monster was reduced to nothing but a few balls of xp and two ghast tears. Another ghast remained, angered by the loss of its friend. It shot multiple meteorites at the NPCs and the user-that-is-not-a-user. Instead of deflecting the shot, Gamekinght charged towards the beast. The ghast flew down to kill him, but he threw a bed down and sprinted for his life, back to the NPCs.

The bed exploded and the ghast was down.
