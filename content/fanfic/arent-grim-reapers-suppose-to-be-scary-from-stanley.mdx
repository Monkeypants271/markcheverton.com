“Of course she is!  She specializes in researching all about our world and yours.”  
“I’m sure she’ll be able to find out what we need to do with you.”  
“Yeah!  We should go meet her so she can check on you.  Let’s go, Human!”  
“Alright, let’s go.”  
“Are you sure you’re fine?  Does your body feel weird anywhere?”    
Now that she mentioned it I tried moving my body, but the response I got felt weird.  Almost like I didn’t know how to control my body right.  
“You’re right, my body feels foreign to me”  
“Do you need help standing up?”  
“Oh what is this?  Is Suisui flirting with the human?”  
“Huh?! What are you saying?”  
“It’s love at first sight.  How wonderful!”  
Susui turns a deep red.  
“Lov-?!  How could you even thing that?!”  
“There’s no need to be shy, Suisui!”  
“I told you to stop!”  
“You’re getting so red Suisui!  Isn’t she cute, Human?”  
The two girls looked at me.  One eager and the other flustered.  
“Ummmm…”  
“Yeah, she’s very cute.”  
“Of course she is!”  
“Why are you agreeing with here?!”  
“Because it’s true.”  
“That’s not what I’m saying! Just – !  Just get up and leave already!”  
“I thought you were going to help me.”  
“Fine!  I’ll help you, but just leave right away.”  
The apprehensive Susui helped me on my feet along with Neu.  
“Are you ready to go, Human?”  
“Yeah, let’s go.”
