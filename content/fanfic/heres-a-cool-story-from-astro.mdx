**Chapter 1 of my story “Life in Minecraft”**

Thanks to my friends for helping!

Hi. My name is Thunder. I live in a village named Xoria. If that name sounds weird, shut up, because it has a meaning. More on that later. So, I live here in Xoria with my mom, older brother (Sky), and younger brother (Cloud). So you see, my parents LOVED the sky. I mean, I like it as well, but not as much as them. There are some creatures in the sky, such as channalers, Aphantoms, screech birds, etc. they look very cool and have excellent uses that DON’T involves killing them, thankfully. My mom is a sky harvester, so was my dad, before he died. When my dad died, I was a baby, so I barely remember it. Anyways, you are reading this, and want to meet me, well, you will have to piece the clues together. Well, this is a diary, so let’s get on with it. One day, I was going to school. I was excited. Here in Xoria, there is no high school. Well, there is, but it is optional. Some jobs are very tough, so you need classifications from high school if you want a job like that. Me and Astro(my best buddy) are in 8th grade, a very tough year, but the thought that when we are done we can become warriors keeps us going. We both had an iron sword, iron armor, and a shotgun. We put the shotgun in our locker because our first class was close combat fighting. We were excited because today we were learning tactics from Darren the Destructor, a legendary sword fighter. We did not know that today was going to be anything but exciting, a day when we would lose everything. A day that we would almost get ourselves killed…
