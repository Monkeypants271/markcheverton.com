##  **The Surrounded Pike**

**Chapter One – Kidnapped** 

10:38 A.M Lake Michigan

The Northern Pike was born like no other fish. His name was Joe. He had black hair and brown eyes with a nice shaped body with purple spots clothes. One day a huge storm rolled in by the southern portion of Lake Michigan. It was violent!  

A moment later a tiny rock fell out of the cloud and landed in the water. Then Joe saw it and rushed over to check it out. He shredded it apart with his sharp teeth and 7 Piranhas came out.  None of the other fish knew what happened. The fierce Piranhas chased Joe. He ran away, but when he tried to get away the Piranhas swam over to Mr. Catfish’s house. Suddenly, it blew up. Boom, crash!!!! 

Suddenly Joe rushed over to see. if Mr. Catfish was okay, but he wasn’t! He was dying and Joe’s mom and dad rushed out to see if they could help Mr. Catfish. They brought him to the ER and they said he would be okay.

Joe woke up and was in a small room with a cot, a toilet, and a sink. He wasn’t feeling himself and he felt like he had butterflies in his stomach.  Suddenly this massive 50-pound Parana came to the small pike and he said, “Who is this guy?” 

Joe was so frightened that he ran to the corner and passed out for more than an hour. When he woke up, he was gone. This was Joe’s time to escape. He grabbed a large chair and banged it on the wall. Suddenly, it cracked and cracked until the old jail cell broke open. 

He looked out and the whole town was a disaster. There were piranhas everywhere and they all saw Joe and I took off after him.
