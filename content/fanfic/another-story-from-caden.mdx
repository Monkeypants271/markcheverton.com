He spotted some strange looking fish that he thought were aliens but when he told them that they looked hideous they told him that he looked like the ugliest animal they have ever seen. And he told them to take that back. They said never, that’s when they started to fight, then they saw the biggest animal they’ve ever seen in their lives. And that’s when the other fish swam for their lives. The monstrous fish started to chase the small fish, but there was no way they could outswim it, just that minute they were cornered in a small reef and were about to get eaten. Which gave him the chance to swim away. While he was swimming away he heard them scream and he started to laugh. (IDK why he laughed TBH)  
Part 2

When he found a planet that had life, he found the others so he stayed and told them about his adventure. They told him that they had found a terrified fish and he said he wished he had a friend with him on his adventure. He was looking for his family when finally he found a new friend. Then he found out that the other special sharks were his brothers and sisters. He also found out his parents died 🙁  
The end!
