“UGHHHHH!!! We’ve been standing here for hours!!!” Molly yelled. She and her friends, Luna, Vicky and Winter, were standing at a bus stop, in ninety-degree heat on a Monday. Molly was NOT pleased. She let her legs go limp and she collapsed on the sidewalk.  
“C’mon Molly, we haven’t been standing here for that long.” Winter said, rolling her eyes. “Yes, we have” Luna said. Luna was sitting on a bench with a black umbrella and a can of Snapple, head buried in One of Us is Lying. “It’s still worth it to catch the bus though”  
“This is my home now” Molly mumbled “could I have some Snapple, Luna?” “No.” Luna responded. Molly groaned. Vicky glanced at her phone. 2:56. We couldn’t have missed the bus, could we? The field trip started 15 minutes ago. Is this the wrong bus stop?  
“UUUUUGGGGHHHHH” Molly moaned, interrupting Vicky’s train of thought. Winter got on her phone. Luna’s first Snapple ran out, so she got another, and continued to read. Vicky was preoccupied with the maps, and Molly had fallen asleep, on the sidewalk. She and her friends, Luna, Vicky and Winter, were standing at a bus stop, in ninety-degree heat on a Monday. Molly was NOT pleased.
