Gameknight999 frantically looked around, he was cornered by Herobrine, he felt weak and was frozen with fear. //”This can’t be the end, I can’t be destroyed by  Herobrine!” // his thoughts screamed at him. The noise of the deadly battle faded  from him as he looked at Herobrine. “HA listen closly the-user-that-is-not-a-user” Herobrine  sneered, his voice full  of hate  and his eyes bright white and menacing “after everything you caused me I shall take my revenge” He chuckled angrily and drew his enchanted diamond sword. Gameknight999  tried to move but couldn’t he was shaking, he couldn’t die like this? Would he die in the real world to? Would he respawn? What would happen if he died in mine craft? Sighing he looked  down, he had failed all the npcs  who where fighting for him. A small tear  rolled down his blocky face as he looked up at Herobrine. Herobrine could tell he was scared and it made him grin, as he was about to pull the sword down, ending the users  life he stopped as a blinding light filled the area causing everyone, Mobs  and Npcs  alike to turn and see what was happening. Gameknight999 managed to catch a glimpse and gasped as he saw a user  appear, his black beard and brown shirt  meant one thing. “Notch!” Herobrine  stuttered struggling to keep a sneer  on his blocky  face  as he turned to face the user “we finally meet again” he growled taking steps up to the creator  of minecraft. Gameknight999 watched still not able to move as he wondered what was happening “We meet again?”he murmered  wondering what Herobrine meant. Notch looked up, not with rage or anything like that but with grief and pain, “don’t do this to minecraft” he finally spoke, his voice kind and ragged. Herobrinr  glared at Notch  his white eyes flaring “why not” he screamed at him  pointing his sword at the defenseless notch. “I’m sorry” Notch  finally responded, simpthy clearly in his eyes “I didn’t mean for it to happen all those years ago my…brother” He slowly spoke  looking straight to straight at Herobrine. Herobrines  gaze softened but only for at moment as he shook his head “Apologize all you want it won’t save you now” He snarled looking at Notch. Gameknight999 head  spun, “Brother?” he mumbled to himself. Notch sighed “Brother I’m sorry I didn’t mean for you to parish” he sighed louder this time “Please believe me I love you”. Herobrine froze old memories filling his mind, his face went blank, his face paled as if something inside of him was lessening. Notch took slow steps up the  know shaking Herobrine “Let me make it up to you” he murmered  “I was never the brother I should have been but I can be now” He promised. Herobrine  opened  his eyes  something was missing from them, like the hate and rage was lessened, his body quivered “I..I forgive you” he murmered looking at the brother he once lost. Notch nodded before placing his blocky hand on the know weaker Herobrine, before smiling. Herobrine  smiled back, it would take along time to get things back to how they should be but he felt better because finally after so long, He felt happy.
