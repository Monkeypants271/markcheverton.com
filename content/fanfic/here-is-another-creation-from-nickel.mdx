Stories of Minecraft’s past

1 :The Joining

Ninjastorm189 walked through the landscape out from the cold Ice plains and back to the savannah. He was surprised to find every villager with arms free to move,and some with something in their hands.

Ninjastorm’s jaw dropped to the ground. Then realized that that ender dragon jockey was actually a internet virus waiting to taste his blood. Then transporter’s went around on saddled donkeys bringing NPCs to the village.

He couldn’t believe it. Then he walked through the village until three villagers walked up to him. A Crafter,a Ironcutter,and a Enchanter. “What are you doing down in this village?” asked Ironcutter with a large Iron pick in his hands.

“This is my village I built myself a house here,” replied Ninjastorm. He was so shocked he ran 30 blocks away from the village. He was followed by the three villagers. “You are welcome to stay,” informed the Crafter “This village will help you save Minecraft,” The other two villagers agreed to help Ninjastorm189 fight of the internet virus.

That moment was the joining

2: The Great Zombie Invasion

Buster240 stared across the flower forest waiting for anymore zombie attacks. He knew the NPCs were too scared to face the zombies like they did in the The Joining. He eyed the forest knowing that’s where every zombie attack has come so far.

Suddenly pain radiated through his body as a golden sword stabbed through his body knocking him off the ledge. “The zombie laughed as he fell into a lake. Buster240 stared at the ledge of the plain and saw the zombies were heading towards the village.

Buster240 dashed as fast as he could back to the village.

He was beaten to the village,one zombie reached the door,but suddenly WHAM!!! The door was kicked open by a blacksmith. He was relieved then startled that NPCs were coming out to fight the zombie invaders one Iron clad zombie stepped in front of the invading zombie group.

“Ge-Zak orders every zombie to kill every NPC,” ordered Ge-Zak. The zombies were now more confident that their general was leading them. Buster240 pulled out his enchanted diamond sword and fought back against the green decaying creatures.

Soon he stopped to watch the Blacksmith and Ge-Zak clash it wasn’t long until Ge-Zak had devastated the blacksmith. He chuckled vilely. “Now Ho-Mun lead your group to destroy the village,” said Ge-Zak “Yes sire.” replied Ho-Mun. Buster240 killed two zombies before killing Ho-Mun.

Ge-Zak gestured at the zombies. Instantly they retreated.

That was part of the Great zombie invasion

The End
