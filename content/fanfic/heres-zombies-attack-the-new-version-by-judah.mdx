Zombies attack. The new version – 1

Watcher stood atop the tall village watchtower gazing out at the magical far lands far below him.  This was the edge of Minecraft and Watcher was proud of it. He looked down into the village and gazed upon it happily.  Just then Planter appeared. It was as if  Watcher’s day had been magically filled with sunshine and red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and purple rainbows. He gazed at her happily for a long time. Just then Watcher heard a rustling in the woods outside the wall. He turned around and for a brief moment he  saw a tall dark black shadow in front of him. Slowly he readied his bow and pointed it at the thing. steadily he drew back the string but then he stopped. It was probably nothing. Still Watcher was a bit Nervous and was glad for his shift to be over. When it was over he then went down the long cobblestone steps from the tower to his village. With a spring in his step he walked towards the training grounds. As he got there he saw fifteen young NPCs ready to train. Slowly he got in line. There were a few snickers at his appearance. 

“Ok. Everyone,” said the instructor, “time to get ready. Does everyone have a wooden sword?” 

“Yes.” Everyone said. 

“Good now get in line.” Then  he began pointing, saying who would fight whom. Watcher got paired with the worst boy in the village. With a smile he raised his sword and lunged. The boy parried and knocked Watcher backwards straight into a house. After about forty seconds of fighting Watcher had lost what was supposed to be the easiest fight in his life. With a sigh he walked towards a place where he knew he could never fail. Archery. Watcher was the best bowman in the village but everyone ignored that fact comparing it with the fact that he couldn’t fight with a sword. After about three tries with Watcher hitting his mark with each shot he continued home. 

He had barely set foot in the small but comfy home that he, his father Cleric and his sister Winger shared when Some NPC that Watcher had never met but knew he existed sounded the alarm. Zombies were in the village. And just like that the war that would send Watcher on a quest fighting zombies, spiders, skeletons, and withers began.
