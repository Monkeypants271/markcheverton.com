But Sphere was powerful too.  He could shoot dangerous blasts from his legs.  These powerful blasts could cause serious damage or possible death. Sphere could zap Cartanie and Ade to complete Allnie’s quest.  But he didn’t.

BONK!

Sphere had cut the middle of a bag a cantaloupe.   The goodness fell atop Cartanie, crackling across the flat sidewalk. Cartanie staggered. He sailed atop one of the un-cracked cantaloupes. Cartanie landed on the melon goodness…

SNAP!

…And snappily-snap.    
Cartanie, now alarmed began to run for Bowl Aquatic Center.  Sphere tried to follow him zapping all the way.  He couldn’t run forever!  
Soon Cartanie shot through the ticket booth.  Green dollars spilled out of Ade’s void onto the counter.  Sphere didn’t bother paying the cash register lady. Cartanie never saw her again.  
Cartanie had learned that Robotento is not waterproof.  Neither is his void.  This was what has killed Robotento.  Brave had put water in Robotento’s void to overmatch the technology marvel.  Sphere should be the same.
