Story Submission:  
Zombie villagers, they were all zombie villagers. He saw everyone every single last person in the village. He slammed the door shut and repeated over and over again “this cant be happening, this cant be happening….”  
But it was no use it was happening and he couldn’t change it, or could he. Gameknight soon had formulated a plan in his head. There was only 2 pieces and he had found out how to put them together. The first was how he had turned Baker’s wife back into a villager and the second, he was the king of griefers.

Shawny was watching the computer screen with his sister. So into chat he asked Shawny to turn on his add on items hack and quick.He directed Shawny on how to open it up. As soon as Shawny got it up Gameknight asked for 64 golden apples and 64 splash potions of weakness. When he reached into his inventory he got out the apples and potions. Now for the tricky part, getting the stuff to the villagers.

He opened the door and then quickly closed it again only letting out one zombie villager he quickly threw the potion and then gave it the apple, it was working. He repeated this till everyone was shaking back and forth. One by one the villagers returned to normal. And as each one did Gameknight greeted them and sent them up the ladder. The last one to come was Crafter he slowly approached him and whispered “how did this happen.”
