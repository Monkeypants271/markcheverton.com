One fine sunny and gorgeous day in Minecraft AJ and his brother Trever were sent out by their parents to gather supplies from the jungle nearby their house. Trever didn’t want to gather supplies but AJ encouraged him to join him.

Sighing AJ leads Trever into the jungle both with wooden swords, pickaxes and an axe each to help them collect what’s needed.

Groaning Trever looks a AJ, “Why’d we have to do this? Why didn’t they do it themselves?”

“Not sure but we have to do it,” responds AJ.

Entering the edge of the jungle Trever grows nervous.

“You okay Trever?” asks AJ.

“Yeah I just hope we don’t run into any mobs while we’re here is all,” says Trever nervously.

“Me too, that’d suck considering it’s our first time in the jungle,” AJ responds.

Silently the two of them trek deeper into the jungle. The further they went the denser the jungle got.

“I can’t see anything! Let’s go back! This is a waste of time!” shouts Trever.

“No! I don’t want t— What is that? Is that cobblestone?” says AJ in surprise.

“What? Where? Isn’t cobble only found in other places but jungles?” asks Trever.

Noticing the cobblestone they see it’s not a random stack that someone left, it’s a temple.

“Oh my gosh Trever! It’s a Jungle Temple! Let’s go check it out!” AJ shouts.

“Okay! But we need to be careful!” Trever responds.

Both carefully walking over to the doorway of the temple they gasp at the amazing sight.

“It’s old so watch your step,” warns AJ.

“Okay that’s a good idea,” Trever agrees.

Nodding they both enter one at a time into the temple. Looking around they see the wonderful structures built many years ago.

Whispering Trever speaks up, “I wonder what we’ll find here.”

Nodding AJ walks near Trever with his sword out.

“Oh! Look! A chest ahead!” shouts Trever.

“Be quiet!” hisses AJ under his breath.

“Oh sorry,” Trever responds silently.

Getting to the chest AJ stops Trever.

“Hey! Why’d you stop me?” asks Trever.

“Pressure plate, don’t step on it,” says AJ.

“Oh okay. Please be careful,” Trever says when he stops walking.

“I will,” responds AJ.

AJ starts sneaking around the pressure plate in front of the chest. Slowly and carefully AJ opens the chest from the side. Gasping he turns his head towards Trever.

“This is filled with good items!” says AJ excitedly.

Taking everything out of the chest and into his inventory Trever sees that the chest held four diamonds, ten gold ingots and a stack of redstone dust.

“Let’s go,” suggests AJ.

Nodding they both start heading out of the temple together. Once outside AJ stops to show what he’d found.

“We both get half of the loot,” says AJ.

Smiling Trever starts jumping in excitement.

After dividing the loot in half it suddenly starts pouring down rain.

“Great! Rain is just what we needed!” says Trever in anger.

“Let’s get home!” says AJ as he starts heading off towards home.

Making it to the end of the jungle the rain clears as quick as it’d come before.

“Well that’s weird the rain just stopped!” AJ says when looking at the sky.

“Who cares! Mom and dad will be so happy to hear and see what we found!” says Trever.

“Yeah! True!” happily says AJ.

Both of them smile at each other sprinting towards their home to tell their parents of the temple and the loot they’d found inside it.
