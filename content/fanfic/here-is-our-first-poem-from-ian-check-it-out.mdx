Time

Time ticks away endlessly,

An endless hour glass,

But time stops for no one,

Not even me.

Each second, a wave,

Washing away the years,

But time stops for no one,

Not even me.

I walk down a street, as time passes by,

I wave to him,

But time stops for no one,

Not even me.

\-lucifer darker nightshade
