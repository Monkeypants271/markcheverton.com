It has been 2 and a half years since Gameknight has not returned home to Crafter and his friends and Crafter had always wondered where he had went, nor what he is doing. But always had hope and faith to Gameknight’s return to the village. During the last 2 and a half years, Crafter and his friends made improvements to the village, with higher walls with at least 20 to 25 blocks high  with three blocks thick and in the three block gap, there was obsidian so that it is harder for any enemy to cross through, and a bigger village that is 3 times bigger than a normal average village like the rest, their village is so big, it could shelter up to 700 villagers or more. And of course, Gameknight Castle still stood high overhead, looming into the sky like a giant statue looking after Crafter’s Village, with the same blocks still decorating the outside of the castle with stonebricks and obsidian, with some blocks or gold, diamond, and redstone. since they had so much iron down in the crafting chambers, they used it to make giant Iron Golums, that guard the village. Crafter was so proud of how it was upgraded with two new crafters joining into the village with two new crafting chambers, Crafter could imagine how happy Gameknight would have been while he was gone. Digger walked up to Crafter’s side, with his enchanted pickaxe hanging by his shoulder, standing a bit taller than Crafter, and the two Red-haired sisters on the other side and of course, Herder with his wolves by his side. “Gameknight would be proud of what we have done,” Said Digger as he sat down in the shade of a tree, “He would be proud! But when will he be back? I’m starting to think he wont ever come back…” Herder said in a sad voice, looking down to his wolves, scratching behind their ears. “Be patient Herder, he will come back, i promise,” Crafter said, still looking at the amazing view of the sunset. The sun about to kiss the horizon. Suddenly, a loud alarm rang as someone banged on a metal chestplate over and over again, everyone running toward the other side of the village wall and Crafter and his friends could hear them crying and moaning and screams of terror came, like something terrible was coming, Crafter and the others ran down to see what was happening. When the got back to the village, Crafter tried to calm them but none of them did, so Crafter tried asking what was going on but no one answered back, just screams of terror. Crafter looked at Digger wanting a answer of whats going on but all he saw was a look of worry and shrugged. “SHADOW CRAFTERS!!” said Hunter as she was already on top of the obsidian wall, Crafter ran towards the top of the wall and found himself looking at 200 shadow crafters walking striaght toward them with monsters at their side but creepers weren’t there. How are they gonna get through these walls then? “Archers, get to the top of these walls and towers and get ready to fire!” Crafter yelled at the top of his lungs so he could be heard over the marching of the monster army and the screaming of terror, and they did listen for once. “Light crafters! Where are the light crafters?” “here,” a voice sounding like a melodic song from behind but sounding sorrowful, it was Grassbrin with the other light crafters. “Do you know whats going on here?” Crafter asked but no reply came back, just a look of worry but one of them spoke, it was treebrin and he was translated by Grassbrin has he spoke, “I think they want war for destroying their creator or the last time..” “Herobrine” hissed Herder walking up to the group. “I fear this might not end well” Grassbrin had said. Crafter told them to release the iron golums out into the field of battle. Crafter looked down at the army of the foolish monsters and shadow crafters and could see some of them had fear and most had the look of excitement and angry faces on their scarred faces.
