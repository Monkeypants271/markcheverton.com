The Spider as King  
By Abigail

One day King Endermen went out to the wild and saw a creeper. Then he said, “I’m  
stronger than a creeper.” So he wanted to fight and King Endermen came out and tackled  
the creeper. So, the creeper had Diamond Armor and the endermen died and the creeper  
became the king of the universe. And animals were not happy so they fought until a  
spider killed King Creeper and he became king.  
The next day, animals make a plan to make a shredder to shred the spider. So then they  
went to Post World War III and the plan worked perfectly. So the animals lived happily  
ever after.  
The End
