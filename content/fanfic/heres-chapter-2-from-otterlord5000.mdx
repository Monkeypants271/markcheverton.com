Chapter II  
The Otter

Ernest was an otter with a strong sense of will. He had a condition called the Bloodwrath. It made you partially unharmable… for a while. It also makes you extremely mad. You kill everything in sight. Ernest was a warrior. He was a member of the Animal Resistance Against Humans. He was on a mission. Ernest tossed a match through the gas plant window.  
The result was a truly monumental explosion. His mission done, the otter returned to home base.
