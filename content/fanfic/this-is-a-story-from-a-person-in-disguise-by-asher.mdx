**Chapter 1**

“This is awesome!” SamSungTheOnlySong said as he flew around on the Ender Dragon. SamSung had just hacked the codes of Minecraft so he could ride the massive dragon. He wished he were actually in the game but he reminded himself no one had ever done that before. Just then his eye caught a book his mom got him. It was titled ‘The Invasion of The Overworld’ and was about Minecraft.
