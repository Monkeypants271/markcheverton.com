## The Origin of Sonic.exe

One morning Crafter was building a village iron Gollem, but a zombie with many secrets tampered with it making it small, furry, blue, and evil with red eyes.  The Gollem instantly summoned Mario.exe, and Pac-Man.exe.  They attacked random users for long a time when finally one user fought back. His name is Entity 303.  He decided to go easy on it. He attacked with a Diamond Sword, eventually, after many long hours, he used the best tool in Minecraft the Owner Sword. He 1-hitted Sonic.exe. The others fled, and never returned.
