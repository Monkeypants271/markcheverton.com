Click, CliffFlyer joined the unfinished server that he found online. The server was not named and it didn’t even have an icon. CliffFlyer heard that the server is mysterious, so he wanted to try it out. Not bad, until he started trying to move,  there was extreme lag but he tried typing, it was easy and loaded as soon as he touched the keys.“WOW!” CliffFlyer typed, “So Much Lag”. Waiting for a response, CliffFlyer Suffered so much lag. About to rage quit he saw a response, “No, this is awesome!, your computer must stink” , Said a user named Jodo-dasmi. CliffFlyer pressed tab to see who’s on. Amazed no one was there, not even him! Confused he Typed “Umm???”. Too quickly a response said, “What’s wrong?”,  This time it was from enirboreH. ZAP!!!,  “OWW!” Screamed CliffFlyer!

Waking up from the injury, His hands Were blocky, everything was. Even… A sheep! Where where his parents?, he asked himself, now a bit scared. he noticed a huge difference! he could tell this place anywhere, he was in Minecraft. But he was also in a factions spawn to the server called Powered PVP, his favorite. He played many of the game’s, Factions, Prisons, Kit PVP, and many more. he knew where the nearest village was, one that wasn’t raided. He would Venture off to it tomorrow, but for now he had other things to do.
