By Awesomeclash11 star wars mod added

He walked up to the forest and pushed down the two levers to open the Iron door.

Awesomeclash11 and Bladeknight23 pulled out their enchanted blades and walked through the forest temple some user built.

There was a thump thump thump. “What’s that?” asked Bladeknight23 “I don’t know but it ain’t good.” replied Awesomeclash11. They marched forward to get a closer view at the jockey.

The jockey was Gameknight999. “Well hello boys.” said Gameknight999. “Hi.” replied Awesomeclash11. Soon more users came in the temple Sneakfour5, ALSkentucky1, and Enders Zheng burst into the room. “I will allow everyone to stay in this temple,” informed Gameknight.

It wasn’t long until POW!! A huge explosion rattled the room just as creepers broke through the cobblestone walls,and battle droids with blasters fired at the users

“Who added Star Wars mod?!” asked ALSkentucky1 “Me!” shouted Awesomeclash11 as he pulled his enchanted diamond sword while the other 4 crafted lightsabers.

The fight went on as the battle droids were being destroyed.

Soon all of them were destroyed. “Quick follow me.” said Gameknight999. The users followed Gameknight to a village with a cobblestone wall around it. “Where are we?” asked ALSkentucky1. “A village.” replied Gameknight999. Just as Gameknight999 opened the door the villagers were doing something very unexpected.

They had on clone trooper armor with blasters,and there was a hangar bay no horse garage.

Everything was strange. Crafter walked up to them breathing like darth vader through his helmet. “Crafter how did you get all of that?” asked Gameknight amazed “A user named Mr.Star wars.” replied Crafter just then red lazers fired through the village “All troopers to your battle position.” ordered Crafter.

NPC troopers fired through the walls while the pilots stopped the enemy pilots.
