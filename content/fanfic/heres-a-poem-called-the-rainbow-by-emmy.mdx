Pink  
The color of love  
Like a big fuzzy blanket  
Giving me a hug  
Red  
The color of blood  
Keeps my heart pumping  
So, I can live life  
Orange  
The color of Fall  
Cozy chilly breezes  
Keeping me bundled up inside  
Yellow  
The color of sunshine  
Bright warm rays  
Making my day  
Green  
The color of nature  
Leaves and trees  
Giving animals homes  
Blue  
The color of ocean  
Sun shining down on me  
Toes buried in sand  
Violet  
The color of flowers  
Beautiful colorful bouquets  
To show someone you love them
