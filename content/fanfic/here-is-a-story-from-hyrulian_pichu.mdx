Chapter 1  
Screech. An high pitched noise flooded the area as an dark slim creature fell onto his prey, then  
poof, the creature evaporated into a puff of smoke and a group of coins fell on the creatures  
victim. The warrior got up and took off his helmet, taking a deep breath the green armored  
warrior turned around to the one who saved his life. The warrior gave a thumbs up, smiled and  
put his helmet back on and went back into battle. The green warrior dashed into a group of  
enemies and started to slash the group of dark, slim creatures until there was nothing left but  
coins where the creatures had once stood.  
“Anymore coming?” The warrior asked his partner in green robes, who then proceeded to shoot  
an flamming arrow into the distance where a scream could be heard until it was silenced by  
another arrow that hit its prey.  
“Not anymore” the small yellow pokemon clad in green replied to his armored friend.  
“There are a couple more respawning” the pokemon said to his partner, who was turning around  
to the new board of enemies.  
A couple of hours later.  
“And that should be the last of them” the yellow creature had said to his partner, wiping some  
sweat from his face.  
“Nice shooting pichu.” The warrior had said as the small creature half his size walked up to him.  
“Ya, thanks serp. You weren’t that bad today” pichu responded to his friend in a sarcastic tone.  
The two friends had then proceeded to pick up the loot that their enemy’s had dropped when  
they vanished.
