Gameknight ran to the village and was shocked to see the villagers running in terror in the village. He ran through the gravel filled street’s of the village and saw their fear. A huge army of monsters was attacking the rear section of the village. There were mobs of every kind, Creepers,spiders,skeletons and of course herobrine. The vile shadow crafter had an evil sneer of hatred and malice on his face. Gameknight ran to confront the mobs,but hunter was calling him to the tower that sat in the middle of the village. He ran to the tower to find stitcher waiting for him.  They slid down the ladder in a hurry and replaced the block of cobble,then ran through the long tunnel to the room where he first met crafter. When he got there he found NPC’s running to minecarts and jumping in. He found crafter at the center of the activity yelling orders about which minecart to get into. Then there was a bang, and everyone froze.
