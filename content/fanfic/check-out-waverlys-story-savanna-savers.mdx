### **Savanna Savers by Waverly D**

Gameknight rushed through the savanna, leaving his best friends Crafter and Shawny. He raced through the village. His friends called him the user-that-is-not-a-user. He had saved the village from terrible monsters.”Come back!” shouted his friends. “Come back!” they shouted again. “Come with me!” shouted Gameknight. He ran through the village. A spider appeared. “Ahhhhhhh!” screamed Gameknight. He didn’t like spiders. The long fangs were as scary as the rest of it! Shawny came running to save Gameknight. They ran back to Crafter. “I almost  got eaten!” screamed Gameknight, “but Shawny saved me!” “Thank you,” said Gameknight, “That spider could have eaten all of us! We have to get back to the village!”

When they got there no villagers were there except them. Monsters were circling them. “Run!” screamed Shawny. Zombies were chasing them! Zombies, spiders, creepers, and ghasts.  
“Where did they come from?” asked Crafter.  
“The nether,” said Gameknight. “Malacoda must have sent them.”  
“Who is Malacoda?” asked Shawny. He had not been with them when they had to fight the nether king. “The nether king is Malacoda,” Gameknight explained.  
“Ohhhh!” Shawny said.  
“Run run!” screamed Crafter who had just realized they had stopped running.They kept running and explaining how the nether had lava in it. “I didn’t like the nether!” screamed Crafter. “I had to be trapped in a nether fortress!”  
“I didn’t like the nether either!” screamed Gameknight. “I didn’t know what to do without Crafter!” Monsters were circling them again. “Ruuuuun!” screamed Shawny. “I’m sure I wouldn’t like the nether either’” said Shawny after they had started to run again.  
“Terrible monsters!” screamed Crafter.  
“Let’s  go save the village!” said Gameknight.  
“They are back!” said a familiar voice.  
“Malacoda!” screamed Shawny.  
“Shawny! Come back!” screamed Gameknight. “Shawny!”  
Shawny was right in front of the horrible ghast. “Shawny!Shawny!”  
Then all the monsters came out. End, Nether, and Overworld! All the monsters circled them.  
“Oh no!” they all said together. “Not again!” Gameknight jumped and attacked the monsters around them.  
“Thank you!” said his friends.  
“Haha!” said Malacoda. Malacoda was joined by Erebus, the king of the Endermen. Gameknight also saw the Ender dragon.The monsters stepped closer.  
“Come back!” shouted Gameknight and Shawny.”Crafter! Come back!”  
The monsters stepped closer. “Attack!” shouted Malacoda. Gameknight looked around for Shawny and Crafter.  
“Help!” shouted a nearby voice.  
“Shawny?Crafter?”  
“Help! Gameknight!”  
“I’m coming!” shouted Gameknight. Monsters blocked the way to his friends. Attack! Crafter and Shawny jumped out of a house. Gameknight looked around. He was back home. His real home.  Back in his home in the physical world. He then realized…it had all been a dream.  
The End.
