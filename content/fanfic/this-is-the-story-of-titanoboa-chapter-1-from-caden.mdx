Chapter 1

There once was an ancient animal in a legend that was believed to be named Titanoboa who was also believed to be more than 1000 feet but everyone who had looked for it got lost in jungles where they were eaten by huge animals before they could tell everyone else about their trips. and more people went looking for Titanoboa each year that some people think millions of people went looking for Titanoboa. Then one day 400 mudskippers went to go see the Titanoboa and they told him to come to see the humans but not eat them.
