Guard gave Gameknight some chain armor and whispered something to Richard.

“That user isn’t like any other user I’ve seen,” whispered Guard in a low voice. “Take him to the other village. There he will speak with the crafter.”

“O.K., but what about the Uruk-Hai?” replied Richard.

“I’ll take care of that, just take him to the village!”

Guard went back into his tent. He reached for his sword and tossed it to Gameknight.

“Richard will take you to a near by village. There you will talk to the crafter,” explained Guard. “He might know what’s going on.”

“Wait, why can’t you take me to the village that you were talking about before?” asked Gameknight.

“It’s being raided by the Uruk-Hai. There is another one that is closer but not as big.”

“Shouldn’t we be going?” asked Richard.

With that, Gameknight stocked up on food and left Guard’s camp. An eerie glow off in the distance.

“I hope that that’s not the village we’re going to.” said Gameknight to no one.
