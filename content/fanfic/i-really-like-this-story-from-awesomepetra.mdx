Sonic the Hedgehog, minecraft’s hero

I awoke from my comfy bed in the middle of the night. I heard some pounding on my wooden door that sounded like a KNOCK KNOCK KNOCK. I knew that this was important, so I opened the door, still in my PJ’s. “Hello?” I asked. There was nobody that I could see besides for a few blocky zombies in the distance. I went outside to get a closer look, but I got really wet because the rain was hitting my fur. Did I mention I’m a hedgehog? Anyway, I was outside, but saw something I didn’t see before, a name tag. My eyes were blurred out from the rain, so I couldn’t see his exact name. I ran at my full speed to see who he (or she) was, and as I got closer, the thunder seemed to get louder and louder. ‘This decision is important, it can decide if I live or die. Will I talk to him?’ I thought ‘Yes’ and went right behind him. I said with a faint voice “Hi, I’m Sonic, who are you?” The mystery man suddenly looked at me, his eyes were a bright blue, and he seemed to be older then most players I’ve seen. I heard him mumble to himself about “Is this him?” and “It can’t be” for a while, and when I finally decided he’s crazy, he spoke in a real voice: “You need to know a lot about yourself” and then he walked away.
