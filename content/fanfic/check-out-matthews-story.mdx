So Mark Cheverton, you asked for a story for Dr. Bad guy vs. Man of Goodness. Well here it is. By the way this is not first in the series.

So one day, Man of Goodness and Dr.Badguy wanted some new allies but they could not find anybody. Then they remembered that when they were little kids, (Dr.badguy used to be Man of Goodness’s best friend) they had found a scroll where it said that this temple had three very powerful elemental birds, one was fire, one electric, and one ice. The fire was was called flarenza, the electric was one called Zapdos and the ice one was called Articuno but it was well guarded. So first, Dr. Bad guy went to buy something. “This is the ultimate weapon that no bird can resist, when I give it to them, they will be Mine! It is bird food!!!”. Man of Goodness also bought some bird food, “Those birds will instantly become my allies when I give them this bird food!”. But the thing was, they had bought different brands of bird food. Man of goodness had bought “ little birdies” and Dr.badguy bought “Super bird seed” So Dr.Badguy went with the mystery man and Man of Goodness went with Polar knight. When they got to the temple, Dr.badguy had took the right path, he walked on a pressure plate, and all of a sudden zombies came out! “Uh-Oh” the mystery man said. “Don’t worry, I got this.” Dr.badguy reassuringly said as he fended off the zombies with his double sided-spear. A few minutes later, Man of Goodness came. “Stay on guard, Polar Knight” said Man of Goodness. All of a sudden, the walls had started closing! Even worse, they had spikes

It looked like they were doomed, but Man of goodness saw a hatch leading downward. “I’ll try to open this said’ he said. “I’ll help” said Polar knight. Together, they made it down the hatch just in time. Then they got to the room with the birds, and so had Dr.Badguy. Man of Goodness lured them with his bird food, and Zapdos liked his. Dr.badguy lured them with his food, and Articuno liked it. All of a sudden, the walls started to crumble. “I’ll get you next time he said as he rode on articuno. “You’re lucky you got away this time!” said Man of Goodness as he rodd Zapdos. And they both went back to base, the temple crumbling behind them.

(P.S The names Articuno and Zapdos are from pokemon)
