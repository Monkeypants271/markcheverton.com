### CHAPTER 1

“Mom, when can I go play my computer?” Dakota  
shouted down the stairs at her mom.  
“When you learn how to do your homework!” Dakota’s  
mother Tammy said in a serious voice.  
Dakota Patriel is a 13 year old girl who goes to school  
at Husky M. Middle School. Dakota has trouble paying  
attention in school and her grades are very low. Most of  
the time she just wants to be at home playing games.

### CHAPTER 2

“Dakota will you watch Beau while I go to the store?”  
Tammy yelled.  
“Sure if he behaves.” Dakota yelled from her room.  
Of course like always she was playing her video games.  
Her little brother who is 7 always annoys her but she has  
to deal with him anyways.  
“You’re the worst babysitter ever.” Beau said as he sat  
on Dakota’s bed throwing her action figures on the ground.  
“Well you are the biggest brat ever so I guess we both  
have problems.” Dakota said like she didn’t care at all.  
All of the sudden Dakota stood up, slammed her  
hands on the keyboard and screamed “Dang it! I swear  
this guy is cheating he beats me everytime!”  
“Maybe you’re just not as great as you think you are!”  
Beau screamed as if he took her actions personally.  
Dakota started talking to her dog Bruiser after Beau  
stormed off. “Hey Bruiser do you think he is mad at me?”

### CHAPTER 3

“Roof Roof Roof!” Bruiser barked loudly. Dakota  
walked over to her computer, she turned it on, typed in her  
password and saw that she had a message. She thought  
in her head, Who would send me a message I have no  
friends. GameCheater559 that is who the message came  
from. Then she remembered that is the person who had  
beat her a couple minutes ago. The message read,  
Dear Gamer38, You don’t know who I am but  
tomorrow me and all the other gamers on Mixen are  
having a Game Reenactment on the empty lot off Meadow  
Pine Drive at 4:30 this Saturday. You are invited but you  
have to be the Girl warrior, Elemina The Great, if you want  
to battle all the other players and try to win the Golden  
Mixen Helmet. But of course you can’t beat me but I  
challenge you to try.  
Sincerely, GameCheater559  
“Should I go Bruiser? I don’t know this guy he might  
be a psycho for all we know. Or he could just be a selfish  
gamer who thinks he’s better than everyone else. I’ll just  
ask mom.”  
“ Mom are you home yet?” Dakota yelled to the  
kitchen.  
“Yeah I’m in the kitchen cooking the spaghetti.”  
Dakota’s mother shouted from the kitchen.  
Dakota ran downstairs into the kitchen. “Hey mom  
can I go to a game reenactment tomorrow at the empty lot  
down the street?”  
“I will have to come with you but sure.”  
Dakota ran back upstairs to her room and typed back,  
Dear GameCheater559,  
I accept your challenge only if I can be the Great  
Warrior Eloise of the Forest. But I would like to bring four  
people for other characters on my team. They will be The  
King of the Great Slug Palace, The Queen of the Insect  
Cave, The Legend Warrior of Herrencia, and lastly The  
Weapon Keeper of the Game. I will be there no matter  
what you say and I will battle in the reenactment so watch  
your back because I am not a friendly person.  
Sincerely, Gamer38  
I tried to act confident and scary but I’m terrified.  
What if I get there and this guy is amazing! I can’t beat  
him! He knows every map and skill in the game!

### CHAPTER 4

Right away Dakota started getting costumes ready  
and inviting her 4 guests. Jackie, Joey, Tommy, and  
Amber. Dakota’s costume was an old, cut up purple shirt  
that she wrapped around different pieces of cardboard for  
her violet armor. She wore hiker boots for her shoes and  
used an old plastic sword that her brother used to play  
with when he was little. For Joey’s slug costume, she  
would give him rubber dish gloves covered in dish soap  
like slime, a cape and king crown from her brother, along  
with a pair of boots. Amber would be the weapon keeper  
of the game so she would have a wizard robe, cool gloves  
and a headband with a gem on it. For Tommy, The  
Legend Warrior of Herrencia, he will have the golden  
sword which of course was plastic, diamond armor, and a  
mask. Jackie is the Queen of The Insect Cave so she will  
have fake wings, a headband with “antennae” on it, and a  
long cape.

### CHAPTER 5

Dear Dakota,  
I would love to come to your reenactment. I  
bet it will be very fun. Tommy, Joey, and Jackie would love  
to come, too. They want to meet at your house at 3:00  
Sincerely, Amber

### CHAPTER 6

I am so worried only 3 hours until the battle. At least I  
have extra people on my team besides Tommy, Jackie,  
Joey, and Amber. What if they quit because it’s too  
boring? They wouldn’t… or would they? I know they love  
games but what if they only like playing them not acting  
them out?  
Oh they’re here early. “Mom can you get the door  
please?” Dakota yelled.  
“Sure. It’s your friends Dakota.” her Mother yelled  
with question in her voice.  
Dakota ran down stairs as fast as a lightning bolt.  
“Hey guys! Are you ready?” Dakota asked them excitedly.  
“Yeah, we came early to see if you wanted to go to  
the sandwich shop across the lot for lunch.” Tommy said.  
“Of course. Let me go ask my mom.” Dakota walked  
into the living room and asked her mother, “Can we go to  
Eddie’s Sandwich Shop across the lot where we are  
having the reenactment?”  
“Yep I will be ready in about 5 minutes.” Her mother said.  
Dakota and her friends brought their costumes  
and slipped them on over their clothes of course her  
mother didn’t want to wear a costume. Then they  
walked down the street and walked into Eddie’s  
Sandwich Shop.  
They talked about what they wanted and agreed  
on the same thing. Joey walked up to the counter  
and asked the lady, “Can we get 6 ham and cheese  
sandwiches with mayo, lettuce, pickles, 2 bags of  
barbecue chips, 3 bags of potato chips, and 6  
fountain drinks please.”  
“That will be $33.54,” the lady said in the kindest voice.  
Joey put a 20, a 10, 3 ones, 2 quarters and 4  
pennies on the counter. Then he put $4 in the animal  
donation box.  
They sat at a table and ate their sandwiches. Once  
they were finished they walked across the street and  
practiced battling.

### CHAPTER 7

There he was, GameCheater559. Dakota was ready  
to claim her flag and win the prize. The rest of her team  
arrived and they all lined up and stared at the person  
across from them on the other team like angry bulls.  
Everything was slow motion in Dakota’s head, the words  
of someone yelling, “Ready! Set! Fight!” All of the sudden  
Dakota was charging full speed at GameCheater559! She  
had to win!  
She watched her teammates get hit and walk over  
and sit on the bench. But she also saw her teammates hit  
the other teams players. All of the sudden everything  
turned silent when a loud scream was heard and the  
words “Time Out” were yelled. It was Amber. Someone  
kicked her in the ankle. “It hurts really bad!” Amber yelled in pain.  
Dakota walked the limping warrior over to the bench  
and one of the teammates that was out handed Amber his  
ice pack. “Are you going to be okay Amber?” Dakota  
asked her hurt friend.  
“Yeah I’ll be fine but I can’t play anymore,” Amber  
said in a hurt tone.  
Dakota thought for a while, if Amber couldn’t fight  
then they didn’t have enough people to battle. Then she  
got an idea. She ran over to the bench and yelled, “Mom  
can you play?” Dakota pleaded with her mom  
“I would but I don’t know how!”  
“Just run towards the Golden Mixen Helmet and don’t  
let anyone tag you,” Dakota told her mom.  
“ Do you forfeit?” yelled GameCheater559 with a  
mighty voice.  
“No, my mom will take place for Amber, I mean  
Weapon Keeper of the game,” Dakota said.  
“Then on with the game!” GameCheater559 commanded.

### CHAPTER 8

Everybody ran at each other. Nobody really knowing if  
they were close to the prize. Then all of the sudden a girl  
dressed as one of the stealth ninjas crept up behind me  
and I watched her strawberry red braids fly up as she  
jumped and hit me with her fake sword. At that second I  
realized that I was out. Surprisingly my mom was only  
guarded by two stealth ninjas and a bug master but luckily  
Tommy, Jackie, and Joey were right behind her. They  
jumped up and surprised the enemy and my mom snuck  
right through and grabbed the Mixen Helmet! We had  
won! I couldn’t believe it. We left not wanting to brag but  
we walked to the ice cream shop knowing that we won the  
game!
