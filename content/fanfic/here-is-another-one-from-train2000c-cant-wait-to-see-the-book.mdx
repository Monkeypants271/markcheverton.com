It has been a few minutes on the trail and Gameknight felt his eyelids getting heavy. He leaned onto Mithril and tried to sleep but a voice kept on waking him up. The voice said: It is not night time, you cannot go to sleep.  
“Here,” said train2000c. “Have a golden apple, I crafted it a few weeks ago.”  
“Thanks.” Gameknight replied.  
Gameknight put it in his inventory and looked up. He noticed an NPC in the distance. It had a look of a player yet no server thread. Above its head, the words Evil\_Little\_Kelly displayed in red. Judging by the skin, the NPC was a girl. She had blonde hair with a ribbon of red flowers in it. She wielded a diamond sword that seemed to have an enchantment on it. The NPC turned around and charged straight towards Gameknight. Gameknight brought out his mithril sword and swung it striking the NPC red. Evil\_Little\_Kelly swung back but missed striking a blow on train2000c’s diamond armor. Train2000c turned around and pulled out his bow. It too was enchanted.  
“The Maker wants you!” Evil\_Little\_Kelly said. She then attacked again and again. One of her blows landing on Mithril.  
“Don’t worry,” said train2000c. “She’s coded to say that. She’s a Custom NPC made for the player to fight.”  
“Wait,” replied Gameknight. “Is, by any chance, there an NPC named Guard?”  
“Yes, he’s in charge of greeting the players once they join.”  
“Now,” said Evil\_Little\_Kelly. “You will be defeated.”  
“She’s not supposed to say that!”  
Evil\_Little\_Kelly swung three blows onto Gameknight. Gameknight blocked the first blow but missed the second one. Train2000c pulled out a hoe looking item and right-clicked Evil\_Little\_Kelly. In a few moments, she was gone.  
“What happened?” Gameknight asked. “How did you defeat her?”  
“Well,” replied train2000c. “I noticed that she wasn’t coded to say that line so I deleted her so that other players don’t get frustrated.”  
Gameknight looked confused but then turned away and followed the path.
