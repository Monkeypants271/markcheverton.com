One day a villain was walking around. He would walk around in the city. He thinks a lot of people are thinking, “What’s a villain doing walking in a city and in town?” It was dark and the sky was blank all he heard was the wolf’s howling at the moon. The villain felt scared all the time, and the villain was quite small.

He walked, he tripped, and fell asleep. While he was sleeping a wolf came and picked him up. When he woke up he was in a cave. It was dark, and he couldn’t see. He kept hitting walls. He bounced back and forth.

Then the wolf stood right in front of him. He kept backing up then he was trapped! A cage was over him, and he could not move so he just sat down and whistled. The wolf yelled, “Be quiet!” So he was quiet.

He figured out it was dirt so he dug and dug. Finally there was a hole. He slipped and escaped and he was free. With joy, he started walking back home.

The wolf said, “Where did the villain go?”

The villain hoped he would never get trapped ever again when he is sleeping, or he just won’t lay in the open where he can get kidnapped.
