It was a dark, scary night. Sven was about to fall asleep. Then suddenly he saw  
Phantom. Phantom has cold, dead eyes, with chains bumping against his body. Clank, clank,  
clank. He was calling the guards.  
Twelve hours earlier

Sven was arriving at work. He has blue eyes. Blue eyes…blue eyes like the ocean. Sven  
has beautiful blonde hair. All the guards make fun of him because he is scrawny like a twig.  
Sven sees things that aren’t real. Some of the guards boss him around. Sven thinks the things  
he sees are real.  
Phantom was sitting on his throne. The throne was black with Enderman slaves on each  
sides fanning him. His only pet was bat. The bat has fangs and long black wings. Phantom lives  
in a dark cave. He is king of the End. The cave is so dark no one even goes in it. No torches,  
no lamps, and no lights.  
Sven was walking home on an old road not knowing that this road belonged to Phantom.  
A carriage was rolling on the road. It had a window on the side, sparkling gems on all the sides,  
and black horses in front of it and back of it. Phantom walked out of the carriage. “Young boy  
what are you doing on this road of mine?”  
“I… I’m on my way back to my house for lunch. I must be on my way now, ” said Sven in  
a weak way.  
“Ok then Sven.”  
“I never told you my name,”said Sven in a confused way. Sven was about to sneak away  
when Phantom wasn’t looking. Then unexpectedly Phantom grabbed Sven and threw him in the  
carriage. Sven was on the floor of the carriage.“Ow,”said Sven.  
“Shut it Sven,”said Phantom in a mean way. The carriage was headed for his castle  
instead of his dark cave. Sven was sitting in the corner of the carriage. Sven was thinking I’ve  
got to get out of here. Phantom was thinking, he walked right into my trap.  
They soon arrived at the castle. Phantom was getting out of the carriage. Sven was  
being dragged out of the carriage by Endermen. The ender dragon flew over head. He was  
being held captive for four weeks. Sven was starting to be delusional. Phantom walked in said”  
Take me to your city.”  
“Never in a million years Phantom.” The crazy thing was Phantom was never in the  
room. Sven was talking to completely no one. Phantom was sitting on his throne thinking about  
a couple ways to scare him. While Phantom was sitting on his throne, Sven was running around  
in his cell like a banshee.  
Sven saw a little creature in the other cell next to him. It made a little Squeak ! Sven  
jumped back because he was afraid of it he didn’t trust it. He was even afraid of his own  
shadow. The creature walked in Sven’s cell. Sven was so hungry it looked like a little gizzard.  
Sven grabbed a stick and sharpened it. He used his teeth because he did not have anything  
else to use. He poked the creature and killed it. He made a fire to using two stones. Sven found  
some straw. He used that to start a fire.  
Then suddenly Phantom walked in. Before Sven could fry it, the slaves took Sven out of  
the cell. He found himself outside. Outside the castle there were dogs, wolves, and a large  
ghost. Phantom threw Sven back in the carriage. Sven kept his mouth shut.  
Phantom took Sven to the battlefield. Sven made a little sword out of a diamond. In the  
carriage he found a chest full of jewels. He made a chest plate out of diamond. Sven found  
some left over wood in the carriage. He made a bow and a couple arrows out of bones, stones,  
and diamond. He thought I can take down Phantom with this.  
Phantom was in a different carriage. They finally got to the battlefield. Sven was getting  
out of the dungeon carriage. Phantom was in the royal carriage. Phantom was thinking he is so  
easy to trick. He made some weak weapons. When Sven was getting out of the dungeon  
carriage with all his gear and weapons, Phantomś mouth hung low and his eyes were popping  
out of his head. “ Sven how did you make all the incredible tools,”said Phantom in a shocked  
way.  
“Oh I was trained in building tools for the guards. They were not the most intelligent in  
making tools,” said Sven in a very proud way. He went out to the field and said, “ Are you  
coming Phantom?” Phantom did not hear because he was sending a note to his ender dragon.  
Sven was also sending a note to the guards, village dogs, and the blazes that were on his side.  
The battle began when the ender dragon got there, and all the blazes, village dogs, and  
guard. Sven was taking the lead because instead of hitting the ender dragon with the sword he  
shot it, and the arrow was covered in poison. The ender dragon got knocked down in strength.  
One of the blazes got killed by Phantom. Sven snuck up to Phantom and stabbed him in  
the back. Things were going down hill for Phantom. The blazes, the dogs, and guards were  
dealing with ender dragon. Sven was taking care of Phantom. Phantom fell to his knees  
screaming in pain. “This is what you get for taking me captive and sending the most dangerous  
dragon in the land.” Before Phantom could speak he was already half way dead. Suddenly Sven  
was patching Phantom up and putting him in the dungeon carriage.  
Sven was made ruler of the village. Phantom was the apprentice for Sven . He changed  
a new leaf. Sven was a new courageous person. The guards never teased him anymore. The  
village was happier than ever.  
Two weeks later Herobrine showed up!!!!!!!
