## The Creepy Coach

### By Riley B

The new coach seemed peculiar, and it wasn’t  
just because of that third eye. When I first saw  
him, we were at practice when our new coach  
showed up. Mccoy stated, “Wow he looks so. . .”  
One Monday morning I was getting ready for my  
new school the Titans. I was not excited to leave  
Texas, but mom says that I can start a new  
beginning. I disagree, and if I catch mom smooching  
with dad that would be the last thing I saw again.  
Seriously I mean it people!  
Once I was at school, I made a new friend  
named Mccoy. He is on the soccer team and I like  
soccer and he wanted me to join because at recess  
Mccoy said I had mad skills. When we got to Mrs.  
Writesnitters class, I sat two rows behind Mccoy  
and we were partners for reading, math, language,  
and science. I feel like I can trust him more than  
my mom because if I talk to my mom, well she will  
get all emotional and she will get dad and make a  
big deal all about my problems, and if I talk to dad,  
then he will get his whistle and get all  
basketball-ish but, he does have his perks.  
After school Mccoy and I went to my house to  
study for our biology test.  
“What is the condition of the tigers life cycle?”  
Mccoy questioned.  
Then I replied to the question by saying, “They  
can die when they have a heart wound or they  
have a baby.”  
“Yes! But I don’t have any more questions for  
you Katy,” Mccoy answered.  
So I went to pick up a flyer for soccer  
tournament sign ups. It said that there was not  
enough players on the team and was in need of a  
new coach, but that part was crossed off because  
some guy named Phyllis Donkey signed up to be our  
new coach, which his last name is Donkey and that  
is accepted. His nickname is three-eyed guy so  
that must mean that the new coach has THREE  
EYES!!!!!!! Oh this is bad, very bad. I’m in the  
soccer tournament!  
Then I ran to Mccoy’s house right away to tell  
him about the new coach. Ding dong, . . . Bang  
bang bang! “Ok I’m coming, I’m coming,” Mccoy yelled  
angrily. When he opened the door he said, “Oh I’m  
sorry for yelling Katy.”  
“It’s ok Micky,(which is his nickname by the  
way) So what’s up with you today are you going to a  
fancy party or a wedding?” I questioned him. He  
didn’t answer so I was assuming that it was a  
funeral so I didn’t speak.

### 2 minutes later

After I went to Mccoy’s house I went home  
and when I walked in the door I got tackled by my  
big brother Sid. He pushed me on the couch and  
kept asking where I was and I kept saying that I  
was at the ice cream truck with the dog, but he  
didn’t believe me because one, the dog was with  
him all afternoon and two, because I was sweating  
to death, and I sweat when I am lying. If he  
figures out that I went to Mccoy’s, he is going to  
make fun of me and he will tell all his friends and  
his friends will tell my classmates that me and  
Mccoy are dating but he gets into trouble every  
time he does it. I don’t listen to him much but I do  
love to attack him in surprise, so I did and he  
screamed like a little girl.  
After I attacked Sid I went to my room. I looked  
at my pictures on the wall and thought of being on  
a national team and playing for, ding, ding, ding, ding,  
dong. Sid answered the door and shouted,”Hey Katy,  
it’s your dumb friend at the door.” I didn’t reply  
because he is being pathetic right now, so I came  
downstairs and saw micky whimpering with fear.  
“What’s wrong Mickey,” I asked him.  
“It’s about the the COACH!!!!!” he yelled.  
Then I replied, “What about the coach, lets  
talk outside.”  
So we went outside. “When we got to practice  
he was really mean. Meaner than our last coach,  
he’s like the Grinch bad,” he said. So I nodded my  
head and we went to the soccer field and saw our  
coach coaching football. We ducked down and  
watched him for awhile and when I saw him coming  
to get a drink on the bleacher we were under I  
knew we were going to be in sooo much trouble.  
Then Mccoy whispers, “I am glad he didn’t see  
us Katy. We were so quiet even if he had big  
ears.”  
“I’m glad too, if we got caught we would be in  
the alphabet or in soup, alphabet soup!” I said in  
fear.

### One week past

A week later I was in the tournament playing  
soccer with our horrible coach. We were in the  
second half 27 to 27 and my coach cheated and  
put a 8th grader on our team and in the last  
second we won lost.  
When they figured out we cheated the coach  
was arrested and we never saw him again.
