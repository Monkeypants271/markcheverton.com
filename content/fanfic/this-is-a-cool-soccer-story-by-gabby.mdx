## Soccer

The next day came and we had another soccer game. We didn’t win but we played aggressively at least. We got a snack, but I was sad that we didn’t win. We didn’t make it to the championship, but we played well. In the afternoon, everyone was relaxing and talking. We just hung out. I was like, ”Mom, what are we going to do?”

My mom said, ”I don’t know, just go practice outside with Emma and Alayna, ok?”

Next on our schedule was the aggressive Grenta Dragons. I wondered who was going to get hurt this year.
