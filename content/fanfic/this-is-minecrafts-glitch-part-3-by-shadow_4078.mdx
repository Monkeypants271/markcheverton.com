PART 3:  
  Gameknight opened his eyes. His head hurt. He sat up and looked around, rubbing his head slightly. It was dark and cold. He let his eyes adjust to the darkness.  
  He was stuck in a cage and had nothing in his inventory. Gameknight frowned, recalling what had happened a few hours before.  
  He had spawned in the middle of nowhere and something hit him hard, knocking him out immediately. He remembered seeing two glowing white eyes. But that was impossible. Herobrine is dead.  
  The sounds of bones rattling made him come back to reality. 20 skeletons were walking towards the cage. The door was opened and he was shoved out. The skeletons made sure Gameknight couldn’t escape.  
   He was put in another room, slightly brighter. Though it seemed like there was no one there, Gameknight knew something was about to happen.  
   “So,” a voice said breaking the ice “you’re the famous User-that-is-not-a-User”>  
   Gameknight’s eyes widened as he saw a girl with white glowing eyes, similar to Herobrine’s, seemingly appear out of nowhere.  
   “Herobrine?!” Gameknight exclaimed, wondering if Herobrine had possessed the girl.  
   Chuckling, the girl shook her head, “Not quite. I’m Glitch, Herobrine’s sister”.  
   Sister?! Herobrine had a sister?!  
   “Pick it up” Glitch snarled, pointing to the sword. The second Gameknight picked it up, Glitch lunged forward and attacked.  
   Gameknight found it hard to even strike her. Her attack patterns were different from Herobrine’s. Very, very hard.  
   In five minutes, Glitch had knocked the diamond sword out of Gameknight’s hands and had him on the ground with his HP low.  
   Gameknight watched in horror as Glitch raised her own diamond sword to make the finishing blow.  
   Then she stopped. Her eyes faded back to her normal blue eyes. Seeing, Gameknight on the floor she dropped her diamond sword as if it was made of acid.  
  She wanted revenge, but not this. Definitely not this. Her eyes turning white again, she helped him up then shoved him towards the door. He was caught by the guards waiting. They looked surprised. They had expected Glitch to kill Gameknight. They looked at her, questioningly.  
   “We might need information from him” Glitch replied heading towards the exit “Keep him in the cell with 24-hour surveillance”.  
   Stepping out of the underground lair, she sat down on the ground.  
   Somewhere, inside her, there was still a little bit of goodness left.
