The Shadowly Mobs  
When the infamous ShadowGeneral and the hacker Cooly1234 go into Minecraft for some harmless fun and games, it is not long until they find a ruined village, run down and destroyed. Investigating the scene, ShadowGeneral realizes that the attack was from not vanilla mobs, but mobs from a mod that he created, set out to fight the NPCs from the moment they were created. ShadowGeneral begins to investigate the behavior, and find NPCs telling stories about his mobs, doing things that they were never programmed to do and causing things they were never meant to cause. With his very own mod going rouge and convincing the vanilla mobs to join their cause, ShadowGeneral and Cooly1234 must stop them and find out what is causing them to act so unusual before their campaign of death leaks its way into other servers…
