Steve and Alex ran to Steve’s house to get some supplies. They both got a iron sword, a full set of iron armor, a bow with the infinity enchantment and a arrow then they headed out of the village with Watcher, Baker, Digger, Smithy and Stonecutter. The villagers with them each had a iron sword and a full set of iron armor. Then they headed to the hole Steve fell in and built steps down. Once everyone was at the bottom of the hole Steve opened the secret door and followed the tunnel until he was in a cave. He pressed the block sticking out of the wall then gave each of them a potion of invisibility except of Alex. Steve said “Alex you will make sure some of the skeletons see you but don’t make any noise. We will follow you behind with the potion of invisibility on us and attack the skeletons when they get close then when most of the skeletons are destroyed we will leave two skeletons alive then we will ask them if they know anything. If the first one doesn’t know anything we ask the second one. If the second one doesn’t know anything we destroy all the skeletons it and blow the whole cave up with TNT”. “Got it” said Alex. Then Steve and the villagers splashed the potions of invisibility on themselves. The secret door in front of them opened revealing a area with a lot of houses in it and skeletons walking around. Ten skeletons saw Alex and charged at her but as soon as they were three blocks away from Alex Steve and the villagers destroyed them then three more skeletons charged at Alex after they saw her. Steve and the villagers quickly destroyed the skeletons. After a few rounds of skeletons charging at Alex and Steve and the villagers destroying them there was only one skeleton left but the last skeleton left didn’t look like a normal skeleton. It was probably two times the height of a normal skeleton and it wore a crown that looked like it  was made out of golden bones. “I am the skeleton king and you are not allowed to enter skeleton town” The giant skeleton said. As soon as the Skeleton king finished his sentence Steve, Alex and the villagers charged at the same time and hit The skeleton king, knocking him to the ground. The skeleton king only had two heath points left. Steve asked The skeleton king “where is Herobrine hiding?” The skeleton king replied “he is in the nearest zombie town”. “Then how do I find zombie town?” Steve asked. “You find zombie towns in caves where lava meets water” then The skeleton king disappeared leaving behind his crown and his bow along with a few arrows and some rainbow colored experience points. “These experience points don’t look normal” Steve said. “I bet that Skeleton king was made by Herobrine” Alex said. “I don’t think we should collect those experience points” Watcher said. When Watcher finished his sentence Digger started digging holes in the ground then Smithy placed TNT in the holes then Steve made a line of redstone dust leading from the TNT they placed in skeleton town all the way to the stairs they placed to get out of the hole. Once all the villagers got out of the hole Steve placed a redstone torch next to the redstone dust then Steve and Alex ran out of the hole and headed back to the village to gather more villagers to find Herobrine.
