The battle with the blaze king

“ZOOM!”  
Upon reaching the nether,Gameknight and Fisher pulled out their swords and got ready to fight.However,there was not a single zombie pigman or ghast around!

“It must be the bandits or the blaze king’s fault” muttered Gameknight to himself.

As they wandered around the deserted nether,they saw footprints leading to a nether fortress.

“This place is creeping me out” Fisher said in fear.

“Me too”replied Gameknight.

Out of the blue, something fireballed him.It missed him by an inch.

“MUAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA” someone cackled.

It was the blaze king!!!!!
