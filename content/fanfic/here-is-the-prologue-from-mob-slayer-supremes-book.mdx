The Beginning  
AN UNOFFICIAL MINECRAFT ADVENTURE  
Written by:  
Mob Slayer Supreme

Prologue  
“What’s this?” said Hugo, as he played with his best friends , Gary,aka RedstoneMaster185,the brother of Tina, known on the trio’s server, mc.gameknightlegion.com as BedrockGuardian803. Hugo read the e-mail out loud “I, Notch invite you to a tournament in Minecraft tomorrow. A portal will generate on your computer at 16:00 Reply yes to this e-mail and you will be able to participate… Like anyone would believe that! Probably some hacker messing with us. Right guys?” “Yeah!” “Totally!” Gary and Tina said in unison. “Even so, I’ll type yes just for the heck of it. Can you two be over at my place tomorrow at 15:00?” asked Hugo. “Sure!”, said the twins. Suddenly, RedstoneMaster185 started stammering “Creeper creeper creeper creeper breach!” “Where?” Hugo asked, brandishing his diamond sword. He rushed down the steps of Redstone Tower.  
The creeper saw MobSlayerSupreme and rushed up the steps on the stairs with its stubby green little legs. It started to hiss and expand.  However, it didn’t know that the sword Hugo was carrying was enchanted with Knockback, and got struck out a to-be window, plummeting to its doom. “That settles it. I’m never going to place another block for this castle without doing a border patrol everyday!” Hugo exclaimed vehemently. “Chill, bro.” Gary reassured him. “Remember the time when you tried to build Blitzwinger’s house in Yolocraft season 6 and left the door open and without putting ANY torches in it?” “It’s coming back to me now, when you mention it.” Hugo said with a grin. The trio chuckled at the memory of Hugo’s shocked face when he discover his home was occupied by seven creepers, five zombies, eight skeletons, three spider jockeys and two spiders. It took everyone in their server to exterminate the mobs. Back then it wasn’t as famous as it is now, so there were only twenty-something people at the time, and practically a quarter of them were noobs. “Hugo, we’ve enough cobble to fix the wall now.”  Gary reminded him. “I’ll be right there.” said Hugo as he sprinted down the stairs with Tina, eager to finish his creation.

“And… finished!” Hugo let out a deep breath after he placed the final block, afraid a baby zombie would rush through the gap at anytime and give him a heart attack. “Now all we have to do now is fortify the castle with TNT Cannons, traps and the rest of the artillery. Then, Fortress Notch 2.0. will officially be completed!”(Fortress Notch 1.0. was what brought them to fame as the LightCrafter\_Alliance on Youtube.)                                                                          “Hooray!” the brother and sister yelled and typed in chat.
