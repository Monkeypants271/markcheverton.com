“The attacking army was starting to close in on Jackson’s party when suddenly someone yelled, “HELP THAT GROUP!” Coming to a stop, the army general turned around, and fear replaced confidence as another militia joined the fray. “RETREAT!” The army general yelled. As the army started retreating, the other army closed in on them. Panicking, the attacking army tried swinging their swords at the new army, but their swords were quickly batted out of their hands, leaving them defenseless. Someone from the attacking army drew a second sword and hit someone from the new army. This caused everyone from the new army to fall on the individual who hit someone, causing him to disappear with a pop, leaving a set of iron armor and a diamond sword. “We surrender,” The leader of the attacking army said. “Someone take the diamond sword that the individual dropped,” Jackson commanded. Someone quickly walked up and took the diamond sword, then walked back and put the sword in his inventory. “Why did you try to attack us?” Jackson asked. “We were told to come out and harass anyone who might be looking for people that need help.” The leader replied. “Tell your boss that if he wants to try and stop us, he’s more than welcome to,” Jackson said. “Let them go, they’re defenseless.” The two armies backed up, letting the attacking army retreat. “Thanks for helping us,” Jackson said. “You’re welcome,” The leader of the new army said. “My name is James.” “My name is Jackson,” Jackson replied. “How did you know we needed help?” He asked. “We started looking for any of our friends and family that spawned somewhere in the world and anyone who may have needed our help,” James replied. “We were looking in this area for people when we saw your group running from the attackers,” James explained. “We decided to help you since you were outnumbered and might not have known how to deal with them.” “So what are you going to do now?” Jackson asked. “We should stay together, find people we are looking for, and then who knows?” James replied. “We have a small community about a day away from here,” Jackson said. “I think we were near a forest biome, maybe the Sunflower plains.” “Nice.” James said. “How many people are in your community?”

“I Don’t actually know,” Jackson replied. “I haven’t taken the time to count.” Walking back to the unified army, Jackson and James told their groups that they were now one big army. Many people on both sides sighing with relief. “Alright everyone, let’s continue our search.” Jackson said. Nodding their heads, everyone in both groups continued walking into the unknown, looking for anyone that may need help. Walking up to Jackson, Max tried to talk to Jackson, but was interrupted by James. “Do you know what biome is after this one?” James asked. “No,” Jackson replied. “I think there could be a woodland mansion in the next biome though.” “So if it’s true, then we should raid it in case we find anything valuable.” James replied. Jackson nodded his head, then looked straight ahead into the upcoming dark forest biome. “Jackson, should we stop and break camp soon?” Max asked. “No, we’re going to the woodland mansion, seal ourselves in, then take out all of the Vindicators.” Jackson replied. Jackson then turned straight and continued walking ahead to the upcoming dark forest biome.
