As Gameknight999  went through the grassy biome he knew he wasn’t safe, he knew Herobrine would find him very soon.

“I need to find a village and fast” he said out loud to nobody but his voice echoing throughout Minecraft.

Checking his inventory he found a couple pieces of wood and six pieces of iron, suddenly a loud boom came from lightning echoing all around him he knew Herobrine was here for him, Herobrine suddenly used his hacks and summoned a giant, Gameknight999 looked at the giant he thought they were taken out of the game

Why are you doing this Gameknight999 shouted Tell me! his voice booming like the giant’s loud thumps as he charged at Gameknight999, Gameknight was filled with so much anger he jumped as high as he could and struck the giant and killed it in one hit.

“I will get you User-that-is-not-a-user and you wont win just wait soon you will meet your fate’ Herobrine cackled.
