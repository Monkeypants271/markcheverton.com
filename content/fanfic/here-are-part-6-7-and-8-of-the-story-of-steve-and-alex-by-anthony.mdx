Part 6

Steve and Alex headed deeper into the secret passage. “I found a chest!” Steve said. “It might be a trapped chest” Alex said suddenly Woodcutter joined them. “Let’s just open it” Woodcutter said then he opened the chest. “NOOOO!” Alex said. Woodcutter opened the chest halfway then he stopped. The sound of pistons activating was everywhere suddenly all the blocks around them disappeared, revealing hidden command blocks and lava all around them then arrows started flying around them, blocking any escape. Steve could see the villager that fell into the hole was standing on a two by two platform of cobblestone fifty five blocks away from them, surrounded by arrows and lava. Steve, Alex and Woodcutter were standing on a three by three platform. Woodcutter got hit by five arrows, losing half of his health points. Alex got hit by nine arrows, taking serious damage and only having two health points left. Steve was standing in the middle so he didn’t get hit by any arrows. Steve could see the villager that fell into the secret passage get hit by two arrows, having nine health points left. “We need to get out if here” Steve said. After thirty seconds the dispensers shooting the arrows shifted, hitting Woodcutter once, Alex once and the villager that fell into the secret passage once. Now Alex only had half a health point left and Woodcutter had four heath points and half a health point left and the villager that fell into the secret passage had eight and a half health points left. Steve gave Alex an apple and a piece of bread. Alex ate the apple and the piece of bread and got three health points back but a arrow hit her. Now Alex had three health points left. Suddenly the dispensers stopped shooting arrows and the dispensers started shooting buckets of lava instead, slowly burning down the platform they were standing on. The villager that fell into the secret passage managed to use a elytra and fly out of the secret passage, only losing five health points. The villager that fell into the secret passage ate some food and quickly restored his health. “Thats it!” Steve said. “We can use a elytra to fly out of the secret passage!”. “But I don’t have enough health points to use a elytra” Alex replied. “Hang on!” Steve said then he grabbed Alex and opened up the elytra and carried her under him, providing cover from the buckets of lava. Woodcutter shot out from behind on his elytra, flying as fast as he could. Woodcutter quickly got out of the secret passage, eating some food to restore his health. Steve was taking serious damage so he gave Alex a shield and he gave her the elytra then he let go and fell down into the lava. Alex flew out of the secret passage, using the shield as cover. She quickly restored her health points by eating some raw chicken and a few cookies along with a whole cake, then she realized Steve was missing. Steve must have let go and gave me the elytra Alex thought suddenly Steve came flying toward Alex. Steve landed next to her and ate some food then he told Alex what happened. “I used a totem of undying and I survived. I had an extra pair of elytra and I used it to fly out of the secret passage” Steve said. Then they walked out of the woodland mansion and continued on their journey to find Herobrine.

Part 7

Steve and Alex headed out of the roofed forest biome with the villagers. “I see something on the treetops” Watcher said. “and I hear zombies” Woodcutter said. “How far are the zombies?” Alex asked. “I see ten zombies heading right towards us!” Watcher said. “I see that the things on the treetops were spiders and those spiders are about to jump on our heads!” Woodcutter said. “We’re under attack everybody run!” Stonecutter shouted. “I bet some TNT will do it” Builder said. Digger dug a few holes and Builder placed some TNT in them. Steve ignited the TNT then they ran. BOOM! a group of TNT exploded. BOOM! another group of TNT exploded. BOOM BOOM! two more groups of TNT exploded. “Two zombies and one spider left” Watcher said. Boom! one block of TNT exploded. BOOM! BOOM BOOM BOOM! four blocks of TNT exploded. BOOM BOOM! six blocks of TNT exploded. “No monsters left” Digger said. “I see six cave spiders heading our way!” Watcher said. “Good thing I brought twenty buckets of milk” Steve said. Alex quickly destroyed five of the cave spiders but the last of bit her twice then Steve destroyed it for her. “Need some milk?” Steve asked. “I’m good” Alex said. They finally got out of the roofed forest biome then they entered a swamp biome. “That two hits from the cave spider still hurts” Alex said. They sneaked past a witch’s hut then they destroyed a few slimes trying to attack them. “I see a cave” Steve said. They entered the cave and saw Herobrine lying at the end of the cave. They walked to Herobrine then Steve and Alex lifted him up and threw him into a pit of lava and they ran away and closed up the entrance to the cave with obsidian then Steve, Alex and the villagers headed back to the village.

Part 8

Steve and Alex walked with the villagers back to the village. They passed the swamp biome, the woodland mansion, the hole Steve fell in, then finally they reached the village. Watcher went to the watch tower, Steve went to his house, Alex built a new house out of cobblestone next to Steve’s house, then the other villagers went to their houses. “Thirty zombies, ten creepers, seventeen husks, eight charged creepers, two pillagers, and three vexes coming!” Watcher shouted. “Everyone abandon village!” Digger and Stonecutter shouted. “Go to the minecart system Steve and Stonecutter built!” Digger shouted. Steve collected the crops from the farm and took all his items from his chest and took his furnace and his chest. “Everyone take apart your homes and when you’re done start taking apart the defense!” Stonecutter shouted. In a few minutes everyone took apart everything and they quickly went to Steve’s minecart system and closed up the entrance. Steve collected the stone stairs while everyone walked down to the mincart system. “We have all the houses taken apart and the TNT from the traps are all with us” Woodcutter said. Steve passed out the crops to everyone then he took apart the stairs and filled in all the gaps with dirt. Everyone got onto a minecart while Steve and Alex took apart the rails. Steve and Alex got onto a minecart then they followed the villagers to the next village. The minecart system went up then arrived at the the surface of the village. Steve took apart the minecart system with Stonecutter. “This is the farthest the minecart system goes” Steve said. They built a defense around the village then they all built their houses. “Thirty zombies coming!” Watcher said. “Swordsmen and archers ready!” Watcher shouted. “Swordsmen attack!” Stonecutter shouted. “Archers open fire!” Watcher shouted. After two charges from all the swordsmen and three rounds of fire from all the archers they destroyed the zombies. “No injuries from the zombies” Digger reported. “The coast is clear” Watcher said. It was nighttime so everyone went back to their houses. “See you tomorrow” Alex told Steve then they all went to their houses.

Part 9

Steve, Alex, Stonecutter, Woodcutter, Digger, and Watcher traded with the other villagers. Steve traded a enchanted apple for a diamond sword enchanted with the sharpness enchantment. Alex traded two ender chests for a eye of ender, a ender pearl, and two golden apples. Stonecutter traded three slabs of stone and one block of stone for an iron sword. Woodcutter traded two sticks and seven blocks of wood for a leather helmet. Digger traded two gold ingots and a stone sword for a golden sword. Watcher traded a block of gold and a compass for a clock. Baker came and asked “can I trade with you?”. “Sure” Alex replied. Baker traded a cake and a piece of bread for a iron sword. Fisher walked toward them and asked “can I trade this iron ingot and these two gold ingots for a iron sword and a shield?”. “Sure” replied Watcher. They searched the area around the village for any monsters. All they found were a few spiders that they quickly destroyed. Well, for sure we aren’t one of those monsters under his command” a cave spider said. “We worked so hard to find a village to destroy and now we are leaving without destroying a single block?” a enderman said. “That’s just wasting creepers” a slime said.
