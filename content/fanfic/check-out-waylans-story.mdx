There once was a castle that stood in a forest. UnSeen Don’t Run  
stood in the tower wondering what was out there. He adventures for  
miles, all he found was an ocean. The air filled with ocean saltiness. He  
saw a peculiar glow getting closer and closer and then suddenly armored  
squids shot out of the water. He called the warriors to battle, slicing his  
sword like an arrow.  
By the time the warriors got there, he already killed the squids. They  
took a huge breath and went under water. There was a huge drop-o , they  
went lower and lower until they could barely breath. Gurgling and  
gurgling, then they entered a big coradul. The gang walked and walked  
their legs were as tired as a panda. Finally they met the boss. He was so  
scared he ran back so fast they couldn’t even see him. He waited for hours  
and hours and didn’t hear a thing.  
There were rumbles, he could barely stand up. King Kumba said,  
“RETREAT!” Zaky was too strong to defeat, they NEVER EVER would beat  
him.  
Part 2  
Meanwhile Zaky is thinking what his next plan is. “I GOT IT!” Zaky  
screamed. “We will attach their base, so grab your weapons mates!”  
They put a tracking device on the troops. They knew exactly where they  
were.  
At the surface they started to travel to the castle. On their way they  
heard laughing and fireworks, it was a party! Zaky busted in and  
attacked.  
King Kumba was so surprised, he couldn’t react! The warriors were  
CRAZILY shocked. “Was this supposed to happen?” Blurted one of the  
warriors.  
“No.” King Kumba declared. Let’s FIGHT!  
There is no I in team. Running with their swords up right at each other,  
all but UnSeen Don’t Run. He ran up the tower and watched.  
Three hours passed by they were all tired. They agreed we shall battle  
again. Zacky and his troops headed home. King Kumba cleaned the mess  
up and shared the land.
