## The Sad Origin of Entity 303

**Told by: Entity 303**

“Run. He’s gaining on us,” screamed the villager, and he tripped and fell over a tree root. The other villager ran into a cave and said, “I should be safe here.” I appeared out of the shadows and said, “Never assume you’re safe.” The villager fell back in terror and said, “Just leave me alone, you monster.” “My name is Entity-303, you villager,” I yelled. But then I paused and said, “You know, I wasn’t always this way.” I said, “Let me take you, show you how it all started. Let’s go into my past, you will see it began”

“I was kind, generous, super helpful. It was a normal day. I was making trades and gathering resources. I also took a trip to the Nether to get potion supplies.” I told him and continued with my story. “After I filled my inventory, I headed back to my hometown. I arrived and gave the townsfolk what they needed. I headed home, I lived there by myself. Mostly, because my family went on a mission to free the End from the dragon. I made myself dinner and headed to bed. 

Soon, I woke up to the sound of explosions. When I went outside and that is when I first saw him. His eyes were pure white, he controlled the undead, he lit houses on fire, and he called himself Herobrine. I ran into the house, grabbed our family’s Sythe, sword, and my father’s experimental power serum, and ran into the woods.” I paused and let that sink into the villager’s brain and continued, “I lost everything, my house, my friends, my family, and my hometown village. I soon forgot my name, and my purpose, and traded my clothes for a robe. I was dead, a literal glitch. I learned to summon fire with my fist. I soon made the moon and its eclipse was now one of my sources of power. I soon declared myself an Anti-Hero. I also made an oath to hunt Herobrine, the undead hero, down and destroy him.”  

“So, now you know my story. You know why I am doing this.” I said. The villager rose and said, “I am so sorry, for what happened to you.” “Thank you,” I said, “Now where were we, Oh right, you better run.” Let the hunt for Herobrine, his minions, and his followers continue.
