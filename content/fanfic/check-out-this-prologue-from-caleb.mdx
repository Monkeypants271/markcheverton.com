PROLOGUE

Kor-Hunt, the next zombie elected king after Xa-Tul’s demise, was attacking a village head on. He didn’t know which village, and he didn’t care. He was just about to scream his victory when a flaming arrow struck him in the shoulder. Instead, he screamed with fury and pain. He saw a user on the wall, but it had no server thread. And then Kor-Hunt charged back into the forest, and then he was gone.
