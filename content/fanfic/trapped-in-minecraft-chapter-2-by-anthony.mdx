It ended as soon as it started. The world around me slowly turned from black to green and brown. I looked around. Everything was pixelated. Tons of blocky oak trees surrounded me. I looked at myself and realized I was also pixelated. I was actually in a Minecraft forest and I was actually in Minecraft?!! Then I remembered that trick Gameknight999 did when he got sucked into Minecraft. He focused on his keyboard back home, and he was actually able to see what it looked like in the physical world, so it should work the same with me. But first, I need to gather some wood. Right as I finished chopping down my third tree, I heard something that sounded like an enderman teleporting. Wait, it could actually be an enderman! I turned around, ready to fight, only to find my sister’s Minecraft avatar staring back at me! “Wait, you’re also trapped in here?” I asked her. “Yeah, I saw the reality mode screen on your computer, so I supposed it would be something cool.” “Wait, what were you doing in my room?!” I shouted. “I clearly told you not to disturb me!” “Oops, sorry” she replied, but she looked like she didn’t mean it. Just then, I remembered something. A few days ago, I overheard my brother talking about pranking me! He could be heading to my room right now! And he would also get sucked in! I closed my eyes and thought of my room. But it was too late. He selected enable reality mode. Poof!
