### **Music Of The Wind**

By:Madelyn C.

Prologue

Hello, my name is Marianna Khelsey. I am 17 years old, and I go to the college of Natary University in Texas.

I love music, music is my everything. I’ve been listening to music my entire life, and I love all types of music. Country, Hip Hop, Rock, Metal, Jazz, and many more, but my favorite in particular is anything on a music box. I’m also a writer, what do I write? I write music of course.

I’m in a small group with six other people. my best and closest friends. They are, Karen Marlona, Haden Sary, Natasha Hemfire, Evylynn Barim, Sionatta Verilyn, and Carson Mist.

We’re all actually in our own acoustic band (and  it’s a pretty good one too, don’t mean to brag) called ‘_The Sound Of Sparks’_. We all sing, I play piano, Karen and Evylynn play guitar, Carson plays drums, Sionatta plays the violin, Haden plays xylophone, and Natasha plays the harp.

We’ve been doing this since elementary school, and man have we gotten good over the years.

We all met in third grade and started the band two years later, we’ve all been best friends ever since. We all went to the same schools all of our lives, and by some miracle, all ended up in the same college together. Wow what a stroke of luck.

Life was going great until that one awful day.

“Girls!” he said shakily. I shot up out of my seat, immediately knowing what was going on. After I shot up I almost yelled the name, “The Federal Community Eradication Association!?”

Then I noticed Sionatta and Karen copied me right after, and Sionatta said sternly, ”the business that wipes out cities for money?!”

Then I was surprised Karen did it too (since she’s the quiet one) and she yelled, “and all that money goes to that shady business man, Charleston Black!”

The mayor was shot a bit back in his chair. Then we exchanged glances knowing that what we did was disrespectful, so we sat back down and apologized. So I did want to let you know that our city will be wiped out, so I was wondering, if you and your band could perform at our ‘farewell’ party.”

“I’m sorry, but there’s nothing else we can do,” he said.

So we un-happily agreed to perform. On our way back I thought, ‘who knew our butterfly song would be our last, because for all I know, once this happens, I’ll never see my friends ever again.’

Chapter 3: Apples and Apologies

I thought about my older brother, Dakota and how we got along so well, until one day he ran away from home and how my father passed away due to cancer. That music box means the world to me, because that box was given to me by Dakota on his eighteenth birthday. Can you believe it? It was his birthday and he gave it to me, well I guess you now know why I love music boxes so much.

Sometimes I look at it and wonder if he’s alive somewhere out there, looking for me.

I walked over to my desk where the box was sitting and I wrote another wish, _‘I wish my brother Dakota was alive and looking for me’_ I wrote down. I turned the little spicket on the side and opened the box, I then sang along with the song that was playing, “You are my sunshine, my only sunshine, you make me happy when skies are grey.”

I thought about how Evylynn and I were so close but I never really considered her a sister because what we had was never like what I had with Dakota. To this day I still wonder what I ever did to make her hate me so much, because it was _her_ idea we keep our story a secret from all of the others, and make it seem like we’re just friends.
