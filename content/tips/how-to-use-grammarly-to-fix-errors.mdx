Hi everyone, 

Below, you’ll find a video showing how easy it is to use Grammarly to fix errors in your stories. Grammarly won’t find all your errors, but it will find a lot. 

I use Grammarly on every chapter when I’m writing. When I’ve finished my book, and I’ve gone back and rewritten each chapter 3 or 4 times, I’ll use Grammarly to catch my errors. This is important because people will enjoy your story more if there are fewer errors. After I use Grammarly, I then read my story out loud to myself. This will let me catch the last few errors or clunky, poorly worded sentences. 

I put a lot of time and effort into my books, and I want them to look as good as possible before I show them to anyone. You should do the same. Your stories are important; they are a part of who you are, and you should make them as good as you can.
