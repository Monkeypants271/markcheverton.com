-   You receive a message from an alien civilization. It’s a warning.
-   After strange lights appear in the sky, you find an alien spacecraft in your backyard.
-   You’re abducted by aliens, but they need your help to save their planet.
-   One night, you notice a new star in the sky. The next day, aliens land in your town.
-   Your best friend reveals they are actually an alien in disguise.
-   Aliens have been living among humans for centuries. You discover you’re one of them.
-   A UFO crash lands near your home, and you’re the first to investigate.
-   You wake up to find that the entire planet has been taken over by aliens.
-   The alien race that once visited Earth left behind a device. You’ve just figured out how to turn it on.
-   You’re chosen as Earth’s ambassador to a newly discovered alien species.
