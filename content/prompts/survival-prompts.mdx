-   You and your friends get lost in the woods during a camping trip.
-   A storm hits your town, and you have to survive without power for a week.
-   You’re stranded in the middle of the ocean—what do you do to survive?
-   A plane crashes in the mountains, and you must find your way to safety.
-   You discover a secret underground bunker, but it’s not empty.
-   A natural disaster strikes your hometown—how do you survive?
-   You’re trapped in a snowstorm with limited supplies—what happens next?
-   A group of kids is stranded on a deserted island—how do they survive?
-   A character gets lost in the desert—what do they do to find help?
-   You must survive for 24 hours in the wild without any modern technology.
