-   Write a day in the life of your pet, from their point of view.
-   A group of animals forms a secret society. What is their mission?
-   You discover you can talk to animals. What is the first conversation you have?
-   Imagine a world where animals rule and humans are their pets.
-   A dog and a cat team up to solve a mystery. What happens?
-   An owl delivers an urgent message to you. What is it?
-   You find a lost baby elephant in your backyard. What do you do?
-   Write about a world where people can transform into their favorite animals.
-   An animal becomes the mayor of your town. How do they run things?
-   Write a story about an unlikely friendship between a lion and a mouse.
