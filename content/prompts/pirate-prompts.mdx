-   Write about a pirate who’s afraid of water but loves treasure hunting.
-   A pirate ship is cursed to sail forever unless they find a specific island.
-   The captain’s parrot starts giving mysterious advice that always comes true.
-   A young pirate discovers they’re actually royalty.
-   Write about a pirate crew that can only sail at night.
-   A treasure map leads to an island that doesn’t exist on any known chart.
-   A pirate ship sails into a storm that no one believes they can survive.
-   The pirates discover their treasure is cursed. What happens next?
-   A pirate crew encounters a sea monster that guards the lost city of Atlantis.
-   The pirate captain has a secret that could destroy the crew.
