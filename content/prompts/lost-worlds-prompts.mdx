-   You stumble upon a portal to a forgotten world, but there’s no way back. What do you do?
-   A hidden island emerges from the ocean, rumored to be full of treasures, but also dangers. You lead the expedition.
-   While hiking in the mountains, you find an ancient map that leads to an uncharted valley. What secrets does it hold?
-   Deep in the jungle, you find the remains of a civilization that vanished thousands of years ago. How did they disappear?
-   An underwater cave leads to a lost world where dinosaurs still roam. You’re the first person to discover it.
-   You find a lost city in the desert that seems abandoned—until nightfall.
-   A plane crashes in an unknown land where time moves differently. Will you find a way out?
-   You fall through a crack in the earth into a world where nothing works like it does on the surface.
-   An ancient book describes a lost world inside the Earth. Against all odds, you manage to find the entrance.
-   The stars on an old map suddenly align, revealing the location of a hidden world. What happens when you arrive?
