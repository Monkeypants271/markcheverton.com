-   You’ve been transported inside Minecraft. What’s the first thing you build?
-   Write about surviving your first night in a new Minecraft world.
-   You discover a hidden dimension in Minecraft. What’s it like?
-   A new mob appears in Minecraft that no one has seen before. Describe it.
-   Your Minecraft character comes to life. What happens next?
-   You’re building a massive castle in Minecraft when something unexpected happens.
-   Write about finding a village that holds a dark secret.
-   You’ve been challenged to a duel by a Minecraft skeleton. How do you prepare?
-   Describe the most epic Minecraft adventure you’ve ever imagined.
-   You accidentally mine into an ancient, forgotten structure. What do you find?
