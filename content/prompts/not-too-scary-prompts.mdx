-   You hear footsteps in the attic at night, but no one is supposed to be there.
-   The fog is thicker than usual, and strange figures seem to be moving in it.
-   A door in your house suddenly appears where there wasn’t one before.
-   A package arrives with no name, just a strange symbol.
-   Write about an old mirror that shows different reflections.
-   Your shadow starts moving on its own.
-   You’re alone in the forest, and you hear someone calling your name.
-   A strange message appears on your phone, but it’s in a language you’ve never seen.
-   A doll in your house keeps changing positions when no one’s looking.
-   Write about a painting in your house that changes every time you look at it.
