-   In a future where emotions are outlawed, you discover a hidden group that teaches people how to feel again. Do you join them?
-   Society is divided into those who live inside domes and those who survive in the wastelands. One day, you get a chance to switch sides. Do you take it?
-   In a world where water is more valuable than gold, you stumble upon a hidden reservoir. How do you protect it?
-   The government implants chips in everyone’s brains to control their thoughts, but yours is malfunctioning, giving you freedom. What do you do next?
-   All human decisions are made by an advanced AI, but you’ve discovered a way to manipulate the algorithm. How do you use this power?
-   In a future where reading books is banned, you find an underground library. What secrets do the books hold?
-   After a global catastrophe, children are raised by machines. One day, you find out the machines have a hidden agenda.
-   The world is ruled by mega-corporations, and you’re hired to take down one of them from the inside. How do you accomplish your mission?
-   People live their entire lives in virtual reality, but you uncover a flaw that could destroy the entire system. Do you expose it?
-   In a future where time travel is illegal, you become part of an underground group that uses time travel to try to fix the broken world. What challenges do you face?
