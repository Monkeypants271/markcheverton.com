-   You find a magical book that can make anything happen—what do you do first?
-   A character discovers they come from a long line of wizards.
-   You get accepted into a secret school for witches and wizards.
-   A magic spell goes wrong, and you need to figure out how to reverse it.
-   Write about a world where everyone has one magical ability.
-   You find a magic wand, but it only works in unpredictable ways.
-   A magician grants you one wish, but there’s a catch.
-   You accidentally turn your best friend into an animal—how do you fix it?
-   You open a portal to a world where magic is real—what happens next?
-   A magical creature asks you for help with an important quest.
