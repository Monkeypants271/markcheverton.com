-   Two best friends discover a secret about each other that changes everything.
-   A group of friends tries to save their favorite hangout spot from being destroyed.
-   Write about a new student who struggles to make friends but finds one in an unexpected way.
-   Two friends get into a huge argument—how do they resolve it?
-   A shy kid befriends someone who turns out to be a superhero.
-   What happens when two best friends find out they are moving away from each other?
-   Write about a friendship that forms between two people who seem like opposites.
-   One friend must save the other from a tricky situation, but how?
-   A friendship is tested when one friend keeps a big secret.
-   A group of friends starts a club with a strange but exciting mission.
