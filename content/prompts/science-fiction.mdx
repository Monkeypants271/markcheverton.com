**SCIENCE FICTION**

-   Write about a world where robots have taken over all jobs.
-   You wake up to find that the Earth has two moons. How does this change life on the planet?
-   A spaceship crashes into your backyard. What happens next?
-   You are the first human to live on Mars. What do you miss the most about Earth?
-   Design a futuristic gadget that changes the way people live.
-   A time traveler arrives from the year 3021. What advice do they give you?
-   The stars begin to disappear one by one. What’s going on?
-   You invent a machine that can read minds. What happens when you use it?
-   Aliens have invited humans to join their intergalactic council. Write about the first meeting.
-   You accidentally get teleported to an alien planet. What do you see and experience?
