-   You discover a way to enter other people’s dreams, but one night, you find yourself trapped in someone’s nightmare. How do you escape?
-   Every time you dream, you visit the same strange city. One night, the people there start recognizing you. What happens next?
-   You wake up from a vivid dream only to realize that everything in the real world has changed to match what you saw.
-   You and your best friend have the same dream every night. What does it mean, and how do you solve the mystery?
-   A new app allows you to control your dreams, but after using it for a week, you realize it’s affecting your waking life.
-   After meeting a mysterious figure in a dream, you start receiving messages from them in real life. What are they trying to tell you?
-   You find out that the events in your dreams predict the future, but no one believes you until it’s too late.
-   Every dream you’ve ever had starts merging into one continuous story. How do you navigate through it and find your way back to reality?
-   You find a dream journal that belongs to a stranger, but the dreams inside start coming true in your life.
-   In your dreams, you’re the hero of a fantasy world, but one day, the people there beg you not to wake up. Why?
