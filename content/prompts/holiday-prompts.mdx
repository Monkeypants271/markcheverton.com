-   Santa’s reindeer go on strike. What happens to Christmas?
-   You find a secret message hidden in your Christmas stocking. What does it say?
-   Write about a Halloween where all the costumes come to life.
-   You wake up on Christmas morning to find snow inside your house. What’s going on?
-   A group of elves has gone rogue and is causing mischief. How do you stop them?
-   On Halloween, you meet a ghost who needs your help. What do they want?
-   A mysterious gift shows up on your doorstep on Christmas Eve. What’s inside?
-   The holiday lights in your town start behaving strangely. What’s happening?
-   Write about a Halloween where trick-or-treaters disappear into another world.
-   A magical Christmas tree grants you one wish. What do you wish for?
