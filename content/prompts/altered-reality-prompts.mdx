-   One day, gravity stops working. What happens?
-   You wake up and realize everything is in black and white. What changed?
-   Write about a world where everyone can fly but you.
-   Time moves backward for one day. What happens?
-   Every time you make a decision, a parallel universe is created. What are the consequences?
-   You find a pair of glasses that lets you see the future.
-   In your world, people’s emotions change the weather. How do you feel today?
-   One day, everyone in the world wakes up with superpowers – except for you.
-   Write about a place where all your dreams become reality for one day.
-   You discover a hidden switch that can pause time. What do you do with it?
