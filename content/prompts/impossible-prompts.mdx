-   Everyone wakes up one morning with wings, but no one knows how to fly.
-   Time starts running backward. How does your day unfold?
-   Every person in the world can read minds, but only for 5 minutes a day.
-   Write about a world where everyone can walk through walls.
-   Water starts running uphill instead of downhill.
-   What would happen if the sun turned green?
-   You can talk to animals, but they refuse to listen to you.
-   The oceans turn into clouds and the clouds into oceans.
-   Everyone wakes up with an extra arm. How do they adjust?
-   You discover a new color that no one else can see.
