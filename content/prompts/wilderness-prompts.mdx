-   You’re lost in the forest, and the animals seem to be following you. Why?
-   Write about a group of kids who must survive in the wilderness after a camping trip goes wrong.
-   A strange light appears in the sky every night in the wilderness. What does it mean?
-   You find an abandoned cabin in the middle of the woods. What’s inside?
-   A storm traps you in the wilderness with only a backpack. How do you survive?
-   Write about a mountain climb that reveals a hidden world at the summit.
-   A forest ranger discovers strange symbols carved into the trees.
-   You find an ancient map in the wilderness, leading to a hidden treasure.
-   While hiking, you come across a mysterious creature no one has ever seen before.
-   You’re on a camping trip, and your tent mysteriously disappears overnight.
