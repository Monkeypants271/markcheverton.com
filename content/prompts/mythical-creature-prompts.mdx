-   A mermaid saves you from drowning, but in exchange, you must help her find a lost artifact in the human world.
-   You stumble upon a hidden unicorn sanctuary, and now you must protect it from poachers who want their magical powers.
-   After finding a griffin’s egg, you are tasked with raising it while keeping it a secret from the rest of the world.
-   You encounter a mischievous leprechaun who tricks you into a dangerous game for a chance at his hidden gold.
-   A centaur appears at your school, claiming you’re the only one who can solve an ancient prophecy involving their homeland.
-   A group of shape-shifting werewolves has secretly been protecting your town, but now they need your help to stop a growing threat.
-   You discover a hidden portal to a world of fairies, and the only way to return home is to complete a task set by the Fairy Queen.
-   A friendly giant asks for your assistance in finding his lost family, who disappeared without a trace centuries ago.
-   A phoenix offers you a single feather that can grant one wish, but using it comes with unforeseen consequences.
-   You befriend a mischievous kelpie (a water spirit) who drags people into the water but promises to help you if you fulfill a dangerous request.
-   After discovering a satyr in the forest, you learn that he’s the last of his kind and is being hunted by an ancient order.
-   You find a relic that allows you to summon a Gorgon, but controlling her power proves more dangerous than expected.
-   A sphinx appears in your dreams every night, challenging you with riddles that could unlock the secrets of an ancient world.
-   You discover that the family of griffins you’ve been observing are in danger of extinction, and only you can protect their sacred nesting ground.
-   A basilisk has been released from its ancient prison, and you must figure out how to stop it before it turns your town to stone.
