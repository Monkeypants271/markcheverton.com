-   Write about a time you overcame a fear.
-   If you could change one thing about the world, what would it be and why?
-   What does it mean to be brave?
-   If you could speak to your future self, what would you ask?
-   What makes a person truly happy?
-   Write about a time when kindness changed someone’s day.
-   What is one thing you’ve learned that changed the way you see the world?
-   How would you describe the importance of friendship?
-   If you had one day to make the world a better place, what would you do?
-   What does success mean to you?
