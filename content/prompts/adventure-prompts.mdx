-   You and your best friend find an ancient map in the attic. What happens next?
-   A character wakes up in a different country with no idea how they got there.
-   A kid discovers a portal to another world hidden in their backyard.
-   You and your friends go on a hike, but you get lost in an enchanted forest.
-   A treasure hunt leads to a discovery that no one could have imagined.
-   A hot air balloon ride goes off course, and the adventure begins.
-   Write about a time when an ordinary day turns into the adventure of a lifetime.
-   A mysterious stranger leaves a clue that leads to a great adventure.
-   Two friends must solve riddles to unlock a treasure hidden for centuries.
-   A storm traps you on a deserted island—how do you survive?
