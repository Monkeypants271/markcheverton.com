**FANTASY PROMPTS**

-   A dragon is guarding a treasure, but it’s not what you expect. What is the treasure?
-   You discover a hidden portal in your closet that leads to a magical world.
-   Your reflection in the mirror starts talking to you. What does it say?
-   You are a knight sent on a mission to rescue a princess, but she doesn’t want to be saved.
-   Write about a magical school that only teaches students one special skill.
-   An enchanted forest grows overnight in your town. What strange creatures live inside?
-   You wake up with the ability to turn invisible. What do you do with your new power?
-   A mysterious book in the library begins writing itself as you read it. What’s the story?
-   You find a wand that grants three wishes, but there’s a catch to each wish.
-   A talking animal invites you on a magical quest. Where do you go and what happens?
