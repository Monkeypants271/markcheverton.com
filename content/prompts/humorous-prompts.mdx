-   You wake up to discover you’ve turned into your pet for a day.
-   A clumsy wizard accidentally turns your homework into a talking animal. What does it say?
-   Your favorite video game character comes to life and asks you for advice.
-   You’re the mayor of a town where it rains pizza. How do you manage the chaos?
-   Write about the most embarrassing thing that could happen at school – and how you handle it.
-   A group of chickens decides to take over your town. How do they do it?
-   You’re having the worst hair day ever, and your hair seems to have a mind of its own.
-   A magical pencil can only write jokes. What does it come up with?
-   You discover that your teacher is actually a superhero in disguise. What’s their power?
-   Write about a day where every object you touch turns into candy.
