-   You build a robot that starts thinking for itself—what does it do?
-   A kid befriends a robot who’s on the run from a secret government agency.
-   You wake up one day to find that all the adults have been replaced by robots.
-   A robot at your school begins acting strangely—what’s going on?
-   Write about a world where robots and humans live side by side.
-   You program a robot to do your chores, but it goes out of control.
-   A robot built for a specific task starts to rebel—what happens next?
-   A robot from the future shows up at your door—what does it want?
-   You accidentally activate a long-dormant robot army—what do you do?
-   You and your robot friend team up to stop a villainous AI.
