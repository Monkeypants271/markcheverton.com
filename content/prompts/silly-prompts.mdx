-   Write about a cat who starts giving piano lessons.
-   Imagine a world where everyone speaks in rhymes.
-   A snail decides it wants to enter a 100-meter dash race.
-   What would happen if your pet could talk but only said dad jokes?
-   Write about a day where gravity stops working for an hour.
-   A squirrel becomes the mayor of your town. How does it run things?
-   What if the moon was made of cheese and began melting?
-   A giraffe tries to become a famous ballet dancer.
-   Write about a sandwich that grants magical powers.
-   One day, all the cars in your town turn into roller skates.
