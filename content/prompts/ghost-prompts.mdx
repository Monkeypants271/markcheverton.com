-   A friendly ghost just wants to make new friends but keeps scaring people away.
-   Write about a haunted house that’s not scary, but full of mischief.
-   A ghost is stuck in the school library and needs help to move on.
-   You move into a house and discover it has a resident ghost with unfinished business.
-   A ghost can only communicate through song lyrics.
-   Write about a ghost that has to help someone find a lost item before it can move on.
-   A ghost shows up every year on the same day but no one knows why.
-   You find an old photograph, and the ghost in the picture starts following you around.
-   A ghost is haunting a playground, but no one knows why.
-   You’re the only one who can see the ghost that lives in your house.
