-   You’re an astronaut on a mission to Mars, but something goes terribly wrong.

-   You find a mysterious spacecraft in your backyard—what happens next?
-   Your space shuttle crashes on a distant planet filled with strange creatures.
-   A group of friends gets chosen to be the first kids in space.
-   You’re traveling through space when you receive a distress signal—what do you do?
-   Write about a race across the galaxy to find a hidden treasure.
-   A black hole suddenly appears in your town—what’s on the other side?
-   You discover that your school is actually a training ground for future astronauts.
-   A space mission goes wrong, and you’re stranded on a space station.
