-   You find a time machine in your school’s basement—where do you go first?
-   A character travels back in time and accidentally changes the future.
-   You get stuck in a time loop, reliving the same day over and over.
-   Your future self shows up with an urgent message—what is it?
-   A strange watch lets you visit any moment in time for one hour.
-   You travel back to the time of the dinosaurs but lose the way home.
-   A school project accidentally sends you back in time—how do you return?
-   Write about a character who meets a historical figure and learns a surprising truth.
-   You time travel to the future and find out something shocking about your life.
-   You travel to the past, but no one believes you’re from the future.
