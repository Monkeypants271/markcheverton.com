-   You wake up with an amazing superpower—what do you do first?
-   A kid realizes their parents are secretly superheroes.
-   A new superhero arrives in town but has a strange weakness.
-   Your character must decide between using their powers for good or evil.
-   A team of kids discovers they have hidden abilities when they’re all together.
-   Write about a superhero who loses their powers at the worst possible moment.
-   A villain has taken over your school—how do you stop them?
-   You can hear people’s thoughts for one day—what do you learn?
-   A superhero must go undercover at a middle school—what happens?
-   A young superhero discovers their biggest power is not what they expected.
