-   The circus comes to town, but something seems off. What’s going on behind the scenes?
-   You meet a clown who never smiles. What’s their story?
-   Strange things start happening after you visit the circus. What’s the connection?
-   Write about a circus where all the performers have supernatural abilities.
-   One of the circus tents is always closed to the public. What’s inside?
-   You’re invited to join a traveling circus, but there’s a dark secret you must uncover.
-   A circus performer goes missing during a show. What happens?
-   You enter the haunted house at the circus, but it’s more real than you expected.
-   Write about a circus where the animals are more than they seem.
-   A ringmaster offers you a choice: leave now or stay forever.
