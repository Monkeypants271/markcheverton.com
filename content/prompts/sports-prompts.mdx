-   Write about a basketball game where the rules suddenly change halfway through.
-   A soccer team discovers their field is enchanted.
-   The championship game is interrupted by a sudden, unexpected event.
-   A player on your team can control the wind. How does that affect the game?
-   Write about a gymnast who’s scared of heights but has to perform on the high bar.
-   Your team’s star player mysteriously disappears before the big game.
-   The scoreboard starts showing strange numbers no one understands.
-   A runner in a marathon discovers a shortcut but must decide whether to use it.
-   A tennis player finds a racket that grants them incredible skills, but with a catch.
-   A team of underdogs enters a national competition. How do they beat the odds?
