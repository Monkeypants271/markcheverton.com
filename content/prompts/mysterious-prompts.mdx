-   You find an old key buried in your backyard. What does it unlock?
-   A strange letter arrives in your mailbox with no return address. What does it say?
-   You’re in a bookstore, and a book falls off the shelf and opens to a mysterious page.
-   Write about a time when the lights flickered, and something strange happened.
-   A mysterious figure follows you home. What do they want?
-   You discover an ancient map hidden in your attic. What does it lead to?
-   Your town has a secret that no one talks about. What is it?
-   The clock strikes midnight, and suddenly, everything changes.
-   You receive a package addressed to someone else. Inside is something unbelievable.
-   There’s a hidden door in your house you’ve never noticed before. Where does it lead?
